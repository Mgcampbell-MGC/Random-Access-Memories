# SOL Estúdio: the expansion plan

5 Oct 2026, for the founder and Sol. **Nothing here has been tested with a paying client.** The 20 beauty calls come
first; every later step waits for a result from the step before it.

## 1. The rule

- **Grow in this order: prove beauty, sell more to each client, then add markets.**
- **Add a market because it pays more or buys more often, never because the list ran out.** It won't run out:
    - the beauty list has **1.239 brands** with a known owner and a contact. At about 120 contacts a month, that is 9–10
      months of calls;
    - the monthly INPI feed adds about **50–90 new brands with a contact every week**, across all markets.

## 2. Where we start

| What | Now |
|---|---|
| **Market** | Brazilian beauty brands that just filed new products with ANVISA, plus new beauty trademarks at INPI |
| **List** | 3.590 beauty brands: **A 293** (call first) · B 946 · C 2.351 (mostly no contact) |
| **Main product** | O LANÇAMENTO, **R$2.990 per launch** |

| **Expected** | **10–25 paying brands from today's list, about 15 most likely.** This is a guess: no call has been made yet |

## 3. Price

- **Keep R$2.990 for the 20 calls.**
    - Small brands spend less than this today. Every brand budget we found was under US$500 a project. A UGC video
      costs R$100–250 and an AI image R$53–60.
    - A traditional São Paulo launch shoot with a model and a film costs R$6.878–13.255.
- **It is a good rate for Sol, because the machine is fast.** A launch takes her at most 3 hours and four images about
  1 hour (the founder's figures, 6 Oct; the dry runs will record them). After R$52–380 of tool costs a launch earns
  about R$870–980 an hour. The goal needs R$290–360.
- **So the limit is sales, not production.** 4–5 launches a month take about 15 hours of her ~87. Even R$1.490 pays
  about R$370–480 an hour, so a lower price would double the sales needed, not break her hourly rate.
- **Never discount on a call.** If a brand can't pay, offer a smaller product, not a lower price.
- **Lower (already agreed):** after the 20 calls, the KEEP, SWITCH and STOP bars in the one-page manual decide. SWITCH
  means ESSENCIAL R$1.490 + FILME R$990.
- **Raise (new, proposed):** if **10 or more of the 20** spent **R$5.000 or more** on their last launch, quote
  **R$3.900** to the next 10 brands. If they still buy at about the same rate, keep it.

## 4. Make more from each brand

1. **Sell more to the same brand.**
    - The median A brand launched 5 new products in 90 days.
    - One launch plus two more products in the same look every quarter is R$4.370 a quarter, about **R$17.500 a year**.
    - The products for this already exist: EXTENSÃO R$690 · A LINHA (3 products) R$4.900 · TEMPORADA (4 launches
      prepaid, only after 2 purchases) R$9.900.
    - **Measure it: 3 of the first 10 clients must buy again within 6 months.** Below that, the business is a
      treadmill.
2. **Call bigger brands early.**
    - 5 of the 20 calls go to the 176 larger companies in grade B.
    - Log them separately, so they don't skew the main test. They show what the top of the market pays.
3. **Foreign brands in dollars, later.**
    - It is the one way past Brazil's small-business price ceiling.
    - Only after the product works here. English-language selling stays in writing, and US tools are cheaper there.

## 5. New services: only when brands ask

- **AI UGC ad** (an AI person holding the product and talking to camera):
    - an add-on to O LANÇAMENTO only, about **R$990**. Never sold on its own: a real creator costs R$100–250;
    - the label is guaranteed only in shots where the pack is still. Moving-hand shots can't be checked yet;
    - always labelled as AI. The AI person never poses as a real customer;
    - before selling it, make one test ad for the fictional brand (it costs credits, so the founder decides). Time it
      and set the price from that;
    - **build it properly (tracking the moving hand) only if 5 or more of the 20 calls ask for it.**
- **Monthly content packages:** not now. Each monthly client takes 3–5 hours a month of contact, and Sol can hold only
  8–10 clients that way. TEMPORADA is the repeat product.
- **Never offered:** 3D turn, before and after, simulated results, exact makeup colour.

## 7. Not a fit

- **Clothing and jewellery:** the product itself is the picture, not a printed label, and AI changes it. The checker
  can't prove a garment.
- **Electronics:** the device's shape and screen are the picture, not the label.
- **Real estate and dental clinics:** services, not packaged products. No launch list, no label.

## 8. What we measure before each step

| Check | Bar | It decides |
|---|---|---|
| The 20 beauty calls | KEEP, SWITCH and STOP bars (one-page manual) | The price |
| 5 calls to bigger brands | 10 or more of all 20 spent R$5.000+ | Whether to raise to R$3.900 |
| First 10 clients | 3 or more buy again within 6 months | Whether to add markets |
| Each new pack shape | The checker passes on a fictional product, and catches the planted error | Whether to sell it |
| Brands asking for UGC | 5 or more of the 20 calls | Whether to build it |

## 9. What builds the business, not just the next sale

- **The machine and its checker.** Every pack shape it can prove opens a market.
- **Each client's look, kept on file.** The next product takes Sol about an hour and costs the brand R$690.
- **The lists.** The monthly INPI feed never runs dry.
- **The call log.** What brands spent, what they asked for and what they bought. Nobody else has it.
