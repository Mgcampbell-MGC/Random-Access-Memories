**Bridge Substation Fleet (transmission-class mobile substations & large power transformers, 25-100 MVA / 138-345 kV)** — weighted 5.35, honest CoC 6%, net $45,000/unit/yr
- scores: margin/KISS 2 · passivity 7 · demand 9 · moat 5 · abs-cash 2 · finance 7
- Strongest, best-sourced demand teeth of any play (data center earns $0/day until energized, 2-4yr transformer lead times, can't-buy-in-time renter), but the entire revenue line rests on an unpublishable RFQ rate no vendor discloses and no comp verifies, collapsing honest margin to ~8% and CoC to ~6% — a direct fail of the buyer's #1 fat-simple-cashflow filter.
