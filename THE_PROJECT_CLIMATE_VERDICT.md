# PROJECT CLIMATE 2.0 — VERIFIED AND KILLED, 10 Sep 2026

**A fully-written external plan for a Brazilian tropical-performance haircare DTC brand (a portable anti-frizz
"stick" sold via TikTok Shop) was supplied as a candidate for the founder's offer: for the RIGHT brand idea,
she would raise real capital and commit real time beyond the standing US$3,000/87h ceiling. The founder then
corrected the premise — she can start for US$3,000, "even though the plan says more — more of it is padded."
Both questions were run properly: what does a real US$3,000-ceiling version cost, and does the underlying idea
survive primary-source verification. Nine research clusters plus a judge. It does not survive.**

## 1. What was fabricated, understated, or simply real

**A second fabricated statistic, independently re-checked by me.** The plan claims Hidratei's stick shows
*"more than 5.000 ratings."* The workflow found the page's own schema.org data reports **363 reviews**. I
re-checked separately via search and found **346, 4.9★** on the live product page — a different sampling of
the same page, landing in the same low-hundreds range. **Nowhere near 5.000, by an order of magnitude.** This
is the file's fourth-plus caught fabrication pattern, this time in a supplied external document rather than an
agent's own output.

**TikTok's fee structure is understated.** The plan uses 6% + R$6/item (confirmed, current). **TikTok Shop
Brazil's own Seller Center discloses a second, additional 6% "PTE" free-shipping fee layered on top** — real
all-in cost for a competitive free-shipping listing is closer to **12% + R$6**, not 6%. A third, undisclosed
*"Taxa de Transação"* is referenced in TikTok's own ToS but visible only inside a seller's private dashboard.

**TikTok's growth stats: two of three hold.** 102× average daily GMV and 46× active affiliates are both
**CONFIRMED verbatim at TikTok's own newsroom.** The third figure — 151% H1-2026-vs-H2-2025 beauty growth — is
**UNVERIFIABLE at any TikTok primary source**, tracing only to trade press quoting a spokesperson at a
briefing. Do not cite it as TikTok's own data.

**Manufacturers check out.** Hibiscus (500-unit stick MOQ, ≤30-day sample) and HRT (300-unit general lots,
30–60 days) both confirm essentially verbatim at their own sites — the one cluster of the plan that reads as
genuinely primary-sourced.

## 2. ★★★ THE CATALOGUE SCISSORS, FIRING AT FULL STRENGTH — the category-defining finding

**A portable anti-frizz stick is a crowded, price-compressed catalogue category in Brazil today, not an
underserved one.** On Amazon.com.br alone — not even TikTok Shop, the plan's own channel — **64 results for
*"stick finalizador cabelo,"* 30 for *"stick anti frizz cabelo,"* and at least 20–25 distinct brands** already
sell the identical 10–20g format: Alva, K.Pro, Lene, Bioclub, Anaconda, Neez, Hidratei, Stick Fixx, Zero Frizz,
Amend, Forever Liss and more. **Prices cluster R$88–300 — above, not below, the plan's own R$78–120 range**, so
the plan sits at the *low* end of an already-crowded shelf. **47 of 64 listings carry a visible review count,
up to 1.444 on the top listing** — real, established purchase history the plan's own five-competitor table
never disclosed. Mercado Livre and Shopee turn up further non-overlapping brands, pushing the true count past
25–30 SKUs.

> **This is the exact condition this archive's `CATALOGUE SCISSORS` was written to kill: an item any buyer
> reaches in under two minutes without speaking to anyone, at an established price band, with real reviews —
> which means no margin, unless a genuinely differentiated claim is welded on. No such claim currently exists
> that isn't already occupied by a funded incumbent (see below).**

## 3. Two things that could kill it on their own, independent of the market

**(a) RDC 814/2023 "pomada" classification — the single highest-leverage open question in the plan.** The
trigger is narrow and mechanical: full registro fires if the declared physical form is *"pomada,"* the word
appears anywhere on the label in any language, or the product contains ≥20% ethoxylated alcohols. **Every
named competitor calls its product a "stick," never a "pomada" — almost certainly deliberately.** No primary
ANVISA ruling classifying anti-frizz sticks specifically was found. If it tips into registro, R$5.229–7.262
alone consumes a third to half of the entire capital ceiling — not in the §0 budget at all.

**(b) THE REGULATORY COLLISION — a real structural conflict this archive had previously overstated the wrong
direction on.** RDC 894/2024 art. 46 §1º, read at primary source: what is forbidden is outsourcing
cosmetovigilance to an **external autonomous third party** — the role itself is not reserved to a licensed
council. The bar (art. 20) is *"formação adequada à função (nível técnico ou superior)"* — general
technical-or-higher education. Self-designation by the company's own owner may qualify — **if she has that
education, which this research cannot verify and the plan never addresses.**

> **If she does not qualify, the role must go to an employee or a functionally-subordinate arrangement — which
> ANVISA's own guidance rejects a standing autonomous-contractor structure for. An employee is exactly what
> `C3` ("no employees, no contractors, ever") forbids.** Whether "find the money and more time for the right
> idea" also implicitly lifts C3 was never asked. **This plan's viability may hinge on an unanswered policy
> question that has nothing to do with the market.**

## 4. The kill-laws — four of five fire

- **Free front door — FAILS.** TRESemmé already owns the *"instrumentally tested, 72-hour, humidity-resistant"*
  narrative at funded, mass-retail scale.
- **Observed-price ceiling — FAILS.** R$99,90 sits inside an established R$88–300 band held by 25–30
  incumbents with real review histories she does not have.
- **Catalogue scissors — FAILS decisively.** §2, above.
- **Replacement-cost ceiling / speculative-capture — FAILS.** The plan's own stated moat (accumulated field
  tests, creator relationships, the "Climate Lab" dataset) is **empty at launch by construction** — a funded
  copycat using the same open manufacturers and the same TikTok tooling replicates the mechanism immediately.
- **Comfort/C8 — PASSES, but thinly.** Beauty is genuinely her domain. But the plan arrived with **no
  Sol-specific framing** — it draws on no proprietary insight from her modelling background (agency
  deductions, sample sizing, how a humid-climate shoot actually runs) that a generic DTC founder wouldn't also
  have. It fits her world; it doesn't yet use what she specifically knows about it.

## 5. ★★★ THE HEADLINE VERDICT — NO-GO ON THE PLAN AS WRITTEN

**Do not place the manufacturing PO.** The category is provably crowded, the manufacturers who'd build it will
build the identical thing for any competitor tomorrow, the one credible performance narrative is already owned
by a funded incumbent, the honestly-rebuilt budget still likely overshoots the raised ceiling before a single
differentiation dollar is spent, and a live regulatory question could alone consume a third of the capital.
**This is the catalogue-scissors trap this archive has already killed in every other physical-goods costume
it has tried.**

**One narrow gap surfaced, and it earns diligence, not a PO:** TRESemmé's 72h humidity claim is on **liquid**
haircare, not the **stick** format — no stick-format competitor currently pairs a real instrumentally-
substantiated humidity claim with portability. But it needs real testing spend not budgeted anywhere in §0,
offers no durable moat (the same manufacturers build it for the next brand that asks), and a substantiated
performance claim may make RDC 814 registration *more* likely, not less.

## 6. The three riskiest assumptions, and the cheapest test for each — before any PO

1. **RDC 814 classification of a "stick."** Test: one written regulatory-consultant opinion or an ANVISA
   Solicita pre-consultation on the exact planned form and label copy. **~R$300–800 versus a R$5.000+
   surprise.**
2. **True per-unit COGS at a real batch, and whether HRT's 300-unit general MOQ genuinely covers sticks.**
   Test: two written RFQs, free, one email each — this single unverified number already consumes 77%+ of the
   entire ceiling at the plan's own assumption.
3. **Whether Sol's own education qualifies her for the cosmetovigilance role under RDC 894/2024 art. 20**,
   settling whether C3 is even in play here. Test: one written question, free.
