================================================================================
IDEA HUNT PROMPT — V6
A brief for another AI, to help find a business for one specific person.
Written 14 August 2026, after 67+ candidates and roughly 80 documented kills.
================================================================================

READ ALL OF THIS BEFORE PROPOSING ANYTHING. The single most common failure mode
is proposing something that was already killed, for a reason recorded here. The
second most common is proposing something that fails a hard constraint stated in
Section 1. The third is asserting a fact from a vendor's marketing page.

If, after reading, your honest conclusion is "nothing fits", say that. A
well-evidenced "no" is worth more than a manufactured "yes". We have had
sixty-seven manufactured yeses.

SECTION 1 — THE FOUNDER, AND THE CONSTRAINTS THAT DO NOT BEND
================================================================================

She is a ~26-year-old Brazilian woman in São Paulo. Call her the founder. She is
intelligent, organised, an excellent writer, and unusually good at directing AI
tools to build working software. Her background is in luxury and premium brand
events. She holds a Google Project Management certificate and a Google Digital
Marketing certificate. She holds NO professional credential of any kind — she is
not an accountant, lawyer, engineer, auditor, doctor, or licensed anything.

--- THE HARD CONSTRAINTS -------------------------------------------------------

These are not preferences. They are not negotiable. They will not be relaxed
later. A proposal that violates one of them is not a proposal.

C1. SHE NEVER SPEAKS TO ANYONE. No phone calls. No video calls. No meetings. No
    webinars. No voice notes. No audio messages. In any language. Ever. Not for
    sales, not for onboarding, not for support, not for a "quick 15-minute
    intro". WRITTEN COMMUNICATION ONLY — email, chat, forms, documents. Screen
    recordings with no face and no voice are acceptable.

    This kills more candidates than any other constraint. Test it FIRST and test
    it HONESTLY. Do not invent workarounds. "She could hire someone to take
    calls" violates C3. "Most clients won't need a call" is not evidence.

C2. SHE IS PERMANENTLY FACELESS. No camera. No photographs. No personal brand.
    No founder story. No LinkedIn presence built on her identity. No
    audience-building, no content creation as a person, no becoming an
    influencer. She will not be the face of anything, ever. Any model that
    requires her to be known, followed, or personally trusted is dead.

C3. NO EMPLOYEES, NO CONTRACTORS, EVER. Not one, not part-time, not a virtual
    assistant, not a freelancer for overflow. Solo permanently. (A VENDOR or
    SUPPLIER relationship — buying a service from a company — is allowed. Hiring
    a person is not.)

C4. SHE NEVER TAKES CUSTODY OF A CUSTOMER CREDENTIAL. No passwords, no logins,
    no API keys, no certificates, no admin access to anyone's account or system.
    Ever. This kills nearly every "we'll set it up for you" business.

C5. TOTAL BUDGET: A FEW HUNDRED US DOLLARS. Not per month — total. She cannot
    buy ads at scale, cannot pay for a US or EU legal entity casually, cannot
    front inventory, cannot buy data licences, cannot afford professional
    indemnity insurance.

C6. TIME: ~30 hours per week.

C7. HER PERSONAL SÃO PAULO NETWORK IS NOT A SALES CHANNEL. She will not sell to
    friends, family, or acquaintances, and will not use them for introductions.

--- THE VETOES -----------------------------------------------------------------

V1. Construction as a buyer segment. Excluded entirely.
V2. Labour-compliance document chasing.
V3. Physical goods and inventory — UNLESS it is print-on-demand or made-to-order
    with zero inventory and zero capital. (See Section 5: Lulu's Print API is
    already verified and costed, so a printed object IS available to her.)
V4. Anything requiring her to be BELIEVED AS AN EXPERT. She has no credential,
    no track record, and no identity to stake. If the sale depends on the buyer
    trusting her judgement, it is dead.

--- THE TARGETS ----------------------------------------------------------------

Near-term:  R$12.000–15.000 per month  (~US$2.300–2.900)
Year three: R$30.000 per month         (~US$5.780)

Exchange rate used throughout: USD/BRL 5,19 (verified).

Note that R$30.000/month is SMALL. "Too small to matter" is a legitimate moat.
Do not propose venture-scale ideas; propose things a single person can hold.

SECTION 2 — THE LAWS
================================================================================

Each of these was paid for with a dead candidate. Apply all of them.

LAW 1 — THE FREE COMPETITOR LAW
  Content, templates, checkers and tools now cost almost nothing to produce, so
  every funded incumbent gives them away as lead generation. A solo operator
  selling an artefact is not competing with a product; it is competing with
  someone's MARKETING BUDGET, which is designed to give that artefact away.
  METHOD: hunt the free competitor BY FUNDING MODEL. Ask "who is funded to give
  this away, and why?" before anything else. Verified instances: Levelset,
  GleanMark, Estratégia/QConcursos, iNymbus/SPS, Insparisk/ViolationWatch,
  FamilySearch, Sebrae, ANVISA, APEX Accelerators ($55M/yr, 92 offices), Arelle,
  EDItEUR, W3C/DAISY, DDEX, the MLC, Grants.gov, Google Merchant Center, CMS.

CORRECTED LAW A1 — WHEN AN INCUMBENT ACTUALLY KILLS
  An incumbent only kills a candidate when it is FREE, or above ~20% MEASURED
  adoption in the specific target segment. A paid partial substitute does NOT
  kill. (This correction exists because a good candidate was wrongly killed by
  citing Adobe Acrobat at $19,99/month against a product Acrobat could not do.)

THE ARBITRAGE LAW
  A Brazilian cost base is only an advantage against a buyer who does NOT have
  one. Revenue in reais is structurally weak. STRONGLY prefer USD/GBP/EUR.
  Corollary discovered 14 Aug: the arbitrage often runs the WRONG way. Indian
  and Filipino bureaus already publish £4/page and $8–12/hour with unlimited
  revisions. Brazil is not the cheap option in most back-office work.

THE B2B BUYER LAW
  A business buyer brings a negotiation, a diligence process, an incumbent, and
  a $5–50/month price anchor. A consumer brings none of those.
  RUNNING TALLY: THIRTY B2B MODELS ASSESSED. ZERO SURVIVORS.
  This does not forbid B2B. It means a B2B proposal carries a heavy prior and
  must explain why it escapes what killed the other thirty.

THE INTEGRATION TAX
  Every product that lives inside the client's stack (CRM, inbox, helpdesk,
  seller account, accounting system, portal) is sold on a call and requires
  access. A 50-idea list was run through this filter and 38 died on it alone.
  The only version she can sell is the one needing no wiring.

THE VERIFIABILITY LAW (and its 6 Aug correction)
  The question is NOT "does she hold the credential?" It is:
  ***AFTER THE BUYER OPENS THE FILE, CAN THEY TELL WHETHER IT IS RIGHT?***
  If YES — her standing is irrelevant. Nobody asks who wrote their tax software.
  If NO — borrowed authority is the product, and it cannot be borrowed.

THE STAMP LAW  (derived 14 Aug 2026 — read this one twice)
  The property that makes a document business worth $33.600 instead of $99 is
  that A PERSON IS STANDING BEHIND IT — legally exposed, insured, reachable.
  A faceless seller with no credential and no professional indemnity cannot hold
  the stamp, and therefore cannot capture the price the stamp commands.
  TEST: NAME THE PERSON WHO GETS SUED IF THIS IS WRONG. If the business only
  works when that person is the seller, stop.
  This single law retroactively explains ten prior compliance kills: every one
  was the recurring/painful/document-heavy artefact priced as though it carried
  the stamp. It also restates the Brazilian reserved-profession kills — the
  reserved perimeter and the pricing premium are the same feature, because the
  state is deciding who is allowed to hold the stamp.
  Evidence of universality: a $23M/month operator's own list of nine high-value
  compliance categories was checked item by item. NINE OF NINE are stamp-gated.

THE GATE FRAME, AND WHY IT LARGELY FAILED  (14 Aug 2026)
  HYPOTHESIS: a machine can adjudicate in place of a person. Where a portal or
  validator ACCEPTS or REJECTS a submission, the buyer never has to trust the
  preparer — the gate decides.
  We swept SIX gate families in one day: customs filings, retail/marketplace
  data feeds, publishing and music metadata, financial and corporate e-filing,
  product/environmental compliance portals, healthcare claims, and government
  procurement portals. RESULT: ZERO SURVIVORS ACROSS ALL OF THEM.
  Two failure modes closed the entire space:
    (a) THE GATE OPERATOR WANTS YOU TO PASS, so it funds the free validator
        itself. Standards bodies, marketplaces and governments all want more
        conforming supply.
    (b) THE GATE ADJUDICATES IDENTITY, NOT THE ARTEFACT. Amazon checks a LUCID
        number, not a risk assessment. Customs checks a licence and a bond.
  THEREFORE:
  G4 — THE GATE OPERATOR MUST HAVE AN INCENTIVE TO KEEP CONFORMANCE SCARCE.
  Hunt only gates whose operator is INDIFFERENT or ADVERSARIAL to you passing.
  After six families we are not confident such a gate exists in any volume. If
  you propose a gate business, you must clear G4 explicitly and with evidence.

THE TRANSPOSITION LAW  (this one GENERATES — it is the most productive law here)
  A gap exists where two mature industries sit adjacent and neither has crossed
  into the other, because each is branded, priced and staffed for its own side.
  WORKED EXAMPLE: life-story services (StoryWorth, Remento, Memoirji) are
  branded for elders and legacy. Pregnancy keepsakes are a paper-goods industry
  selling blank journals. Two mature businesses, touching, neither crossing.
  The gap was a written pregnancy memoir — a hardcover that arrives already
  written, bought as a baby-shower gift. That is the file's best candidate.

THE CHAT-WINDOW TEST
  If a competent person with ChatGPT could produce the deliverable in one
  sitting, it is dead. The deliverable must be something a chat window CANNOT
  produce: A PHYSICAL OBJECT, A THIRD PARTY CONTACTED, OR CUSTODY HELD.
  Since she cannot hold custody (C4) and cannot contact third parties by voice
  (C1), a physical object is the most reliable escape.

THE SUBSTITUTE-COUNT LAW
  A print-on-demand product with a thousand substitutes needs bought traffic.
  One with ZERO substitutes needs only to be found once. The manufacturing model
  does not determine the economics; the substitute count does. A t-shirt design
  is a commodity. A book about one specific person's life has exactly one
  possible buyer and no substitute at any price.

NEVER SELL THE AGENT, SELL THE OUTPUT
  Any AI agent worth selling is free on GitHub within a year. Sell what it
  produces, never the thing itself, and never a guide to configuring one.

THE ALERT FINDING
  When the recipient of an alert is an OPPORTUNIST who profits by acting on it,
  the alert sells by itself at real prices (PropertyRadar $119–599/mo, Owler
  $39–350/mo). When the recipient is the SUBJECT of the problem — their own
  violation, liability or cost — the alert is free and all the money is in the
  fix. NO EXAMPLE WAS FOUND, ANYWHERE, of anyone selling a pure alert at a
  meaningful price to the party the alert is about.
  RULE: sell the trigger to whoever profits from it, never to whoever it
  happens to. If the only interested party is the one the event happened to,
  the alert is a lead magnet and the business is the remedy.

GATE 0 — CAN THE BUYER PAY?
  Prove it with a MEASURED size distribution before designing anything. This
  gate exists because a complete business case was written and committed before
  anyone checked the target population's bank balance: 70% of the target were
  under US$50.000 annual revenue. An organisation running on $40k/year does not
  pay $119/month.

ADVERSE SELECTION
  Companies carrying a visible public defect are frequently defective BECAUSE
  they are under-resourced. Neglect and poverty are the same phenomenon seen
  from two angles. The mechanism that makes a faceless seller credible — quoting
  the buyer's own public defect — simultaneously selects for the buyers least
  able to pay.
  Three escapes: (W1) sell to a different, solvent party who bears the exposure;
  (W2) target defects that scale with SIZE and COMPLEXITY rather than neglect;
  (W3) find a counterparty with power who withholds money until proof arrives.

"NOBODY SELLS THIS" HAS TWO EXPLANATIONS, AND THE SECOND IS MORE COMMON
  Absence of a competitor is evidence of an opportunity OR evidence of no value.
  Before building on an unserved defect, ask what happens TODAY when it goes
  uncaught. If the answer is "a free system catches it" or "nothing, it
  self-corrects", the space is empty for a reason.
  TEST: name the specific person who loses a specific amount of money when this
  defect goes undetected, and the mechanism by which they lose it. If the loss
  is "minutes of rework", stop.

THE TWO-BUCKET RULE — how to kill honestly
  BUCKET A — STRUCTURAL. These kill. Only five kinds qualify:
    A1  Someone ships the IDENTICAL product to the IDENTICAL buyer
    A2  A NAMED law reserves it to a credential she lacks
    A3  THE LIST DOES NOT EXIST — the buyer cannot be enumerated
    A4  THE PHYSICS FAIL — e.g. required send volume exceeds one domain
    A5  A cheaper substitute does THE WHOLE JOB, or a regulator gives it free
  BUCKET B — UNPROVEN. THESE MAY NOT KILL ANYTHING. Whether buyers pay this
    price; whether the gap over a PARTIAL substitute is worth paying for; the
    conversion rate; whether the buyer knows they have the problem; whether they
    trust an unknown vendor. Every Bucket B objection must arrive WITH THE
    CHEAPEST TEST THAT SETTLES IT — under two weeks, under US$200, with a
    numeric pass threshold. A Bucket B objection without a test attached is not
    a finding; it is a guess wearing the costume of rigour.
  WHY THIS MATTERS: a false kill costs more than a missed risk. A missed risk
  shows up in a cheap test and gets corrected. A FALSE KILL NEVER SHOWS UP AT
  ALL — the business simply never exists, and nothing ever contradicts it.

THE PRICING MECHANISM NOBODY HAS USED YET
  All 67 candidates were priced cost-plus or against a substitute. NOT ONE was
  priced against WHAT THE BUYER LOSES. A $33.600 certification quote was paid
  without negotiation because it was priced against the downside.
  Absorbing a LIABILITY downside requires the stamp. But there is a downside
  that requires no stamp and no credential: A MISSED WINDOW. A launch date, a
  season, a deadline, a shipping cut-off. Nobody signs an indemnity for speed —
  you either hit the date or you do not, and the buyer can see which.
  THIS IS UNEXPLOITED. If you can build on it, do.
