# THE PHYSICAL-PRESENCE HUNT — 2 Sep 2026
**Hunt agent 2 of 5. Brief: what does the world pay a person to be physically present for? It returned the
sharpest screen of the day, one legal clearance worth banking, two candidates, and an explicit refusal to
invent a third.**

## ★★★ THE SCREEN — one line that sorts the entire lens
At **US$2.200–2.800/month gross** and **43 h/month** she must realise **US$51–65 per hour of her time.** In São
Paulo a single field stop costs **3–4 hours door-to-door** once travel and write-up are counted.

> ### **ANY TASK THAT DOES NOT CLEAR ~US$180–250 PER STOP CANNOT BECOME THIS BUSINESS.**

Against observed prices: gig platforms **US$3–20** · WeGoLook **US$12–30** typical · US home-watch
**US$40–100/visit**. **All three to twenty times short.**

> **What clears it is never the walking. It is an observation that feeds a LEGAL OR FINANCIAL DECISION MADE BY
> A FIRM WITH MONEY AT STAKE. That is the whole of the survivable space.**

**★ And the second structural fact, which decides the shape:** physical needs are almost all **event-driven, not
recurring**. Nothing in this lens produces a monthly subscription except retail observation, which the gig
platforms already commoditised. **So recurrence must come from the BUYER being a repeat firm, not the task
repeating** — i.e. wholesale to 6–10 foreign firms, which is exactly her client cap.

## ★★ THE LEGAL CLEARANCE — bank this, it took a statute read to establish
**Fact-gathering for hire in Brazil is NOT a credentialled profession.** Lei 13.432/2017 (the investigator
statute) had **arts. 1, 2 §2, 3, 4 and 12-V VETOED**; the surviving text imposes **no licence, no council, no
course and no diploma.** Art. 7 requires only that services be recorded **in writing**.
**Contrast the adjacent lenses, all closed:** valuation (CREA/CAU) · notarial acts (cartório monopoly) ·
customs (despachante) · law (OAB). **The lens is legally open to her; its neighbours are not.**

## ★ CANDIDATE — CAMPO · the São Paulo field-verification desk for foreign due-diligence firms
She goes to an address and records what is actually there — occupancy, staff, signage, stock, whether the
"factory" is a room — and returns a dated photographic report. **She never signs a conclusion.** Sold wholesale
to US/UK corporate-investigation and enhanced-due-diligence firms who resell it inside their own reports.

**What forces the purchase:** FCPA and UK Bribery Act third-party due diligence, and AML onboarding. A file
saying *"we verified the counterparty"* cannot be closed from a desk. **Receita, JUCESP and Serasa tell you a
company is REGISTERED; none tells you the address contains an operation.**
**`THE SPLIT TEST` passes:** the party on the hook is the foreign firm; the input is generated by the Brazilian
subject being assessed.

## CANDIDATE 2 — HERANÇA · probate field desk *(cut down by the agent's own checks)*
Foreign probate genealogists earn ~20% of an estate **only if the heir is found and signs**, and cannot be in
Brazil. Observed price: Morse Genealogical publishes *"$1.500 minimum per side… $80 per year… **$275 per person
located**."* Batres Genealogy covers Brazil for *"international probate companies, banks, law firms."*
⚠ **But two ten-minute checks removed most of the work:** Brazilian civil certificates are now orderable
**online nationwide** (Arpen/CRC, ~R$78–102, delivered abroad) — **record retrieval is dead** — and under **CNJ
Provimento 100/2020** a public power of attorney can be granted **by videoconference** through e-Notariado.
**What remains is only the door-knock and the hand-holding.** Volume is unmeasured and the agent tried and
failed to measure it: the UK unclaimed-estates list is downloadable (5.494 rows) but carries **no nationality
and no place-of-birth column.**

## The honest headline
**The lens is legally open and arithmetically viable only above ~US$180–250 per stop, which happens only where
a firm's own money rides on the observation. That leaves two buyers — the compliance file and the
contingency-fee estate — and NEITHER COMPOUNDS.** Both are per-unit human labour sold to a handful of repeat
foreign firms. **Both are answerable by one written email round to ~25 firms asking a supplier-side price:
under US$100, no phone.**
