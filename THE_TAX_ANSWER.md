### ★ FINDING 2 — LUCRO PRESUMIDO BEATS SIMPLES, AND *FATOR R* IS A TRAP FOR HER

**This inverts standard Brazilian advice, and it inverts it BECAUSE of her two hard constraints.**

1. **Her real margin is ~95%. The presumption is 32%.** Lucro Presumido taxes her as if she earned a third of
   what she earns *(Lei 9.249/1995 art. 15 §1º III "a")*.
2. **Exporting deletes PIS, COFINS and ISS.** What remains is IRPJ + CSLL on a 32% base = **7,68%**.
3. **The CPP inside the Simples DAS is dead weight.** It is **43,40% of the whole Anexo III DAS** — she would
   be paying a payroll tax on a payroll of zero *(C3 forbids employees)*.

**And the killer detail:** LC 123 **art. 18 §14** strips out of the export DAS exactly **COFINS, PIS, IPI, ICMS
and ISS** — *"precisely where Anexo III's advantage over Anexo V lived."*

> **For an exporter, Anexo III and Anexo V converge, and the R$194.000/year of pró-labore needed to hold
> Anexo III costs more than it saves. Chasing *fator R* — the thing every Brazilian accountant optimises for —
> costs her about US$600/month of extra gross revenue.**

*(Pró-labore **does** count toward fator R: LC 123 art. 18 **§24**, *"incluídas as retiradas de pró-labore."*
Whether the CPP inside the DAS also counts is **UNVERIFIED** — neither the statute nor CGSN 140/2018 art. 26
resolves it, and the RFB's normas system is a JS app whose API returns 403. It would move Anexo III export
from 14,6% to 11,3% — **it does not change the ranking.**)*

**HARD REQUIREMENT on this path: full *escrituração contábil*.** IN RFB 1.700/2017 art. 238 §2º II only
exempts distributions above the presumed base where books kept "com observância da lei comercial" show the
real profit. Without them, ~R$400.000/year of her distribution is taxed at 27,5%, and CARF has treated the
excess as a **35% exclusive-source payment without proven cause** *(Lei 8.981/1995 art. 61)*. **This is the one
place where cheaping out on the accountant destroys the entire structure.**

### ★ FINDING 3 — A ROYALTY STREAM MUST BE IN THE COMPANY'S REGISTERED OBJECT

**Lei 9.430/1996 art. 25, II** puts *"as demais receitas… não abrangidas pelo inciso I"* into the lucro
presumido base **at 100%, not 32%.**

> **Book royalties, a share of someone else's product, or a passive licence held by a company whose registered
> object is something else are taxed at roughly 34% INSTEAD OF 7,7%.**
>
> **A one-line drafting decision at incorporation, worth about a quarter of the revenue.** If a royalty or
> licensing stream is ever built, the company's *objeto social* must be the exploitation of that right.

## 3. Exported services — where the whole answer comes from

**ISS.** LC 116/2003 art. 2º I exempts service exports, subject to the heavily-litigated carve-out for
services *"cujo resultado aqui se verifique."* **São Paulo's current position is the favourable one:**
Parecer Normativo SF 02/2016 — which would have killed every service export from São Paulo — **was revoked by
Parecer Normativo SF 04/2016**, which locates the *resultado* by where the person or economic interest the
service falls upon is located, and states it *"independe da entrega do respectivo produto ao destinatário
final."* **It is a municipal parecer, revocable by the next one, and it remains the single most litigated
point in this analysis.** *(From 2027 the test changes: **LC 214/2025 art. 80** defines export as supply to a
non-resident **and consumption abroad**, with art. 79 granting IBS/CBS immunity.)*

**PIS/COFINS.** **MP 2.158-35/2001 art. 14 III** exempts COFINS on services to a non-resident *"cujo pagamento
represente ingresso de divisas"*, and **§1º** extends it to PIS. **SC COSIT nº 34/2026** — very recent, directly
on point — softens the *divisas* requirement: receipts may arrive *"em reais ou em moeda estrangeira"*,
conversion may be before, during or after, and where the money is kept abroad under Lei 11.371/2006 the relief
*"independe do efetivo ingresso de divisas."*

**She may lawfully keep USD abroad.** Lei 11.371/2006 art. 1º as worded by **Lei 14.286/2021 art. 26**, for
*"pessoas físicas ou jurídicas"*. **Bacen CBE reporting only becomes mandatory at US$1.000.000** of assets
abroad *(Resolução BCB 279/2022)*.

**Siscoserv is gone and nothing replaced it.** IN RFB 2.045/2021 definitively revoked IN RFB 1.277/2012. **No
successor declaration exists.**

**And a provision nobody in this file had cited:** LC 123 **art. 3º §14** doubles the Simples ceiling —
R$4,8M domestic **plus** R$4,8M of export revenue, counted separately for rate purposes under §15. **The
Simples ceiling can never be what caps this business.**

## 4. Dividends did change — and it barely touches her

**Lei nº 15.270, de 26 de novembro de 2025**, effective **1 January 2026**. Three mechanisms: a **10%
withholding on the full amount** where one PJ pays one resident PF more than **R$50.000 in a single month**
(art. 6º-A, *"São vedadas quaisquer deduções"*); the **IRPFM** minimum tax where total personal income exceeds
**R$600.000/year**, rising linearly to 10% at R$1.200.000; and a *redutor* where the combined effective rate
exceeds 34%.

**Actual cost to her: R$2.854/year — an IRPFM rate of 0,455%.** The redutor does not help because her combined
rate is ~8%, nowhere near 34%.

> **But there is a cash-flow trap.** Her monthly dividend at target is **R$52.275 > R$50.000**, so art. 6º-A
> withholds 10% of the FULL amount every month — **R$62.730/year withheld against R$2.854 actually owed**,
> refunded only at the next annual return.
>
> **Fix: cap monthly distributions at R$50.000 and take the remainder (~R$2.275/month) as pró-labore.** It
> falls under the R$2.428,80 IRPF exemption, costs ~R$3.000/year of personal INSS, **buys her actual
> social-security coverage she otherwise has none of**, and neutralises the separate risk that an auditor
> treats a working *sócia-administradora* on pure dividends as disguising remuneration.

## 5. CORRECTION — the merchant-of-record kill does not transpose to services

**`THE_MERCHANT_OF_RECORD_KILL.md` and `CLAUDE.md` both state that RIR/2018 art. 162 §1º makes her
*equiparada à pessoa jurídica* by operation of law.** True — **for the case it was written about**, which was
buying and reselling *goods* (§1º II, *"por meio da venda a terceiros de bens"*).

**Art. 162 §2º excludes from equiparação:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **§2º II** | *"profissões, ocupações e prestação de serviços **não comerciais**"* |
| **§2º VII** | *"exploração de **obras artísticas, didáticas, científicas**, urbanísticas, projetos técnicos de construção…"* |
| **§2º III** | *"**agentes, representantes** e outras pessoas sem vínculo empregatício que… não os pratiquem… por conta própria"* — found independently by the S6 agent |

> **Two agents, working on unrelated questions, both found escape hatches in the same paragraph. The
> *equiparação* kill applies to the GOODS LEG ONLY. It must not be cited against a services candidate, a
> creative-works candidate, or a commission-agency candidate.**

## 6. Does the business SHAPE change the tax? For an export business — almost not at all

**Every service takes the same 32% presumption; every export takes the same zero on PIS/COFINS/ISS. Written
work, software, a subscription, a licence — all 7,68%.**

> **A genuinely useful negative result: TAX IS NOT A TIEBREAKER between shapes for the export case. It can be
> removed from the comparison.**

**Four exceptions that do bite:**

1. **Software licensing is a SERVICE at 32%** — following STF ADI 1.945 and ADI 5.659, confirmed in SC COSIT
   36/2023 and **SC COSIT 120/2025** (which fixes 14/02/2023 as the cut-off after which the old 8%/12% goods
   percentages stop applying). **Not privileged, but not penalised.**
2. **Passive royalties outside the corporate object: ~34%.** See Finding 3.
3. **Domestically, shape moves ISS by 3 points.** São Paulo Lei 13.701/2003 art. 16: 5% default, 2% for
   listed subitens. **Software development and licensing (1.04, 1.05) were REMOVED from the 2% band by Lei
   16.757/2017 and now pay 5%**, while **1.09 — *"disponibilização, sem cessão definitiva, de conteúdos de
   áudio, vídeo, imagem e texto pela internet"* — was ADDED to the 2% band by Lei 18.066/2023.** For domestic
   Brazilian sales, **a hosted content product beats a licensed software product by three points.**
4. **CBS/IBS in 2026: zero cash impact.** LC 214/2025 art. 348 III "c" disapplies the 2026 test rates to
   Simples opters, and §1º waives collection for those meeting ancillary obligations.
