# THE CARTA KILL — 3 Sep 2026, same day it was crowned

**CARTA was put at the top of the board this afternoon with the words "every load-bearing fact verified at a

## The kill

**CARTA's entire premise:** a Brazilian who needs a US taxpayer number must either post their original passport
to Texas for 7–11 weeks, or hand it to one of only four IRS-authorised Certifying Acceptance Agents in a
country of 215 million. **I verified that the cheap Brazilian substitute — a *cartório* authentication — is
worthless. That was true. It was also irrelevant, because the Brazilian notary was never the substitute.**

**Instructions for Form W-7, verbatim** (`irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/iw7.pdf`, lines 328–335 of the extracted text):

> **"Certified copies.** You can submit copies of original documents if you do any of the following.
> • Have the copies certified by the issuing agency.
> • **Have the officers at U.S. embassies and consulates overseas provide certification and authentication
> services.** Contact the Consular Section, American Citizens Services of the U.S. Embassy or Consulate in
> advance to determine the hours of operation for these services."

**And the US Mission to Brazil sells precisely that, today** (`br.usembassy.gov/services/notarials/`, retrieved
by me 3 Sep 2026 — WebFetch 403s, `curl` with a browser user-agent returns it):

> **"True copy of Foreign Passport** — This service is only available if you are applying for an Individual
> Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN) and need your identification verified before sending your application
> to the Internal Revenue Service (IRS)… Please bring your passport to your appointment."
>
> *"The fee for the notary services listed below is **US $50 per consular seal**."*
>
> *"Notarial services are available for **all nationalities** by appointment."*

> ### ⇒ THE CHEAPEST SUPPLIER OF THE THING CARTA SELLS FOR US$350 IS THE UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT, AT US$50, AT FIVE LOCATIONS IN BRAZIL, WITH NO INTERVIEW AND NO COURIER.

**And the funded incumbent closes the rest.** Openbiz — one of the four listed agents, in São Paulo — sells the
whole package at **US$350 one-time** (`openbiz.io/pricing`) while subsidising it inside **US$3.997/yr and
US$6.000/yr plans that bundle 2 and 4 ITINs**. She would be defending a US$300 gap over a government service,
against a subsidised incumbent, with no back end. **`THE NO-MARGIN-ON-THE-FLOW LAW`, exactly as written:
whoever earns margin on the future flow funds the identification layer to zero.**

## What survives, and why it is not enough

Everything else I verified is still true, and **the open question resolved in CARTA's favour**: Rev. Proc.
2006-10 §4 does permit a foreign person; §7 does contemplate a sole proprietorship outside the United States;
Pub 4520 does exempt foreign nationals without an SSN residing abroad from the background check; the programme
is open, 60-day electronic processing since 19 Jan 2024; the interview requirement is real and video is
permitted. **The population figures hold: Brazil 3.288 ITIN filers against four agents, and the Taxpayer
Advocate's finding that non-CAA applicants are "over twice as likely" to be rejected is genuine.**

**None of it matters.** What remains sellable is the Certificate of Accuracy and careful form preparation —
which is *production*, the thing this whole hunt was premised on racing to zero.

## ★ My error, stated exactly, because the process failure is the reusable part

**I fetched the W-7 instructions and asked a question about them instead of reading them.** The tool returned a
*summary* answering my narrow question — are notarised copies accepted? — and it correctly said no, except for
US military dependents. **It never surfaced the embassy clause sitting a few lines away, because I had not
asked about embassies. I then wrote "verified at source" over a summarised answer and put the candidate at the
top of the board.**

The archive already has the law for this and I broke it: **`A COUNT OF STRUCTURES IS NOT A CHECK OF CONTENT.
PRINT THE FIRST ROW AND READ IT.`** *(Fourth occurrence of this failure mode in the file. Three of them mine.)*

**And I broke my own newest rule in the same afternoon.** `PRICE, PERMISSION AND POPULATION FIRST` — I ran
permission and population exhaustively and ran **price against the wrong universe**: I priced CARTA against
other Certifying Acceptance Agents, and never asked *what else discharges this need*. **The substitute was not
a competitor. It was a government service, and it was one clause away in a document I had already downloaded.**

## ★★ And the one that stings: THE ACCEPTANCE FINDING predicted this and I did not run it

Hours before this kill I wrote that a guarantee *"is credible only in proportion to what BACKS it"* and that
CARTA prices at 100× the passport-photo business because a federal authorisation stands behind the signature.
**The correct next question was: what does the party that ISSUES the authorisation charge to do it itself?
US$50.** The law was on the page and pointed straight at the kill.

## The laws

> ### ★★★ PRICE AGAINST THE NEED, NOT AGAINST THE CATEGORY. LIST EVERY WAY THE BUYER'S PROBLEM GOES AWAY, THEN PRICE THE CHEAPEST ONE.
> CARTA was priced against other Certifying Acceptance Agents — the right answer inside the wrong universe.
> The buyer's actual need is *"get a certified copy of my passport to the IRS without posting the original"*,
> and it is discharged by **the issuing agency, a US consulate, or an agent**. **Two of those three were never
> checked.** ⇒ **Write the buyer's need as a sentence with no vendor in it, then enumerate every route that
> satisfies it — including the regulator's own.**
> ★ **And the special case that should now be a reflex: THE STATE OFTEN SELLS THE SERVICE ITSELF, CHEAPLY.**
> A US consulate certifies a foreign passport for **US$50** for ITIN purposes specifically. `THE FREE FRONT
> DOOR` has a sibling — **THE CHEAP STATE COUNTER** — and it is not free, which is exactly why it hides: a
> US$50 government price does not look like a free incumbent and does not trip the free-validator screen.

> ### ★★★ A SUMMARISED FETCH IS NOT A PRIMARY SOURCE. DOWNLOAD THE DOCUMENT AND GREP IT.
> A retrieval tool answers the question asked and is silent on the clause you did not think to ask about.
> **The W-7 instructions gave a correct answer about notarised copies and never mentioned embassies, because I
> asked about notaries.** ⇒ **For any fact a candidate rests on, fetch the actual PDF or HTML and read the
> whole section — `curl` + `pdftotext` + `grep` costs thirty seconds.** *(It also beats the 403: WebFetch was
> blocked on both `travel.state.gov` and `br.usembassy.gov`; `curl` with a browser user-agent returned both.)*

> ### ★★ THE SAME-DAY CROWNING IS THE TELL. A candidate that survives every screen in one afternoon has not been screened hard enough.
> CARTA went from generated to top of the board in under four hours, with five separate "verified at source"
> claims. **Every one was true and the candidate was still dead.** The archive's own record — six candidates
> killed in a single day on sub-30-minute checks — says the checks are cheap; **the failure is choosing which
> ones to run, and the one I skipped was the cheapest.**
