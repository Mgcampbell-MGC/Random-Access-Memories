# THE MEMORIAL BOOK — death veto lifted 21 Aug, three versions pressure-tested, and a new constraint that reprices the board

**Two founder inputs, both load-bearing:** (1) the death veto is withdrawn — *"she likes the idea now"*;
(2) **she wants materially less screen time.** Clarified: the business stays digital, the machine runs itself,
and she does oversight/QA at roughly **8–12 h/week** rather than 30.

---

## 1. The veto lifts, legitimately — and one caveat carries over

The 17 Aug veto read: ***"anything built around death — she is uneasy about it, and it is her life, not an
argument."*** **The stated basis was her feeling, not a structural finding. Her feeling changed, so the veto
goes.** A veto written as "it is her life" cuts both ways.

⚠ **But `SELECTION ≠ PERSISTENCE` applies here with unusual force.** She chose GATELEDGER on 20 Aug and
rejected it on the 21st. Death work has a specific version of that risk: **it is emotionally heavy on every
single unit, forever, with no light months.** The question is not *do you like the idea* — it is
***"could you still be opening files about strangers' dead parents, alone, in year three?"***

## 2. ★★ C9 — THE SCREEN-TIME CONSTRAINT, and it makes the arithmetic gate HARDER

| | h/month | required $ per hour of her time |
|---|---|---|
| C6 as written (30 h/wk) | 130 | **$85** |
| **C9 (~10 h/wk screen)** | **43** | **$258** |
| C9 upper bound (12 h/wk) | 52 | $213 |

**Her time just got three times more expensive.** Every candidate must now be re-priced against **~43 h/month
and $258/hour**, not 130 h and $85.

**★ THE INCUMBENT IS ALREADY IN THE GAP: Tribute Technology (which owns Frazer Consultants) sells a product
literally called "Tribute Book."** And **Frazer's personalisation software is FREE to funeral homes** — *"the
software is free to download, they would just need to purchase the DVDs."* Wholesale stationery is a mature
catalogue: **Matthews Aurora · Hilton Funeral Supply · Bright Corporation · Regal Line**, plus FedEx Office.

**The market is barbelled — $4,90 catalogue at one end, $5.000 bespoke-with-conversations at the other — and
the middle is occupied by the funeral home's own software vendor, giving the tool away.**

## 4. The three versions

### V3 — she writes the life stories. **DEAD, twice over.**
At 43 h/month she can produce **5–11 units**, which requires **$1.012–2.024 per unit**. The only real
comparable at that level (~$5.000) **includes client communications by design** — interviews, drafts,
approvals. **C1-fatal.** And the failure mode is unsurvivable in writing: getting a fact wrong about
someone's dead mother demands a phone call, at the worst possible moment. *(This is candidates 70/71/72
again — questionnaire → she writes prose → deliverable.)*

### V2 — fully automated, zero prose. **DEAD on two laws firing together.**
`THE CATALOGUE SCISSORS` (it exists in a catalogue at $4,90 ⇒ no margin) **and** `THE FREE FRONT DOOR`
(the funeral home's own vendor ships the personalisation tool free) **and** a named incumbent already
selling "Tribute Book." Nothing here threads the needle the way the scan keepsake machine does.

### V1 — hands-on, high ticket, low volume. **The only one that passes arithmetic, and it dies on distribution.**
It genuinely works on the numbers: **$500/unit at 60 min ⇒ 23 units/month**, or **$400 at 45 min ⇒ 29 units**
— inside the 43 h budget with room for QA. **But 25–30 units/month needs roughly 30 funeral-home partners**
(US homes average ~100–150 funerals/year; assume a ~10% attach). **Thirty partner relationships is a sales
operation, not bounded setup calls — which is exactly what killed W1 in the wedding hunt.**

And the wholesale escape does **not** rescue it: `THE WHOLESALE ESCAPE DOES NOT TRANSPOSE TO PHYSICAL GOODS`
— per-unit human handling means white-labelling changes who gets credit and nothing else. *"Does the
partner's marginal order cost her marginal minutes? If yes it is not the wholesale escape, it is a job."*

### V4 — memorial photo restoration, considered and rejected on price
True inaccessibility (no new photograph of this person can ever be taken) but **commoditised at $25–60**, so
**218–272 units/month** are needed — ~12 min/unit, and a channel far larger than V1's. Remini, MyHeritage and
MemoryCherish hold the floor. *(Separately: generating **new** images of a deceased person — as opposed to
restoring real ones — is ethically loaded and carries a complaint path that requires a conversation. Not
recommended.)*

## 5. ★★★ THE FINDING THAT MATTERS MOST — C9 IS A SECOND BLADE ON THE CATALOGUE SCISSORS

`THE CATALOGUE SCISSORS` says: an item either exists in a catalogue (no margin) or is custom (her hands on
every unit). **The escape has always been the second blade — add her craft, charge a high ticket — and at
130 h/month that was survivable.**

> ### **At 43 h/month it is not. Craft caps out at 23–29 units, which forces a $400–500 ticket, which forces channel volume, which forces ~30 partner relationships, which forces C1. THE CRAFT ESCAPE IS CLOSED. The only remaining escape from the catalogue scissors is a GENUINE MACHINE.**

**And this validates candidate #3 rather than threatening it.** The scan keepsake machine is designed to a
**≤3 min/unit** bar — at 43 h/month that is **~860 units of capacity**, an order of magnitude of headroom.
**C9 does not touch the one candidate built as a machine; it damages everything that was relying on her
hands.** That is a strong, independent argument for #3 that did not exist yesterday.

## 6. Verdict

**The domain is real** — deaths are exogenous volume, the buyer is price-insensitive at the moment of need,
and the channel exists and already sells add-ons. **No version of the artefact survives**, because the
incumbent gives the tool away, the catalogue holds the floor, and the only tier with real margin is defined
by conversations she cannot have.

**If she wants to stay in this domain, the thing to hunt is not a better book.** It is an artefact that is
(a) machine-producible at ≤3 min/unit, (b) absent from the Matthews Aurora / Hilton / Bright catalogue, and
(c) not already inside Frazer's free Tribute Center. **Those three filters can be run against a supplier
catalogue and a software feature list in an afternoon, before anything is built.**
