## ★★★ 2. HER HOURS CAP CLIENTS, NOT UNITS — and that sets a hard ticket floor
Contractor delivery decouples her from **units**. It does **not** decouple her from **clients**: a retainer
costs 3–5 h/month of contact, QA, escalation and collection no matter who delivers.

`43 h − 9 h sustaining sales = 34 h ÷ 4 h per client ⇒ **8–10 concurrent clients, permanently**`
⇒ **REQUIRED TICKET ≈ R$13.200–14.000/month.** This one number kills **six of sixteen in ten seconds**, before
any research.

## ★★★ 6. THE LEGIBILITY–MARGIN ANTI-CORRELATION
**A money-first lens is biased toward ZERO-MARGIN categories by construction.** The transactions easiest to
observe — tender awards, rate cards, salary surveys, job postings — are precisely those priced **per unit of
commodity labour**, because commodity labour is what buyers procure in writing at published prices.
**Margin lives where the price is quoted bespoke and never published.**
⇒ **Amendment: start from observed money PAID FOR AN OUTCOME, never per delivered head.**

## ★★ Two more ten-second screens
- **THE OBSERVED-PRICE CEILING.** Nine of sixteen kills reduce to one sentence: *the anchor was a ceiling, not
  a floor.* Every observed price is held by an incumbent because of something she lacks — a US address (AG
