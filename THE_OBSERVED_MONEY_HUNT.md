## ★★ CORRECTION 3 — ONLY THREE NON-AUDIENCE CHANNELS EXIST IN THE WHOLE CORPUS
Every other operator's channel was **themselves**. The three that are not:
1. **MARKETPLACE SEARCH** — a buyer with intent types a query into somebody else's index and the operator
   appears. Fiverr, Chrome Web Store, The Knot/WeddingWire, inspection dispatch panels, mystery-shop panels.
   **Costs nothing, needs no followers, works on day one.**
2. **GOOGLE / LLM SEARCH** — comparison and "alternative to X" pages, bought domain authority, or placement
   inside pages that already rank. Slow, compounding, written-only.
3. **MANUAL OUTBOUND** — and the corpus splits: it worked for a human-delivered design service (automated cold
   email, $8k MRR) and is **explicitly reported as failing** for a B2B software product. **Signal: cold
   outreach sells someone's HOURS, not someone's SOFTWARE.**

## ★ THE ONE DOCUMENTED COLD START IN NINETEEN OPERATORS
**Eryn Andrews, corporate voiceover.** No audience, no network, no credential, no reputation. Spent **US$200**,
created a Fiverr profile, priced low deliberately to build reviews, earned **~US$1.000 in month one**. Now
**~US$32.000/month at 1–8 hours a week**, and *Fiverr is still her primary channel*. Clients include Coca-Cola
and PetSmart. **CNBC states it reviewed financial documents** — the only [VERIFIED-DOC] entry in the table.

**There is no step in that story requiring anything Sol does not have** — except the deliverable itself, which
is an American-accented voice. **The transferable structure is: a personal attribute scarce in the buyer's
market, delivered as a file, sold through the marketplace's own index.** Her equivalent attribute is native
Brazilian Portuguese sold into an English-speaking buyer market.

⚠ **And this file's own settled finding bounds it rather than repealing it:** *"written deliverables on Fiverr
price at $5–45, sampled three times."* **Andrews's deliverable is not written.** GVAA published rates for
non-written work run **US$15–55 per finished minute** for e-learning and corporate narration, **US$425 per
finished hour**, **US$200–500 PFH** for audiobooks — *but those are English/US-market rates and whether a
Brazilian seller realises them is **UNVERIFIED and is the whole question.***
**THE TEST, one afternoon: pull twenty live pt-BR voice or localisation listings and count how many price above
US$120 per unit. Under five ⇒ the category prices at Brazilian domestic rates and this dies.**

## Methodological findings worth keeping
- **The disclosure pool is structurally biased against her.** Of 19 operators: **13 are developers, 15 sell
  software, 4 used the disclosure itself as their acquisition channel.** Non-technical service operators do not
  publish numbers, because for them a revenue figure buys nothing.
- **Hours are the least-disclosed field in the entire literature — 3 of 19.** Indie Hackers interviews ask
  about revenue, channel, stack and mindset and **never ask how many hours a week.** Every "solo, part-time,
  lifestyle business" claim in this space is therefore **unevidenced on the axis that binds her hardest.**
- **★ Two sources are now dead and should be struck from future hunts.** The **Indie Hackers product directory
  has degraded into self-reported spam** — the $2.000–15.000/month band currently contains *"Buy Old Gmail
  Accounts"*, *"Bitcoin Investment"* and *"Flats for sale in Mohali"*, none payment-verified. **Reddit is
  entirely unreachable from this environment**, so any hunt leg depending on it is unrun, not negative.
- **Two operators at US$10–17k/month sell the identical product** — submitting other people's startups to
  directories — and both acquired customers by being publicly watched. **Real business, real money,
  non-transferable channel.** The cleanest example of the trap in the corpus.
