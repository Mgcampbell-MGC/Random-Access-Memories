## 3. ★ THE SUBSCRIPTION-SEAT COLLISION WITH C5

The tool the episode is selling costs **$200–300/month = $2.400–3.600/year**. **C5 is US$500–2.500 ONE
TIME.** A monthly agent seat consumes her entire capital in eight months before any revenue.
**Her production machine must be cron + API on a ~$5 VPS, never a per-seat agent subscription.**
*(The GateLedger capture already is exactly that — this is a constraint she already satisfies and had
not banked as an advantage.)*

## 4. RULE 3 — her version of the whole model

The model is **not** "newsletter." Stripped of its monetisation it is:

> **A tiny agent team runs a daily research-and-publish loop over a scattered source, at near-zero
> marginal cost, and the operator's own hours only ever do SELECTION and one relationship.**

That is `THE BUILDABILITY THRESHOLD` restated by a practitioner, and it confirms rather than extends it:
**buildability changes her cost of operating, never her right to charge.** The loop is free; what the loop
accumulates is the whole question. **Her version therefore keeps the production machine and replaces
attention-rent with a per-unit sale that arrives in writing** — which is the board she already has.
