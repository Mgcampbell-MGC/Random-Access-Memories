# O MOLDE — assessed, 4 Sep 2026

**The "find something better" hunt returned 30 candidates through a fixed pipeline (generate generously →
rebuild → screen hard). Grades: 0 STRONG, 0 PROMISING, 8 WEAK, 22 DEAD.** The judge was required to name a
best anyway. It named **O MOLDE**, and — separately — claimed a kill on one of the founder's three picks.

---

## The candidate

**Cast synthetic training bones.** Rigid-polyurethane-foam replicas of human bones — distal radius, proximal
femur, tibial plateau, calcaneus — that orthopaedic surgeons **drill, saw, tap and destroy** in hands-on
training. Print a master, take a silicone mould, pour two-part PU foam at cortical density, demould, pack.

**The buyer:** Brazilian orthopaedic implant makers running surgeon courses (GMReis, Baumer, Traumec,
Ortosíntese), SBOT residency programmes, congress workshop organisers, veterinary orthopaedics.
**The claim:** *"the printer makes the mould; the mould makes the bone"* — a 3D-printed PLA bone is geometry
without mechanics, and a screw stripping in it kills the sale in ten seconds. **What is bought is a foam that
behaves like bone under a drill.**

## ⚠ MY OBJECTION, WHICH THE JUDGE WAVED AWAY: IT FAILS LIMB 3 OF THE MACHINE TEST

The founder's revealed screen has three limbs: no relationship to hold · no belief required · **a machine makes
the unit, not her hands.** O MOLDE passes the first two cleanly. **It does not pass the third.**

**She pours, demoulds, deflashes and packs every unit — 8–12 minutes of her hands, on every single one.** The
judge's answer is that *"the mould is the machine."* **That is rhetoric.** Compare the scan keepsake, where a
script produces the artefact in 264 ms and her hands never touch it.

⚠ **Note the distinction that matters: it does NOT fail on CAPACITY — 3–5 hours a month is trivial. It fails
on SHAPE.** And shape is exactly what the founder selected on when she rejected six of ten candidates.
**That is her call to make, not mine, and it should be put to her as the specific question it is.**

**Three smaller objections:** it is **physical goods with inventory, chemicals, cure times and shipping**, which
needs a workspace in São Paulo rather than a laptop *(the archive's veto permits physical goods only
made-to-order — casting to order arguably passes)*; **US$2.000 of tooling before unit one**, against a keepsake
machine that is already built; and its **demand test needs replies**, where the keepsake's needs nobody.

## ⚠ AND THE eCTD KILL IS NOT ESTABLISHED — I checked and the judge overreached

The judge claimed eCTD publishing should be dropped from the founder's three because *"US agents bundle the
whole seat at US$649/year"* and five same-family candidates died to a free front door in this hunt.

**US Agent representation and eCTD publishing are different services.** A US Agent is a regulatory
representative for a foreign establishment; eCTD publishing is preparing and validating an electronic
submission. **The bundling claim does not transfer, and I could not verify that it does.**

**What I did find cuts the other way:** a search surfaced **FDAbasics advertising eCTD publishing "starting
from US$350"** — which, if real, is the *first observed price in the category* and **partially answers eCTD's
blocking question rather than killing it.** ⚠ **UNVERIFIED — the page returns HTTP 202 with a 210-byte
challenge to `curl`, so I could not read it at source.**

**What IS confirmed:** `intuitionlabs.ai` occupies the top two search results — **the exact AI content farm the
archive already identified as the sole source of every eCTD price figure.** The category's real property is
that prices are unpublished, i.e. `THE MONITORING PINCER`, not a free front door.
⇒ **eCTD stays on the board. Its blocking question is unchanged and still cheap: twelve written RFQs.**

## ★★ The methodological finding, which may outlast the candidate

> ### 26 OF THE 30 CANDIDATES WERE INFORMATION PRODUCTS. Six independent lenses — verification, channel artifact, short-lookback record, compelled artefact, granted distribution, completed history — converged on one shape.

**Fourth time this archive has diagnosed its own monoculture.** And note *which* shape it converged on: the one
already closed on 3 Sep by `INFORMATION DOES NOT PRICE`. **The winner is the single candidate that is not in
that family — it is a physical object.**

⇒ **The pipeline fix worked at the top and not at the bottom.** Separating generation from screening took the
hunt from **zero candidates to thirty**, which was the whole point. But 22 of 30 still died, and mostly to the
same law. **Generating more ideas does not help when they all walk into the same wall — which is the argument
for recombining verified PARTS rather than generating whole candidates.**
