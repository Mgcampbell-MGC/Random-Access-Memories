## The pitch in sixty seconds

Every Brazilian kid who ever came home late knows two sounds: their own key turning in the lock, and, a second later,
the click of a light switching off behind her bedroom door. She said she was asleep. She was not. For twenty-odd
years, someone in that house didn't really sleep until you were home. There was always one light on: the yellow bulb
at the gate, the light under her door, the TV left on with no sound, your plate covered with another plate in the
microwave. **You never once came home to a dark house.** You thought that was how houses worked. It was her.

And all she ever got back was one word, in lower case, with no full stop: **cheguei.**

So that word becomes the brand. **cheguei** is a candle brand built on the smallest love letter in Brazil. Every
candle is named after an hour of the night she stayed up for, and a light she left on. The glass is the colour of a
house at night, with a line of light at its base like the light under her door. For Dia das Mães the ritual turns
around: **this time, you light it.**

Funny, because it's true; moving, for the same reason. The joke is always on the kid who came home at 03:07, never on
her.

It isn't descriptive of candles, so it's a stronger mark than an adjective (UNVERIFIED at INPI). It outlives Mother's Day: *acende quando chegar* is a ritual for every arrival home. | Common word, so possible collisions in other classes (delivery apps, shop names). **INPI search in classes 4 and 3 not done.** |
| **B** | **ACESA** | "Lit" and "awake/alert" at once: *a luz acesa* and *ela fica acesa até você chegar*. | The double meaning is beautiful, and it's feminine like *mãe*. | **Descriptive for a candle**: every candle is *acesa*, so it's a weak mark and probably unregistrable in class 4. It's used in copy instead: *"Quanto mais tarde, mais acesa."* |
| **C** | **FRESTA** | The crack of light under a door. | The most visual of the three. | Out of context, *fresta* also means a draught, which is exactly what a candle should be kept away from. It names the graphic device, not the feeling. **Kept as the name of the device and the vessel window.** |

**Rejected on sight, with reasons worth keeping:**
- **ME AVISA:** that's mum's voice, which is concept A's territory, and it reads one letter away from ANVISA (a
  trap concept A also noticed).
- **PLANTÃO:** "mum on the night shift" is lovely, but *plantão* also means hospital shifts and the TV breaking-news
  sting.
- **ATÉ CHEGAR:** it carries a funeral undertone (*até chegar a hora*). This brand must never read as a memorial
  (§10).
- **LUZ DO CORREDOR:** five syllables of description.

### Pick: **cheguei**

Written as the wordmark **cheguei**: lower case, no capital, no full stop, exactly as typed at 03:07. In running text
it is "cheguei" (or "CHEGUEI" in legal capitals). Handle: `cheguei` / `chegueivelas`, **availability not checked**.

**Why it wins.**
- **The W+K test, the name contains the platform.** You can't say *cheguei* to a Brazilian without them hearing the
  rest of the sentence: *…mãe*.
- **The Droga5 test, the name is a cultural object people already use every day.** We aren't asking anyone to learn a
  word, only to see one they send without thinking.
- **The Collins test, the name generates the system.** *cheguei* plus a timestamp is a message. A message plus a light
  is a label. That is the whole brand.
- **Against concepts A and B:** both are built in mum's voice (A, her vocabulary; B, her *não pode*). **C is the only
  concept in the child's voice**, which is the white space `culture.md` §2 names.

## 2. Tagline, brand idea and insight

### Tagline (permanent, every piece)

# **acende quando chegar.**

- **It's an instruction and a ritual.** It's the first thing you do when you get home, any day of the year.
- **It's mum's sentence, answered.** Her *"me avisa quando chegar"* becomes the brand's *"acende quando chegar"*.
  Every Gen Z Brazilian will hear her cadence in it, and nobody has to explain it.
- **It's safe by construction.** You light it when you are *there*. It never says *deixa acesa* ("leave it lit"),
  which would encourage the dangerous use CONAR art. 33 forbids (`label_rules.md` §9).

### Brand idea in one sentence

> **PT:** *cheguei* transforma a palavra que ela esperou acordada — a menor carta de amor do Brasil — numa luz que você
> devolve.
>
> **EN:** *cheguei* turns the one word she waited up for, the shortest love letter in Brazil, into a light you give
> back.

### Dia das Mães 2027 platform

# **Você nunca chegou numa casa apagada.**

It's provocative in the good way: the reader's first reaction is *"…é verdade"*, and the second is a memory of a
specific light. It doesn't say *mãe* and it doesn't need to. It's true for children with one mother, two mothers, a
grandmother, an aunt, or anyone else who waited up; *culture.md* §1 asks the copy to leave room for *a mãe que você
tem*.

**The ritual line, the call to action of the date:** **Dessa vez, quem acende é você.**

### The insight (the Gen Z truth)

> **Ela mandava apagar todas as luzes da casa. Menos a que esperava você.**

Three layers, and the campaign uses all three:

1. **The comic layer, which everybody shares.**
   - She claimed to be asleep and heard your key from the bedroom.
   - She shouted *"apaga essa luz, tá pensando que eu sou sócia da companhia de luz?"* (`culture.md` #13).
   - She left the yellow bulb on at the gate.
   - She dozed on the sofa with the TV on and swore she was *"descansando a vista"*.

   Specific, affectionate, and the joke lands on the child who came home at three, never on her (`culture.md` §6,
   *zoeira com respeito*).
2. **The tender layer.** Waiting up is the purest form of attention one person can give another. For two decades you
   were someone's last thought every night you were out. Gen Z, the generation that measures love in replies and read
   receipts, got the longest-running read receipt there is and never noticed.
3. **The turn, which makes people send the ad to a sibling.** Gen Z is now moving out, paying a first electricity bill
   and coming home to a dark flat for the first time in their lives. That bill explains two things at once: why she
   made you switch everything off, and why she never switched off *that* one.

**Why the insight belongs to a candle and nothing else:**
- **A candle is literally a light you leave on for someone.**
- Lighting one is an act that needs presence, since you can't leave it burning unattended. That's the same thing she
  did by staying awake.
- **No other Mother's Day gift category can say this sentence.** Perfume can't, flowers can't, a Pix can't. *"Pix não
  acende."* (§6) puts the CNDL/SPC tension in our own words: 47% think mum would rather have a Pix, and 54% still
  want the emotion of an object (`culture.md` §5).

> ⚠ **Evidence status. The core behaviour is UNVERIFIED as a measured fact. It is a cultural observation, very
> widely known.**
> - The sentiment appears in Brazilian amateur writing indexed online: *"Mãe não vai dormir enquanto o filho não chega"*.
>   That is a search snippet whose page (recantodasletras.com.br) returned HTTP 403, so it was **not read at
>   source**.
> - The TV habit is sourced: *"Dormir com a TV ligada é um hábito comum em muitos lares brasileiros"*
>   ([Correio Braziliense, 31 Jan 2026](https://www.correiobraziliense.com.br/cbradar/o-que-significa-dormir-com-a-tv-ligada/), fetched 6 Oct 2026).
> - *Descansando a vista*, the plate covered with a plate, and the yellow gate bulb are **UNVERIFIED** cultural
>   observations.
>
> **R$0 written check before any client use** (`CLAUDE.md`: the test design that works). Send one written question
> to 20 people aged 18–28: *"Quando você chegava tarde, sua mãe (ou quem cuidava de você) tava dormindo?"*
> - **Bar, committed in advance:** at least 14 of 20 say she was awake, pretended to sleep, or texted.
> - **Second question:** *"Que luz ficava acesa?"* Count unprompted mentions of a specific light. Every answer is
>   also a future product name (§7.6).

## 3. The launch product: Coleção *Noite em Claro*

**Why the name.** *Passar a noite em claro* is the Portuguese idiom for a sleepless night, and taken literally it
means "a night in light". The idiom already holds both halves of the brand: the vigil and the lamp. No translation is
needed, and no other language puts it this way.

**The naming system.** Every candle is a sent message plus the light that was on: **cheguei [HH:MM] · [a luz]**.
The time is the comedy and the light is the tenderness. The small line under each is something she said, quoted with
love.

**Why these four.**
- **They are one night, in order.** Dinner left out, the gate light, the TV on, the coffee already made. Line the
  four up and they read as a timeline, the night she stayed up for.
- **Each smell belongs to a Brazilian house at night.**
  - *Dama-da-noite* (*Cestrum nocturnum*) is the shrub that perfumes Brazilian gates and backyards after dark.
  - Arroz-doce and cumaru are the kitchen.
  - Cotton, iris and cashmeran are the sofa throw.
  - Café coado and rapadura are dawn.
- **That gap is real.** Gourmand appears in only 5 of 102 Brazilian candle names, and nobody does Brazilian home
  food (`category.md` §3.6). Floral/botanical is the favourite profile for 74% of Gen Z (`category.md` §6,
  secondary). That is why the hero is the floral.
- **Each time is a self-aware joke on the child**, never on her: 21:40 is the respectable one, 23:58 is
  *"technically"*, 01:17 is late, and 05:52 is *very* late.

**Olfactory note status.**
- This is **direction for a fragrance house, not a formula.** Accords (arroz-doce, dama-da-noite, café coado,
  rapadura) are briefed as accords.
- **Allergens: cumaru brings coumarin.** Every RDC 1.029/2026 allergen the fragrance house's certificate shows above
  0,001% is declared on the box, **and lilial is prohibited** (`label_rules.md` §7).
- **IFRA category for candles: UNVERIFIED.**

## 4. The vessel and the pack

### 4.1 **O copo da fresta** (the glass with the light under the door)

**The idea in one sentence:** the vessel is a door at night, seen from the hallway. It is dark, glossy and closed,
with a thin line of light at its base.

**Silhouette.** A **tall, straight-walled glass tumbler**, with no shoulder, no taper and no embossing on the wall.
- **H/D ≈ 1,29** makes it noticeably taller and slimmer than the Brazilian shelf, than concept A's tumbler (1,11) and
  than concept B's squat pot.
- In thumbnail it reads as a door or a lit window.
- It's the easiest object in the world to render convincingly: a cylinder.

**Geometry, worked out from the 200 g fill (design direction, not CAD):**

| | Value |
|---|---|
| External | **Ø 70 × 90 mm**; wall 3 mm; solid base 9 mm; rim radius 1,5 mm |
| Internal | Ø 64 mm → floor area 32,2 cm² |
| Wax | 200 g ÷ ≈0,90 g/cm³ ≈ 222 cm³ → **69 mm** of wax; surface at 78 mm, i.e. **12 mm below the rim** (the 8–12 mm norm in `category.md` §7.1) |
| **The fresta** | A **clear, uncoated band 3 mm tall, all the way round, from 17 to 20 mm above the base**. Its top edge sits **≈11 mm above the inner floor**, which is the "stop at ≈1 cm of wax" level of the NCA safety advice (`label_rules.md` §4) |
| Lid | Steel cap Ø 73,5 × 9 mm with a skirt that slips over the rim |
| Base | A 1,5 mm recessed foot ring; the base sticker sits inside it (LOTE/FAB/VAL, never on the lid) |

**Material and finish.**
- **The body: clear soda-lime glass, spray-coated on the outside** in **Madrugada** `#17110E`, a warm near-black,
  **high gloss**. The 3 mm band is masked off during coating, so the fresta is bare glass.
- **The print: a two-ink screen print**, on the coating, in **Cera** `#FFF1DA` plus the candle's light colour.
  Screen-print ink sits **satin on gloss**. That difference in roughness is what makes the render read as a real
  object and not a 3D mock-up.
- **No recycled-content, reuse or eco claims.** The coating's heat resistance, the clean edge of the masked band, and
  cost are **UNVERIFIED** supplier questions.

**What the fresta does: three jobs, one detail.**

1. **The brand image, unlit.**
   - In daylight the band shows the cream wax inside.
   - On a dark glass it reads instantly as **light under a door**, even before the candle is lit.
2. **The brand image, lit.**
   - In a dark room the wax column glows warmly where the flame's light scatters down through it.
   - The band shows that glow: a dim warm line at the base, which is exactly what light under a door looks like.
   - **As the candle burns down, the flame gets closer to the band and the line gets brighter.** Hence the product
     truth: ***"Quanto mais tarde, mais acesa. (Igual a ela.)"***
3. **A safety function.**
   - On a dark glass you can't see the wax level. The fresta lets you.
   - The back panel says it in brand voice: ***"A fresta avisa: quando a cera chegar na linha, é hora de parar."***
     So mum's *"olha a hora!"* becomes a burn-down indicator.
   - **UNVERIFIED as a safety device.** A candle maker must validate the stop height and confirm the band causes no
     thermal-stress or hot-spot issue.

**The lid: *boa noite.***
- **Material and finish:** a steel cap, powder-coated **gloss in the candle's light colour**: Lâmpada yellow on the
  hero, Tangerina, Rosa Manta, Lilás Aurora. The four lids are the only bright thing on a dark glass, so the shelf of
  four reads as four lit windows on one building at night.
- **The top:** debossed **boa noite.** in Young Serif, plus a 22 mm line debossed under it.
- **Its use completes the ritual:** ***acende quando chegar · tampa quando for dormir.***
  - The second half is brand ritual and safety instruction at once: you don't go to sleep with it lit.
  - **Snuffing by lid is UNVERIFIED for this geometry.** A fresh candle's flame rises above the rim, so the lid must
    be tested. If it fails, ship a separate *abafador* and keep *boa noite.* on the lid as a closing gesture.

**Two craft notes that matter for compliance (checked on the font files):**
- **Young Serif's default figures are oldstyle**: descending 3, 4, 5, 7, 9 and ascending 6, 8. A "4 mm figure" would
  be ambiguous. **Set the net weight with the `lnum` feature**: lining figures, measured 750–762 units tall on the
  1.000-unit em.
- **Young Serif's x-height is 500/1.000**, so a "g" at the figures' size is exactly ⅔ of a lining figure, right on
  Inmetro's minimum (`label_rules.md` §2). **Set the unit at 110%** so it clears ⅔ unambiguously.
- Measure both on the final print proof.

**The design principle: two reading distances.**
- **From the shelf or a phone:** a dark glass that says ***cheguei*** with a glowing time under it. Exactly what she
  sees on her phone at 23:58.
- **In your hand:** the light, her line and the weight.
- Nothing in between.

becomes **a primeira vez**, and her line becomes *ela tava acordada.* Everything else stays fixed. The variable fields
are four strings in `label_copy.json`, set by code and checked by the fidelity tool like any other master.

### 4.3 The gift box: **a caixa de fósforo**

**The archetype.**
- The **sliding matchbox**: sleeve plus drawer. It's the object in every Brazilian kitchen drawer next to the gas
  stove, the thing she lit the stove with every morning.
- It's a ritual object by nature: you slide it open and you strike.
- **No Brazilian candle in the sample uses it** (`category.md` §5.4). Concept A's box is a book and concept B's is a
  tear-band carton.

**Geometry.**
- Sleeve **104 (W) × 84 (D) × 100 (H) mm**.
- The drawer slides out sideways and holds the candle **upright** on a die-cut card collar.
- An oversized matchbox on purpose: the scale shift is the Gen Z wink.
- Two rectangular boxes, so it's trivial to render.

**Sleeve graphics.**
- **Colour:** the candle's light colour (hero: **Lâmpada** yellow) in uncoated board.
- **Front face:** the classic matchbox label grammar. A framed panel in **Madrugada** with:
  - the wordmark;
  - the Doto time;
  - *a luz do portão*;
  - the fresta line;
  - **vela aromática · PESO LÍQUIDO 200 g** (lining figures 4 mm, `label_rules.md` §2: the box front must carry it too).
- **Long sides:** printed **striker-strip texture** in Fósforo red-brown on Madrugada.
  - **Decorative only; not a striking surface.**
  - Small text: *não contém fósforos.* Matches or lighters are not included unless Inmetro-certified
    (`label_rules.md` §8).
- **Back:** the full Route A legal block (`label_rules.md` §11.4), set by code.

**The unboxing ritual, five beats, each one a line:**

| Beat | Action | What she reads |
|---|---|---|
| **1. O fósforo** | She picks up a giant matchbox. | Front: **cheguei 23:58 · a luz do portão** |
| **2. A gaveta** | She pushes the drawer. As it slides, the inside of its front wall appears first. | *"Por todas as noites que você esperou acordada."* |
| **3. O parêntese** | The drawer comes fully out. Smaller, under it: | *"(E pelas 3 da manhã: foi mal.)"* |
| **4. O cartão-cheguei** | Tucked beside the candle: a card designed like a message notification. | Printed **cheguei** header; write-on fields **pra: ______** (mãe · mainha · mamis · dona ____), **que horas eu chego: __:__**, three lines, **de: ______**. |
| **5. A tampa** | She lifts the candle out; the lid is on top. | **boa noite.** |

- **Under the candle**, on the drawer floor: *"agora a luz é sua. acende quando a gente chegar."* It closes the
  loop: she lights it when the family arrives.
- **Also in the box:** an A6 sticker sheet of our own drawing:
  - *cheguei · chegou? · tô vivo · foi mal · dorme com deus · tô descansando a vista · olha a hora*;
  - the four light icons (§5.3);
  - **the same set as a WhatsApp sticker pack**, a gift that travels. It is distributed without any WhatsApp mark or
    look (`codes.md` §3).

**The Noite em Claro set** comes in a **long kitchen-matchbox** (4 × 62 mm bays) with a timeline label: **21:40 ·
23:58 · 01:17 · 05:52**, under it *"uma noite inteira. ela tava acordada em todas."*

### 4.4 Render notes (Blender Cycles; everything is a render, nothing is generated)

- **Glass and coating.**
  - Clear dielectric IOR 1,5 inner body.
  - A 0,2 mm outer shell with Principled BSDF, base `#17110E`, **Coat weight 1,0 / coat roughness 0,03**, base
    roughness 0,12.
  - **The fresta is an alpha mask on the coating shell**, so the inner wax is truly visible through it.
- **Print.** A decal shell, 0,05 mm, roughness **0,35** (satin) over the gloss. Cera and the light colour come from
  the label master texture: **the label is the master file, UV-wrapped, never redrawn.**
- **Wax.**
  - Principled, base ≈`#F3E4CB`, **subsurface weight 1,0, radius ≈(8, 5, 3) mm**. A generous radius is what makes the
    column glow down to the fresta in NOITE mode.
  - Top surface: a slight concave meniscus, a 1 mm memory ring at the wall, and a faint soot freckle on the inner rim
    after the "second burn".
- **Wick and flame.**
  - Wick: a twisted cotton curve, 9 mm proud, slightly off vertical, carbon tip.
  - Flame: a teardrop emissive mesh, gradient from a small blue root to a 1.850 K body, plus a 3 mm point light at the
    flame centre.
  - Flicker: noise-driven scale ±4% and light strength ±8% at 6–10 Hz, low amplitude. **Never a "magic" glow halo.**
- **Lid:** steel, gloss powder coat in the light colour, a fingerprint smudge (roughness map at 3%), deboss by
  displacement.
- **Keys** (the campaign's one prop):
  - three keys modelled from curves, brass and nickel, on a split ring;
  - one blank acrylic tag in Lâmpada yellow, **with no text on it**;
  - a 4 mm scratch on the brass.
- **The verification gotcha, and the fix.**
  - In NOITE mode the flame and the warm practicals legitimately shift the label's colour, and the studio's checker
    would read that as recolouring.
  - **So verify label geometry and letters on Cycles' *Diffuse Color* (albedo) pass**, which is unlit and must match
    the master, **and judge colour on the beauty pass by eye.**
  - Same rule as the studio's planted-error control: plant a mirrored patch in a copy of the albedo pass, and the
    checker must catch it.

## 5. Visual identity: the brand world ***A casa acordada***

**The world in one line:** a Brazilian home at night, where everything is dark except one light, and that light is
waiting for you.

**The Collins test:** can the system make its hundredth execution without a designer inventing anything, and still be
unmistakably *cheguei*? Yes, because every execution answers three questions, and only three:
**what time is it, which light is on, and who is speaking.**

### 5.1 The five rules of the system

1. **Uma luz só.** Every frame has one dark field and exactly one light. The light is one of four colours, or the
   flame. No second light competes.
2. **Times are names.** Always 24-hour, always with a colon, always in Doto: **23:58**. A time plus a light is a
   product, a post, a label, an edition.
3. **Two voices, one rule of punctuation.**
   - **Hers has capitals and full stops** and signs off *"Beijos, Mãe."*
   - **Yours is lower case, with no full stop.**
   - Both are set in Young Serif, so the warmth is shared and only the manners differ.
   - The night itself (clocks, statuses, timestamps) speaks in Doto.
4. **The fresta closes everything.** A line of light sits at the bottom of every layout, like the light under a door:
   under the wordmark, at the base of the glass, at the end of the film.
5. **Lights switch, they never fade.** In motion, nothing dissolves. It clicks on or clicks off.

|---|---|---|---|

**Checked on the actual files** (`research/raw_codes/fonts/`):
- **Both have every Portuguese diacritic**, plus `·`, `:`, `°`, `º`, curly quotes, `–`, `—` and `…`.
- **Neither has `→` or `✓`.** So arrows are drawn as vector shapes, and read receipts are written as words (*visto*,

**Not used, deliberately:**

- **Bricolage Grotesque and Instrument Serif**, now defaults;
- **BBH Bartle and Hegarty**, which lack Portuguese accents;
- **any handwriting face.** Concepts A and B give mum a pen. **In this brand mum speaks the way she actually reaches
  you at night: by text, with full punctuation and a signature.**

### 5.4 Graphic devices: the modular kit

1. **A FRESTA (the line of light).** The master device.
   - What it is: a horizontal bar in Lâmpada (or the candle's light colour), **aspect 24:1**, with a soft bloom
     generated by code (a Gaussian blur of the bar at 40% opacity, radius = bar height × 3). Deterministic, not AI.
   - Where it appears:
     - the logo symbol;
     - the divider;
     - the progress bar in stories;
     - the base of every layout;
     - **the signature transition: the line expands vertically to fill the frame with light, which is the door
       opening** (§5.6).
2. **O VISOR (the display).** Doto lines that glow:
   - the time;
   - the status: *visto 23:59 · digitando… · acordada há 4h12*;
   - the bus sign: *→ CASA*;
   - the date: **09.05**.
   - Always one line, sometimes two, never a paragraph.
3. **A CONVERSA (the night thread).** A messenger of our own design.
   - Your bubble: small, right-aligned, light-colour fill, Madrugada text, lower case: ***cheguei***.
   - Her bubble: big, left-aligned, Cera fill, Madrugada text, Young Serif, full punctuation: ***"Graças a Deus. Vai
     dormir que já é tarde. Amanhã me liga. Dorme com Deus. Beijos, Mãe."***
   - **The size difference is the joke.**
   - No green, no logo, no double ticks, no app name (WhatsApp brand rules, `codes.md` §3).
4. **AS LUZES DA CASA (the icon set).** Eight two-colour vector icons with a code bloom:
   - the gate bulb, the microwave, the TV, the bedside lamp, the open fridge, the hallway ceiling light, the
     bathroom light left on, the phone screen.
   - Used for stickers, the store's category tiles and the UGC archive (§7.6).
5. **A HORA (the variable).** The time field, set by code, is the system's generative engine:
   - a new scent;
   - a regional edition;
   - a birth time (A PRIMEIRA VEZ);
   - a fan's story (*cheguei 02:14 · a luz da cozinha*).
6. **O PARÊNTESE (the verbal device).** A sincere line, then the joke on the child in parentheses:
   - *Você nunca chegou numa casa apagada. (Nem às 03:07.)*
   - *Quanto mais tarde, mais acesa. (Igual a ela.)*
   - The brackets are part of the visual grammar: always set one size smaller, in the light colour.
7. **A CAIXA DE FÓSFORO (the frame).** The matchbox-label layout: a framed panel, the time as the emblem, the striker
   strip as a side border.
   - It works as a box, a carousel cover, a price tag and a pop-up shop sign.
8. **A CHAVE (the prop).** Three keys on a ring with a blank yellow tag: the campaign's only object besides the candle.
   - It means *someone just got home*, so every lit-candle still shows a present person by implication (§10).
   - **Merch idea:** the same tag in acrylic with the debossed fresta, *pra chave da casa dela*.

**The logo lockup.**
- **cheguei** in Young Serif, with one custom detail: **the dot of the *i* is a small square, like a lit window**,
  instead of a round tittle. No flame anywhere in the logo, because every candle brand puts one on the *i*.
- **The fresta line sits under the wordmark**, at 72% of its width, with bloom.
- **App icon / avatar:** a Madrugada square with only the glowing line, 78% of the way down. *A door at night with
  someone awake behind it.* That reads at 40 px.

### 5.6 Motion grammar (the system moves)

- **Switch on, switch off.**
  - A light goes 0→100% in **one frame**, then one dropout frame, then on: *on-off-on* across 3 frames at 24 fps,
    like a real switch with a tired bulb.
  - Off is a single frame.
  - **No cross-fades, anywhere.**
- **A porta abre (signature transition).** The fresta line expands vertically from 6 px to the full frame in
  **8 frames**, ease-out, so the screen floods with the light colour. Then cut to the next scene.
- **O visor esquenta.** Doto text appears with `ROND` animating **0→100 over 10 frames** and `wght` 300→700. The
  pixels become round, glowing dots, like a filament warming.
- **Mensagens.**
  - Bubbles **pop**: scale 0,92→1,02→1,00 over 6 frames.
  - Her bubble is preceded by ***digitando…*** in Doto for **at least 18 frames**. The waiting is the joke.
  - Yours is never preceded by anything.
- **Câmera (3D).** Locked off for type. For product, a dolly at walking pace toward the light, ≤3% scale over 4 s.
  Nothing orbits, flies or rotates the pack.
- **Fim.** Every film ends on the lid going on, or on the fresta alone in the dark.

### 5.7 Sound

- **Sonic logo, *a chegada* (≈1,4 s):**
  1. A key turning in a lock: two dry clicks, *trec-trec*, 0,6 s.
  2. A wall light switch: *clic*, 0,1 s.
  3. A match strike and the flame catching: *fsst… whmp*, 0,7 s.

  **Every Brazilian kid knows the first sound, and every Brazilian mother was listening for it.** TikTok's Ipsos
  figure, recall *"over 8x"* with distinctive brand sounds (`codes.md` §0.8), is the reason it exists. Record it as
  foley; **do not synthesise it.**
- **Bed: the house at night, and that's all.**
  - Room tone, the fridge hum, a distant motorbike, one dog three streets away, a TV murmuring through a wall with no
    words.
  - The sounds are the score.
  - Where a track is needed, it is AI-made and assigned to Sol (studio rule), sparse, and never a "cinematic" swell.
- **Her message:** a soft generic two-note *ping* of our own design, never an app's notification sound.
- **The ad voice:** whispered or low (§8.1).

### 5.8 Two render modes, both Blender Cycles

| | **NOITE** (night) | **FLASH** |
|---|---|---|
| **What it says** | The insight: the one light in the dark. | The fun: the night out, the party snapshot, the gift. |
| **Key light** | **The flame**: emissive mesh plus a 1.850 K point light. | **An on-axis hard flash**: a small area light at the camera, 5.600 K. |
| **Fill** | One very dim warm practical: a hallway lamp camera-left at ≈5%, so the label reads. A thin warm rim from behind-right separates the dark glass from the dark ground. | None. Hard shadow behind the object; a specular hotspot on the gloss. |
| **Ground** | Madrugada and Corredor; dark lacquered wood that catches the flame's reflection | A saturated seamless in the candle's light colour (hero: Lâmpada yellow) |
| **Truth details** | Soot freckle, wax sink ring, wick curl, the fresta glowing dimly, dust on the gloss under raking light | **The flame looks washed out under flash, as it does in a real flash photo**, plus fingerprints on the lid and keys thrown down mid-gesture. |
| **Exposure rule** | The flame never clips to a white blob; keep the inner flame hue. The label must be readable at 100% in every final frame. | Shadows are allowed to be harsh; the label still stays fully legible. |

### 5.9 What it must never look like

1. **The spa shelf:** frosted glass, bamboo lid, eucalyptus, rolled white towels, *bem-estar*, *ritual de
   autocuidado*. That is the Brazilian default (`category.md` §2).
2. **A memorial.**
   - **The single biggest risk of a candle plus *mãe* plus "a light left on"** (§10).
   - Never:
     - white tapers;
     - a glass that resembles a *vela de sétimo dia*;
     - religious imagery;
     - sepia photographs of a mother;
     - flowers beside a flame in the dark;
     - *saudade*, *em memória*, *eterna*, *luz que guia*, *anjo*;
     - **ever** *"acende uma vela pra ela"* (in Brazil, lighting a candle *for* someone is what you do for a saint
       or the dead).
   - **She is always alive in this brand: she texts back, she pretends to sleep, she switches the light off.**
3. **The tearjerker:** soft-focus hugs, *rainha do lar*, *guerreira*, slow piano. Boticário owns tears
   (`culture.md` §2).
4. **The *bom dia* glitter kitsch**, played straight or ironically. It isn't our world: our mum is the one at 1 a.m.,
   not 7 a.m.
5. **Neon-sign "good vibes" décor**, "cozy hygge" bed-and-blanket flat lays, or a lit candle near bedding, curtains,
   children, pets or a sleeping person (CONAR art. 33).
6. **AI tells:**
   - glow halos;
   - bokeh orbs;
   - smoke wisps;
   - lens flares;
   - teal-and-orange grade;
   - a perfect wick on a perfect wax surface;
   - waxy skin;
   - any letter drawn by a generator (`codes.md` §8).
7. **Anyone else's marks:**
   - WhatsApp's green, ticks or logo;
   - a Pix logo or receipt (`codes.md` §3);
   - Enel, Light, Cemig or Coelba logos;
   - Uber, 99 or any app UI;
   - a real ice-cream or matchbox brand's trade dress (Fiat Lux, Paraná).
   - **Our matchbox borrows the genre, never a design.**

## 6. Tone of voice

### The rules

1. **Say the true thing, then laugh at yourself.** A sincere sentence, then the child's confession in parentheses.
   Never the other way round, and the joke never lands on her age, body, tech skills or cooking.
2. **Two manners.**
   - **Her:** capitals, full stops, a blessing, a signature (*Beijos, Mãe.*).
   - **You:** lower case, no full stop, one word if possible.
   - The brand, when it speaks as itself, writes like you: lower case and short.
   - Headlines in sentence case are allowed because the headline is the truth being told.
3. **Present tense.** She is awake right now, somewhere, waiting for someone.
4. **Her vocabulary is quoted, not borrowed.** *Descansando a vista*, *olha a hora*, *tem comida em casa*,
   *dorme com Deus* appear **in quotes or in her bubble**, as things she said. Gen Z slang is limited to the
   evergreen: *foi mal*, *tô*, *valeu*. No *aura*, *slay* or *delulu* (`culture.md` §6).
5. **Verbs of the night:** chegar, acender, avisar, esperar, tampar, dormir (or pretend to).
6. **Banned:**
   - *aconchego*, *gratidão*, *rainha*, *guerreira*, *mãe é tudo*, *padecer no paraíso*, *vibes*, *relaxa*,
     *acalma*, *aromaterapia*;
   - *rsrsrs*, yellow emoji, exclamation marks (she may use one in her bubble, never the brand);
   - ***"a mãe tá on"***;
   - every memorial word in §5.9.

### Fifteen lines (PT)

**Headlines**
1. **Você nunca chegou numa casa apagada.**
2. **Ela mandava apagar todas. Menos uma.**
3. **Ela tava dormindo. (Claro.)**
4. **Dessa vez, quem acende é você.**
5. **Quanto mais tarde, mais acesa. (Igual a ela.)**
6. **Pix não acende.** *(the tension line for the final week; `culture.md` §5)*
7. **Em cima da hora? Ela tá acostumada a esperar.** *(last 48 hours; 59% buy in the final week, Sebrae MT)*

**Label and pack micro-copy**

8. **cheguei 23:58 · a luz do portão** / *tecnicamente, antes da meia-noite.*
9. **A fresta avisa: quando a cera chegar na linha, é hora de parar.**
10. *(lid)* **boa noite.**
11. *(inside the drawer)* **Por todas as noites que você esperou acordada. (E pelas 3 da manhã: foi mal.)**

**Social captions**

12. *qual luz ela deixava acesa pra você? conta aqui. a gente transforma em vela.*
13. *ela ouve a sua chave do quarto. com a porta fechada. dormindo. (ela diz.)*

### 7.1 The key visual: ***A única luz***

**Format and rule.** 1080 × 1920 master (PORTA template). Crops at 1080 × 1350 and 1080 × 1080 are taken from the

so the same image drops into O ANÚNCIO's 9–15 s block unchanged.

**The set (Blender, NOITE mode).**
- **Darkness, almost total.** Madrugada falling off to black at the edges.
- **The surface:** the top of a dark lacquered wood *aparador* (hallway sideboard) in Corredor, from y≈1030 down. Its
  gloss reflects the flame as a soft vertical smear, and its front edge catches a hairline of warm light at y≈1180.
- **The candle:** **cheguei 23:58** stands centred, **lid off and out of frame**, flame lit. Body height **≈400 px**;
  the flame tip reaches ≈470 px above the base.

## 8. O ANÚNCIO

**Structure (fixed by the studio):**
- **0–6 s:** the presenter talks to camera, no product.
- **6–9 s:** a gesture or reaction.
- **9–15 s:** the approved key visual (§7.1, our render) with a headline set by code.

**Each ad has two alternative 0–1,5 s hooks**, so 3 concepts × 2 hooks = 6 creatives. The presenter **never holds,
names or describes using the product**, and **never** says *eu uso*, *comprei*, *minha mãe amou* or *me salvou*.
She talks about *her* mother's habits, never about a candle, so CONAR art. 27 §9 (testimonials) is never engaged;
she is a model speaking under *licença publicitária* (`label_rules.md` §9).

## 9. Why this beats AVELUNE, FAÍSCA and VOLTA, and why it is not AI slop

### What the previous kits were

Three well-made single-product beauty launches:
- **AVELUNE:** a hair mask in a plum pot, a 54-year-old fictional model.
- **FAÍSCA:** a gloss in a coral bottle, a fictional model.
- **VOLTA:** a styling cream with a real model.

Each is built the same way:
- a generated person carries the emotion;
- a pack is placed beside her;
- a line describes the product category in a poetic register: ***"O cuidado percorre o fio."***, ***"Chegue em
  cor."***, ***"O movimento é seu."***

### What this one adds, point by point

1. **It passes the swap test; theirs don't.** Put *"Chegue em cor"* on any lipstick, *"O movimento é seu"* on any
   styling cream, *"O cuidado percorre o fio"* on any hair mask: they still work, so they belong to nobody.
   ***"Você nunca chegou numa casa apagada"*** only works on a light, and *cheguei* only works as this brand.
2. **The insight is a cultural truth, not a category description.** It comes from a Brazilian house at night: the
   key, the click, the yellow gate bulb, the plate covered with a plate. No previous kit had an insight; they had a
   mood.
3. **The product is the idea.** Before, the pack was a well-labelled container. Here every part of the object carries
   the platform:
   - the fresta, light under a door and a burn-down indicator in one;
   - the time-names;
   - the lid that says *boa noite.*;
   - the matchbox with a reveal in the drawer.
4. **It is devastating with zero generated people.**
   - The KV, the six launch images, the 15 s film and both cuts are **Blender renders plus code-set type**.
   - The people are a bonus layer. The previous kits stand or fall on a synthetic face; this one doesn't need one.
   - That matters for the business too: it is exactly what Sol can produce locally, verified, at quality.
5. **It is a system, not a palette.** Five rules, eight modules, three templates, a motion grammar and a sonic logo.
   *What time is it, which light is on, who is speaking* generates the hundredth execution without a designer. The
   previous kits each had a palette, one face and DM Sans or Cormorant.
6. **It has a sound.** *trec-trec · clic · fsst.* The previous kits were silent by design.
7. **It shows off the studio's one real capability.**
   - A PRIMEIRA VEZ prints a different, exact label for every buyer, set by code and checkable. That is the
     guarantee no generator can make, turned into the product's most moving feature.
   - **The sample case finally demonstrates the label pipeline instead of merely obeying it.**
8. **Legal and safety are brand, not footnotes.**
   - *Tampa quando for dormir* is a safety instruction written as a goodnight.
   - The fresta is a stop line written as mum's *olha a hora*.
   - The keys make every lit-candle image honest about presence.
9. **It is Gen Z without a single expiring slang word.** The humour is in the punctuation (her full stops, your lower
   case), in the timestamps and in the parentheses. All are evergreen, all native to how Gen Z already writes.

### Why it is not AI slop

- **Specificity no model would volunteer.**
  - The light under the door switching off one second after the key turns.
  - *"Descansando a vista."*
  - The 20-word reply to a one-word text.
  - A cardigan with one button missing.

  Slop is generic because models average; this is built from the particular.
- **Every letter is set by code.** Every label is a master file UV-wrapped in Blender or composited, and verified on
  an albedo pass with a planted-error control. Nothing on any pack was ever drawn by a generator.
- **The core imagery is physically modelled CGI**, with the imperfections stated as a spec: soot freckle, sink ring,
  wick curl, washed-out flame under flash, a fingerprint on the lid. **It is disclosed as computer graphics, not
  passed off as photography**, and the AI-generated layer is separately disclosed as AI.
- **Restraint as a rule.** One light per frame: no particles, no bokeh orbs, no glow halos, no smoke, no lens flares,
  no teal-and-orange.
- **A human idea first.** The campaign could run as a typewritten sentence on a black poster and still work. The
  tools only make it beautiful.

## 10. Risks, checks and open points (UNVERIFIED unless sourced)

1. **The memorial reading.** Candle plus *mãe* plus "a light left on" can tip into remembrance (Brazilian practice
   of lighting candles for saints and the dead).
   - **Mitigations, built into the system, not bolted on:**
     - she is always alive and present tense, texting back and switching lights off;
     - every piece carries a joke in parentheses;
     - colour lights, never white tapers;
     - A PRIMEIRA VEZ is about a birth;
     - the banned-word list in §5.9.
   - **Check:** show the KV and Ad 1 to 10 people aged 18–28 with one written question: *"Em uma palavra, do que
     esse anúncio fala?"* **Bar, committed in advance: zero of 10 answer "luto", "morte" or "saudade".** One such answer
     triggers a copy review; two kill the dark-only KV in favour of the FLASH version.
2. **The core insight is a cultural observation, not a measurement** (§2): the R$0 20-person written check, bar
   ≥14/20.
3. **Trademark.** *cheguei* is a common word: INPI classes 4 (candles) and 3, domain and handles **not searched**.
   Possible collisions with delivery apps and shops in other classes.
4. **Manufacturing questions, all UNVERIFIED, all for a supplier:**
   - the clean masked edge and heat tolerance of a spray-coated glass with a bare band;
   - whether the band creates a hot spot;
   - the stop-line height as a safety device;
   - lid-snuffing on this geometry, where the flame rises above the rim;
   - coating cost against a R$139 retail price.
5. **Regulatory.**
   - Route A (cosmetic Grau 1) is modelled. ANVISA's preferred route for candles is **UNVERIFIED**
     (`label_rules.md` §1).
   - Under Route B the box's *"não contém fósforos"* line is harmless, and nothing here imitates food, drink or toys.
   - Allergen declaration follows RDC 1.029/2026 on the fragrance house's certificate.
6. **Night palette against "fun".** If the dark world reads moody rather than playful to the founder, **FLASH mode
   becomes the lead and NOITE the reveal**. The system already holds both, and only the KV order changes.
7. **Personal data (A PRIMEIRA VEZ).** Birth time and date are collected for a single print and deleted after.
   LGPD review **UNVERIFIED**.
8. **Type at small sizes.** Young Serif at 7 pt for legal text, and the 4 mm `lnum` figures with the "g" at 110%,
   must be measured on a real print proof. Doto is never below 3 mm in print.
9. **Fingerprints.** A glossy dark glass shows every touch. That is good in a render (it's a truth detail), and a
   handling question for real retail.
