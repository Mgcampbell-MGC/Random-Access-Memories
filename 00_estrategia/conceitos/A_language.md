# MÃENÊS — Concept A (language-led)

**Tradução: eu te amo.**

Sample case for SOL Estúdio · O LANÇAMENTO + O ANÚNCIO · Dia das Mães, Sunday 9 May 2027.
Fictional brand. Names are working names. INPI, domain and social-handle availability are **not verified**.
Consumer copy is in Brazilian Portuguese; strategy notes are in English. Research references point at
`research/culture.md`, `research/category.md`, `research/codes.md` and `research/label_rules.md`.

---

## The pitch in sixty seconds

Brazilian mothers rarely say "eu te amo" in those words. They say *"leva um casaquinho"*, *"já comeu?"*,
*"me avisa quando chegar"* and *"você não é todo mundo"*, thousands of times. Gen Z spent their teens rolling
their eyes at every one of these lines. Now, living away from home, they hear themselves saying them to their friends. They have
worked out that mum's nagging was a **language with one meaning**, and that they are fluent in it.

So we name the language and make it a brand: **MÃENÊS**. Each candle is a dictionary entry, a *verbete*,
printed on the glass in mum's own words. Each one comes with the same translation: **eu te amo**. Each lid prints
the reply the child never gave: the glass says *"leva um casaquinho"*, the lid says *"levei."* The object is a
conversation twenty years late.

On a shelf where every candle promises calm, MÃENÊS promises nothing. It just repeats what she said.

## 1. Brand name

### Options

| | Name | Read | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **MÃENÊS** (*mãe-NÊS*) | "Mum-ese": the language mothers speak, built like *português*, *inglês*, *japonês*. | **The name is the insight.** A coined word, so it is the strongest kind of mark to own (fanciful, not descriptive). Gen Z will say it unprompted: *"fluente em mãenês"*. It has a real linguistic cousin, ***manhês***, the Brazilian term for how adults talk to babies ([Unicamp repository](https://www.repositorio.unicamp.br/acervo/detalhe/175844); [PUC-SP](https://revistas.pucsp.br/dic/article/download/8270/6140)). *Manhês* is how she spoke to you at one; *mãenês* is how she speaks to you at twenty-four. Its two accents, **~** and **^**, become the identity (§5). | Two diacritics complicate URLs and handles (use `maenes`). A quick web search on 6 Oct 2026 found no exact use of "mãenês"; **that is not a trademark search. UNVERIFIED at INPI.** |
| **B** | **DITO** | "Said", as in *dito e feito*, *eu não disse?*, *o dito popular* | Four letters, global, easy, works on a lid. | A common word, so weak as a mark. It describes the act of speech but not whose speech it is. Names the format, not the feeling. |
| **C** | **CHEGOU?** | Mum's single most-sent message | Instantly recognised; the question mark is the logo. | One phrase cannot carry a range of phrases. The brand would be about arrival, not about her whole vocabulary. Awkward in a sentence: "uma vela da Chegou?". **Kept as copy, not as a name.** |

**Rejected on sight, with reasons worth keeping:**
- **AVISA.** It is one letter from **ANVISA**, the regulator that has banned candles in this very category
  (RE 762/2025, `label_rules.md` §1). A name that reads like the regulator's is both a joke and a liability.
- **DONA.** It collides with Dona Benta, a household-name food brand. *Dona ___* stays as a personalisation
  field.
- **MAINHA.** It is beautiful and regional (Northeast, `culture.md` #31). As a national name it excludes most of
  the country, so it becomes a **regional lid edition** instead.
- **RECADO.** A generic noun, and too close to "bilhete" stationery.

### Pick: **MÃENÊS**

Written lowercase as a wordmark, **mãenês**, the way a dictionary sets a headword. In running text it is
"MÃENÊS" or "Mãenês". Handle and URL: `maenes`.

**Why it wins.** W+K would say the name has to *contain the platform*, and this one does: you cannot say it
without explaining the brand. Collins would say the identity has to *already exist in the material*, and it does:
the **til** and the **circunflexo** are the two marks only Portuguese makes this famous, and both sit inside
the word *mãe*.

## 2. Tagline, brand idea, insight

### Tagline (permanent, every piece)

# **Tradução: eu te amo.**

**Why this line and not the alternatives.** *"Fluente em mãe."* and *"Ouvi todas."* were both on the table.
*"Tradução: eu te amo."* wins because **it is a template**: it attaches to any phrase, forever. *Leva um
casaquinho → Tradução: eu te amo. Já comeu? → Tradução: eu te amo. Pavê ou pacomê? → Tradução: eu te amo.* It is
dry: a dictionary label, a colon, then the heart. And it only works on this product: put it on a frosted spa
tumbler and it means nothing. **That is the swap test the previous kits' lines fail** (§9). The other two lines
survive in the system: *"Ouvi todas."* is printed on the gift card, and *"Responde."* is the Dia das Mães call to
action.

### Brand idea in one sentence

> **PT:** MÃENÊS é a primeira marca escrita na língua que toda mãe brasileira fala: cada vela é uma frase dela,
> e toda frase dela quer dizer a mesma coisa.
>
> **EN:** The first brand written in the language every Brazilian mother speaks. Each candle is one of her
> phrases, and every one of her phrases means the same thing.

### Dia das Mães 2027 campaign line

# **Responde.**

*Vinte anos de "leva um casaquinho". Este ano, responde.* The child finally answers, in her language. **The lids
are the answers.**

### The insight (the Gen Z truth)

> **You rolled your eyes at every one of her phrases. You memorised all of them. And now you say them.**
> **Você revirou os olhos pra cada frase dela. Decorou todas. E agora você fala.**

**What holds it up:**

1. **Mum's love really is a repeated vocabulary,** and the Brazilian press already frames it as a *dialect*
   ([Jovem Pan, "a lógica por trás do vocabulário das mães brasileiras"](https://jovempan.com.br/noticias/brasil/entenda-a-logica-por-tras-do-vocabulario-das-maes-brasileiras.html);
   lists of "frases de mãe" in `culture.md` §4). We did not invent the language; we named it.
2. **The mother–child language gap is itself viral content.** Ingrid Guimarães posted her 16-year-old's Gen Z
   slang and got 195.000+ likes; her reply was *"Você está perdendo o português, minha filha"*
   ([Diário do Nordeste](https://diariodonordeste.verdesmares.com.br/entretenimento/zoeira/conversa-entre-ingrid-guimaraes-e-filha-diverte-web-com-girias-da-geracao-z-1.3760519)).
   **We flip it: the child is fluent in mum's language, not the other way round.**
3. **Gen Z leads the view that mum would prefer a Pix (47%), yet 54% still want a physical gift for the emotion**
   (CNDL/SPC, `culture.md` §5). The gift has to do what a transfer cannot: be held, smelt, and say something
   back.
4. **The white space is exact.** In 2026 every campaign spoke *about* mum or *as* mum; **none spoke as the
   child, in the child's register** (`culture.md` §2). And no candle brand owns the date: zero of ~100
   Brazilian candle names are about mothers (`category.md` §0).
5. **Memes work with Gen Z, forced slang does not** (*"se o adulto forçar, vira cringe"*, ES Hoje). **Mum's
   phrases are evergreen and the slang expires,** so the brand is built from her vocabulary, never from theirs.

### Positioning against the shelf

| The Brazilian candle shelf (`category.md` §2) | MÃENÊS |
|---|---|
| Frosted or clear glass, bamboo lid, small white label | Opaque glossy colour, giant type, a printed lid that talks |
| Ingredient or place names (*Lavanda, Ipanema*) | Her sentences (*Leva um casaquinho*) |
| Spa, self-care, *bem-estar* | Comedy, memory, a conversation |
| Promises calm | Promises nothing. Repeats her. |

## 3. The launch product: *Dicionário Mãenês · Vol. 1*

Four entries, each with its own number, colour, scent and lid reply. **Hero SKU: nº 01, LEVA UM CASAQUINHO.**

### 3.1 The four verbetes

Each verbete has five parts:
- **the phrase**, printed on the glass;
- **the definitions**, printed small on the glass, where **the last one is always "eu te amo."**;
- **an example** in mum's hand, on the back;
- **the reply**, on the lid;
- **a scent pyramid**: direction for a perfumer, not a formula.

| | **nº 01 · leva um casaquinho** *(HERO)* | **nº 02 · já comeu?** | **nº 03 · me avisa quando chegar** | **nº 04 · você não é todo mundo** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Respelling** (stressed syllable in CAPITALS, wght 700; in Tomate only on Papel grounds) | le·va um ca·sa·**QUI**·nho | já co·**MEU**? | me a·**VI**·sa quan·do che·**GAR** | vo·**CÊ** não é to·do **MUN**·do |
| **Class** | *expr. mãenês* | *interj. mãenês* | *expr. mãenês* | *loc. mãenês* |
| **Definitions** | 1. vai esfriar. 2. não vai. 3. eu te amo. | 1. você emagreceu. 2. tem comida em casa. 3. eu te amo. | 1. não vou dormir. 2. manda a localização. 3. eu te amo. | 1. não. 2. nem adianta insistir. 3. eu te amo. |
| **Example** (back, in Playwrite BR) | *"Leva um casaquinho, vai que esfria."* — sua mãe, 31 °C | *"Já comeu? Tem bolo."* — sua mãe, 23h40 | *"Me avisa quando chegar, viu?"* — sua mãe, 2h17 | *"Você não é todo mundo."* — sua mãe, toda sexta à noite |
| **Regional variants** | *bota um casaco* · *olha o sereno* | *come, que você tá magrinho* · *cara feia pra mim é fome* | *manda mensagem quando chegar* · *chegou?* | *enquanto morar debaixo do meu teto…* |
| **Lid reply** (the child, finally) | **levei.** | **comi.** | **cheguei.** | **nem você.** |
| **Glass colour** | Rosa Tricô `#FFA9C8` | Amarelo Fubá `#F7C948` | Laranja Abajur `#FF9A52` | Tomate `#C93A25` |
| **Character** | the hug that comes with the telling-off | bolo de fubá coming out of the oven | the hall light she leaves on | nothing like anything else |
| **Top notes** | bergamota, pimenta-rosa | erva-doce, raspas de laranja | flor de laranjeira, neroli | folha de tomate, pimenta-preta |
| **Heart notes** | acorde de flor de algodão, íris, acorde de lã morna | acorde de fubá tostado, manteiga, leite morno | acorde de lençol limpo, jasmim-sambac | figo verde, rosa seca |
| **Base notes** | baunilha, almíscar branco, cashmeran, sândalo | baunilha, cumaru (fava tonka), açúcar mascavo | âmbar suave, cedro, almíscar | vetiver, couro suave, cedro |

**Why nº 01 is the hero.**
- "Leva um casaquinho" is the **most-cited mum phrase on every list** (`culture.md` #1), and the funniest in a
  country where it is 31 °C.
- It maps onto the most giftable scent family: soft, musky, vanilla, a sweater in smell.
- Its reply, ***levei.***, is the cleanest joke in the range: a whole childhood of refusal answered in five
  letters.

**Why the nº 04 rule exception is the point.**
- Every glass is printed in one ink, Caneta blue, except nº 04, which is printed in Papel cream on Tomate.
- **The only verbete that breaks the system is "você não é todo mundo".**
- Its lid reply, *"nem você."*, flips the line back to mum: she isn't everyone either.

**The same system extends beyond May.**
- **Paiês** for Dia dos Pais in August: *"pavê ou pacomê?"*, *"vou passar aí rapidinho"*.
- **Avoês** for Dia dos Avós on 26 July.
- One grammar, a new language per date. *(A brand-architecture idea, not a launch commitment.)*

### 3.3 The personalised SKU: *A FRASE DELA*

**The idea.** The buyer types their own mother's phrase, up to 28 characters.

- **Code sets it** as the headword on the glass, using the exact verbete layout.
- **Fixed definition:** *"1. ver eu te amo."* This is a dictionary cross-reference, and Fraunces has no arrow
  glyph, so it is written as a word.
- **Lid:** the reply the buyer writes.
- **Moderation:** a human approval queue blocks profanity, real people's names, trademarks and anything hateful.
  Nothing prints unseen.
- **Why it matters:** the language becomes *hers*. *"Toda mãe tem uma frase que não está no dicionário.
  Ainda."*

## 4. The vessel and the pack

### 4.1 O COPO (the glass that stays)

**The archetype.**
- It is the **printed Brazilian kitchen glass**: the requeijão or jam glass that came decorated and became the
  house's drinking glass (`category.md` §7.1).
- It sits in the empty cell on the Brazilian shelf: **opaque saturated colour plus giant type**. In the
  category sample, 8 of 11 candles were clear or frosted glass and none carried big type (`category.md` §0).

**Geometry, worked out from the 200 g fill.**
- **External size:** Ø 74 × 82 mm.
- **Glass:** 3 mm wall, 9 mm heavy base.
- **Inside:** Ø 68 mm gives 36,3 cm² of floor. 200 g ÷ ~0,9 g/cm³ ≈ 222 cm³, so the wax is **61 mm tall** and
  sits **12 mm below the rim**. That matches the research's 8–12 mm rule.
- **Rim:** radius 1,5 mm.
- **Proportion:** H/D 1,11, the classic tumbler (Diptyque is 1,17).
- **Status:** wax density is an estimate. This is design direction, not CAD.

**Material.**
- **Finish:** opaque, high-gloss coloured glass, a single solid colour per verbete.
- **Production route:** an external gloss lacquer plus a one-colour screen print. The alternative is opal glass
  coloured through the body.
- **UNVERIFIED** for heat resistance and cost; validate with a supplier.
- **No claim about recycled content.**

**Print.**
- Printed **directly on the glass, not on a paper label**. Screen-print ink is **satin on gloss**, and that
  roughness difference is what makes a render read as real.
- **Ink: one colour per glass.** Caneta `#1B37A8` on nº 01–03; Papel `#FFF6E6` on nº 04 (Tomate glass). One
  ink is cheaper, cleaner, and the only option that clears contrast.
  - A Tomate accent on the coloured glasses was tested and **rejected**: it measures **2,86:1 on Rosa, 3,26:1 on
    Fubá and 2,43:1 on Abajur**, below the 4,5:1 the 2 mm respelling needs.
  - So **stress is marked by CAPITALS and weight (wght 700), never by colour alone**, which also makes it
    readable without colour vision.
  - Tomate appears only on Papel grounds (box, KV, posters, film), where it measures 4,76:1.

**Print map (cylinder wrap).**

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Circumference | π × 74 = 232,5 mm |
| Print band height | 60 mm, from 11 mm above the base to 11 mm below the rim |
| **Front panel** | **120°, 77,5 mm wide**, centred on the camera axis |
| Back panel | 120°, centred opposite |
| Sides | 2 × 60° bare glossy colour. Lets the object read as pure colour in silhouette, and keeps the front and back panels from meeting in one view. |

**Front panel, top to bottom** (nº 01 shown):
- **Strap line** (Fraunces opsz 14, wght 500, ~2 mm cap height, tracking +40):
  - left: **`mãenês ^~`**;
  - right: **`nº 01`**.
- **Headword, two lines:**
  - text: **`leva um`** / **`casaquinho`**;
  - face: Fraunces opsz 144, wght 600, **SOFT 50, WONK 1**, lowercase, Caneta;
  - size: "casaquinho" fills the 77,5 mm width (≈42 pt, cap height ≈10 mm).
  - *This is what reads from across a room and at thumbnail size.*
- **Respelling and class** (Fraunces opsz 14, ~2,2 mm):
  - text: **`le·va um ca·sa·QUI·nho   expr. mãenês`**;
  - the stressed syllable is in capitals at wght 700, in the same ink;
  - "expr. mãenês" is in italic.
- **Definitions** (Fraunces opsz 9, wght 450, ~2,2 mm cap height):
  - text: **`1. vai esfriar.   2. não vai.   3. eu te amo.`**
  - *This is the second read, which only exists in the hand.*
- **A 0,3 mm rule.**
- **Legal line:**
  - text: **`vela aromática`** at left; **`PESO LÍQUIDO 200 g`** at right;
  - **figures 4,0 mm tall**, "g" ≥2,7 mm, Caneta on glass colour (≥4,5:1).
  - Portaria Inmetro 249/2021 requires ≥3 mm for >50–200 g; the house rule is 4 mm (`label_rules.md` §2).

> **The design principle: two reading distances.** From the shelf or a phone screen you read **her phrase**.
> In your hand you discover **the translation**. Everything in the system is built for those two distances and
> nothing in between.

**Back panel.**
- Example in **Playwrite BR** (wght 300, ~3 mm), Caneta: *"Leva um casaquinho, vai que esfria."*
- Fraunces opsz 9: *— sua mãe, 31 °C*
- **`var. reg.: bota um casaco · olha o sereno`** (Fraunces italic, opsz 9).
- **`notas: bergamota, pimenta-rosa · flor de algodão, íris · baunilha, almíscar, cashmeran`**
- **ATENÇÃO**, four lines (Fraunces opsz 9, wght 600):
  - *Nunca deixe a vela acesa sem supervisão.*
  - *Mantenha fora do alcance de crianças e animais.*
  - *Acenda longe de cortinas, tecidos e papéis, sobre superfície firme.*
  - *O copo esquenta: não toque nem mova a vela acesa.*
- *Advertências completas e modo de uso: ver embalagem externa.*
- **`PROTÓTIPO FICTÍCIO · CASO DEMONSTRATIVO · NÃO COMERCIALIZAR`**

### 4.2 O FUNDO (the base)

**The joke.** Brazilian mothers turn things over to see where they came from and what they cost. So the base
is **embossed in the glass**, in a ring around the **^~** monogram:

# **SABIA QUE VOCÊ IA VIRAR.**

This is the brand's last laugh, found by the person it was written for, at the moment she does exactly what it
predicted.

**Emboss.**
- Fraunces opsz 36, wght 700, 3 mm caps, on a 52 mm ring.
- **UNVERIFIED** as manufacturable at that size; a supplier question.

**Base sticker.** Required on the jar, never on the lid (RDC 907 art. 13 I c; `label_rules.md` §6):
`LOTE A0000 · FAB MM/AAAA · VAL MM/AAAA · Indústria Brasileira`.

### 4.3 A TAMPA-BILHETE (the lid is the reply)

**Object.**
- A flat disc, Ø 76 × 10 mm, in **cream-glazed ceramic** (Papel `#FFF6E6`, gloss glaze).
- A food-grade silicone ring (Ø 67 mm) seats it in the mouth.
- It doubles as a **coaster** when the candle is lit.
- **Underside:** the **^~** monogram, debossed.

**Top: the reply, in mum's hand turned around.**
- Face: Playwrite BR, wght 400, Caneta, ~16 mm x-height, centred.
- One word: **levei.** / **comi.** / **cheguei.** / **nem você.**
- Under it, small in Fraunces: **`— resposta, finalmente`**.

**Personalised lids** (set by code at checkout):
- Example: **`pra Dona Cida: levei.`** / **`— Lu`**.
- **Regional lid editions** carry the nickname: **mainha · mamis · mãezinha · Dona ____** (`culture.md`
  #31–34).
- **Never "minha véia" or "coroa".**

### 4.4 O LIVRO-CAIXA (the gift box)

**What it is.** A rigid box made as a **thick hardcover dictionary**: 130 × 185 mm, with a **105 mm spine**.
"The thickest dictionary in the world: hers."

**Outside.**
- **Cover:** cloth-textured paper in Caneta `#1B37A8`. Title in Papel ink: **Dicionário Mãenês–Português** /
  *vol. 1*.
- **Spine:** **MÃENÊS–PORTUGUÊS · PORTUGUÊS–MÃENÊS · 1ª ed. · 2027**.
- **Page edges:** printed in Tomate, like a heavy dictionary's sprayed edge. Four printed **thumb-index
  half-moons** read **`leva · já · me · você`**; they are printed, not die-cut, to hold cost.
- **Front panel legal line:** **`vela aromática · PESO LÍQUIDO 200 g`**, with figures 4 mm (Inmetro: the net
  weight goes on the box front too).

**Inside.**
- The cover opens on a magnetic flap.
- **Endpaper:** a pattern of every verbete in tiny Fraunces, *"o dicionário completo"*, with the line *Para uso
  de quem ouviu todas.*
- **Page block:** foam wrapped in page-edge paper, with a **cut-out** holding the copo upright, **lid up** so
  the reply is the first word she reads.
- **A slot** in the inside cover holds **A FICHA**:
  - a 100 × 150 mm index card in Papel, ruled like a library card;
  - **front:** a blank verbete:
    `_______________________ (expr. da sua mãe)` / `1. _______________ 2. ver eu te amo.`;
  - **back:** pre-printed in Playwrite BR: ***ouvi todas.*** and a signature line.
- **A folded leaflet** carries the full warnings and modo de uso (CDC art. 8 §1 allows *impressos* that
  accompany the product).
- **No matches or lighter** unless Inmetro-certified (`label_rules.md` §8).

**Back panel.** The full Route A block (`label_rules.md` §10–11). Shown as placeholders, all marked
**FICTÍCIO**:
- INCI with declared allergens, plus a Portuguese composition line;
- razão social, CNPJ, AFE nº, Processo Anvisa nº;
- SAC;
- **Indústria Brasileira** *com destaque*;
- VAL;
- *tempo de queima estimado: aprox. 40 h*.

**The simple copo** ships in a **printed sleeve** that wraps it like a dictionary dust jacket, in Papel with
the verbete in Caneta.

### 4.5 The unboxing ritual (five beats, each one a line)

1. **"Um livro?"** She thinks it's a book. Gen Z films her face.
2. **The endpaper:** *Para uso de quem ouviu todas.*
3. **The lid, face up:** ***levei.*** Her own nag, answered.
4. **The ficha,** in her child's handwriting: *her* phrase, the one that is in no dictionary, defined as *ver eu
   te amo*.
5. **She turns the glass over** to see the price. **SABIA QUE VOCÊ IA VIRAR.**

Each beat is a **ten-second unboxing video** (the share format, `culture.md` §5), and none needs a word of brand
copy beyond what is printed on the object.

### 4.6 Regulatory fit (Route A, cosmetic Grau 1; `label_rules.md`)

- **Claims stay inside IN 474/2026 group 2:** *perfuma o ambiente, aroma agradável.* No *relaxa*, *acalma* or
  *aromaterapia*. Nothing *para gestantes* or *para o quarto do bebê*.
- **"O copo que fica" is a design intention, not a label claim.**
  - We never say *"vira copo de beber"*: food-contact safety of a candle glass is unverified, and it would be a
    CDC risk.
  - Store images show it reused as a **pen pot**, filled with blue pens; the brand ink, again.
  - ⚠ **On Route B (saneante) the whole reuse idea dies** (*"Não reutilize a embalagem vazia para outros fins"*,
    RDC 989 art. 45). This concept therefore **requires Route A or Route C**. Say so to any real client.
- **"Já comeu?" is a gourmand scent in a yellow glass. It must never look edible.**
  - No cake, plates or cutlery beside it.
  - No food-shaped vessel on any route (RDC 989 art. 29; CDC arts. 8–10).
  - **The name asks about food; the object is a glass.**

### 5.3 Graphic devices: the modular kit

1. **O VERBETE.** The core module, a dictionary entry with fixed slots:
   - headword;
   - respelling (stress in capitals; Tomate on Papel grounds);
   - class in italics;
   - numbered definitions;
   - example in Playwrite;
   - regional variants;
   - number.

   **Rule: the last definition is always "eu te amo."** The module scales from a 2 mm label to a 6 m
   lambe-lambe poster without being redesigned.
2. **O ^~ (casa e voz).** The monogram is the two accents in *mãenês*, stacked:
   - **the circunflexo ^ is a roof**, and **the til ~ is a voice, a flame, a curl of smoke** under it;
   - together: **a light on inside a house**, which is *"me avisa quando chegar"* drawn with two keystrokes;
   - it can be typed on any keyboard, so the brand mark can be sent in a text.
3. **A MÃO DELA.** The Playwrite BR layer. Wherever mum "speaks", it is in her school cursive; wherever the
   dictionary "explains", it is in Fraunces. **Two voices, two faces, never swapped.**
4. **O MARCA-TEXTO.** A flat Amarelo Fubá bar that wipes behind a word. It is the caption highlight in the ad
   and the emphasis tool in print, because the dictionary is studied, and Gen Z studies with a highlighter.
5. **A PÁGINA.** The dictionary page grid, used on posters, carousels and the KV:
   - **guide words** top-left (*casaquinho — chegar*);
   - a **folio** top-right (always **9**, for 9 May);

   - a **thumb tab** bleeding off the right edge, printed *mãe*.
6. **A FICHA.** The index-card module: blank verbete plus *ouvi todas.* It carries personalisation, the social
   templates (*"escreve a dela"*) and the CRM.
7. **~~~ (digitando…).** The typing indicator is **three tildes**: used in chat devices, in loading states and
   in the film.
8. **A NOTA DE VOZ DE TILS.** The voice-note waveform drawn as a row of tildes of varying height, with a

9. **FIGURINHAS.** A pack of die-cut vector stickers:
   - the phrases;
   - the lid replies;

### 5.4 Photography and render style

- **"Prancha de dicionário"** (the default): the product as a **figure in a reference book**.
  - Frontal, centred, hard flash, crisp single shadow, flat Papel ground.
  - A code-set caption in the dictionary manner: ***fig. 1 — leva um casaquinho, 200 g.***
  - It is the most effortless-looking way to show an object, and TikTok's own guidance asks for *"a DIY or
    not overly polished style"* (`codes.md` §0), while every edge is controlled.
- **"Luz acesa"** (the emotional mode): darkness, one flame as the key, the glass's gloss holding a single warm
  highlight. Used only for nº 03 and for the "única luz" line.
- **People (bonus layer, generated later):**
  - eye-level, natural window light, phone-honest framing, real skin, no glow;
  - the product is always a composited still, **never in a moving hand**.

### 5.5 Motion grammar (the system moves)

| Move | What it does | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| **Derrete** | Fraunces **SOFT 0→100** (plus WONK 0→1) on "eu te amo." The crisp serif softens like warm wax. | 18 frames, ease-in-out, once per piece |
| **Respira** | The ~ of the monogram rocks ±3°, like a flame in still air | 2 s loop |
| **Digita** | ~~~ pulses, then the text appears word by word | 4 frames per word |
| **Salta** | On a cut, the incoming still scales 96→100% (squash and settle) | 4 frames |
| **Grifa** | The marca-texto bar wipes left-to-right behind the active word | 6 frames |

### 5.6 Sound

- **The sonic logo is the match:** strike (0,4 s), then the flame catches (0,6 s).
  - A candle owns this sound, and TikTok's own research reports brand recall *"over 8x"* with distinctive
    brand sounds (`codes.md` §0).
- **Percussion is the pen:** ballpoint scratches and a heavy-book thump.
- **Music:** an original track made by AI and assigned to the studio (studio rule), sparse. Dry handclaps and a
  plucked bass at ~92 bpm, so the edit can land on the pen.
- **Voice:** a licensed synthetic PT-BR voice. **No real voice is cloned or imitated.**

### 5.7 What it must never look like

- **The frosted spa tumbler** with a bamboo lid and a whisper label: the Brazilian shelf.
- **The amber jar with a kraft label:** the US shelf.
- **"Live, laugh, love" script.**
- **The tearjerker:** soft-focus mother-and-child hug, piano, slow motion. Boticário owns it.
- **The *bom dia* image played straight:** glitter, roses, Comic Sans. We quote mum's codes; we never become
  them.
- **Flork,** or anyone's meme character.
- **WhatsApp's green, logo or exact interface.**
- **The Pix logo or a Pix receipt** (BCB marca; `codes.md` §3).
- **AI tells:** waxy skin, glow halos, floating particles, bokeh orbs, a teal-and-orange grade, perfect
  symmetry, a flawless wick.
- **Chrome 3D type.**

## 6. Tone of voice

### The rules

1. **Deadpan, then warm.**
   - The dictionary never jokes; it just defines, and the joke is in the definition.
   - The warmth arrives once, at the end, in *eu te amo*. **Rule: "eu te amo" only ever follows a joke.**
2. **Lowercase. One punctuation mark. No exclamation marks.**
   - This avoids the 2021 cringe list (*rsrsrs*, perfect capitalised grammar; `culture.md` §3).
   - **Mum is the exception:** in quotes, she writes with capitals and full stops, like a letter.
     *"Beijos, mãe."*
3. **Her vocabulary, never theirs.**
   - No *aura*, *slay* or *delulu*. *POV:* is allowed as a format.
   - Mum's lines are evergreen; Gen Z slang expires before May.
4. **The joke lands on the child, never on her.**
   - The forgotten casaquinho, the unanswered *chegou?*.
   - Never her age, body or tech skills. ***Zoeira com respeito.***
5. **No testimonial, ever.**
   - No *eu uso*, *comprei*, *minha mãe amou* or *me salvou*.
   - The brand speaks *about* her to *you*, in the second person.
6. **Never** *"a mãe tá on"* (it means a woman is single and flirting), never *chinelada*, never *engole o
   choro*.
7. **Scent words stay inside the rule:** *perfuma, aroma, aconchego, cheiro de casa*. Never *acalma*, *relaxa*
   or *aromaterapia*.

## 8. O ANÚNCIO

### 8.1 The presenter: **Z01, *a Intérprete***

- **Who:**
  - a **fictional adult**, created from scratch, appearing **about 24**;
  - must **not resemble any real person or celebrity** (CONAR art. 34);
  - disclosed on every frame.
- **Role:** a deadpan **interpreter of mãenês**. Half news anchor, half the friend who explains your mum to you.
  - **She never says "eu" or "minha mãe"; she speaks to *you* about *sua mãe*.**
  - She is a narrator, not a character with a life the ad could be mistaken for. This is the cleanest way to
    honour CONAR art. 27 §9 and Anexo Q: no testimonial can exist if she never claims an experience.
- **Look:**
  - Brazilian; light-brown skin with a warm undertone and **freckles across the nose**;
  - thick, straight dark brows; dark brown **3B–3C curls** in a high, loose bun with **a yellow unbranded pencil
    pushed through it** (the dictionary nerd, in one prop);
  - small gold hoops; no other jewellery; short unpainted nails;
  - bare skin with visible texture, brushed brows, clear balm.
- **Styling:**
  - an **oversized hand-knit cardigan in Rosa Tricô** over a plain white ribbed tank;
  - **she is wearing the casaquinho**, and it is clearly a warm day. The gag is never mentioned.
  - **No blue or cyan anywhere** (the chroma rule, §5.1).
- **Energy:**
  - deadpan newsreader: still head, minimal blinking, one raised eyebrow at most;
  - the joke lands on a **held look**, not a face pull;
  - **the warmth cracks through once,** on *eu te amo*: the eyes soften and a small closed smile, then it is
    gone.
- **Voice:**
  - licensed synthetic PT-BR, mid-low, dry, unhurried, a São Paulo urban cadence;
  - **falling pitch at the end of lines; never sing-song, never "oi gente"**.
- **Set:**
  - a **Papel-coloured wall**;
  - at frame left, a **small blank whiteboard on an easel**, completely empty;
  - window key from camera right with a white bounce;
  - **camera locked off at eye level**, about a 26 mm phone-equivalent, framed chest-up.
- **Why the board is blank:**
  - **the locked camera lets code write on it** (the "lesson" lines, in Caneta, Playwrite or Fraunces) with no
    tracking;
  - she never moves into the board area, so no mask is needed;
  - the generator is told to keep it blank. *Rule 1 holds: every letter is ours.*

**Structure, every ad.**

| Time | Content |
|---|---|
| 0–6 s | She talks to the lens. **No product.** |
| 6–9 s | A gesture or reaction; **the match strike lands at 8,6 s.** |
| 9–15 s | **The approved KV as a single image layer**, global 2D push 100→104% over 9–14 s, last second still. V.O. continues. |

**Fixed throughout.** The disclosure tag:
- text: **`imagem ilustrativa criada com IA · publicidade`**;
- position: top-left, y ≈ 280–330, on a Papel plate shaped like a sticker, ≥34 px, Caneta (≥4,5:1).

**Captions.**
- TikTok Sans 800 at 58 px, lowercase;
- max two lines of ≤30 characters, baseline band y 1000–1240 (moving to y 300–520 once the KV fills the lower
  half);
- **the active word gets the Amarelo Fubá marca-texto bar**;
- ≤17 characters per second.

### 8.5 Production notes for the generated layer (later, in Manus or Seedance)

**What gets generated.** Only the presenter takes, 0–9 s. **The KV (9–15 s) is our render, inserted
conventionally, and is never sent to image-to-video.**

**Takes.**
- **Format:** 6 s independent takes, 9:16, audio off.
- **Each take:** one intention, and a locked camera.

**Generation prompt (EN, base):**

> One continuous locked-off vertical shot, chest-up, eye level, of a fictional adult Brazilian woman appearing
> about 24 (not resembling any real person): light-brown skin with a warm undertone and freckles across the
> nose, thick straight dark brows, dark brown 3B–3C curls in a high loose bun with a plain yellow pencil pushed
> through it (no text or logo on the pencil), small gold hoops, bare natural skin with visible texture. She
> wears an oversized hand-knit cardigan in soft candy pink over a plain white ribbed tank. Plain warm-cream
> painted wall; at frame left a small whiteboard on an easel, **completely blank, no writing, no marks**. Soft
> window light from camera right, white bounce, natural colour, no grade. Deadpan expression, still head,
> looking straight into the lens. No product, no candle, no jar, no packaging, no text anywhere, no blue or cyan
> objects or clothing, no phone screen visible, no glow, no particles, no extra people.

**Lip-sync.** Speech to camera needs a convincing sync pass.

- **If sync is not convincing, fall back** to the FAÍSCA rule: she performs the beats silently (looks, the
  pencil, the cuff, the joinha), and **the lines play as V.O. plus captions**.
- **The comedy survives,** because it was built on timing and stillness, the two things generators do best,
  not on complex action, which they do worst.

**Reject a take if:**
- the board shows any mark;
- a logo appears on the pencil;
- the face or curls drift between takes;
- the cardigan changes colour;
- fingers are invented.

**Six versions:** three ads × two hooks; only the first 1,5 s differs.

## 9. Why this beats AVELUNE, FAÍSCA and VOLTA, and why it is not AI slop

### Better than the previous kits

1. **An idea, not a mood.**
   - *"O cuidado percorre o fio."* and *"Chegue em cor."* are well-made lines that pass **the swap test badly**:
     put either under another brand in the category and it still works.
   - *"Tradução: eu te amo."* only works on a product that prints mum's phrase on itself.
   - **The previous kits had taglines; this has a platform**, a language that generates a product, a
     campaign, a store and a calendar (Paiês, Avoês).
2. **The copy is the product.**
   - The previous kits kept copy *off* the pack and the label quiet. Here the label is the headline, the lid is
     the punchline and the base is the button.
   - **The object does the advertising after the ad ends,** on her kitchen counter and in every unboxing video.
3. **A system, Collins-grade, not a set of images.**
   - Rules that generate: the verbete module, *the last definition is always "eu te amo"*, two reading
     distances, the ^~ mark, the nº 04 exception that carries the meaning, the motion grammar and the
     microcopy library.
   - A new phrase becomes a new SKU, poster, lid and ad **with no new design work.**
4. **It fits what the studio can actually make, and makes the most of it.**
   - AVELUNE, FAÍSCA and VOLTA depend on generated people in every hero piece, and their campaigns are still
     marked *PENDENTE*.
   - **Here every hero asset is vector type plus Blender renders the studio can produce locally to finished
     quality:** the KV, the six images, the film and both cuts.
   - **People are a bonus layer;** if every generator failed tomorrow the campaign would still ship complete.
5. **A register the portfolio didn't have.**
   - The earlier kits are earnest and premium. This one is **funny, Gen Z and deadpan without slang**, which
     proves the studio can do comedy as well as care.
   - Humour is the most-engaged motherhood theme in Brazil (`codes.md` §0.11).
6. **Commercial thinking built into the creative.**
   - A countdown cut for the 59% who buy in the last week.
   - Cart recovery in mum's own deferral.
   - A personalisation SKU that raises the ticket.
   - A sticker pack that travels into the family group.
   - An opt-out for the people the date hurts.
7. **Regulatory fluency without breaking the idea.**
   - The Inmetro 4 mm net weight, the Route A choice (and why Route B would kill the reuse idea), claims inside
     IN 474, CONAR art. 33 safety lines.
   - **No testimonial is even possible**, because the presenter never claims a life.

### Why it is not AI slop

- **The idea is a human, sourced truth,** with its own linguistic cousin in the literature (*manhês*), not a
  vibe.
- **The brand is typographic, and type is the one thing generators do worst.** **Every letter is set by code**:
  label, lid, base, board, captions, chat. A generator never draws a word.
- **The product is modelled, not generated:**
  - real geometry from the fill weight;
  - satin ink on gloss;
  - a sink ring, a 5° wick lean, one dust speck.
- **No glossy AI face in any hero piece.** The presenter's comedy is stillness and timing on a locked camera,
  so the generator is asked for the least it can get wrong.
- **The copy sounds like a kitchen table, not a brand deck:** *levei.* · *nem você.* · *sabia que você ia
  virar.*

### Risks, stated plainly

- **Clever can read as cold.** The dictionary conceit could feel cerebral. Three things answer it:
  - the colour;
  - the lid replies, which are pure warmth;
  - the presenter's single crack of a smile.

  *Test the KV on five people under 28 before production.*
- **Fraunces is popular.** Its distinctiveness depends on SOFT, WONK, opsz 144 and the Playwrite pairing.
  Without the motion it is a 2024 startup serif.
- **Manufacturing is UNVERIFIED:**
  - opaque gloss glass with a one-colour screen print on a 74 mm cylinder;
  - 3 mm embossed base text;
  - a ceramic lid with a decal;
  - the cost of a rigid book box.

  For the demo, these are renders.
- **The name, domain and handles are UNVERIFIED** at INPI and the registrar.
- **The ANVISA route is open** (`label_rules.md` §12). This concept needs Route A or C.
- **Blue ink against a chroma-blue workflow** is handled by rule (§5.1), and it must be enforced in every
  generated prompt.
- **The personalised SKU needs human moderation** before anything prints.
