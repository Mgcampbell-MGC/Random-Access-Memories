## The pitch in sixty seconds

Almost every Brazilian of this generation had the same first stage: **the Dia das Mães presentation at school.**
You were five, dressed as a sunflower, in front of a TNT backdrop (the cheap non-woven fabric every Brazilian school
decorates with) covered in paper hearts. You forgot the choreography, cried in the second chorus and waved at the wrong
mother. In the front row, one woman filmed the whole thing with a finger over the lens, then stood up and applauded
as if it were a stadium.

She has been your fan ever since. Reunião de pais, festa junina (she drew the moustache), feira de ciências (she also
built the volcano), the pronto-socorro at 3 a.m., the graduation, and four car trips to move you into your first flat.
She came to every date of the tour, and she always sat in the audience.

Twenty years later you are the most fan-literate generation Brazil has produced. You will camp for 48 hours on the
pavement outside the Allianz Parque for a ticket, you know the setlist before the doors open, and you fight for the
*grade* (the barrier at the front). **And you have never once been her fan.**

**HOLOFOTE** is a candle brand built on one inversion: **for one day the spotlight turns round. She is the headliner,
and you are the opening act.**

- **The candle is tour merch.** The glass is printed as a São Paulo *lambe-lambe* show poster: her name as the
  headliner in giant woodtype, and *abertura: você* (opening act: you) at the bottom in the smallest type on the pack.
- **The back is the tour T-shirt.** It lists the dates of the tour she sat through, ending on 09.05.2027: *a atração é ela.*
- **The lid is the stage mark,** a gaffer-tape X where the star stands.
- **The gift box is a road case.** It opens onto a dressing-room mirror ringed with light bulbs, so the first thing she
  sees is herself: *olha a atração.*
- **The wood wick crackles like a small round of applause.**

Every other Mother's Day keeps her at home, in her role: the saint, the comic or the cook. This one takes her out of
the house and puts her on stage.

**holofote nela.**

### 0.1 Why this is the wildcard: the contrarian move

It refuses three reflexes.

1. **The category's reflex: a candle is a sedative.** The Brazilian shelf is spa, ritual and *bem-estar* in frosted
   glass with a bamboo lid (`category.md` §2). HOLOFOTE is the only loud candle on that shelf: a show, applause,
   day-glo colour, a black stage.
2. **The date's reflex: mum is cast in her role.** Brazilian Mother's Day advertising has three ways to cast her:
   - **the saint**, with sacrifice and tears (Boticário's *Despedidas*, 40M+ views, `culture.md` §2);
   - **the comic**, quoting her phrases (Tim, Natura, Rede Bom Lugar);
   - **the servant**, through domestic gifts (the cringe list, `culture.md` §6).

   All three keep her doing the job. M&M's 2026 trend list names *"women beyond motherhood"* (`culture.md` §2). No one
   has made it funny, and no one has made it giftable.
3. **The tournament's reflex: the truth lives in her house.** Concepts A–D each found something beautiful inside it:
   - her phrases (A);
   - her cabinet of good things kept for guests (B);
   - the light she left on (C);
   - her voice notes (D).

   **E leaves the house and uses the child's world (shows, fandom, merch) instead of hers.**

**The rule it keeps:** the joke always lands on the child (*abertura: você*, *o seu pior show*), never on her.

## 1. Brand name

### Options

| | Name | What it carries | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **HOLOFOTE** (*o-lo-FÓ-tchi*) | *Spotlight*. Dicio gives a literal sense, *"projetor… usado em palcos de teatro ou shows"*, and a figurative one: being the centre of attention, with the example *"recusava-se a estar sob holofotes!"* ([Dicio](https://www.dicio.com.br/holofote/)). | **A candle is the oldest spotlight**: a light you aim at someone. The figurative sense is her whole biography, *a vida inteira longe dos holofotes* (a lifetime out of the spotlight), and the brand is the antidote to that idiom. It is a four-syllable word that is fun to say and already a Gen Z joke (*"ela ama um holofote"*). Its **three O's are a lighting rig**, and lighting one gives us the logo (§5.4). Not descriptive of candles, so stronger as a mark than *acesa* or *chama*. A web search for "Holofote" + *vela aromática* found no candle brand (§11). | A common noun, so it needs the wordmark to register. Its figurative use can mean "attention-seeker", which we turn into a joke: *"Ela vai dizer que não precisava. Precisava."* **INPI search not done: UNVERIFIED.** |
| **B** | **PRIMEIRA FILA** | *The front row*, where she sat for every show you gave. | The most emotional of the three, and natively inclusive: whoever sat in your front row. | Two words and six syllables. It names a seat, not a light, so it does not touch the product. *Primeira Fila* is a common title for TV and music products (UNVERIFIED). **Kept as the personalisation field and as launch image C02.** |
| **C** | **MAIS UM!** | The encore chant. Brazilian crowds shout *"Mais um! Mais um! Bis! Faltou aquela!"* ([DOL, 2018](https://dol.com.br/entretenimento/musica/noticia-531155-toca-mais-uma-onde-surgiu-o-famoso-bis-dos-shows.html)). | Pure energy; everyone has shouted it. | As a brand it reads as a quantity (*"comprei mais um"*), and punctuation inside a mark is fragile. **Kept as a SKU name** (§3). |

**Rejected, with the reasons worth keeping:**
- **BIS.** The perfect word, and also **Bis, Lacta's chocolate wafer**, a household brand in Brazil. Dead on arrival.
- **DIVA / MÃE (as slang).** On the Brazilian internet, calling a pop singer *"mãe"* is a fan's highest praise. A
  communication study traces that use to the LGBTQIAPN+ community: the diva as the one who gave *acolhimento*
  (shelter and acceptance), as in *"mãe dos LGBT"* (search summary of an academic paper in the journal *ECOM*, §11;
  **page not reachable today, so secondary**). For many queer kids, giving a singer that word is about chosen family,
  sometimes because the biological one failed. **A Mother's Day candle must not take that word back for the
  biological mother as a punchline.** HOLOFOTE never uses *"mãe"* as diva slang and never says *"trate sua mãe como
  você trata a diva."* It borrows the fandom's **institutions** (tour, setlist, wristband, fan club, merch), which
  belong to everyone, and leaves its vocabulary alone.
- **CAMARIM.** It names a room, not an act, and is a common name for salons and bars. **Kept as a SKU name.**
- **APLAUSO.** It sounds like an awards night or a corporate recognition programme.

### Pick: **HOLOFOTE**

Always set in capitals in the wordmark, *HOLOFOTE*. In running text it is "a HOLOFOTE" or "Holofote", and the handle is
`holofote`.

**Why it wins.** W+K would say the name must already contain the action, and it does: *holofote nela* is the brand name
used as a verb, and the audience says it unprompted. Collins would say the identity must already be in the word, and it
is: three O's, three lamps, one switched on.

## 2. Tagline, brand idea, insight

### Tagline (permanent, every piece)

# **holofote nela.**

Two words in lower case, ending in a full stop. It works as an imperative to the buyer (*point the light at her*), as a
description of the object (a candle *is* a small spotlight) and as a social caption (*"bota o holofote nela"*). It
outlives Mother's Day: the front row also includes grandmothers, godmothers, the aunt who raised you and the father who
came to every recital (§3.4).

### Brand idea in one sentence

**HOLOFOTE inverte o palco: no Dia das Mães, a atração é ela e quem aplaude é você.**

### The Dia das Mães 2027 platform

# **MÃE AO VIVO**
**Line:** *Ela aplaudiu de pé o seu pior show.* **Call:** *Sua vez.*

### The insight: a Gen Z truth about mothers

**She was your first fan. You have never been hers.**

It rests on three observable things, none of which is a guess.

1. **The school Dia das Mães presentation is a national ritual, and she was the audience.**
   - Brazilian schools stage presentations for the date, with children singing and dancing on a stage while mothers
     watch, film and cry.
   - One Manaus school titled its 2017 show **"Minha mãe, uma diva"**. Its children performed Madonna, Beyoncé and Lady
     Gaga choreographies at the Teatro Manauara, and a mother told the reporter she always gets emotional watching her
     son's presentations ([Portal Amazônia, 14 May 2017](https://portalamazonia.com/especial-publicitario/criancas-encaram-coreografias-de-divas-do-pop-em-homenagem-ao-dia-das-maes-em-manaus/)).
   - Municipal schools publish the same events, for example São José dos Campos in 2019 ([SJC](https://www.sjc.sp.gov.br/noticias/2019/maio/11/escola-da-regiao-leste-reune-comunidade-para-comemorar-dia-das-maes/)).
   - **The first stage of a Brazilian life was a Mother's Day show, and she watched it.** The sunflower costume,
     the forgotten steps and the finger over the lens are cultural observations: **UNVERIFIED** as measured facts,
     and fine as creative material.
2. **Gen Z is the most committed fan generation Brazil has had.**
   - Before Taylor Swift's 2023 São Paulo dates, *"muitos jovens estão acampados no local há mais de 48 horas"*
     outside the Allianz Parque ([Metrópoles, 12 Jun 2023](https://www.metropoles.com/sao-paulo/taylor-swift-fas-acampam-no-allianz-parque-para-comprar-ingressos)).
   - Fans began camping months ahead (search summaries of [Tribuna do Sertão](https://www.tribunadosertao.com.br/variedades/2023/06/05/505118-fas-de-taylor-swift-encaram-chuva-e-assalto-em-acampamento-no-allianz),
     **secondary**).
   - One headline that week read *"Preocupados, pais escoltam fãs de Taylor Swift"* ([Gazeta SP](https://www.gazetasp.com.br/entretenimento/preocupados-pais-escoltam-fas-de-taylor-swift/);
     **headline only, the page returned 403**). **The parent was in the queue too, as the escort.**
   - Festivals now hand out collectible cups by the hundred thousand: Itaú's **100 mil copos colecionáveis** at Rock
     in Rio 2026 ([Trademap, 5 Sep 2026](https://trademap.com.br/noticias/news-8273940)). **Gen Z keeps the merch.**
3. **Her biography is the idiom.** *Longe dos holofotes* is how Portuguese describes a life of work without applause
   (Dicio's figurative sense, §1). A brand named after the spotlight is a reply to the phrase.

**The tension.** Gen Z already knows exactly how to be a fan: queue, scream, buy the merch, keep the wristband. Mother's
Day has never given them that role. **HOLOFOTE hands them a part they have rehearsed for years and gives it a new
headliner.**

**What it unlocks, in one move:**
- **Funny:** the target is *your* worst show.
- **Moving:** *she stood up.*
- **Inclusive:** the front row belongs to whoever sat in it. In May 2026 some schools began replacing the Mother's Day
  party with a *"Dia de Quem Cuida"* so that *"avós e tios… recebem a devida valorização"*
  ([Jovem Pan, 10 May 2026](https://jovempan.com.br/noticias/brasil/entenda-por-que-algumas-escolas-estao-trocando-a-festa-de-dia-das-maes-pelo-dia-de-quem-cuida.html)).
  A headliner field that takes any name answers that.
- **Gift-shaped:** fans buy merch. The product does not need a justification; it is the souvenir of her show.

**Both readings are true.** Gen Z reads a lineup-poster joke about themselves. She reads that she is the star. Neither
audience is excluded.

## 3. The launch product: **A TURNÊ** (Dia das Mães 2027)

Four candles, one for each moment of her show: **before, during, the encore, after.** Each smells like that moment
smells in her life, not in a theatre. Each wears its own poster colour. All four read **MÃE** as the headliner, so a
shelf of them becomes a lambe-lambe wall: MÃE MÃE MÃE MÃE.

**Perfumery notes for the brief to a fragrance house (studio direction, not a formula):**
- **CAMARIM** is the classic "lipstick" accord: iris and violet over aldehydes, with a raspberry glint. It is the
  smell of her getting ready to go out, and the most personal of the four. *Who she is beyond being your mother* is
  bottled here.
- **AO VIVO** is radiant floral-amber, the broadest-appeal profile. Floral or botanical is the favourite profile of
  **74% of Gen Z** in a trade survey (2026 Gift Book survey via Gifts & Decorative Accessories, `category.md` §6;
  **secondary, sample size not stated**). Rose and orange blossom are a Brazilian Mother's Day vocabulary without
  being a cliché.
- **MAIS UM!** is grown-up gourmand: orange, cocoa and tonka, never cupcake. The name is a show word, not a food
  word, and the vessel never imitates food (`label_rules.md` §8).
- **ACÚSTICO** is clean, soft and calm: the only "calm" candle in the range, and it is named as the quiet *version*
  of a loud show. That is how HOLOFOTE stays loud even when it whispers.
- **Formula rules:** no lilial (butylphenyl methylpropional, now prohibited by RDC 1.029/2026). Muguet accords are
  out for that reason. Declare allergens above 0,001% from the supplier's certificate (§4.8).

## 4. The vessel and the pack

### 4.1 **O COPO DO SHOW** (the show cup)

The festival collectible cup, rebuilt as a heavy glass tumbler. People keep festival cups, and Brazilian mothers keep
every printed *copo de festa* ever brought home. **The glass is designed to stay**, the category's *"copo que fica"*
recommendation (`category.md` §7.1A), and it stays as the souvenir of her show.

| | Spec (direction, not CAD) |
|---|---|
| **Form** | Straight-walled cylinder, no taper. **The label is a simple cylinder wrap (rule 4).** |
| **Size** | **Ø 78 mm outside × 88 mm tall**; wall 3,5 mm; **heavy base 14 mm** (the weight of a good party glass in the hand); rounded, polished rim. |
| **Wax geometry** | 200 g ÷ 0,88 g/cm³ ≈ 227 cm³. Inner Ø 71 mm gives 39,6 cm², so the wax is about 57 mm deep. Its top sits about **17 mm below the rim**, enough headroom for a wood-wick flame. |
| **Body finish** | **Opaque, high-gloss coloured coating on clear soda-lime glass**, one day-glo colour per SKU (§5.2). Print references: fluorescent Pantone 803 C (yellow), 806 C (pink), 804 C (orange), 801 C (blue). **Hex values are screen approximations; confirm on a physical guide.** |
| **The honest edge** | The coating stops **1 mm below the rim**, so a thin line of clear glass catches the light at the mouth. That one detail tells the hand and the camera that this is real glass, not plastic. |
| **Print** | **Direct one-colour screen print, 360°**, between 20 and 82 mm of height (a 245 mm circumference). **Matte ink on a gloss body**: the change in sheen is the premium tell. Preto-palco ink on amarelo, rosa and laranja; papel-cartaz ink on azul (contrast in §5.2). |
| **Base** | Embossed ring in the glass: *HOLOFOTE · A TURNÊ · 2027*. A lot sticker inside the ring (§4.2). |
| **Why not black glass** | Black glass with a pink label is Boy Smells' system (`category.md` §4). |
| **Why not clear or frosted** | That is the whole Brazilian shelf (`category.md` §2). |
| **Why opaque day-glo** | No opaque saturated vessel and no big type exist on the Brazilian shelf: the empty cell (`category.md` §3). Day-glo poster paper is also São Paulo's own street vernacular (§5). |
| **After the candle** | *"A temporada acabou. O copo fica."* Clean with warm water and soap. Use it for brushes, pens or flowers. **We never suggest drinking from it** (fragrance residue and coating food-contact are **UNVERIFIED**). Under the saneante route this suggestion is prohibited (§4.8). |

### 4.6 The unboxing ritual: *a passagem de som*

Four beats, each with its own sound and its own photograph. **The roles are inverted inside the box itself: she does
the star's steps; you do the crew's.**

1. **abre o case.** The magnet goes *clac*.
2. **olha no espelho.** The first thing in the box is her own face, framed by bulbs: *olha a atração.* **The child sees
   nothing; the mother sees herself.** That is the moment people film.
3. **acesso liberado.** She slides the wristband off the glass and onto her wrist.
4. **terceiro sinal.** In Brazilian theatre, the third bell means the show starts. The lid goes down X-up, the candle
   on top, and **you** light it: *hoje você é a equipe técnica.* The wood wick crackles.

**No matches or lighter in the box:** they would need Inmetro certification (`label_rules.md` §8). The setlist assumes
the household's own.

## 5. Visual identity: the world **O SHOW DELA**

Not a logo and a palette. **A stage, a street and a set of rules that can produce endless executions.** The world has
two states, and the campaign moves from one to the other:

| State | When | Light | Surface | Job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **O MURO** (the wall) | The announcement: teaser weeks, shelf, e-commerce | **Hard on-camera flash**: hard shadow, slightly overexposed edges, the party-photo look | Layered *lambe-lambe* posters on rough plaster; concrete ledges | "Something is coming." Lists, dates, the lineup. |
| **O PALCO** (the stage) | The show: the day, the KV, the film, the ads' end card | **One theatre spotlight with a hard edge**; everything outside the pool is black | Black lacquered stage floor with scuffs; gaffer tape; a velvet curtain far behind | "She is on." The product lit, alone, in the pool. |

**The vernacular.** São Paulo's *lambe-lambe* is the woodtype street poster, pasted on walls. It is now preserved only
by the workshop Gráfica Fidalga (Rico Lins, *Limpa, Linda, Muda*, [ricolins.com](https://www.ricolins.com/en/?p=5616);
ESPM paper, `codes.md` §3, **secondary**). It is the show poster of the city: *HOJE · SHOW · DIA 09*. It is native,
Gen Z reads it as current (indie parties use it), and her generation read it on every wall.
**We borrow the grammar, never a real poster.** A real commission from Fidalga is a future upgrade, not a dependency.

### 5.1 The seven rules of the system

1. **Ela é sempre a maior palavra.** Her name (or MÃE) is always the largest element in any layout, and *você* is
   always the smallest. **The hierarchy is the joke and the brand.**
2. **Toda linha ocupa a largura inteira.** Woodtype posters justify by changing the type size and width of each line.
   We do it with Special Gothic's width axis (75–125) and its Condensed and Expanded cuts. Every line fills the measure;
   ragged lines never appear.
3. **A luz tem borda.** One source. A hard-edged pool. The product stands in the pool, and **type never enters the
   pool.** No soft glow, no bokeh, no haze beyond a faint, physically lit cone.
4. **Cada fonte é uma voz** (§6):
   - **Special Gothic in capitals is the Locutor**, the announcer;
   - **Special Gothic Condensed, small, is the Produção**, the crew;
   - **Shantell Sans is the Fã**, the fan, which is you.
5. **Tudo é papel de verdade.** Every flat element is a physical object: paste wrinkles, torn edges, tape, a 1–2 px
   misregistration between ink layers, perforations, lamination glare. **Never a glossy app interface.**
6. **Duas horas do dia:** O MURO announces and O PALCO performs. Never mix the two lights in one frame.
7. **A segurança está no roteiro.** Every lit candle in every frame follows the setlist:
   - attended (or framed as the show itself);
   - on a firm surface;
   - **30 cm clear of paper, fabric and people.**

   This is CONAR art. 33, turned into a design rule (`label_rules.md` §9).

### 5.7 Motion grammar (the system moves)

- **CLAC.** Lights snap on with a 2-frame filament warm-up at 24 fps, never a fade. Off in 1 frame.
- **Corte seco.** Hard cuts on the beat (a 120 BPM fanfarra means a cut every 12 frames). **No dissolves**, anywhere.
- **A batida do cartaz** (the poster slam). Each lineup line enters in 4 frames: `wdth` 75 → 128 overshoot → settles at
  125, weight 700. A *squash* that feels like gummy and woodtype at once (Pinterest's "Gimme Gummy", `codes.md` §0.3).
- **A letra da fã.** Shantell strokes draw on at about 12 frames per word, with `BNCE` oscillating ±20 slowly, as if
  written on a moving sign.
- **Varas** (fly bars). Type exits *upward*, like scenery flown out, never sideways.
- **Câmera.** A crane down, or a slow push in. A product turn of at most 20° (the 3D label is exact, §7.4). **No
  orbiting product hero spin**: that is the stock-candle-ad cliché.

### 5.8 Sound

- **Sonic logo, *a ovação de uma pessoa só* (one-person ovation, 2,0 s).** **CLAC** (a big theatre lamp switching on),
  then 0,8 s of stadium roar, then a hard cut to **one pair of hands clapping three times** in a small room. A whole
  stadium of applause, delivered by one person. That is the gift.
- **Music.** An AI-generated track assigned to Sol (studio rule) in the language of a **school *fanfarra***: snare roll,
  bass drum, brass stabs. It is the sound of school events and parades (her era and yours) and of a stadium drumline.
  120 BPM.
- **Foley:**
  - wood-wick crackle (record a real candle; this is the brand's quiet applause);
  - match strike;
  - the case's magnetic *clac*;
  - the wristband clasp click;
  - a theatre bell (*primeiro sinal*);
  - one audience cough in silence, used as a timing gag.
- **The crackle-to-applause morph.** In the film the wick's crackle crossfades over 1,5 s into distant applause. **The
  candle applauds her.**
- **Every film ships in two versions,** with sound and silent (studio rule). The silent version carries the joke in
  type.

### 5.9 Photography and render style: *luz de show, verdade de objeto*

- **Products** are always **Blender Cycles renders** of the real geometry with the master label texture, in one of the
  two states (§5). **No product is ever generated.**
- **The night stage (O PALCO)** uses one spot at 3.200 K, spot size 24–28°, blend 0,03–0,05 (a hard edge), at 50–60°
  elevation from front-left.
  - The floor is black lacquer (base #0A0A0B, roughness 0,15–0,30 varied, fine scratches, one tape-residue ghost).
  - The curtain sits **at least 4 m behind any flame**.
  - An optional faint cone uses a world volume of density ≤0,002 with no noise texture: physical, not "magic".
- **The wall (O MURO)** uses a small area light 15 cm from the lens as the flash, a hard drop shadow offset down-right,
  and an exposure that clips the poster paper by half a stop. The posters are our own code-generated sheets, pasted
  with displacement wrinkles.
- **The people layer** (generated later in Manus or Seedance, disclosed) has two looks:
  - **the mum as a pop star's press photo**: hard on-camera flash, but in her own living room, with reading glasses on
    her head;
  - **the presenter on an empty school stage** under one spot.

  **The pack is never in a generated frame except as a flat matte chroma-blue stand-in** (studio rule 1).

### 5.10 What it must never look like

- **A spa candle:** frosted glass, bamboo lid, eucalyptus, rolled towels, "ritual de bem-estar".
- **A tribute to a real artist.** No Taylor Swift "eras", friendship bracelets, K-pop logo pastiche or real lightstick
  designs, and **no real festival, venue, artist or ticketing brand**, ever.
- **Drag or ballroom costume, or the *"mãe/mother"* diva slang** (§1).
- **The trophy cliché:** gold cups, *"Mãe nota 10"*, *"melhor mãe do mundo"* mugs, a red carpet with gold stanchions,
  the Hollywood Walk of Fame star.
- **"Rainha do lar", glitter crowns, roses-and-gold:** that is mum's *bom dia* image played straight (concept A's
  territory, played ironically there).
- **A 2000s neon rave flyer:** four fluorescents fighting on one piece.
- **AI-slop tells:**
  - floating confetti or particles;
  - smoke wisps;
  - lens flares;
  - bokeh orbs;
  - glowing halos round people;
  - a perfect wick;
  - a perfect wax top;
  - generated lettering anywhere.
- **A dangerous candle:** lit near curtains, paper, chairs, beds, children or pets; carried while lit; left alone in a
  room (CONAR art. 33).

### The rules

1. **The joke lands on the child.** Never on her age, body, technology skills or cooking. *Zoeira com respeito*
   (`culture.md` §6).
2. **Show vocabulary, not slang.** The brand's dictionary is the *show* in Portuguese: deixa, camarim, coxia, primeira
   fila, terceiro sinal, bis, temporada, ingresso, credencial, setlist, abertura, atração. **It is timeless where
   Gen Z slang expires** (`codes.md` §5). At most one wink per campaign: *a grade*.
3. **Never "obrigada, mãe".** Every card on the market says thank you. HOLOFOTE never thanks her; it *applauds* her.
4. **No testimonials, ever.** No *"eu uso"*, *"comprei"*, *"minha mãe amou"* (CONAR art. 27 §9). The presenter speaks
   to *você*, about *ela*.
5. **No calm claims.** Nothing that *relaxa*, *acalma* or *equilibra* (therapeutic claims are banned, and they are not
   this brand anyway).
6. **Lower case for the Fã and the Produção; capitals only for the Locutor.** No exclamation marks, except inside the
   name *MAIS UM!*, where the crowd is shouting.

## 9. Why this beats AVELUNE, FAÍSCA and VOLTA, and why it is not AI slop

### What the previous kits were

Three competent, well-governed beauty briefs:
- **AVELUNE:** a hair mask, a mature model, plum and pearl, *"O cuidado percorre o fio."*
- **FAÍSCA:** a lip gloss, a model with a bob, a blush street, *"Chegue em cor."*
- **VOLTA:** a styling cream, a real model, a mineral interior, *"O movimento é seu."*

Their value was **method** (fidelity, the chroma-blue stand-in, honest claims), not **idea**. Each tagline could sign
any brand in its category. Each world was a palette and a light. **None had an occasion, a joke, a sound or a ritual.**
And every image was a prompt waiting to be generated: *"Nenhuma cena de campanha, anúncio ou vídeo final foi
produzido"* (AVELUNE §13).

### What this one adds, point by point

| | Previous kits | HOLOFOTE |
|---|---|---|
| **Idea** | A mood line about the product | **A cultural truth with a date and a nation** (the school Dia das Mães show), turned into a role reversal anyone can say in four words: *holofote nela* |
| **Brand** | Name + palette + two fonts | **A world with seven rules, two states, three voices and twelve modular devices**, five of them data-driven. It generates executions without a designer in the loop (§5). |
| **Product** | A jar given a label | **A product designed by the idea:** the glass, back panel, lid, seal, box, insert, card, wick and even the safety copy all carry the concept |
| **Producibility** | 100% dependent on future generation | **The whole core launch (KV, 6 images, F15, 2 cuts, store shots) is producible today, locally,** in Blender Cycles plus code-set type, with **zero generated pixels**. The people layer is a bonus, not a crutch. |
| **The pack in motion** | *"exigiria outra rota… não promete"* | **The pack rises, turns and is lit in the film**, label-exact by construction, because it is real geometry wearing the master texture |
| **Sound** | Room tone | **A sonic logo with a meaning** (one person's ovation), a crackle-to-applause morph and a fanfarra |
| **Humour** | None | **Self-deprecating, aimed at the child**, the register Gen Z actually rewards (`culture.md` §3) |
| **Gift and ritual** | None | **A four-beat unboxing** with a mirror reveal, a wristband she wears and a card |
| **Inclusion and safety** | Disclaimers | **Built into the concept:** the front row means *whoever sat there*; the safety rules *are* the setlist |

### Why it is not AI slop

1. **Specificity is the antidote.** A sunflower costume, a TNT backdrop, white monobloc chairs, a handbag saving a seat,
   a finger over the lens, a moustache drawn with eyeliner, a 3 a.m. pronto-socorro. Slop is generic; every frame here
   is a *named* thing from a Brazilian life.
2. **Every letter is set by code;** every product pixel is a physical render of the master texture.
3. **The vernacular is real and local:** São Paulo woodtype lambe-lambe, a school fanfarra, festival cups.
   It is not a "global Gen Z aesthetic" from a trend deck.
4. **Imperfection is specified:** tape lifts, scuffs, paste wrinkles, misregistration, a soot freckle, a fingerprint, a
   wax sink ring (§4.7).
5. **The forbidden list is enforced:** no particles, smoke, flares, halos, bokeh or floating confetti (§5.10).
6. **Disclosure is designed, not hidden:** *render 3D ilustrativo* on the renders, *criada com IA* on the presenter.
   **Being honest about which is which is itself the brand's credibility.**
7. **It survives the Coca-Cola test.** The 2024 AI Christmas ad failed because AI replaced warmth on the one date where
   warmth is the product (`codes.md` §7). HOLOFOTE's warmth lives in a true memory and a real object. **The machine only
   lights it.**

## 10. Risks, checks and open points (UNVERIFIED unless sourced)

1. **"Mãe" as diva slang.** It has LGBTQIAPN+ roots of *acolhimento* (secondary source, §1). **Mitigation:** never used;
   the brand borrows fandom institutions, not that word. Brief every writer and every generated script on it.
2. **Some schools are replacing the party with *"Dia de Quem Cuida"*** (Jovem Pan 2026). **Mitigation:** the headliner
   field takes any name; the copy says *quem sentou na primeira fila* wherever "mãe" would exclude.
3. **Grief and estrangement.** **Mitigation:** the CRM opt-out line; no ads about loss; the headliner field allows
   memory names quietly.
4. **Fire safety in imagery (CONAR art. 33):**
   - no lit candle near the curtain (4 m), paper (setlists 60 cm away), chairs (stage edge, 2 m) or people (P01: unlit,
     70 cm away);
   - **the candle never moves while lit** (F15);
   - **no confetti in any lit shot.**

   **Check every frame against this list before delivery.**
5. **Fluorescent coatings fade under UV and heat.** The rim zone of a candle glass runs warm. **Coating, heat and
   lightfastness tests are required.** **Fallback:** saturated non-fluorescent "near-neon" colours with the same hexes
   on screen.
6. **Wood wick sizing** (tunnelling, soot) and the real burn rate: **candle-maker test.** The 40 h claim stays "aprox."
7. **Regulatory route.** Route A is modelled (§4.8). Under Route B, *"o copo fica"* must go. Under Route C, the ÁLBUM
   DUPLO is not artisanal. **Which route ANVISA prefers is UNVERIFIED** (`label_rules.md` §1).
8. **Reusing the glass for drinking** is never suggested: food contact of the coating and fragrance residue are
   **UNVERIFIED**.
9. **Dated stock after 9 May.** **Mitigation:** a limited tour run plus a permanent undated line; the date is the
   collectible's value.
10. **Trademarks.**
    - **HOLOFOTE:** INPI classes 3 and 4 not searched.
    - **"ACÚSTICO"** is a generic word, but *Acústico MTV* is a known title: avoid any MTV styling.
    - No real artist, festival, venue or ticketing look anywhere.
    - **Lei Cidade Limpa (SP, Lei 14.223/2006):** no wild-posting.
11. **The case's unit cost** (mirror, foil, foam, PVC card, woven band) may push R$159 to R$169 (§3.3).
    **Costing UNVERIFIED.**
12. **Carteirinhas as a mailed object, the sunflower costume, festa junina moustaches, *não precisava*:** cultural
    observations, not measured facts. They are fine as creative material; never quote them as statistics.
13. **Third-party sounds:** the theatre bell, crowd and applause come from licensed libraries or our own recordings; the
    music is AI-made and assigned to Sol. **No real chant, song or artist audio.**
14. **Fidelity on 3D frames.** The standard checker compares a flat master against a composited pack. For 3D renders,
    **render a label AOV pass** (an emission-only pass of the label texture through the same camera) and check it
    against the master unwrapped through the same UV map. **The method needs a one-day build and test before any
    claim of "verified" on the film.** Until then: *render 3D, texture from the master*, and say no more than that.
