entries (items 1405–1417)** including **Butylphenyl Methylpropional (Lilial, CAS 80-54-6)** and
**Hydroxyisohexyl 3-Cyclohexene Carboxaldehyde (Lyral)**, and removes four. Art. 5 sets **12 months** to adapt
already-regularised products, **18 months** for Lilial and Lyral — so ≈15 June 2027 and ≈15 December 2027, as
claimed. **No fabrication anywhere.**

**And it still does not produce a business, for four reasons that stack.**

| | The kill | Why it is fatal rather than a discount |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **`THE SPLIT TEST`, cleanly failed** | The compelled party is the cosmetics company, and **the input is its own formula sheet.** It already holds every ingredient list. Matching 50 SKUs against **thirteen named substances** is a spreadsheet filter, not a service |
| **2** | **The buyer is legally required to already employ the person who does this** | RDC 894/2024 obliges a *técnico responsável*, and **art. 46 §1º expressly forbids outsourcing the role** — ANVISA's own Q&A: hiring a PJ for it *"não é considerada compatível."* `THE COMPELLED-BUT-CREDENTIALLED TRAP` fires from the other side: she cannot hold the role, and the buyer must |
| **3** | **ANVISA publishes the lookup free** | Its ingredient panel searches by **INCI or CAS number, allows multiple selection, and exports to .xlsx** — `THE FREE-VALIDATOR SCREEN`, exactly as predicted: the body that defines a standard gives the test away |
| **4** | **The category floor was already measured in this file** | `COSMETICS_VERDICT.md`, 14 Aug: a **complete** document bundle sells at **$30** on Fiverr, £131,25 at Phoenix, and ANVISA's own notification is **free in three days**. R$5.900 for a 13-item screen against that floor is not a price, it is a wish |

**The second opinion sees kill 4 coming and answers it** — *"nobody will pay R$5,900 for a glorified lookup;
they must be buying portfolio execution, supplier routing, evidence and professional review."* **That answer
relocates the product into unbounded per-unit human work**: chasing fragrance houses and raw-material

**One more thing to fix, because it fails in the direction of a false positive:** a public product page listing
Lilial is **not** evidence of non-compliance — the transition runs to 2027 and the page may show an old label.
The second opinion says this itself. But a cold email whose entire hook is *"we found a banned ingredient in
your product"* to a company that has nine to fifteen months and an in-house regulatory professional is not
urgency; it is noise that damages the sender.

> **KEEP FOR THE PARTS BIN:** RDC 1.030 is a genuine **clock-start with two named dates and an enumerable

### 3c. The three things that could kill it, in the order they should be checked
1. **★★ `THE REPLACEMENT-COST CEILING`, and it is the sharpest one.** Strip the language away and the weekly
   delivery is: run saved searches, eyeball listings, paste URLs into a **free** portal's bulk CSV form, email a
   tally. **The proposal itself prices the whole automated version at under US$100/month in tools** and says a
   Python script removes 80% of the labour. **A buyer can rebuild that with a virtual assistant, and the machine
   is built over inputs the buyer can also reach.** US$450/month is a price for *accountability*, not for
   scarcity — which is a real thing to sell, and a much smaller thing than the proposal implies.
2. **★★ `THE DISPUTE-IS-THE-PRODUCT LAW`, and here it is measured rather than predicted.** Brazil's public
   complaint record on the BPP is about **wrongful takedowns**: *"denúncias vêm sendo aceitas de forma
   automática, sem análise técnica mínima quanto à titularidade real"*, sellers with genuine goods and notas
   fiscais losing listings, accounts suspended, and lawyers advertising *Ação de Obrigação de Fazer* to reverse
   it. **The hard cases are not counterfeits — they are grey-market resellers of genuine stock**, whose position
   under LPI art. 132 III is generally lawful, and telling the two apart is a legal judgement (the expert veto).
   **The proposal's "Zero Personal Liability — Sol acts purely as an administrative portal user" is an assertion
   with no source.** She is the person who clicked submit. **This is the class of question that killed PRUMO,
   RECURSO and the trademark-maintenance candidate, and it needs one lawyer's opinion before a single notice.**
3. **The fashion exclusion.** The founder's rule book says **"NOTHING IN FASHION OR MODELING."** GUARDA's buyer
   is explicitly apparel, swimwear, eyewear and accessories. The *work* is IP administration, not fashion — but
   this is her call in one word, exactly as GateLedger was, and it should be asked **before** any build.

## 4b. ★★★ THE DEADLINE-HARVEST LAW — the sharpest point made about Conformidade, and it is correct

> ### **A compulsion with a ONE-TIME TRANSITION DATE creates a BACKLOG, not a business. The stock is bought once; what remains afterwards is the FLOW — new entrants, renovations, ownership changes and whatever periodic revision the rule compels. Size the FLOW before calling a deadline a market.**

Applied here, and I accept it: **Conformidade is a September–December 2026 cash campaign, not a company.**

- **Before ≈11 Dec 2026:** every existing dental establishment in Brazil shares one dated problem. That is the
  stock, and it is large.
- **After:** the rule survives and enforcement continues through municipal vigilância inspections, licensing,
  complaints and audits — but nobody is fined the next morning. The offer degrades from *"prepare before the
  deadline"* to *"repair your noncompliance"*, which is a smaller, slower, more support-heavy sale against
  incumbents already at R$679.
- **The only compelled recurrence in the rule is art. 111 IV — revision at least every two years.** That is
  exactly the failure this file already recorded when it killed CONFORME: *"both clocks are 5- and 10-year
  clocks in monthly-subscription costume."* **A 24-month clock is a subscription in a slow costume.**
- **The maintenance pivot does not rescue it under C9.** At R$250–500 per clinic per month it needs **25–50
  active clinics** for the bar — and a maintenance retainer is contact, chasing and responsibility, which is
  precisely the load `HER HOURS CAP CLIENTS, NOT UNITS` says tops out at **8–10**. The pivot is arithmetically
  the wrong shape, not merely harder.

**⇒ THE FLOW IS THE NUMBER NOBODY HAS MEASURED, and it decides the after-December value.** How many dental
establishments *open* in Brazil each year? **Measure it free:** the CNES API returns every establishment with a
`data_atualizacao`, and the Receita CNPJ file carries `DATA DE INÍCIO DE ATIVIDADE` for CNAE 8630-5/04 — count
the 2024 and 2025 cohorts and the annual flow falls out. **UNMEASURED as at 2 Sep 2026.** If the flow is a few
thousand a year nationally, the after-deadline business is 8–10 packs a month at best and the campaign framing
is the honest one.

**What this does NOT mean: that the campaign is worthless.** C5 caps her at **US$3.000, once.** A four-month
harvest that clears R$20–40.000 is *more than ten times the entire capital budget of this project*, and it
would be earned rather than spent. **The correct framing is a funding event with a hard stop, run in parallel
with building the durable business — not the main business wearing a deadline.** State it that way to her, and
put the stop date in writing before starting, because a campaign that is allowed to drift becomes the job.

## 7a. ⚠ FIRST, A CORRECTION TO MY OWN FRAMING — the 13.100 is NOT a compelled pool

WIPO and the Brazilian IP bar both sell the Madrid Protocol on precisely the opposite point:
> *"Uma das vantagens da gestão centralizada pelo Protocolo de Madri é a **não obrigatoriedade de contratar um
> representante/agente local** em cada país de interesse."*

**So the 1.007-a-month holding a granted Brazilian mark with no procurador are not an unserved market — they
are a correctly-served one.** They have no duty until they act. `THE PRESENT-TENSE DUTY TEST` applied to my own
finding: the obligation is **conditional**, and I let the merge framing drift into treating it as standing.
**The addressable number is not who lacks a representative; it is who lacks one AT THE MOMENT A DEADLINE LANDS.**

## 7b. The number that replaces it — measured from the same four weeks

Counting foreign-held processos carrying a **hard-deadline event** (exigência de mérito, notificação de
oposição, indeferimento, caducidade, nulidade, recurso, sobrestamento — anything that must be answered or the
right is lost):

| | Four weeks | Annualised |
|---|---|---|
| Foreign-held processos with a hard-deadline event | 1.442 | ≈18.700 |
| — already have a Brazilian procurador | 1.075 **(75%)** | — |

> ### **≈4.100 foreign companies a year hit a Brazilian deadline they cannot answer without appointing someone resident here. That is the real market, it is a FLOW, and she needs five.**

## 7c. ⚠ AND THE FINDING THAT BREAKS THE "CATCH THEM AT THE CRISIS" PLAY
**Of the 367 unrepresented hard-deadline processos, ZERO were Madrid designations.** Every Madrid case carrying
an exigência or refusal already had a procurador printed. **By the time the gazette publishes the crisis, the
appointment has already happened** — INPI notifies, and they appoint within days. **So the gazette cannot be
used as a distress signal; it is a lagging record of a solved problem.** The addressable 313 are
**national-route** filings by foreign parties who filed directly. *(INPI expressly permits this: any interested
party may act before it **"em nome próprio, diretamente, sem a interveniência de qualquer procurador."**)*

## 7e. LEGS — one exceptional strength, three untested weaknesses

> ### ★★★ THE STRENGTH IS AN AMENDMENT TO MY OWN LAW. `THE REPLACEMENT-COST CEILING` says a machine prices at its rebuild cost, and that the only escapes are **accumulated history** (zero at launch), **a credential** (expert veto) or **an access right owned by a platform** — *"all three are closed to her."* **The enumeration was incomplete. There is a fourth: AN ACCESS RIGHT OWNED BY HER OWN RESIDENCE.** A foreign buyer cannot commission Brazilian residency at any price, it costs her nothing to hold, it needs no credential *(confirmed twice: "may be an IP agent, a specialized lawyer, or **any Brazilian citizen** (since June 2014)")*, and it does not decay. **In ~130 candidates this is the only one that passes that ceiling.**

**The three weaknesses, none of them measured, in the order they should be tested:**
1. **Will a foreign company appoint an individual it met by email?** The role receives legal notices. The
   default instinct is to ask a law firm, and law firms hold the relationship at exactly the moment of need.
   **This is the whole business, and it is untested.**
2. **★ THE APOSTILLE FRICTION, and nobody has priced it.** The power of attorney *"must be executed in the
   applicant's country and either notarized and apostilled… before the agent can act."* That is a one-to-two
   week client-side errand with a notary — **for a US$400/month service.** A compelled purchase with a
   two-week paperwork tail is not an impulse close, and it is the likeliest reason a warm reply goes cold.
3. **A regulator can delete the duty.** Precedent from next door: **Res. Conjunta BCB/CVM 13/2024** waived the
   representative requirement for small foreign investors from 1 Jan 2025. Quarterly diary on IN RFB 2.119 and
   LPI art. 217.

*(And one honest constraint note: 15–20 light clients is more relationships than she asked for, even at 1–2 h
each. The business is a portfolio of small standing obligations, not a machine.)*
