# THE RENEWAL SCISSORS — and the first structural defeat of the Stamp Law

**17 Aug 2026. S2 hunt, agent 6 of 6. Fifteen forced non-physical renewal cycles examined.**

> **The model exists, is statutory, and is abundant. Every single one of the fifteen sorts into one of
> three buckets, and there is no fourth.**
>
> **1 · The artefact is a FORM — because the regulator deliberately made it one. $20–30 on Fiverr.**
> **2 · The artefact is genuine CONTENT — because a named person carries the consequence. Gated.**
> **3 · The regulator publishes the template FREE. Law 1 fires.**

---

## 1. THE RENEWAL SCISSORS

> **A rule-maker who needs an annual filing from 100.000 entities MUST make the artefact cheap to produce,
> or compliance collapses. So high-volume forced cycles are structurally GUARANTEED to be forms. The only
> non-form forced cycles are low-volume and high-liability — and those are exactly the ones where a named
> person must sign.**
>
> **Volume and margin are inversely coupled by the REGULATOR'S OWN DESIGN INTENT. The forcing rule and the
> price ceiling are set by the same fact.**

**And the corollary that explains seventy-two candidates of failure:**

> **G9 DOES NOT TRANSPOSE TO DOCUMENTS.** OSHA can force an annual respirator fit test because a machine
> and a technician are required. **No statute can force an annual DOCUMENT to be expensive.**

**As a search instruction — THE FORM PARADOX:** stop looking for a *high-volume* forced document cycle with
margin in it. **It cannot exist by construction.** Search only cycles binding **a few thousand** entities,
then immediately check whether the reason it is expensive is a signature.

## 2. THE DIRECTOR-SIGNATURE PROPERTY — the genuinely new thing

Across fifteen cycles, **exactly one** had a structure where the stamp cannot be inserted:

> **THE DIRECTOR-SIGNATURE RULE — search for rules where the STATUTE ITSELF names the customer's own
> director or board as the signatory. Where the law compels the BUYER to sign personally, it has
> structurally FORBIDDEN a professional from signing instead — and that is the only class of recurring
> document where a faceless preparer is not competing against a stamp.**

**This is the first structural defeat of the Stamp Law in this file.** The Stamp Law has killed roughly
fifteen candidates by the same mechanism: the price belongs to whoever is legally exposed, and she cannot be.
**Here the statute has already assigned the exposure to the buyer, so there is no stamp to lose to.**

**Verified instances:**
- **UK Modern Slavery Act 2015 s.54(6)** — the board approves and **a director signs**
- **Canada's forced-labour report** — signed by *"a governing body member… I have the authority to bind"*

**Sharper than "no credential required,"** which is what this file has been filtering on and which SDS
passes — and SDS prices at $10.

**The unexamined family, named by the agent as the single most promising unexplored direction from this
hunt:** **s.172 statements · gender pay gap reports · SECR disclosures.** All three share the
director-signature property. **Only modern slavery was verified. The rest is open ground.**

## 4. The survivor, and why the agent refused to sell it

**Modern slavery / forced-labour annual statements** — the only row of fifteen passing all three tests.

**What is genuinely strong:**
- **Recurrence is statutory and unconditional.** UK MSA s.54: prepare a statement *"for each financial year"* **even if nothing changed.** Canada: filed *"on or before May 31 of each year"* — a hard calendar date
- **Genuine prose, not a form.** Six or seven narrative areas: structure, policies, due diligence, risk, remediation, training, effectiveness. **No portal, no fields**
- **The signature belongs to the customer's own director** — the rarest property in the survey
- **G2 in document form.** Year 1 builds the spec; years 2–10 are deltas
- **The list is free, public and enumerable.** The UK registry holds **23.238 unique statements across 34.046 registered organisations**, publication threshold £36m turnover. **Every prior statement is readable, so a defect is computable before contact**

**And the four reasons it is still not recommended:**

1. **No published price found anywhere.** Every seller is quote-only, meaning negotiation, meaning
   conversation. *"That is not proof of a price floor; it is proof I could not measure one."*
2. **Free templates exist** — Rocket Lawyer, VinciWorks, the Ethical Trading Initiative. **Law 1 fires at
   the small end; the £36m+ end uses law firms.**
3. **Enforcement is weak.** UK s.54 carries **no fine**; the sanction is a Secretary of State injunction
   which appears never to have been meaningfully used. **The real forcing functions are procurement pressure
   and reputation — a softer clock than a statute**, and the buyer can produce a bad statement cheaply and
   suffer nothing.
4. **The softest form of the Stamp Law still bites.** The document is published under the company's name, on
   its website, board-approved, indexed in a government registry, and read by activists and journalists.
   *"A FTSE-250 general counsel is not going to source that text from an anonymous seller with no name. The
   exposure is reputational rather than professional, but it is still exposure, and it still buys a name."*

> **The agent's verdict, quoted: "this is a better candidate than anything else in the space, and it is
> still probably not a business."**

**The free test that settles it:** pull 200 UK statements from the registry and count how many are
**(i) verbatim-identical to the prior year, (ii) missing three or more of the six s.54(5) areas, or
(iii) not updated since 2023.** High defect rate → a defect-evidence channel exists. Low → the market is
served, stop.
