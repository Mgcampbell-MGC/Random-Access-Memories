# THE VETO LAW — Assembly 3's handoff half, killed with a general mechanism

**17 Aug 2026. Agent 7 of 10. Nine verticals examined; the assembly dies and takes a family with it.**

---

## The law

> **THE VETO LAW — lost knowledge never funds a purchase, because the loss lands after the money is
> spent. It funds a purchase only when a THIRD PARTY HOLDS A VETO: someone who will not let the payer
> proceed without the document. Rank contexts by who holds the veto, not by how badly the loss hurts.**

**And the corollary, which is the part that kills:**

> **Wherever a veto exists, the veto-holder or a funded vendor publishes the artefact FREE — because
> the veto guarantees demand, and guaranteed demand is exactly what makes a template worth giving away
> as lead generation. The veto that creates the market creates the free competitor.**

This is **F8 (adverse buyer)** and **LAW 1 (if the gate matters, someone funded gives away the tool)**
turning out to be **two halves of one mechanism.** Without a veto there is no buyer. With a veto there
is a free competitor. That is the whole vice, and it explains all nine verticals at once.

**It also retroactively explains the youth-sports kill.** The handoff catastrophe there is real and
severe — *"a binder the last parent dropped off after the banquet"* — and **nobody holds a veto.** No
third party stops a league electing a new treasurer with a shared login. There was never a buyer.

### The survivor, and what it rests on

**Franchisors have a genuine statutory veto.** 16 CFR § 436.5(k)(6) requires the franchisor to
*"disclose the table of contents of the franchisor's operating manual… state the number of pages devoted
to each subject and the total number of pages,"* and the exception only applies if the franchisor
*"offers the prospective franchisee the opportunity to view the manual before buying the franchise."*
**Both branches require the manual to exist.** You cannot lawfully sell a franchise without having
written down how the business runs.

| Who | Price |
|---|---|
| Business-in-a-Box | **FREE** |
| GoAudits, Lark Templates | Free, as lead generation |
| Franchise Prep | **$249** Word template, instant delivery, no call |
| Market rate per a franchise law firm | **$9.000–20.000 professionally developed** |
| Franchise Manuals (franman.net) | Three tiers, **no published price**, "contact us to schedule a no obligation conference" |

**One genuinely encouraging structural fact:** the questionnaire-built middle tier already exists and is
written-only. One provider states outright that *"the questionnaire takes the place of the on-site
information-gathering visit."* **Constrained input → finished artefact, no voice required** — the same
engine shape as everything else on this board.

**What it rests on, unmeasured:** whether a franchisor will buy a $1.500–2.500 questionnaire-built
manual **from a page, with no call.** Nobody's published data answers it. And **F2 (the AI supply vise)
bites hard** — an AI writes a manual from a questionnaire, which is precisely what the Fiverr prices
below already prove.

## The addition worth keeping, though she can never sell it

**Contractor licence qualifiers — the Stamp Law delivered by the market instead of by inference.**

**Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 7068.2:** if the responsible managing officer or employee disassociates, the
licensee has **90 days to replace them**, and on failure *"the license shall be automatically suspended
or the classification removed at the end of the 90 days."*

A construction company with payroll and bonded jobs loses its right to operate because one person
walked. Companies pay RMO/RME holders continuously, in advance, to prevent exactly that.

> **It proves the buyer for key-person catastrophe exists and pays in advance — and that what they buy
> is a licensed human being, not a document.** That is the cleanest possible statement of why this
> entire problem family resists a written product. *(Construction is vetoed as a buyer segment anyway.)*
