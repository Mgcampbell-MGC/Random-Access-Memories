# ACOLHE — newborn kits for municipal social assistance, measured 11 Sep 2026

**The founder supplied ACOLHE SUPRIMENTOS ASSISTENCIAIS: a one-founder, zero-inventory supplier of standardised
*kit natalidade / enxoval* to municipal social-assistance secretariats (Benefícios Eventuais, LOAS art. 22),
components bought from manufacturers, packed by a consolidation partner, run by an AI tender desk.** Same
shape as FIELDTAG GOV (`THE_FIELDTAG_BRASIL.md`), applied to a niche in her world with a birth clock behind it.
Every number below was fetched today from PNCP (`pncp.gov.br/api`) and the statutes.

---

## 1. The verdict in one paragraph

**The market is real, large, recurring and almost entirely municipal; the plan's cited tenders are mostly real;
the shape passes the machine test if packing is outsourced; and it is the first government-supply candidate
that is unambiguously HER world.** What the data adds that the plan lacks: **the factories bid directly** (the
biggest awards in the sample went to *confecções*, not resellers), municipal results are thinly published (the
"incumbent rail" is weaker than federal), the estimate-to-award haircut is a median 21% with a p75 of 50%, and
**the binding constraint is the same as FIELDTAG's — municipal cash timing — only larger, because the lots are
larger.** Build it as a NICHE ON THE SAME DESK as the thermal-media business, not as a separate company.

## 2. The market, from PNCP

- **≥600 distinct newborn-kit editais published in 2026 through 11 Sep** (six keyword sweeps, deduplicated:
  616 across all statuses, 405 already closed; 546 and 379 of them dated 2026). **97% municipal.** Concentrated
  in BA, MA, PE, PA, AL, RN, CE, then MG, PR, PI, SP.
- **Sample of 44 recent editais:** 31 pregão eletrônico · 11 dispensa · 2 presencial; **23 are price-registration
  (SRP) with parcelled delivery**; estimated value **median R$102.339 (p25 R$57.305 · p75 R$308.027 · max
  R$4,33M)**. Closed sample of 49: estimated median R$112.388; **homologated median R$58.475** (n=16).
- **Kit prices:** estimated unit median R$133 (p25 R$68 · p75 R$334, n=49, partial kits included); full kits
  homologated at **R$161,80 (Itapetininga/SP) · R$187,17 (BA) · R$210 (Saloá/PE) · R$215 (Pedro Canário/ES)
  · R$1.035,95 for a 19-item kit ×400 = R$414.380 (a Fundo Municipal in RJ, won by a manufacturer)**.
- **Haircut estimate → award: median 21% (p25 2,7% · p75 50%)**; Carutapera R$320.100 → R$199.998 (−37,5%).
  `THE REFERENCE-PRICE ILLUSION` holds here as everywhere: price off awards, never off estimates.
- **Small-business preference is structural:** of 112 kit line items, **69 carry "Cota reservada para ME/EPP"
  or "Participação exclusiva para ME/EPP"** (LC 123 art. 48).
- **Who wins:** manufacturers (Dalida Confecções, Melodimm, FB Comércio de Enxovais, Vilavi), distributors
  (Master, BQS), a *representações* firm (VSB, two lots in Maribondo/AL), and **small MEs including
  person-named ones (Jamim de Oliveira Dourado Neta — R$24.332; Radiany F Malheiro ME — R$12.492)**.
- ⚠ **Only 15 of 109 closed kit items had results published on PNCP.** Municipal buyers post the edital and
  often not the award. The winner/price database the plan calls its moat is buildable, but from portals
  (Portal de Compras Públicas, Licitanet, BLL) and diários, not from one API.

## 4. The screens

| Screen | Result |
|---|---|
| `THE MACHINE TEST` | **Passes if — and only if — consolidation is a vendor's job.** The kit is a bundle; someone packs it. The plan outsources packing; the buyer verifies by inspection against its own spec; a contract is not a relationship. If she ever packs kits herself it becomes MOLDE PÚBLICO with softer objects. |
| G2 buyer pushed by rule | Yes: PNCP publication, dispensa auto-email for the small ones, ME/EPP cotas and exclusivity, and a birth clock the buyer does not control (`THE CONSUMPTION-CLOCK TEST` — a município cannot stretch one kit across two births). |
| `THE FREE FRONT DOOR` / factories | **Fires, partially.** The largest sample award (R$414k) went to a confecção. Manufacturers bid directly on big lots. Her space is the **R$50–150k lot in a small município a factory will not chase**, and the SRP with parcelled calls a factory dislikes. |
| `THE CATALOGUE SCISSORS` | The components are catalogue goods (bodies, mantas, toalhas) — no margin on the pieces. **The margin is the bundle + logistics + bid discipline**, priced by the haircut, not by the SKU. |
| `THE REFERENCE-PRICE ILLUSION` | Measured: 21% median haircut, 50% at p75. The plan's 15–22% contribution is plausible only if the bill of materials is priced from Brás/Bom Retiro wholesale, not from the edital's estimate. |
| C8 / comfort | **The strongest fit of any government-supply candidate:** babies, mothers, social assistance, Portuguese, paperwork. No face, no claim. |
| `SELECTION ≠ PERSISTENCE` | Year three is municipal portals, freight to the interior of Bahia, and a CRAS coordinator complaining that the *mijão* size runs small. Tolerable, not electric. |
| **C5 — the one that binds** | Lots are 5–10× thermal media's. A single SRP call of 100 kits at ~R$150 cost is R$15k of goods — **her whole cushion, once**. Municipalities are the slowest payers in the system; Lei 14.133 art. 141 orders them chronologically and prioritises ME/EPP only on a showing of risk. **AntecipaGov reaches state/municipal suppliers since IN 82/2025 but only where the município has adhered — UNVERIFIED for any specific buyer.** |

## 5. The arithmetic at her cash

| Configuration | Monthly billed | Contribution at 18% |
|---|---|---|
| One SRP, one 100-kit call per month, prepaid, municipal payment ~45–60 days | ≈R$20–25k | ≈R$3.500–4.500 |
| + factoring of attested NFs (private, 2–4%/month; AntecipaGov where adhered) | ≈R$40–50k | ≈R$7–9k − ~2% |
| + supplier terms from two confecções after clean deliveries | bounded by credit, not cash | full margin |
| The plan's "Proof" stage R$250–750k/yr | R$21–62k/month | R$3.700–11.000 |
| The bar (≈R$10–13k/month net) | needs ≈**R$70–90k/month billed** at 18% after Simples | i.e. the plan's "Comfortable" stage — year two, on supplier credit |

**Same cap, same ladder as FIELDTAG-BR: prepay small → factor → earn supplier terms → move to SRPs.** The kit
business needs more capital per contract and pays slower; it compensates with bigger contracts and a niche a
factory ignores.

## 6. What the plan gets right, and the four things it must add

Right: parcelled SRPs as the target shape; never counting the ata ceiling as revenue; no private label; the
ACOLHE BOX; shadow-bidding before bidding; "financing before winning, never after"; the diaper caveat (MDS
guidance routes disposable diapers to health policy — Orientações Técnicas sobre Benefícios Eventuais no SUAS).

Add: **(1)** price the BOM from wholesale RFQs, never from estimates — the median haircut is 21%; **(2)** treat
factories as the competitor on any lot above ~R$150k and stay below it; **(3)** the results database must be
built from four portals plus diários, because PNCP carries results for one closed item in seven; **(4)** the
subcontracting clause: Presidente Venceslau's 2026 edital forbade subcontratação — the parser must read it before
the packing partner is assumed, and counsel must say once whether "bought packed from a supplier" is procurement
or subcontracting under Lei 14.133 art. 122.

## 7. Where it sits on the board

**Not a second business — a second niche on the FIELDTAG-BR desk.** The Scout/Reader/BOM/Quote machine is the
asset; the niche is a parameter. Thermal media to federal buyers is the cash-cycle-friendly niche (federal
payers, R$2–4k lines, factories absent); newborn kits to municípios is the comfort-and-size niche (her world,
R$50–300k lots, slow payers, factories present on the big lots). **Run both on one SICAF registration, one ME,
one desk; let the cash cycle decide the mix.** Cheapest test, no phone: shadow-bid ten closed kit tenders from
the sample against three wholesale RFQs and count how many the modelled price would have won at ≥15%.
