## #1 · CONFORMIDADE — the compelled-document machine *(dental wedge)*
**Shape: S5 + compulsion. Brazil-domestic. Survives by DEFAULT — nothing was entered against it.**

Every dental establishment in Brazil must hold a **Série de Documentos de Boas Práticas** under **ANVISA
RDC nº 1.002/2025** (DOU 16/12/2025): **17 mandatory documents** (art. 113), **360 days to comply**
(art. 182 ⇒ **≈11 Dec 2026**), new establishments compliant from day one, non-compliance an *infração
sanitária* under Lei 6.437/1977. **Art. 122 §2º expressly permits outsourcing the PGRSS.** Revision at
least every two years (art. 111 IV). Questionnaire → deterministic generator → delivered as a file.

|---|---|
