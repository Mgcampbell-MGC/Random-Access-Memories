# THE WEDDING-INSURANCE PROBE — one policy read at source, one angle killed, 19 Aug 2026

**Context:** weddings/events is being deep-hunted as a female-affinity domain. The strongest structural analogy
to the live candidate is the claims layer — a recovery industry whose published policy demands evidence. Before
the hunt reports, I read one actual UK policy wording (Save the Date, v5 Jan 2026, 33 pp, fetched from the
insurer's own asset CDN and text-extracted).

## THE FINDING — the vendor-failure evidence angle is DEAD on the policy's own text, three ways

**§2, Financial Failure of Suppliers, verbatim:**

> *"We may, at our discretion, rely on **publicly available records (such as Companies House or The Gazette)**
> to validate the financial failure of your supplier."*

**Definition, verbatim:** *"Financial Failure — The bankruptcy, compulsory liquidation, insolvency, compulsory
winding-up and/or the appointment of a receiver or administrator…"*

**Exclusion 9, verbatim:** *"The financial failure of any supplier **not registered on Companies House**."*

1. **`THE GAZETTE LAW`, exactly.** Covered "failure" is defined as formal insolvency — a gazetted, permanently
   archived state event. Nothing perishable is in scope.
2. **The insurer NAMES ITS OWN FREE VALIDATOR inside the policy.** The adjudicator has designated a permanent
   state register as its source of truth. There is no evidence gap to sell into.
3. **The perishable case is the EXCLUDED case.** A vendor who simply ghosts — website dark, no formal insolvency,
   not on Companies House — is precisely where dated web-state evidence would matter, **and it is excluded from
   cover entirely. The perishable half is the uninsured half.**

Plus the claimant's required artefacts are their own (exclusion 6: *"no valid written contract… will not be
considered valid for claims purposes"* — contract + proof of deposits): `THE CLAIMANT-EVIDENCE LAW` and
`THE SUBSET LAW`, again.

## ★ THE REUSABLE SCREEN

> ### **READ THE VALIDATION CLAUSE, NOT ONLY THE COVERAGE CLAUSE. When a claims policy names a state register as how it validates ("we may rely on Companies House or The Gazette"), the evidence layer is closed by designation — the adjudicator has already chosen a permanent, free source of truth, and `THE GAZETTE LAW` applies.**
>
> The ocean-demurrage seed differs on exactly this: no carrier tariff designates any register for gate state,
> because none exists. **The absence of a designated validator is a necessary condition for any evidence
> product.** Add it to the contemporaneous-evidence screen.

**Scope note:** one insurer, one wording. Other insurers' definitions (e.g. WedCover's "ceased to trade…
established with reasonable evidence", £500 cap) differ at the margin — but the £500 cap independently kills the
economics, and the deep hunt will sweep the rest. **This kill is of the ANGLE (perishable vendor-state evidence
sold into wedding claims), not yet of the whole domain.**
