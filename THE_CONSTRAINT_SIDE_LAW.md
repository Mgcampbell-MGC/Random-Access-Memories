## 1. THE LAW

The transcript's qualifying question for choosing what to automate:

> ***"What breaks if the company doubled its customers tomorrow?"***

Pointed at a buyer instead of a process, it splits every buyer this file has ever considered into three
states, and only two of them will pay for a time-saving deliverable:

| State | The buyer's actual shortage | Do they buy hours back? |
|---|---|---|
| **Demand-constrained** | Customers | **NO.** They already have spare hours. That is the problem |
| **Supply-constrained** | Capacity — turning work away | **YES.** An hour saved converts directly to revenue |
| **Margin-constrained** | Neither — volume is fixed by something outside their control, and their cost is their own hours | **YES.** An hour saved converts directly to margin |

> ### THE CONSTRAINT-SIDE LAW — "this saves you four hours" can only be sold to a buyer who is short of hours. Most sole traders are short of CUSTOMERS, and to them a time-saving deliverable is worth approximately nothing: they would rather keep the four hours and the money. **Before pitching time savings, establish which of the three states the buyer is in.**

**And the corollary that does the real work here:**

> ### THE EXOGENOUS-VOLUME SPEC — the buyer to look for is one whose VOLUME IS SET BY SOMETHING THEY DO NOT CONTROL and whose COST IS THEIR OWN HOURS. Deaths. Renewal dates. Statutory deadlines. Settlement dates. Pay cycles. That buyer cannot sell more by working more, so the only lever they have is cost per unit — and cost per unit is her product.

## 3. THE CELEBRANT ARITHMETIC — the expensive number

**Two primary figures, both fetched:**

| | |
|---|---|
| Marriages registered in Australia, 2025 | **118.804** — ABS, *Marriages and Divorces, Australia*, released 28 Jul 2026: *"118,804 marriages were registered in 2025, 1.7% fewer than in 2024."* (2024: **120.844**) |
| Commonwealth-registered marriage celebrants | **10.832** — register parsed at 217 pages × 50, recorded in `THE_POSTAL_LIST_LAW.md` |

**The unconditional ceiling — no assumptions, no secondary sources, no celebrant-share figure required:**

> **118.804 ÷ 10.832 = 11,0 ceremonies per celebrant per year, IF CELEBRANTS PERFORMED EVERY MARRIAGE IN
> AUSTRALIA. They do not. Ministers of religion and registry offices take a share, so the true figure is
> below eleven.**

Applying the civil-celebrant share of ~83% *(Australian Institute of Family Studies, citing ABS — **the AIFS
page returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch and this figure is carried from a search summary, so it is
SECONDARY, not primary.** ABS itself stopped publishing the celebrant-type breakdown from 2022 and now sells
it through paid data consultancy)*: **~9,1 ceremonies per celebrant per year**, and lower again once
registry-office ceremonies are removed from the civil share.

**Nine ceremonies a year is not a business customer. It is a hobby with a registration number.** It also
matches, from an independent direction, agent 3's estimate of **A$100–400 of annual spend per celebrant** —
two different methods landing on the same small wallet.

**And it re-prices the product in a way that walks into a veto.** A celebrant with nine ceremonies and free
weekday afternoons is not buying time. The only thing they would buy is something that **wins the booking or
the review** — which converts the deliverable from a time-saver into a differentiator, and a differentiator
is bought on the seller's reputation. **That is the "must not need to be believed as an expert" veto,
arriving by the back door.**

> **I ranked this #1. On this test it is last. Recorded as a correction, not softened.**

## 4. Three parts kept

### H1 · NAME THE NUMBER BEFORE BUILDING

> *"Name the number you're aiming to move"* — time saved, mistakes cut, or money made.

**This is the same object as RISKSTORY's Broker Proof Sheet, one level up.** The proof sheet makes the
DELIVERABLE checkable; naming the number makes the PURCHASE checkable. Together they are the only mechanism
in this file that lets a faceless anonymous seller be believed — because nothing rests on her, it rests on
an arithmetic the buyer can run themselves. **Pairs with G17 (twenty instances, count how many clear a
numeric bar).**

### H2 · THE BUYER SUPPLIES THE "BEFORE"

The transcript's sales artefact is the before/after: *"this used to take four hours — watch it take ten
minutes."* **As stated it is worthless to her, because she would be asserting the "before" and she cannot be
believed.**

> **Sharpened: SHE NEVER SUPPLIES THE BEFORE. The buyer sends a file they have already done themselves,
> with their own hours and their own result attached. She returns the after. Nothing is claimed; two objects
> sit side by side and the buyer does the comparing.**

**This is the first sales artefact in the file that survives the expert veto**, because it makes no
assertion at all. It is also free, written, faceless, asynchronous, and needs no credential — and it doubles
as the qualifying step, since a buyer who cannot produce a "before" is a buyer who does not do the work
often enough to be worth having.

### H3 · THE DOUBLING QUESTION AS A COLD-EMAIL LINE

*"What breaks if your bookings doubled next month?"* is one sentence, it is written, it needs no call, and
**the answer sorts the buyer into §1's three states before a minute is spent on them.** Cheapest
qualification mechanism found so far.

## 5. What it says about her, which is the uncomfortable part

The transcript's actual economic claim — and it is right — is that the premium goes to **the person who
applies AI inside a domain, not the person who knows AI.** The domain is the scarce half.

**She has no domain.** Not funerals, not insurance, not genealogy, not weddings. Every candidate in this
file has quietly required her to acquire one, and the expert veto forbids it.

> **So the only workable division of labour is: THE BUYER SUPPLIES THE DOMAIN, SHE SUPPLIES THE THROUGHPUT.
> She is capacity, not expertise.** Which means the buyer must simultaneously have (a) the domain,
> (b) exogenous volume, and (c) no spare hours. **That is the one-line buyer specification, and this file
> has never written it down before.**

filed as positioning. It is not positioning — it is the only structure in which she can trade at all.)*
