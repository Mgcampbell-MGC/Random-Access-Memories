# THE BRAZILIAN RECOVERY CENSUS — 16 industries, 20 Aug 2026

**The US census found exactly one recovery industry (of 24) whose claims need a transient third-party public
fact nobody archives. The Brazilian sweep was run under the same directive — hunt poster ≠ invoice-issuer
splits inside refund-side industries — and returns EMPTY, for one extra reason: the Brazilian state closes the
gaps itself.** Sixteen industries, zero new instances of the structure. **Candidate #1 hardens again: forty
industries censused across two jurisdictions, one instance of its structure.**

## 1. ★★ THE FOUR BRAZIL-SPECIFIC KILLS

> ### ★★ THE STATE-AS-ARCHIVIST KILL — in Brazil, check `dadosabertos` BEFORE checking whether the fact is transient.
> Brazil's biggest consumer refund industry (flight compensation — **Resolvvi's own page: 35% success fee on
> flights, 30% INSS, 40% negativação**) runs on evidence the regulator publishes as permanent open data:
> **ANAC's VRA — per-flight scheduled vs actual departure/arrival and REALIZADO/CANCELADO status, 2000–2026,
> monthly static CSVs, plain GET, no login** (first rows printed). The transient airport fact is nationalised
> on a two-month lag. **The evidence market cannot exist because the state already runs it.**

> ### ★★ THE FREE-DISCOVERY DOUBLE BARREL — stronger than the DSAR kill.
> **CDC art. 6º VIII** (inversão do ônus da prova) **+ CPC arts. 396–400** — and art. 400 is the sharp edge:
> refusal to exhibit means **the facts are admitted as TRUE**, with coercive measures, inside the same lawsuit
> (widened to *categories* of documents by Lei 14.195/2021). **No consumer evidence market can exist above
> free discovery with an adverse-inference sanction.** Killed five industries independently of their other
> deaths.

> ### ★ THE AUTOMATIC-COMPENSATION KILL — the regulator designed the split test to fail.
> Where ANEEL compels compensation (DIC/FIC/DMIC continuity breaches), it is **computed by the offender, from
> the offender's own records, and auto-credited to the bill within two months. Poster, issuer and payer are one
> party.** Expect the same in any concession-regulated utility.

> ### ★ THE CONTRACTUAL-DEADLINE KILL — a deadline created by CONTRACT has no public clock.
> Glosa-response deadlines (Lei 13.003/2014 / RN 363/2014) start at a TISS portal submission and end at a TISS
> portal response — **both timestamps generated and held by the two parties, exchanged privately, posted
> nowhere.** Only STATUTORY deadlines with published inputs can feed an evidence product.
