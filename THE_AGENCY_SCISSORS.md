## 1. ⚠⚠ THE CORRECTION — THE ANTI-DISINTERMEDIATION STATUTE IS A DEFAULT, NOT A SHIELD

`CLAUDE.md` recorded art. 31 / UK reg. 7(2) / EU art. 7(2) as *"the statutory answer to the question that kills
every two-sided business."* **It is not. It is a gap-filler, and in all three jurisdictions it is freely
contracted away.**

### ★ VERIFIED BY ME, directly at source — the EU and UK halves

| Instrument | The actual non-derogation clause | Is the territory-commission article protected? |
|---|---|---|
| **Directive 86/653/EEC art. 5** | *"The parties may not derogate from the provisions of Articles 3 and 4."* (art. 19 adds arts. 17–18) | **NO. Article 7 appears in no list.** |
| **SI 1993/3053 reg. 5(1)** | *"The parties may not derogate from regulations 3 and 4 above."* | **NO. Regulation 7 is not included.** |

*(Both fetched and read today — [EUR-Lex CELEX 31986L0653](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:31986L0653),
[legislation.gov.uk reg. 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/3053/regulation/5/made). The file's claim
that these were "non-derogable" was simply wrong.)*

### The Brazilian half — STJ says the same

> **STJ, 3ª Turma** (General Cable Holdings Spain × Coeletra Representações Elétricas), j. 24/10/2023, ementa:
> *"4. A exclusividade de zona ou zonas, prevista no art. 31, caput, da Lei nº 4.886/1965, **pode ser
> expressamente restringida ou afastada pela vontade das partes**. […] 6. Na presença de cláusula contratual
> expressa afastando a exclusividade de zona ou zonas, as vendas efetuadas diretamente pelo representado ou por
> intermédio de terceiros configuram **exercício regular de direito**."*
> *(Via `BR/STJDadosAbertos`, doc `BR-STJ-000858598`. **The REsp number and inteiro teor were not obtained —
> the citation is incomplete. UNVERIFIED as a full citation**, though the ementa is unambiguous on its face.)*

> ### **RESTATED LAW: art. 31 pays her on untouched business only in a contract nobody's lawyer drafted. Any principal with counsel deletes it in clause 1. It protects her against sloppiness, not against intent.**

**What remains true** (verified verbatim from the statute yesterday, and unaffected): art. 31 IS the default
where the contract is silent · art. 33's silence-is-acceptance windows · art. 27(j)'s 1/12-of-lifetime
indemnity. **What is false is that any of it is a shield she can rely on against a competent counterparty.**

**★ And the trap inside the good news:** the parts of Lei 4.886 that genuinely favour her — art. 27(j)
indemnity, art. 39 home forum, art. 43 prohibition of *del credere*, art. 44 ranking her credits as labour
credits in the principal's bankruptcy — **are precisely why a sophisticated principal refuses a Lei 4.886
contract at all.** And **CPC/2015 art. 25** lets an international contract elect a foreign forum outright:
*"Não compete à autoridade judiciária brasileira… quando houver cláusula de eleição de foro exclusivo
estrangeiro em contrato internacional."* *(Whether Lei 4.886 is overriding mandatory law that survives that
election is **UNVERIFIED** and needs Brazilian counsel, which C5 does not buy.)*

## 2. ★★ THE AGENCY SCISSORS — why the shape closes

> ### **Wherever a Brazilian B2B order is generated IN WRITING, the buyer can already broadcast that order to every qualified supplier for free — so the introduction the agent sells is worth nothing. Wherever the introduction IS worth something, the order is generated by a conversation, and C1 forbids it. The written-order screen and the why-appoint-her screen cannot both be passed by the same candidate.**

Written, catalogue-and-RFQ procurement is **a technology for destroying relational access** — it exists so a
buyer can reach every qualified supplier at zero cost without knowing any of them. Mercado Eletrônico
transacted **>R$100bn of purchase orders in 2020 across ~8.000 buyers and ~1 million suppliers**
*(company-sourced trade press — **SECONDARY**, and six years stale; the order of magnitude is what the argument
needs)*. Petrobras runs the same shape in-house via Petronect, replacing *"papel, envelopes e remessas
físicas"* end to end.

**What else can an agent supply instead of access? The complete matrix, and her position on each:**

| | Available to her? |
|---|---|
| A regulatory/registration path (ANVISA holder, INMETRO/ANATEL rep) | **No** — needs a Brazilian entity accepting product liability; and it is the expert veto |
| Stock, credit, importer of record | **No** — physical-goods veto; title triggers the RIR art. 162 §1 *equiparação* |
| Language and market interpretation | **No** — `THE_CAPABILITY_ANSWER` records translation as a **loss** |
| Statutory territory rights | **No** — §1 above |
| Manufactured demand | **No** — that is advertising, and it needs an audience (C2) |
| **Procedural capability the principal lacks (a bid desk)** | **Yes — the only surviving cell** |

**And the survivor is not an agency.** A public-tender bid desk sells *procedural capability*, and she personally
produces every unit: one edital, one dossier, one deadline. **That is `THE_REBUILD`'s arithmetic-dead shape
wearing an S6 costume.** It also fails C4 (submitting on Compras.gov.br needs a *procuração* and usually a
*certificado digital* inside the principal's account) and is already occupied by *assessoria em licitações*
firms that also make the phone calls.

## 3. ★ THE MARGIN LAW — and the only primary commission datum in this file

> ### **The commission rate is a function of the PRINCIPAL'S GROSS MARGIN, not of the agent's effort.**

**PRIMARY, OBSERVED — a real manufacturer's commission circular, quoted inside a judgment.**
[TJPR, 7ª Câmara Cível, Ap. Cível 581.757-2](https://portal.tjpr.jus.br/jurisprudencia/j/1865020/) (Yaskawa
Elétrico do Brasil × Poitec Representações, j. 29/09/2009):

> *"segue NOVAMENTE o critério válido para pagamento de comissões válido desde janeiro de 2004. 1. **Peças =
> 5%**, Inversores/servos/motion/ fator acima de 0.72 = **6%**, fator entre 0.66 até 0.71 = **3%**… 4.
> **Treinamento = 5%. Serviços de Campo e Assistência Técnica = não tem comissão.**"*

with the principal's director on the record: commissions *"entre 3% a 6%"*, exceptionally **10%**.

**Physical goods with a factory behind them: 3–6%. Zero-marginal-cost inventory: 25–40%.** *(The latter —
ad-rep, licensing-agent, SaaS-referral rates — is **SECONDARY**, sourced to agency marketing and partner-program
roundups, and is exactly the class of source the evidence standard forbids.)*

**★ And the same judgment is primary evidence that the job is SPOKEN**: the rep worked a trade-fair stand at
Interplast with a borrowed robot, held in-person meetings, and telephoned the director to authorise each
discount. **The one document that proves the rate also proves the constraint violation.**

**There is no published Brazilian commission schedule.** CORE-SP's public *Simulador de Valores* computes
registration fees only; CONFERE publishes none. Every "3–15%" figure in circulation traces to accountancy blogs.

## 4. ★ THE TAX CORRECTION — and the AGENT EXEMPTION is inverted

**`CLAUDE.md`'s US$11.100/month assumes 8,1%, the Lucro Presumido rate for a service EXPORTER. A commission for
finding BRAZILIAN buyers is not an export.** The RFB's own test appears in the mirror-image ruling,
**SC Cosit nº 76/2018**: commissions to agents abroad escape PIS/COFINS-Importação *"por não haver na hipótese
serviço prestado no Brasil **ou cujo resultado aqui se verifique**."* Reverse the geography and the *resultado*
occurs in Brazil. *(Inference by symmetry from a binding ruling, **not** a ruling on her facts — **UNVERIFIED as
applied**.)*

| Vehicle | Load | Gross to net US$10.000 |
|---|---|---|
| PJ, Lucro Presumido, domestic | **13,3–16,3%** | **≈US$11.800–12.000/mo** |
| PJ, Simples Anexo V (fator R <28%) | ≈17% | ≈US$12.000/mo |
| **Pessoa física** (RIR art. 162 §2º III) | **≈28–32%** | **≈US$14.300/mo** |

> **★ The *pessoa física* escape hatch `THE_AGENCY_FINDING` celebrates is the WORST of the three vehicles by
> ~US$2.400/month.** It avoids PIS/COFINS on gross goods value — which was never going to apply to an agent
> anyway — at the price of a 27,5% marginal IRPF.

**Working gate US$12.000/month.** At the measured industrial rate of 3–6% that is **R$12,5–25 million a year of
invoiced sales through her territory.** One person, faceless, by email, in 12–18 months, does not do that. **The
arithmetic kills every physical-goods instance before a single constraint is applied.**

## 5. ★ THE FIELD MEASUREMENT I RAN, AND WHY IT DOES NOT SETTLE ANYTHING

The report proposed a vocabulary count over rep vacancies. I ran it and **got a false result first, then
diagnosed it — recording both, because the diagnosis is the useful part.**

**Attempt 1: 30/30 postings "clean" of any spoken/physical word.** Every extracted text was exactly 219 or 417
characters. **That is a template string, not a vacancy.** The `/empresas/` pages on portaldorepresentante.com.br
are **company marketing profiles, not job postings** — no requirements, no terms — and contact is gated:
*"Você precisa acessar sua conta para entrar em contato."* **The measurement was an artifact of my extractor.**
*(Fourth instance in this file of a confident number that was a schema misreading. The antidote held: the result
was too clean to be true.)*

**Attempt 2, on the real vacancy summaries** (title + truncated description, which live on the *listing* page):

| | |
|---|---|
| Unique vacancy postings | **55** |
| Total positions advertised | **744** |
| Contain a spoken/physical word (*visita, pasta, mostruário, carteira de clientes, feira, veículo, rota, presencial…*) | **4 / 55** |
| Free of every one | **51 / 55** |
| Explicitly remote/online | **1** — *"Representante Comercial PJ – Tecnologia e IA \| Remoto… remuneração exclusivamente comissão"* |

> **⚠ THIS DOES NOT PASS THE TEST, AND MUST NOT BE READ AS PASSING IT.** Listing descriptions are truncated to
> ~150 characters, so **absence of a spoken word is an artifact of truncation, not evidence of a written-only
> role.** The full text and the contact both sit behind an account. **The test as designed cannot be run from
> outside the login.**

**What the sample does honestly show:** the appointment door in Brazil is genuinely open and free — 744
advertised positions, self-serve — which **inverts the US finding** in `THE_AD_REP_INDUSTRY_EXISTS.md`, where
no publisher had ever been shown to appoint a rep in writing. And the sector mix is mostly the relational,
van-and-sample-case trade (supermarkets, cosmetics, textiles, doces, pesca) — **but not exclusively: ERP,
industrial automation, tax auditing and a remote commission-only AI/tech role all appear.** The claim that the
door "opens only onto the wrong room" is **too strong on this evidence.**

## 7. VERDICT

**S6 commission agency is closed to her.** The property she needs on the buyer side (written orders) is the same
property that makes an intermediary unnecessary. The property she needs on the principal side (statutory
territory commission) is a default term any competent counterparty deletes. And the rate she can charge is set
by the principal's gross margin, which for physical goods puts the required territory volume at **R$12,5–25
million a year.**

**If it is ever reopened, it reopens on the one cell that survived the matrix — zero-marginal-cost inventory,
where rates are 25–40% and the volume needed falls to ~R$2,5M/yr — and it reopens only after someone finds a
publisher with a numeric rate card and an email-only insertion order. Nobody has.**
