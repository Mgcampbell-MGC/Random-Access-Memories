## 4. The laws bought today

### THE PLACEMENT SCISSORS
> Where supply is **active**, the matching layer is already free on both sides — SINE (*"gratuito tanto para o trabalhador quanto para o empregador"*), the professional councils' own job boards, and the scheduling platforms. Where supply is **passive**, filling the slot *is* twenty hours of outbound calling — agency work with the invoice reshaped. **There is no third region.**

### LAW A, SHARPENED — the vendor's word is never the buyer's word
Five nulls in this project turned out to be vocabulary failures, and the pattern is exact:

| The buyer says | The vendor says |
|---|---|
| supplier follow-up | **diligenciamento** |
| warranty claims portal | **gestão de pós-venda** |
| document collection | **gestão de terceiros** |
| artwork proofing | **prova digital / aprovação de arte** |

**Every wrong null in this file was a search in the buyer's vocabulary.**

### FACELESSNESS IS A FEATURE IN DELIVERY AND A DISQUALIFIER IN DILIGENCE
> Her asymmetry is **many low-trust Portuguese WhatsApp transactions with small firms.** White-label converts that into **few high-trust senior partnership sales** — the highest-trust B2B transaction that exists, because the partner puts its own brand on her output.

Related: firms at 50+ employees run *cadastro e homologação de fornecedor*. **A three-month-old one-person CNPJ fails that gate at exactly the firms solvent enough to pay.**

### THE CLEARING PARTY CAN PRICE YOU INSTEAD OF KILLING YOU
> **Mercado Livre IS the clearing party — it earns margin on goods flowing past, which is why its seller tooling is free and its take rate is 12–19% plus shipping.**

First time in 60+ candidates that Law 0 was survivable rather than fatal. **Law 0 kills software and prices goods.**

### THE THREE-CONDITION RULE FOR ASSEMBLY BUSINESSES
> A business built on assembling scattered parties needs three things at once: a statute that **publishes** the tail, a statute that makes the tail **pivotal**, and a **solvent** party who must satisfy it. **Art. 45 of Lei 11.101/2005 supplies the second, and no other Brazilian provision does.**
>
> Without pivotality, a published list of people owed money is just a list of poor people, and every buyer for it is the State, a saturated fund, or a lawyer.

### MEAN IS THE WRONG STATISTIC; THE BAND COUNT IS THE RIGHT ONE
> **Banks own the money, not the seats.** Where financial institutions hold 74–80% of Class III *value*, they occupy 41 and 15 creditor slots against 619 and 204 trade PJs. **Value concentration and headcount concentration are different facts, and only headcount drives unit economics.**

## 6. THE SURVIVOR — CONFERÊNCIA DE CRÉDITO

**What it is.** When a company files recuperação judicial it must publish a *relação nominal de credores* (art. 51, II) built from **its own** AP ledger. It is routinely wrong — invoices omitted, values stale, wrong class. **The creditor's credit is frozen at the debtor's number** unless it proves otherwise within 15 days of the edital (art. 7º §1º).

She takes the creditor's own AR data, reconciles it line-by-line against the published list, and delivers an evidence-linked file plus source documents. **The creditor's own lawyer files.**

**Three properties nothing else in 62 candidates had:**

1. **The data problem solves itself by statute.** Only one side is the client — the counterparty's number is published by court order. She never has to persuade two parties to trust her.
2. **The lead list is published, free, dated, and carries the amount at stake on every row.** No scraping, no credentials, no gov.br.
3. **Volume is measured and at a record.** 977 RJ processes / 2.466 CNPJs in 2025 (Serasa Experian), +13%, highest of the series.

**The population, measured — nine editais machine-parsed, parser validated to the centavo:**

| Case | Passivo | **Non-financial PJ ≥R$250k** | ≥R$1M | FIN % of Class III value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Americanas | R$48,5 bi | **619** | 312 | 74% |
| Oi | R$3,06 bi | **404** | 190 | ~0% |
| Galvão Engenharia | R$1,94 bi | **204** | 59 | 80% |
| SuperVia | R$1,35 bi | **25** | 15 | 6,7% |
| OBER (têxtil) | R$58,8 M | **20** | 7 | 4,6% |
| Reginaves (agro) | R$75,8 M | **13** | 3 | 61,7% |
| Fasttel (engenharia) | R$43,9 M | **26** | 5 | 1,8% |
| SOEMOC (educação) | R$147,7 M | **9** | 6 | 0,6% |
| Castor (hortifruti) | R$20,4 M | **4** | 1 | 40,2% |

**Large cases: median 204, range 9–619.** At ~10% of 977 filings being >R$100M, that is **~20.000 qualifying creditors a year.** She needs ten.

**Targeting, which inverts the obvious.** Agro is **30% of all filings and the worst hunting ground** — bank-heavy by value (Reginaves 61,7% financeiro, 13 qualifying). **Indústria and serviços are trade-heavy** (OBER 4,6%, SOEMOC 0,6%, Fasttel 1,8%). And **size does not predict count — breadth of supplier base does**: SuperVia at R$1,35 bi yielded only 25, because concessions have narrow supplier bases.

**The Law 7 boundary, stated as she must state it:**
> *"Não classificamos o crédito, não fixamos juros, correção ou prescrição, não protocolamos, não representamos o credor perante o administrador judicial ou o juízo, não emitimos parecer nem laudo. Entregamos a conciliação linha-a-linha com o documento-fonte anexado. O advogado e o controller do cliente aprovam todo parâmetro jurídico e assinam a peça."*

The *divergência* in the 15-day window is **administrative, before the administrador judicial** — no lawyer required. Only *habilitação retardatária* (art. 10) and *impugnação* (art. 8) are judicial.

**Arithmetic:** R$12.000–25.000 per engagement. At Anexo V, R$13.500 net needs R$15.500 gross → **10–13 engagements/year**, against ~20.000 qualifying creditors. Roughly 0,05%.

### The three risks that remain

1. **THE ÊXITO LAWYER — the last open question and the one that kills candidates in this file.** If Brazilian firms do habilitação/divergência on contingency, she charges R$15.000 against a free alternative. **Under measurement now.** The counter-hypothesis worth holding: the OAB's Código de Ética restricts *quota litis*, and the seccional fee tables publish minimums — if fees are predominantly fixed, the threat evaporates and the lawyer becomes a **channel** rather than a competitor.
2. **The haircut.** Fixing a R$200.000 understatement in a class recovering 20 cents returns R$40.000. Americanas' Class III cleared at 73–95% discounts. The candidate may only work on large credits, or on class reclassification — **which is a legal judgement and walks back into Law 7.**
3. **The 15-day window** fights a cold WhatsApp sale plus a 7–10 day artifact. *Retardatária* has no deadline, but **art. 10 §3 strips retardatária creditors of their vote** — half the value.
