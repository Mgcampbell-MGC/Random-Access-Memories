# TEST 01 — the first buyer contact in this project

**Written 13 August 2026. Cost: R$0. Duration: 8–10 working days.**
**59 candidates. 59 desk kills. Zero conversations with a buyer, ever. This ends that.**

## 1. What is being tested

**The mechanism, three times converged.** Two agents searching different halves of the Brazilian
economy — wholesale/freight and manufacturing — independently arrived at the same job, which is also
the job the record's highest-scoring candidate did in construction:

> **The buyer holds a contracted service provider's monthly invoice until that month's labour
> document pack arrives. Today a person chases it by hand. The product chases unattended by
> WhatsApp, checks, and releases the gate.**

The pack: folha de pagamento, GFIP/FGTS **com comprovante de pagamento**, cartões de ponto, ASO,
ficha de entrega de EPI, CND. Typically 6–12 documents per provider per month, every month, for the
life of the contract.

**The legal engine, verbatim — Súmula 331, IV, TST:**
> *"o inadimplemento das obrigações trabalhistas, por parte do empregador, implica a
> responsabilidade subsidiária do tomador dos serviços"* — expressly covering *"vigilância e de
> conservação e limpeza"*.

Plus **Lei 6.019/74 art. 5º-A §5º**, which imposes on the contratante the duty to *fiscalizar*.
The folder is the buyer's own defence, at R$20–80k per claim. **It is defensive evidence, not
incriminating** — which is what separates this from ESCALA DA FESTA, killed by the
liability-evidence law.

  specific target segment exceeds ~20%.* **None of the eighteen is free. None publishes a price.**
- **That a clearing party exists.** Searched by class, twice, in two segments: **nobody earns margin

**The test buys exactly two facts, and nothing else.**

## 4. The channel — WhatsApp and a page, not a phone round

**Correction adopted 13 Aug 2026, and it invalidates the channel design of every prior test in this
file.** The record measured *"97,2% têm telefone"* from the Receita base and treated it as
reachability. **Having a landline and answering a cold call are different facts.** Brazilian B2B
runs on WhatsApp. A test whose mechanism is *"get the decision-maker on the phone"* fails on the
channel before it ever tests the product.

**The motion:**

1. **A one-page site plus a one-page PDF, launched the same day.** The page does the explaining, so
   nothing depends on her performing a pitch live — and nothing depends on a face, which keeps the
   permanent faceless constraint intact. Company-branded, no personal name, no photo.
2. **Cold WhatsApp, sent by hand, personalised, at low daily volume**, to the commercial WhatsApp
   number the company publishes itself. Message is two sentences and a link. Never a broadcast,
   never a list import, never an automation tool.
3. **Click-to-WhatsApp ads** pointed at the segment, where **the prospect messages first**. This
   satisfies Meta's opt-in requirement perfectly and by construction, and CPC in this market is
   ~US$0,35.
4. **The whole conversation happens in text.** She answers in Portuguese, asynchronously, from a
   script. This fits *"automated on a system, no real phone convo"* far better than dialling ever
   could — the two constraints now agree instead of fighting.
5. **Money by Pix, in the chat.**

**The opening is the prefilled-claim shape already in PLAN §2:** find something public about that
specific company, build the page about *them*, and open with *"posso te mandar a página já
preenchida?"*

### The Meta boundary — stated plainly, because this is what killed BALCÃO

Business-initiated WhatsApp messages to people who have not opted in violate Meta's Business
Messaging Policy. **BALCÃO died on exactly this**, failing opt-in, display-name matching and
impersonation rules simultaneously.

**What keeps this test inside the line:**
- Sent from the **WhatsApp Business app by a human**, not the Cloud API, and never through a
  bulk-sending tool. Personalised per company.
- **Low volume** — roughly 15–20 messages a day, not hundreds. A new number blasting a list gets
  banned within days, and that is not a policy risk, it is a certainty.
- **Warm the number first** for a week or two of ordinary use before any outreach.
- To numbers the company **publishes as its own commercial contact**.
- **Click-to-WhatsApp is the clean half of the funnel** and should carry as much of the volume as
  the budget allows, because inbound opt-in is unambiguous.

**Once a customer exists, the product's own WhatsApp use is opt-in by construction:** the buyer
sends its service provider a link, **the provider writes first**, and monthly reminders then go as
**UTILITY** templates — never MARKETING. Measured cost: R$0,035 per Utility message, about 2,3% of
the subscription. No chip warming, no unofficial accounts, no rotation, ever.

### Volume arithmetic

Cold WhatsApp reply rates in Brazilian B2B beat e-mail by a wide margin but do not reach a
dial-and-connect rate. **Budget roughly 200–300 outbound contacts to obtain 60 real conversations**,
spread over 10–14 days at 15–20/day across the two segments. E-mail is not a fallback: the Receita
e-mail field was measured returning **the accountant's address**, so it is dead for this purpose.

## 5. The two questions

Asked in the chat, of whoever handles fornecedores, compras or the financeiro. Both closed.

> **Q1 — ADOPTION.** *"Vocês já usam algum sistema ou portal para receber a documentação mensal dos
> prestadores de serviço — limpeza, manutenção, portaria, esse tipo de contrato?"*

Settles the only thing standing between us and corrected Law A1. **PASS: fewer than 20% report a
working portal in use.**

> **Q2 — THE MANUAL JOB.** *"E o prestador manda pelo portal, ou continua mandando por WhatsApp e
> e-mail para alguém aqui conferir?"*

This is the load-bearing hypothesis the record has flagged against its own interest three times:
*the small provider ignores the buyer's web portal, so the job stays manual even where a portal was
bought.* **PASS: 20 or more of 60 say the provider still sends by WhatsApp or e-mail.**

Note the evidence currently runs *against* this: LNP Consultorias sells a service to providers that
fills the incumbent portals in for them, which suggests providers pay a human rather than refuse.
**If Q2 fails, the wedge collapses to price alone and both candidates die.** That is the correct
outcome and it costs R$0.

## 6. The money bar

**Interest, "send me more information", positive feedback and "we would use this" do not count.**

> **PASS: 4 or more of 60 conversations commit money in the chat** — a paid 60-day pilot at the
> segment price, **Pix sent during the conversation**, not promised for later.
>
> **Fewer than 4 → both candidates stop.**

Four of sixty is 6,7%, and at six customers total, **closing four in the test round is most of the
business.** That is the point: at this price the test is not a proxy for the business, it very
nearly is the business.

**The disqualifying line, reused verbatim from the best-specified test in the record because it
refuses the merely curious:**
> *"Se vocês não têm prestador de serviço com contrato mensal — limpeza, manutenção, portaria — não
> faz sentido testar agora."*

## 7. The list

Filter: CNAE in {46.2–46.9} or {divisions 10–33} · **porte EPP or DEMAIS** · situação ATIVA ·
matriz · UF = SP. Target **200–300 companies**, split **50/50** between wholesale and manufacturing
so the segment comparison is clean.

**Then the contact step that the Receita base cannot do:** the base gives the CNPJ, the razão social
and a landline. It does **not** give the company's WhatsApp. That has to come from the company's own
public presence — site, Google Business profile, Instagram bio — which is a per-company lookup and
is exactly the kind of bounded, repetitive work the relaxed labour rule now permits.

**Outstanding work before the first message:**
1. The processed Receita Federal CNPJ base (~4,9 GB, reference 2026-07) is not in this container —
   it was built in an earlier session and the container is ephemeral. Re-pull and re-filter, or
   build a smaller list from a lighter source. **One afternoon.**
2. Enrich to WhatsApp numbers for the 200–300.
3. Build the page and the PDF. **Same day.**
4. Warm the WhatsApp Business number for 7–14 days before the first cold message.

## 8. How to read the outcome

| Result | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 pass · Q2 pass · ≥4 paid | The segment is virgin and the job is manual | **Build. Smallest sellable version.** |
| Q1 pass · Q2 **fail** | Portals are rare *and* they work when present | **Kill both.** The wedge was channel; there is no channel gap |
| Q1 **fail** in one segment only | The incumbents reached that segment, not the other | **Run the survivor**, drop the other |
| Q1 fail · both segments | Corrected Law A1 fires on measured adoption | **Kill both.** The mechanism is closed in the real economy |
| All pass · **<4 paid** | Real pain, no budget at this price | Re-price once, re-run the round. **Not a rebuild** |
| **Fewer than 60 conversations from 300 contacts** | The channel failed, not the product | Diagnose before concluding anything about the candidates. A dead channel invalidates the whole reading |

**That last row is new and it matters.** Every prior test in this record assumed reachability from a
phone-number fill rate. **Reachability is now a measured output of the test, not an assumption going
into it.**
