# THE WHATSAPP DISTRIBUTION MODEL — she carries demand, someone else carries stock

**Assessed 14 Aug 2026.**

> **VERDICT: the generic version fails on arithmetic and on facelessness. One specific version
> survives, and it is not "a catalogue" — it is being the only route a supplier has into a segment
> it cannot reach.**

---

## 1. The good news first: the model is legally clean and cheap to run

**No inventory required, by law.** Lei 4.886/65 defines *representação comercial* as
*"a mediação para a realização de negócios mercantis, agenciando propostas ou pedidos, para
transmiti-los aos representados."* **Intermediation, not resale.** She never owns a box.

**CORE registration is contested, not a hard gate.** Lei 4.886/65 as amended by Lei 8.420/92 requires
registration with the state CORE — **but courts have held that arts. 2 and 5 were not received by
the 1988 Constitution**, on the reasoning that the activity requires no specific technical
qualification and compulsory enrolment cannot be imposed. **This is not a Law 7 vise. It is
paperwork, not a credential.**

**The tooling exists and costs almost nothing.** [Gopage](https://gopage.bio/catalogo-digital-b2b-guia-pratico-atacadistas-distribuidores/),
[Zydon](https://www.zydon.com.br/lp/catalogo-digital-b2b), [MeuEcomm](https://meuecomm.com/) and
others sell exactly this: a per-representative digital catalogue link, shared by WhatsApp, where the
client orders themselves, with commission tracking and centralised order capture — **from
R$39,90/month.** She builds nothing.

**And there is a real protection in the law:** art. 27 provides an indemnity of at least 1/12 of
total commissions earned if the represented company terminates without cause. **The supplier cannot
cheaply cut her out once she has built the accounts.**

## 2. The bad news: the tooling being cheap is a warning, not a comfort

**Every distributor in Brazil already has this.** A R$39,90 catalogue link with a WhatsApp button is
not an edge — it is the standard kit issued to every rep in the country. **She would be one of
hundreds of thousands of representantes comerciais using the identical tool.**

**And commission rates are thin.** Published ranges: **3–10% typical, 5–20% depending on the
product.** No official table exists; it is set by market.

### The arithmetic, which is where the generic version dies

| Commission | Sales volume she must personally originate for **R$15.000/month** | For **R$30.000** |
|---|---|---|
| 5% | R$300.000/month | R$600.000 |
| 10% | R$150.000/month | R$300.000 |
| **15%** | **R$100.000/month** | **R$200.000** |
| 20% | R$75.000/month | R$150.000 |

At an average B2B order of **R$1.400**, R$100.000/month is **71 orders a month** — every one
opened, negotiated, entered, chased for payment and followed up **in writing, alone, by one
person.**

> **This is linear labour with no leverage: every additional real requires another conversation.
> It is the same structure that killed the R$349 pregnancy book, in a lower-margin form.**

### And the channel has a hard ceiling she did not set

**Meta's policy caps cold WhatsApp outreach.** From the record's own earlier work: hand-sent from
the Business app, personalised, **15–20 messages a day from a warmed number** — anything above that
gets the number banned, and that is a certainty rather than a risk. **That is ~400 cold contacts a
month, maximum, forever.**

At a 2% cold-to-paying conversion — optimistic for a faceless stranger — that is **8 new accounts a
month.** Six months to build a base of ~50.

## 3. The version that survives — and it is not a catalogue

The generic rep sells *persuasion*. **Persuasion is the one thing a faceless non-speaker cannot
scale.** So the surviving version must sell something else:

> ### THE BRIDGE, NOT THE CATALOGUE
> **She represents a supplier into a segment that supplier structurally cannot reach — where the
> barrier is language, documentation or size, not effort.** Her value is not that she is persuasive.
> It is that **without her there is no route at all.**

**Concretely, and it lands exactly where the BioCore document was pointing:**

- A **foreign brand** — Korean, Turkish, Spanish — whose product is already **imported and held by
  an existing Brazilian distributor.** She reps for the *distributor*, so: **no import, no ANVISA
  filing, no customs, no capital, no inventory, no RT farmacêutico.** All of it is someone else's
  balance sheet.
- Into a segment that distributor does not cover: **small clinics and independent estheticians
  outside the capitals, and newly-opened businesses with no supplier relationships yet.**
- **She brings what the distributor lacks:** English and Portuguese, written technical material
  translated properly, and the patience to work a long tail of small accounts that no travelling
  rep will drive to.

**Why the supplier cannot replace her:** the distributor's own reps chase the big accounts, because
that is where their commission is. The tail is uncovered by design. **She is not competing with
them — she is selling the segment they have declined.**

### The niche test — four conditions, all required

| Condition | Why |
|---|---|
| **Buyers are enumerable from public data** | Brazil publishes the CNPJ base. Filter by CNAE, porte, município, **and opening date** |
| **The purchase repeats monthly** | A consumable, not equipment. Reorders are where the leverage finally appears — the second order costs almost nothing to win |
| **Order value ≥ R$1.000 and commission ≥ 15%** | Below R$210 per order the arithmetic never closes |
| **The buyer is reachable and comfortable in writing** | Brazilian small-business owners live in WhatsApp — this is the one constraint the country actually favours |

**The dated trigger, and it is the strongest part of this whole model:** *a business whose CNPJ was
opened in the last 60 days.* **It needs everything, has no incumbent supplier, and is publicly
listed the day it registers.** That is an enumerable, dated, recurring buying event — the same
structure that made "channel expansion" work for the catalogue business.

### The angle — what she says that a rep cannot

Not *"here is my catalogue."* Every rep says that, and Law 1 applies: catalogues are free and
worthless.

> **"You just opened. Here is the starter list for your first 90 days, the prices, and what your
> margin is on each item at your local ticket price. Reply with the numbers and I place it."**

**The deliverable is the arithmetic, not the catalogue** — their cost, their sell price, their
margin, per item, for their kind of business. That is written, organising, data work. It is exactly
what she is good at, and it is the only version of a catalogue message that is not spam.

## 4. Honest arithmetic on the surviving version

| | |
|---|---|
| Average order | **R$1.400** |
| Commission at 15% | **R$210** |
| Orders for R$15.000/month | **71** |
| Active accounts reordering monthly | **~50** |
| Time to build 50 accounts at 8/month | **~6 months** |
| Revenue at month 8 (~50 accounts) | **≈R$10.500** |

**Against her own bar — month 8 at R$3.000 is failing — that passes.** But it rests on **one
unproven number: a 2% cold-WhatsApp-to-paying conversion, faceless, with no reputation.** Everything
else is arithmetic; that one is a hypothesis.

## 5. What kills it

1. **The 2% conversion is fiction.** At 0,5% she reaches 12 accounts in six months and the model
   never starts. **This is the single load-bearing assumption and it is cheap to test.**
2. **The number gets banned.** One over-eager week at 60 messages a day and the channel is gone,
   permanently, with no appeal.
3. **The distributor takes the accounts back** once they are proven — art. 27's indemnity is the
   defence, and it requires a written contract signed before the first order.
4. **The tail is poor.** Small clinics outside the capitals order R$400, not R$1.400, and the
   arithmetic collapses.
5. **It never stops being a sales job.** There is no version of this where the work compounds — no
   asset accumulates, no rule library, no product. **In year three she is doing exactly what she
   does in month three, just more of it.** That is the honest structural difference between this and
   the catalogue business.

## 6. What to check first — free, and it settles the whole thing

1. **Pull the CNPJ base**, filter one CNAE (e.g. 9602-5/02 *atividades de estética*), porte ME,
   opened in the last 60 days, in three states. **Count them.** If a segment does not produce 200+
   new businesses a month, the funnel has no top.
2. **Ask three Brazilian distributors of imported professional products, in writing, two questions:
   what commission do you pay a representante, and do you cover the interior?** Three WhatsApp
   messages. If commission is under 12% or they say they already cover it, the model is closed.
3. **Send 40 cold messages over two weeks at 15–20/day** with the margin-arithmetic opener. **Count
   replies, not sales.** Under 5 replies from 40 means the 2% is fiction and this dies for R$0.

## 7. Where this sits against the other candidates

**It is the fastest to cash and the weakest to own.** No build, no capital, revenue possible in
month two — and nothing accumulating behind it. Compare:

| | Catalogue landing (66) | WhatsApp distribution |
|---|---|---|
| Time to first revenue | ~8 weeks | **~3 weeks** |
| Build required | ~190 hours | **None** |
| Does the work compound? | **Yes** — rules, mappings, a monitor | **No** — every real is another order |
| Ceiling | R$42.000/month modelled | R$15–20.000, then hours run out |
| Language of the work | English, written | Portuguese, written |

**They are not mutually exclusive in principle** — but they are in practice, because both consume
the same 30 hours. **If the point is cash while something else is built, this is the better one. If
the point is a business that is worth something in three years, it is not.**
