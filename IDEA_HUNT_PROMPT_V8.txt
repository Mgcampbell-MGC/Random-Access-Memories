IDEA HUNT — PORTABLE BRIEF V8
A self-contained instruction set for a frontier LLM with sub-agent capability.
Written 19 Aug 2026. Supersedes V1-V7.

HOW TO USE THIS FILE
--------------------
Paste the whole thing as your opening message to a frontier model that can spawn
parallel sub-agents. It assumes the model knows NOTHING about this project. It
contains the mission, the arithmetic, the constraints, everything already proven
dead, the evidence standard, and a ten-agent deployment plan with a full brief
for each agent.

Do not summarise it before pasting. The specificity is the value. Two weeks and
seventy-two dead candidates produced the "already settled" section, and an agent
that has not read it will spend its budget re-deriving conclusions.

SECTION 1 — THE MISSION
================================================================================

Find a business for one specific person. Not a category, not a "space" — a
business with a named buyer, an observed price, and a route to that buyer.

THE FOUNDER
  A 26-year-old Brazilian woman in Sao Paulo. Call her the founder. She works
  alone and will always work alone. She is intelligent, literate in English and
  Portuguese, and capable of sustained careful work. She has no professional
  credential, no industry background, no track record, and no reputation.

THE TARGET
  US$10,000 per month NET TAKE-HOME — in her hands, after Brazilian tax.
  Not gross revenue. Not company profit.
  To be reached within 12-18 MONTHS, with revenue beginning by roughly month 6.

CAPITAL
  US$500-2,500. ONE TIME. Not monthly. This buys a company entity, tooling,
  domains, data acquisition and one small acquisition test. It does NOT buy paid
  customer acquisition as a channel, a legal or regulatory opinion, or the
  purchase of an existing cash-flowing asset. If a candidate needs more, say so
  explicitly rather than quietly assuming it.

MARKETS
  Brazil domestic, USA, UK, EU, Canada, Australia. All open. Brazil domestic is
  a FIRST-CLASS option and has historically been under-weighted — see Section 8.

SECTION 2 — THE GOVERNING ARITHMETIC. READ THIS BEFORE PROPOSING ANYTHING.
================================================================================

  Target, net, in her hands            US$10,000/month
  Brazilian burden, working figure     ~25% (being verified; range 6-33%)
  Gross revenue required               ~US$13,000/month  (~US$156,000/year)
  Her time                             30 h/week = ~130 h/month
  Of which actual delivery             ~78 h/month (60%, generous for a solo)
  REQUIRED REALISATION                 ~US$167 PER HOUR OF DELIVERY TIME

WHAT THIS KILLS, AND IT IS NOT A MATTER OF OPINION:

  >>> THE PRODUCTION CEILING <<<
  At this target, ANY business in which she personally produces each unit of
  output is arithmetically dead. Not hard — dead. Her 78 delivery hours must
  BUILD OR OPERATE AN ASSET. They must never MAKE THE UNITS.

The proof, so you do not have to take it on faith. The best-designed
hand-production business this project ever received priced a professional
written deliverable at US$325 per unit, covering up to 150 source pages, one
clarification round and one revision. To gross US$13,000/month she must deliver
FORTY of them per month inside 78 delivery hours — UNDER TWO HOURS EACH,
including intake, reading, drafting and revision. It is not credible.

Meanwhile, observed live listings for written deliverables on freelance
marketplaces sit at US$15, US$30 and US$45 — realising perhaps US$15-30/hour.
The gap to US$167 is five to eleven times. No pricing discipline closes it.

THEREFORE, revenue must come from one of only four places:

  A. THE ASSET PRODUCES THE UNITS
     She builds a machine once; it serves customer 2 through customer 400 at
     near-zero marginal cost. (Software, a dataset, a watcher.)

  B. A SLICE OF SOMEONE ELSE'S FLOW
     Commission or take-rate on transactions she introduces but does not
     perform. Her hours per unit are trivial against the value transferred.

  C. A SPREAD SHE OWNS
     Buy at one price, sell at another. NOTE: closed for physical goods — see
     Section 5.

  D. RENT ON SOMETHING SHE OWNS
     Licensing a work, a system, a right. A sub-case of A.

Every candidate you propose must state WHICH of these four it is. If it is none
of them, it is a job, and it does not reach the target.

SECTION 3 — HARD CONSTRAINTS
================================================================================

These are real. They are not preferences and they are not negotiable by
argument. Two of them were recently relaxed and the relaxations are stated
precisely — do not extend them further.

C1  NEVER SPEAKS IN ONGOING OPERATIONS.
    No customer calls, no video, no meetings, no voice notes, no webinars, no
    podcasts, in any language, ever. Written only.
    RELAXED: a small, bounded number of ONE-OFF SETUP calls at launch is now
    acceptable — opening a supplier account, passing a payment-processor risk
    review, instructing counsel, signing a partner agreement. Ongoing customer
    contact remains written-only, forever.

C2  PERMANENTLY FACELESS.
    No camera, no photograph, no personal brand, no audience-building, no
    name-based reputation. This forbids the entire "build in public / grow a
    following / become a known voice" playbook, which is how most solo software
    is actually sold. Governs PUBLIC facing only — private KYC to a payment
    processor is fine.
    ON PERSONAS: a company brand or a pen name is available ON ARGUMENT, but
    the default is anonymous, and a persona must NEVER be the thing that
    rescues an otherwise weak idea.

C3  NO EMPLOYEES, NO CONTRACTORS, EVER.
    Vendors, suppliers, software and APIs are all allowed without limit.
    Note the asymmetry: CAPACITY IS PURCHASABLE AS MACHINES BUT NOT AS PEOPLE.

C4  NEVER TAKES CUSTODY OF A CUSTOMER CREDENTIAL
    and never holds standing access to a customer's systems. Work arrives as a
    file the customer sends. This closes most B2B SaaS (which needs OAuth into
    the customer's account), all bookkeeping and ops work, and all monitoring
    that requires access. What survives: products that process an uploaded
    file, and products that watch something PUBLIC.

C5  CAPITAL: US$500-2,500, ONE TIME.

C6  ~30 HOURS PER WEEK.

C7  HER PERSONAL SAO PAULO NETWORK IS NOT A SALES CHANNEL.

VETOES — absolute:
  * Anything requiring her to be BELIEVED AS AN EXPERT. She has no credential,
    no domain and no name. This has the highest body count of any rule here. It
    kills advisory work, interpretation, opinion, assurance, and anything priced
    on credibility.
  * Construction as a buyer segment.
  * Labour-compliance document chasing.
  * Physical goods and inventory, unless made-to-order.
  * Anything built around death. (She is uneasy about it. It is her life, not
    an argument. Do not relitigate it.)

WHAT SURVIVES THE EXPERT VETO — the three known mechanisms:
  1. THE ARTEFACT PROVES ITSELF. Ship the deliverable with a proof sheet: every
     material statement paired with its source file and page; contradictions
     surfaced rather than silently resolved; unsupported claims listed in a
     "not used" section. Nothing is asserted on her authority.
  2. THE BUYER SUPPLIES THE DOMAIN, SHE SUPPLIES THE THROUGHPUT.
  3. THE BUYER SUPPLIES THE "BEFORE." She never claims a baseline. The customer
     sends work they already did, with their own hours attached; she returns the
     after; the two objects sit side by side and the buyer does the comparing.

SECTION 4 — THE EVIDENCE STANDARD. NON-NEGOTIABLE.
================================================================================

FOUR FABRICATED STATISTICS HAVE ALREADY BEEN CAUGHT IN THIS PROJECT. Two more
were caught the same day by agents auditing their own sources. Assume you will
be audited.

  * EVERY NUMBER NEEDS A PRIMARY-SOURCE URL. Acceptable: the company's own
    pricing page or documentation; a statute; a regulator or government
    publication; a trade body's own survey; a live listing you actually opened;
    a peer-reviewed or arXiv paper; a court or regulatory filing.

  * WRITE "UNVERIFIED" EXPLICITLY wherever you cannot meet that bar. A flagged
    gap is worth more than a plausible figure. A clean "there is no reliable
    public data on this" is a valuable sentence, not a failure.

  * NEVER CITE SEO CONTENT FARMS, LISTICLES, ROUNDUPS, OR VENDOR MARKETING as
    evidence that a requirement, a market or a price exists. Vendors overstate
    requirements in order to sell things. Whole genres of this material are
    fabricated end to end and cite each other in circles.

  * OBSERVED LISTINGS BEAT PUBLISHED CLAIMS. In this project, freelance-market
    price bands quoted at US$500-1,500 came from SEO blogs; the live gigs,
    when actually opened, were US$15, US$30 and US$45.

  * FIELD-INSPECT, DO NOT ASSUME. "Publicly available" and "obtainable at
    scale" are different properties. Of eight public registers inspected in
    this project, exactly ONE published email addresses. Open the record layout
    before claiming a list exists. In one case an agent corrected the
    principal's own briefing: a gazette assumed to be PDF-only turned out to be
    published as documented XML, which destroyed the moat the candidate rested
    on. That correction was the most valuable thing in the report.

  * WHEN A CORRECTION LANDS, PROPAGATE IT to every conclusion that depends on
    it. Do not fix a claim in one place and leave it standing in another.

  * A CLEAN NEGATIVE WITH A NAMED CAUSE OF DEATH IS A GOOD RESULT. You are not
    being scored on finding something. You are being scored on being right.
    Do not manufacture a positive.

SECTION 5 — ALREADY SETTLED. DO NOT RE-DERIVE. DO NOT PROPOSE.
================================================================================

Each of these cost real research. Treat them as closed unless you bring NEW
primary evidence that overturns them.

DEAD BUSINESS FAMILIES
  * Digital assets on marketplaces — templates, stock, fonts, SVGs, presets,
    prompt packs, mockups, icon sets. AI is either banned there (her skill is
    worthless) or allowed (price goes to zero). No third door.
  * Written deliverables sold on freelance marketplaces. Observed at US$15-45,
    sampled three times across unrelated categories. Audit exposure raises a
    category's ceiling but never creates a floor, and the ceiling is held with a
    credential and a name — so she gets the floor of every category she enters.
  * Verification of anything. A physical asset needs a body in a place; a
    digital asset needs a credential into the system that hosts it. She can have
    neither.
  * Lost-knowledge and documentation products. Where no third party holds a
    veto there is no buyer; where a veto exists, the veto-holder or a funded
    vendor publishes the artefact free.
  * Consumer mobile app stores. 12-18 months to a top-quartile solo outcome,
    and organic discovery for a new indie app is effectively zero.
    IMPORTANT: this finding is about CONSUMER MOBILE stores. B2B SOFTWARE
    marketplaces are a different animal and are NOT closed.
  * Reselling physical goods / merchant of record. Closed by Brazilian tax law:
    RIR/2018 art. 162 par. 1 makes a natural person who habitually trades goods
    "equiparada a pessoa juridica" by operation of law, registered or not. Two
    non-creditable turnover taxes then stack in opposite directions — Brazilian
    PIS/COFINS on her gross selling price (not an export, no input credits) and
    US state sales tax on her cost (no resale certificate). Separately,
    LC 126/2007 art. 20 bars a natural person from buying the foreign insurance
    that trade customers contractually require.
  * Any candidate whose buyer segment is construction, or whose subject is
    death. See the vetoes.

DEAD OR HEAVILY DISCOUNTED CHANNELS
  * Organic SEO as a PRIMARY channel — too slow for an 18-month clock, and
    materially damaged by AI answers. (Its successor — being cited BY AI
    assistants — is NOT settled and is an active research question. See Agent 8.)
  * Paid advertising as a channel rather than a test. At US$500-2,500 it buys
    an experiment, not a customer base.
  * Content, community, audience, personal brand. Forbidden by C2.
  * Her personal network. Forbidden by C7.

ONE OPERATIONAL FACT
  * One large craft marketplace could not be read at all, by four separate
    agents, using every available method — a bot-detection service blocks
    everything. Zero prices, sales counts or review counts have ever been
    obtained. Any conclusion about it requires a human with a browser.

================================================================================
SECTION 6 — THE LAWS. THE REUSABLE HALF OF SEVENTY-TWO FAILURES.
================================================================================

These are the transferable findings. Use them as tests. If your candidate fails
one, say so and explain why it survives anyway — or drop it.

ON WHETHER ANYONE WILL PAY

  THE CONSTRAINT-SIDE LAW
    "This saves you four hours" can only be sold to a buyer who is SHORT OF
    HOURS. Ask: what breaks if their volume doubled next month? Three states:
    DEMAND-CONSTRAINED (short of customers — will NOT buy time back; they
    already have spare hours and would rather keep them); SUPPLY-CONSTRAINED
    (turning work away — an hour saved is revenue); MARGIN-CONSTRAINED (volume
    fixed by something outside their control, cost is their own hours — an hour
    saved is margin). Most sole traders are demand-constrained.
    Worked example: 118,804 marriages registered in Australia in 2025 divided
    by 10,832 registered celebrants is 11.0 ceremonies each per year even if
    celebrants performed every marriage in the country. Realistically ~9. A
    person with nine jobs a year is not short of hours.

  THE EXOGENOUS-VOLUME SPEC
    The buyer to hunt has VOLUME SET BY SOMETHING THEY DO NOT CONTROL and COST
    THAT IS THEIR OWN HOURS. Renewal dates, statutory deadlines, settlement
    dates, pay cycles, filing seasons. They cannot sell more by working more,
    so cost per unit is their only lever.

  THE INVERSION CHECK
    Not "does the buyer possess this data?" but "COULD THE BUYER PRODUCE THIS
    ANSWER IN AN AFTERNOON?" If yes, there is no business.

  THE FREE FRONT DOOR
    "Knowing which one" is almost always the customer-acquisition cost of
    whoever holds the stock or the paying work — so they give it away. Before
    selling identification, matching, crossing or lookup, NAME WHO IS ALREADY
    PAID TO GIVE IT AWAY FREE.

  THE VETO LAW
    Knowledge only funds a purchase when a third party holds a veto. But
    wherever a veto exists, the veto-holder or a funded vendor usually publishes
    the artefact free. Check both halves.

  THE EMPTY-NICHE WARNING
    A category with zero competitors is WORSE evidence than a crowded one. A
    crowded market with visible prices proves demand. An empty one usually
    proves someone already tried.

ON PRICE

  THE CEILING-HOLDER LAW
    Regulatory or audit exposure raises a category's CEILING but never creates
    a FLOOR, and the ceiling is held with a credential and a name. A faceless
    seller lands at the floor of every category she enters.

  THE MONITORING PINCER
    Self-serve monitoring products cluster at US$0-100/month, because the raw
    signal is free and a competent solo builder always undercuts. Above
    US$300/month they are sold by a sales force. The rare products that are BOTH
    self-serve AND above US$300 share exactly one property: THE BUYER'S OWN
    REVENUE IS A DIRECT FUNCTION OF THE WATCH. So the test is not "is this
    watchable" but "DOES ONE ALERT, ACTED ON, PAY FOR A YEAR OF SUBSCRIPTION?"

  THE RENEWAL SCISSORS
    A regulator that needs a filing from 100,000 entities makes the artefact
    cheap BY DESIGN. No statute can force an annual DOCUMENT to be expensive.

  THE ASSURANCE COUPLING
    Enforcement teeth and the absence of a credentialed stamp are
    ANTI-CORRELATED by legislative design. Where a real fine exists, the statute
    also conscripts an auditor. Where no auditor is conscripted, no fine exists.
    And THE DOWNSTREAM STAMP: a stamp need not appear ON the deliverable to
    price it — only on something the deliverable is nested INSIDE.

  THE REPEATABILITY TRAP
    The property that grants her leverage (it is repeatable, so a machine can do
    it) is the same property that destroys pricing power (it is repeatable, so
    anyone's machine can do it). Every candidate must answer this.

ON DISTRIBUTION — THE ACTUAL BINDING CONSTRAINT

  Seventy-two candidates died at the same place: the point where a stranger has
  to decide to give her money. She has unlimited production and zero
  distribution. Production has never once been the problem.

  THE DISTRIBUTION SCISSORS
    Being FACELESS pushes toward MANY SMALL self-serve customers, because a
    relationship requires a person. Having NO AUDIENCE AND NO BUDGET pushes
    toward FEW LARGE customers, because reaching 400 strangers requires a
    following or money. THE TWO CONSTRAINTS POINT IN OPPOSITE DIRECTIONS. This
    tension is the real strategic problem and most candidates ignore it.

  THE WHOLESALE ESCAPE  *** the most important finding in this file ***
    There is exactly ONE channel in which a faceless supplier is PREFERRED
    rather than merely tolerated: SELLING THE MACHINE TO A FIRM THAT RESELLS IT
    UNDER ITS OWN NAME. The reseller supplies the face, the credential, the
    interpretation and the liability. She supplies the throughput. Price is then
    set by the RESELLER'S CLIENT REVENUE, not by the self-serve comparable.
    It is the only structure found in seventy-two candidates in which C1, C2 and
    the expert veto stop being costs and become the product's shape.
    Arithmetic: 20 firms at US$650/month = US$13,000. Twenty, not three hundred.
    Positioning line that tested well: "You own the relationship. We are your
    invisible department."

  THE ONBOARDING LAW
    Written-only is compatible with SELLING. It is NOT compatible with BEING
    ONBOARDED. Reseller setup, acquirer risk review, vendor security diligence,
    fiscal-representative appointment and dispute escalation are all
    conversations. ASK THIS OF EVERY CANDIDATE: how many separate parties must
    onboard her, and can each be done in writing?

  THE POSTAL LIST LAW
    A public register gives name, street address, phone and an expiry date. It
    does not give email. Of eight registers field-inspected, exactly ONE
    published email at scale. ENUMERABLE AND CONTACTABLE ARE DIFFERENT
    PROPERTIES, and the second is rare. Field-inspect before believing.

ON WHERE SHE IS

  THE CURRENCY DIRECTION LAW
    Earn hard currency, spend soft. The property is in the BILLING, not the
    cargo.

  THE ASYMMETRIC PAIR
    Put the SOURCE in Brazil and the BUYER abroad. Brazilian raw material is
    cheap, published in enormous volume, badly formatted, and protected by
    language — that is her arbitrage and it costs her nothing. Foreign buyers
    pay hard currency. The configuration satisfying both: SOMETHING OBTAINABLE
    ONLY IN BRAZIL, SOLD TO SOMEONE WHO IS NOT IN BRAZIL.

  THE PHYSICAL-PRESENCE PARADOX
    Local access is her single best arbitrage and it requires a body in a place.
    Domestically she HAS a body in the place. Abroad she never can.

ON METHOD

  FILTER LAST, NOT FIRST
    A constraint applied early deletes the COMPONENT along with the candidate,
    and the component was the valuable half. Hold parts loose, recombine, THEN
    filter.

  NAME THE SHAPE OUT LOUD
    Before proposing a candidate, state its shape (Section 7). If it is the same
    shape as the last one proposed, STOP and change shape first. Optimising for
    her constraints drives everything toward "questionnaire, she writes prose,
    deliverable," because that is the most constraint-compatible activity in
    existence. IT IS A LOCAL OPTIMUM AND THE SEARCH ROLLS BACK INTO IT EVERY
    TIME UNLESS SOMETHING FORBIDS IT. Three consecutive "new candidates" were
    once one idea with three different buyers.

  PORT THE WHOLE MODEL, NOT ONLY THE PARTS
    When an outside business model is supplied, ask what HER VERSION OF THE
    WHOLE MODEL is — not merely which components it donates. Three industrial
    models were once mined for eighteen parts, and the parts were then applied
    back onto the existing business. Nobody asked what her version of the model
    itself was.

  THE TEST DESIGN THAT WORKS
    Twenty real instances, and COUNT HOW MANY CLEAR A NUMERIC BAR. Not "get
    three customers" — measure the RATE, because the rate predicts everything
    after it. And prefer the test that costs nothing and needs no phone.

SECTION 7 — THE SIX SHAPES
================================================================================

Every candidate must be labelled with one.

  S1  WRITTEN WORK — she writes prose to order.
      STATUS: over-explored AND now arithmetically dead at this target (Section
      2). Do not propose. If you believe you have an exception, you must show
      the per-hour arithmetic explicitly.

  S2  REPLENISHMENT / MERCHANT OF RECORD — she buys and resells.
      STATUS: CLOSED by Brazilian tax law. Seven agents, primary statutory and
      supplier sources. Do not re-open without new statutory facts.
      NOTE the surviving escape hatch, unexplored: resale is closed by tax law
      and brokerage is closed by professional referral-fee rules, so THE
      SURVIVING INTERSECTION IS COMMISSION AGENCY IN AN INDUSTRY WITH NO
      REFERRAL-FEE RULE.

  S3  CONTINUOUS OPERATION — the product is that something was watched.
      STATUS: hunted once. Produced THE MONITORING PINCER and THE WHOLESALE
      ESCAPE. The self-serve version is dead; the WHOLESALE version is the
      leading structure in the whole project.

  S4  A COMPOUNDING DATASET — she accumulates what nobody else has.
      STATUS: under active research. Arithmetically ideal (structure A).

  S5  SOFTWARE OR A SYSTEM SOLD AS A PRODUCT.
      STATUS: consumer mobile app stores are dead. B2B software marketplaces
      are NOT settled and may be the best remaining self-serve channel.

  S6  MATCHING TWO ENUMERABLE POPULATIONS — she connects, never produces.
      STATUS: under active research. Never previously hunted.

SECTION 8 — THE THREE LIVE CHANNELS, AND THE TWO DOORS
================================================================================

Given 12-18 months and US$500-2,500, exactly three channels are cheap enough,
fast enough, and compatible with having no face:

  1. COLD EMAIL. The only channel that is simultaneously cheap, fast and
     faceless. Its constraints are legality and deliverability for a foreign
     sender. NOTE: Canada's CASL and Australia's Spam Act 2003 require consent
     and effectively bar cold B2B mail; the US permits it under conditions
     (15 U.S.C. 7704); the UK and EU sit in between; BRAZIL'S LGPD POSITION IS
     AN OPEN QUESTION AND MAY BE THE MOST PERMISSIVE OF ALL.

  2. BORROWED INTENT — a marketplace that already has buyers with wallets out.
     Costs nothing and supplies the trust she cannot manufacture.

  3. WHITE-LABEL PARTNERS — ten to twenty relationships at US$650-1,000/month.
     Opened by the C1 relaxation. See THE WHOLESALE ESCAPE.

Everything else is too slow, too expensive, or forbidden.

THE TWO DOORS through THE DISTRIBUTION SCISSORS:
  DOOR 1 — BORROWED INTENT. Sell self-serve at US$99-299 inside a marketplace
           that supplies the distribution and the trust.
  DOOR 2 — THE INVISIBLE DEPARTMENT. Ten to twenty white-label resellers, each
           putting their own name on her machine's output.

BRAZIL DOMESTIC — deliberately promoted to first-class, having been
under-weighted for seventy-two candidates. Selling domestically REMOVES: the
cross-border tax pincer that closed S2; the foreign-insurance bar; most of the
onboarding-by-voice problem (instant domestic payment rails have no acquirer
risk review and no chargeback arbitration conducted by telephone); and the
foreign-anonymous-vendor trust penalty. It ACTIVATES local access, the only
genuine arbitrage identified in the entire project. It COSTS: lower price
points, and earning the soft currency. See THE ASYMMETRIC PAIR for the
reconciliation.

SECTION 9 — HOW PREVIOUS HUNTS FAILED. DO NOT REPEAT THESE.
================================================================================

  1. GENERATING BUYERS INSTEAD OF IDEAS. Three consecutive "new candidates"
     were one idea with three different buyers. Name the shape out loud.

  2. FILTERING TOO EARLY. Constraints applied at the start deleted valuable
     components along with the candidates.

  3. MINING A MODEL FOR PARTS AND APPLYING THEM BACK ONTO THE OLD BUSINESS
     instead of asking what her version of that whole model would be.

  4. ACCEPTING SECONDARY SOURCES. Four fabricated statistics. Two research
     premises that turned out to be sourced only to the marketing of the firms
     that benefited from them.

  5. ASSUMING A REGISTER IS A MAILING LIST. Field-inspect the record layout.

  6. CONFUSING GROSS WITH NET. Every revenue figure in seventy-two candidates
     was gross. The founder's tax burden was never computed once.

  7. SELF-SCORING. Documents arriving with a self-assessed "8.8/10, conditional
     GO" alongside a plainly stated capital requirement ten to forty times over
     budget. Read the budget and the org chart; ignore the score.

  8. PROPOSING A BUSINESS THAT IS ACTUALLY A JOB. If she must be present for
     each unit, it is a job, and it does not reach the target.

SECTION 10 — THE DEPLOYMENT PLAN. TEN AGENTS, TWO WAVES.
================================================================================

Give EVERY agent, without exception: Section 1 (mission and target), Section 2
(the arithmetic), Section 3 (constraints), Section 4 (the evidence standard),
and the relevant laws from Section 6. An agent that has not read the evidence
standard will return listicle numbers.

Tell every agent explicitly: A CLEAN NEGATIVE WITH A NAMED CAUSE OF DEATH IS A
SUCCESSFUL RESULT. Do not reward finding something.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WAVE 1 — EIGHT AGENTS IN PARALLEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AGENT 1 — THE WHOLESALE BUYER HUNT.  *** HIGHEST PRIORITY ***
  THE WHOLESALE ESCAPE says the only channel where facelessness is an ADVANTAGE
  is supplying a firm that resells under its own name. Find the buyers.
  Answer: which small professional firms routinely white-label an outside
  vendor's output under their own brand, and in which industries is this NORMAL
  rather than exceptional? Look at: IP boutiques and trademark filing services,
  accountancy practices, insurance brokerages, marketing agencies, translation
  agencies, recruiters, property managers, patent annuity services, corporate
  secretarial and registered-agent firms. For each: is white-labelling an
  established practice with published partner or wholesale programmes? What is
  the observed wholesale-versus-retail spread? How is a wholesale supplier
  onboarded, and CAN IT BE DONE IN WRITING? What does the firm's
  professional-indemnity insurer require of a subprocessor? How many such firms
  exist and are they contactable by email?
  Deliverable: a ranked list of reseller populations, with the arithmetic for
  reaching US$13,000/month from 15-25 of them.

AGENT 2 — THE COMPOUNDING DATASET (S4).
  What dataset can one person accumulate in 30 h/week that someone pays a
  recurring subscription for?
  Start from the DEMAND side with observed prices — find real niche data
  products that publish a number, and record it with a URL. Then the
  defensibility question, which is where these die: a dataset assembled from
  public sources can be re-assembled by anyone with a scraper and an AI agent.
  So which datasets are NOT re-assemblable? Candidates: data that only exists
  because someone recorded it AT THE TIME and it then disappears; data requiring
  years of accumulation so a new entrant starts far behind; data scattered
  across hundreds of sources where the labour of unification IS the asset; data
  locked in non-machine-readable formats.
  MANDATORY legal perimeter: what may lawfully be scraped and resold? Cover the
  hiQ v. LinkedIn line of cases; the EU DATABASE SUI GENERIS RIGHT (Directive
  96/9/EC), which is a real and underappreciated barrier; government open-data
  licences (US federal works, UK OGL, EU Open Data Directive 2019/1024), which
  are the safest raw material; and website terms of service as a CONTRACTUAL
  barrier distinct from copyright. WATCH FOR NO-DERIVATIVES LICENCES on
  government portals — at least one major national procurement portal publishes
  under CC BY-ND, which forbids a derived data product outright.
  Deliverable: three specific datasets — not categories — each with source,
  why it compounds, why it cannot be copied, the named buyer, an observed
  comparable price, and the honest reason it might fail.

AGENT 3 — MATCHING AND COMMISSION AGENCY (S6).
  Two questions. FIRST: which industries lawfully pay a commission to an
  UNLICENSED, UNREGISTERED intermediary? Work from the negative — map where
  referral fees are prohibited or licence-gated (US real estate licensing acts
  and RESPA 12 U.S.C. 2607; insurance producer licensing; broker-dealer
  registration; legal professional conduct rules; healthcare anti-kickback;
  mortgage) — then name the industries with NO such rule. Look hard at
  manufacturers' sales agency and independent sales representation (there is an
  organised trade-body world here that publishes commission-rate surveys),
  freight and logistics brokerage (check carrier/broker licensing and surety
  bond requirements), commodity and ingredient trading, industrial equipment,
  export sales agency, IP and licensing brokerage, and software reseller and
  affiliate programmes. FOR BRAZIL SPECIFICALLY: commercial representation is a
  REGULATED PROFESSION under Lei 4.886/1965 with mandatory registration in a
  regional council — read the statute and establish whether an unregistered
  representative can lawfully collect commission at all.
  SECOND: shape S6 proper. Find two-sided matches where BOTH populations are
  enumerable AND email-reachable, transaction value is high enough that a small
  percentage is real money, the matching itself is genuinely hard, and no
  licence is required to introduce them. Address the chicken-and-egg problem:
  which side can she acquire first, and why would that side join an empty
  marketplace?
  Compute, for every commission rate you find with a source, the monthly flow
  required to gross US$13,000.

AGENT 4 — B2B SOFTWARE MARKETPLACES (S5) AND VERIFIED SOLO SOFTWARE REVENUE.
  Consumer mobile app stores are settled dead. B2B software marketplaces are
  not. For each of the major business-software marketplaces (e-commerce,
  accounting, CRM, project management, developer tooling, API marketplaces, and
  the equivalents inside the Brazilian fiscal and ERP ecosystem): what is the
  revenue share, what does listing cost, what review process exists, what entity
  and country are required, WILL THEY PAY A BRAZILIAN (check the payout country
  list and tax-form requirements in the platform's own documentation — make this
  an explicit column and mark exclusions clearly), and is there ANY published
  evidence of what a mid-ranking app earns?
  Separately: find real solo software businesses at or above US$10,000/month
  with PUBLICLY VERIFIABLE revenue — open revenue dashboards, verified-revenue
  listings, founders' own published books. For each, record how customers were
  acquired, and then answer THE CRITICAL QUESTION: what fraction were acquired
  through a channel requiring a FACE, a NAME, an AUDIENCE or CALLS? If the
  honest answer is "almost all of them", say so plainly — that is the most
  valuable finding available to you.
  Also: C4 forbids standing access to a customer's systems, which kills most
  B2B apps at the OAuth step. Which product categories do NOT require it?
  BEWARE: "top 10 micro-SaaS success stories" content is the single biggest
  fabrication risk in this topic. Do not cite it once.

AGENT 5 — THE FACELESS DISTRIBUTION AUDIT.
  Audit CHANNELS, not products. For each: does it work, what does it cost at
  US$500 / US$2,500, what kills it, and is it compatible with no face and no
  voice? Cover: cold email (the STATUTES first — US, UK, EU, Canada, Australia,
  and Brazil's LGPD, which is the open question; then deliverability mechanics
  and the major mailbox providers' own published bulk-sender requirements);
  marketplaces with built-in buyer intent; white-label and partner distribution;
  affiliate and referral networks used in reverse, so that others sell for her;
  directories, aggregators, comparison sites and public tender portals; and
  anything you find that is not on this list — that last part is the most
  valuable thing you can return.
  For Brazil specifically: establish whether the national company register
  publishes EMAIL ADDRESSES in its free bulk dataset. If it does, that is one of
  the largest contactable B2B lists on earth and it changes this project's
  central problem. VERIFY BY INSPECTING THE PUBLISHED RECORD LAYOUT. Do not
  assume.

AGENT 6 — BRAZILIAN TAX, STRUCTURE AND MONEY-IN.
  Nobody has ever computed this and every revenue figure in the project is
  gross. Determine: how much GROSS revenue must a solo Sao Paulo operator earn
  to net US$10,000/month, and which legal structure minimises it?
  Cover every available structure including operating as a natural person with
  no company; the simplified regime and the test that determines which schedule
  applies for someone with NO payroll; and the presumed-profit regime. Include
  corporate taxes, social contributions, municipal service tax, and the personal
  income tax and social security she pays on her own draw. ESTABLISH WHETHER
  DISTRIBUTED PROFITS ARE STILL EXEMPT FROM PERSONAL INCOME TAX — this may have
  changed recently and it is load-bearing. Cite the law and its effective date.
  Then EXPORTED SERVICES: does selling abroad change the answer? Address the
  municipal service tax exemption on exported services and its heavily-litigated
  carve-out, the federal contribution treatment, and any separate export
  sub-limit in the simplified regime.
  Then MONEY-IN: how does a Brazilian entity or individual lawfully receive
  foreign currency from foreign customers, what reporting is required, what does
  it cost, and what is the current exchange rate with a dated source.
  FINALLY, AND THIS MAY CHANGE WHICH BUSINESS SHE CHOOSES: is software or
  licence revenue taxed differently from a written service? Is royalty income
  different? Look for it specifically.
  Sources: the statutes and the tax authority's own publications. NOT
  accounting-firm blog posts, which are wrong constantly — use them only to
  locate the statute, then read the statute.

AGENT 7 — THE EXOGENOUS-VOLUME BUYER HUNT.
  Under THE CONSTRAINT-SIDE LAW, the buyer worth having has volume set by
  something they do not control and cost that is their own hours. Enumerate
  those populations systematically, EXCLUDING anything involving death or
  construction. Think: renewal and expiry calendars, statutory filing deadlines,
  publication-triggered legal clocks, settlement and closing dates, payroll
  cycles, licence and certification renewal, tax and regulatory seasons,
  insurance renewal, patent and trademark annuities, grant acquittal deadlines,
  academic and admissions cycles, import and customs windows.
  For each: what sets the volume, how large is the population, IS IT
  ENUMERABLE AND EMAIL-CONTACTABLE (field-inspect, per THE POSTAL LIST LAW),
  what do they currently pay to get the work done, and does ONE person decide?
  Rank by wallet times contactability. This agent produces the target list that
  Agents 1, 2 and 3 aim at, so bias toward breadth.

AGENT 8 — AI ANSWER ENGINES AS A DISCOVERY CHANNEL.
  Organic SEO is ruled out as a primary channel. Its successor is not settled.
  This may matter more for this founder than for anyone, because BEING CITED BY
  AN AI ASSISTANT REQUIRES NO FACE, NO NAME, NO AUDIENCE AND NO VOICE — it may
  be the only discovery channel natively compatible with permanent anonymity.
  ANSWER FIRST: is a source cited because it was in TRAINING DATA (a lag of a
  year or more, useless on an 18-month clock) or because it was fetched by LIVE
  RETRIEVAL at query time (a new site can be cited in days)? Establish this from
  the providers' own documentation, per product. THIS SINGLE DISTINCTION
  DETERMINES WHETHER THE CHANNEL IS VIABLE AT ALL.
  Then: is the shift real in measured numbers — what has happened to
  click-through when an AI answer is present, and how much referral traffic do
  assistants actually send in ABSOLUTE terms? A channel growing tenfold off a
  tiny base may still be too small in 18 months.
  Then: what mechanically determines citation? Read the actual academic work on
  generative engine optimisation. Establish whether the proposed machine-readable
  standards are ACTUALLY CONSUMED by any major provider or merely discussed.
  Read the AI crawlers' own documentation and establish which assistants depend
  on which underlying search index.
  Then THE QUESTION THAT DECIDES IT: search engines rank on domain authority and
  backlinks, which take years. DO AI ASSISTANTS HAVE THE SAME BIAS, or do they
  cite whatever best answers the question? Try to OBSERVE IT DIRECTLY — find
  queries where assistants cite small, obscure, single-purpose sites, and record
  what those sites have in common.
  WARNING: this topic has the worst signal-to-noise ratio of any assigned here.
  It is a marketing-agency land grab and most writing on it is content marketing
  stuffed with invented percentages. Acceptable sources: academic papers, the AI
  companies' own documentation, standards proposals in original form, companies
  whose actual business is measuring web traffic publishing their own measured
  data, court and regulatory filings, and direct observation. NOTHING ELSE.

AGENT 9 — THE RED TEAM.
  Take the two or three strongest survivors of Wave 1 and try to KILL them.
  Mandatory checks, each answered explicitly:
    * Who is already paid to give this away free? (THE FREE FRONT DOOR)
    * Could the buyer produce this answer in an afternoon? (THE INVERSION CHECK)
    * Is the buyer demand-, supply- or margin-constrained? (THE CONSTRAINT-SIDE
      LAW)
    * How many separate parties must onboard her, and can each be done in
      writing? (THE ONBOARDING LAW)
    * Does one alert or one unit, acted on, pay for a year? (THE MONITORING
      PINCER)
    * Does the property granting leverage also destroy pricing power? (THE
      REPEATABILITY TRAP)
    * Is the list actually contactable? Open the record layout. (THE POSTAL LIST
      LAW)
    * What is the cheapest existing substitute, with a URL and a price?
    * What does this look like if a global economic downturn arrives in
      2027-2028?
  Be adversarial. Default to killing. A survivor of a genuine attempt to kill it
  is worth ten unexamined candidates.

AGENT 10 — THE FREE TEST DESIGNER.
  For each survivor, design a test that COSTS NOTHING, NEEDS NO PHONE, AND
  NEEDS NO CUSTOMER, and that measures a RATE rather than producing an anecdote.
  The best tests produced by this project so far: pull the published price list
  of twenty firms and count how many contain a specific line item; email thirty
  organisations one written question and count the replies containing a NUMBER;
  write the same deliverable twice and time both, because if the second takes
  under half the first it is a machine and if it takes the same it is a job;
  parse four weeks of a public gazette and count how many events touched each of
  twenty named firms.
  Each test must state the numeric bar IN ADVANCE and what result kills the
  candidate. A test that cannot fail is not a test.

IF YOU CAN ONLY RUN THREE AGENTS

Run Agent 1 (the wholesale buyer hunt), Agent 7 (exogenous-volume buyers), and
Agent 9 (the red team) on whatever those two produce. That is the shortest path
from nothing to a defensible candidate.

================================================================================
SECTION 11 — SYNTHESIS
================================================================================

When the agents report, do NOT simply concatenate them. Do this:

  1. LIST THE CONTRADICTIONS FIRST. Where two agents disagree, name it and
     resolve it on the evidence, saying which one you believe and why. In this
     project, agents correcting each other — and correcting the principal — has
     produced more value than agreement.

  2. SEPARATE THE LAWS FROM THE CANDIDATES. The reusable finding is worth more
     than the business. Write down every new structural rule discovered, with
     the evidence that produced it, whether or not any candidate survived.

  3. BUILD ONE NUMBERS LEDGER for the whole synthesis, with three columns:
     VERIFIED PRIMARY / SECONDARY-FLAGGED / UNVERIFIED-AND-NOT-USED. Anything
     you could not verify goes in the third column BY NAME rather than being
     quietly dropped.

  4. FOR EACH SURVIVING CANDIDATE, state: the shape (S1-S6); which of the four
     revenue structures (A-D); the named buyer; what breaks for that buyer
     without it; the observed comparable price with a URL; the number of
     customers needed for US$13,000/month; the channel that reaches them; the
     capital tier; the onboarding count; and THE HONEST REASON IT MIGHT FAIL.

  5. RANK BY WHAT IS LEAST LIKELY TO BE WRONG, not by what is most exciting.

================================================================================
SECTION 12 — WHAT A GOOD ANSWER LOOKS LIKE
================================================================================

  * It names a buyer, not a market.
  * It has an observed price with a URL, not an estimate.
  * It states the arithmetic to US$13,000/month explicitly.
  * It says which of the four revenue structures it is, and therefore why her
    78 delivery hours are not the bottleneck.
  * It answers THE ONBOARDING LAW with a count.
  * It has a free test with a numeric bar set in advance.
  * It flags its own weakest link before anyone else has to.
  * It includes at least one thing that corrects this brief.

  And a bad answer, which this project has received many times:
  a well-written blueprint with a self-assessed score, a capital requirement ten
  times over budget stated on page nine, a market size from a content farm, and
  a buyer who is short of customers rather than short of hours.
