## THE VERDICT — no clean ALIVE. Six MARGINAL, six DEAD, one named top candidate.

Every candidate that reached the board was independently audited twice. Where the two auditors split, the
**structural** finding controlled over a pure floor-arithmetic finding — a free, public route that removes Sol
from the value chain entirely beats a "the corrected number is below $1,000/month" finding, because the former
kills the shape and the latter only narrows the number. On that rule, three of the twelve carry a structural
refutation from at least one auditor and are DEAD regardless of what their paired auditor found.

### MARGINAL (6) — ranked best to weakest

**#1 — A ESCRITURA** (property-closing portrait, wholesaled to real-estate brokerages)
Shape: **S5** — a machine-personalised printed object sold through a channel partner, a wholesale
closing-gift mechanic never tried in this archive.
One line: a script renders a stylised street-map/satellite print carrying the buyer's name and closing date;
Printi prints it; it ships on the brokerage's own standing order every time **it** closes a sale — Sol never

Why it is the strongest survivor: the reachable pool is an **observed transaction count** — SECOVI-SP's own
monthly new-unit closing figures — not a chain of invented multipliers. When the arithmetic auditor went hunting
for a fresher primary source than the repair had used, the pool got **bigger** (6.980/month, stale Dec-2023, versus

entire batch that came out of audit *stronger* than it went in. The free-front-door auditor independently
confirmed the load-bearing legal fact the repair had left open — OpenStreetMap data may be freely printed and
resold commercially, and OSMF's own Attribution Guidelines wiki has a dedicated clause covering exactly this

willingness-to-pay.
**Next test (R$0, no phone, bar committed in advance):** email 15–20 CRECI-SP-registered São Paulo imobiliárias
(a free, live public register) asking what they currently give a client at closing and whether they'd buy a

## Laws this hunt derived

**1. THE PUBLIC-MARKETPLACE-IN-A-PRIVATE-CHANNEL COSTUME.** A "vendor-dispatch" or "correspondent network" repair
that routes orders through an already-public, self-serve marketplace does not create a channel — it exposes one
that was already open. Measured twice: Cronoshare's own detetive-particular price guide and named-provider
listings are reachable by any buyer with no login (killed CAMPO v2); Eye.Photo's AI iris-editing SaaS is
self-serve, Portuguese-capable and marketed directly at the same buyer A Íris needed (killed A Íris). Before
proposing a correspondent/vendor network as the fix for a channel problem, check whether the vendor pool itself is
a public marketplace the end buyer can already reach directly — if so, her entire margin is the cost of one extra
click.

**2. THE ORIGINATOR'S OWN FREE ARCHIVE.** Before building a paid ledger of past events, check whether the body
that generates the underlying event already publishes its own full historical archive on its own site, in plain
HTML, no login required. ACE (the Cup of Excellence auction body itself) serves the complete cross-year,
cross-origin, named-lot results at ordinary human-facing URLs, not a hard-to-find API. Generalises **THE FREE
FRONT DOOR**: the search must include the event's ORIGINATOR, not only competing resellers — an originator has
zero incentive to charge for its own past results, because charging protects no future flow it depends on.

**3. AN ANCHOR BUILT ON ADVANTAGES YOU LACK.** A demand-pool estimate anchored to a comparable company's current
reach is invalid unless that reach is achievable on the founder's own timeline and budget. Creative Brazil's
cited 400+-store network took its founder 23 years (~17,4 net new stores/year) to build, via in-person channels
closed to Sol by construction; the micro-export desk's pool derivation used that reach as if available to a
faceless, one-time-US$2.500 entrant in year one. **THE OBSERVED-PRICE CEILING** extends to observed-*reach*
ceilings — ask not only what the comparable charges but what it cost the comparable, in years/capital/
relationships, to get where it is, and discount the pool by that gap.

**4. AUDIT YOUR OWN REPAIR'S ARITHMETIC BEFORE TRUSTING ITS HEADLINE.** Real ~10×-scale and multiplication errors,
each caught only by independent recomputation from the repair's own stated inputs: A Carteira do CTF's reachable
pool overstated 10× (a 30-vs-300-clients-per-partner slip); Brasil Verifica's "required share is only 1–6%" was
actually 75–96% at the pool's own low end; O Livro do Leilão's Brazil-only required share was 90–120%, not the
stated 51–113%; the iFood repair's own stated inputs multiply to R$8.000–45.000, not the R$5.000–20.000 it
headlined. A repair agent's "required share" or "pool" figure is never self-verifying, however well-sourced its
underlying facts — recompute every multiplication from the document's own stated inputs before ranking on it.

**5. CHECK THE BLOG AND THE SITEMAP, NOT JUST THE PRICING PAGE, FOR A BUNDLED FREE FEATURE.** The repair agent
for A Carteira do CTF checked Ambisis's marketing page, found no mention of RAPP, and concluded the bundling risk
was low. The auditor checked Ambisis's blog — reachable only via its sitemap.xml — and found a live, SEO-titled
post already bundling RAPP guidance into an existing paid subscription serving "hundreds" of consultancies. A
free-front-door search that stops at a competitor's homepage under-detects: vendors bundle a feature that guts a
narrow product's differentiation quietly, in content marketing, not on the price list.

**6. THE STACKED-UNVERIFIED-ASSUMPTION DISCOUNT.** A pool built by multiplying four or more independently
unverified numbers (partner count × clients/shop × visits/year × conversion rate, for the grooming keepsake) can
be internally consistent arithmetic and still be almost entirely speculative — none of the four inputs had a
primary-source comparable anywhere, in Brazil or abroad. Count the independently-unverified multipliers in any
pool derivation; treat a pool built from ≥3 stacked UNVERIFIED inputs as a hypothesis for the next test to
falsify, never as a number to rank a candidate on.

**7. AN OBSERVED-TRANSACTION-COUNT POOL BEATS A PARTNER-CEILING POOL, EVEN WHEN BOTH ARE "REACHABLE."** A
Escritura's pool is a real trade body's own current transaction count (SECOVI-SP's monthly new-unit closings)
times an assumed price; the grooming keepsake's pool is an assumed ceiling on how many partners she could ever
recruit, times three more assumed numbers. Auditing the former made it bigger and the case better; auditing the
latter knocked its headline floor down by ~40%. Prefer a pool derived from "how often does the underlying event
already happen, per a primary count" over "how many relationships could she plausibly build" — the former is
falsifiable and tends to survive scrutiny; the latter is stacked speculation wearing a ceiling's clothing.

**8. A CANDIDATE THAT FAILS ONE OF THE TWO REVEALED PREFERENCES SHOULD RANK BELOW ONE THAT PASSES BOTH, EVEN IF
ITS ARITHMETIC IS CLEANER.** AS FITAS cleared every economic gate well enough to be repaired and to survive both
audits without a structural refutation. But both the repair and both audits agree, in terms, that ORIGINATION
ONCE VS FOREVER is not cured. The founder's own stated priority order puts the two revealed preferences first,
ahead of the structural kill-laws and ahead of economics — the board must re-apply that order at the end. A
MARGINAL stamp earned by clean arithmetic does not excuse a candidate from ranking behind ones that also pass the
preferences the founder herself revealed.

## The name, and how it differs from ACOLHE

**A ESCRITURA** — a personalised closing-day print, sold wholesale to São Paulo real-estate brokerages, generated
by a machine from public map data with zero prose and nothing for a buyer to trust beyond recognising their own
house.

It is a genuinely different business from ACOLHE, on both axes the founder asked for. **Niche:** ACOLHE sells to
municípios through competitive government tenders (pregão/dispensa) for a statutory child-welfare benefit; A
Escritura sells to private real-estate brokerages as a discretionary closing gift — no government buyer, no bid,
no tender parser, no public-procurement statute anywhere in it. **Business model:** ACOLHE bids and fulfils — she
wins a competitive tender, then waits weeks for a município to pay after formal ateste, with real working capital
tied up in goods in transit the whole time. A Escritura is a standing wholesale order from a private partner — no
competitive bidding, near-zero inventory (each print is made only after a real closing happens), and a private
company pays on ordinary commercial terms, never a municipal payment queue.

It is not a proven business. Nobody has asked a single brokerage whether it wants this. That is exactly the next
step, and it costs nothing: 15–20 written emails to CRECI-SP-registered São Paulo imobiliárias, a bar of ≥4 real
replies naming a number ≥R$30 committed in advance, before anything is built.
