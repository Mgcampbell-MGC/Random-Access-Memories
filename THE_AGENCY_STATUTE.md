# LEI 4.886/65 — THE STATUTE, VERBATIM AND VERIFIED, 19 Aug 2026

**Why this file exists:** `CLAUDE.md` records THE ANTI-DISINTERMEDIATION STATUTE as settled, but the claim had
never been checked against the actual text. It has now been. **The statute is MORE favourable than the file
claimed, in three separate places — and it carries one duty that cuts directly against C1.**

## 1. ★★ THE UPGRADES — three places where the real text beats what was recorded

### ★ ART. 31 — the zone commission is the DEFAULT, not a negotiated extra

> **"Art. 31.** Prevendo o contrato de representação a exclusividade de zona ou zonas, **ou quando este for
> omisso**, fará jus o representante à comissão pelos negócios aí realizados, **ainda que diretamente pelo
> representado ou por intermédio de terceiros.**" *(Redação dada pela Lei nº 8.420/1992)*
>
> **"Parágrafo único.** A exclusividade de representação não se presume na ausência de ajustes expressos."

**The file recorded that an exclusive-territory agent gets paid on business she never touched. The text is
stronger: the entitlement applies where the contract provides for zone exclusivity OR WHERE THE CONTRACT IS
SILENT.** The default runs in the agent's favour.

**And note the distinction the parágrafo único draws — it is doing different work than it first appears.**
*Exclusividade de ZONA* (caput) is the default-favourable rule. *Exclusividade de REPRESENTAÇÃO* — the
principal's promise not to appoint other agents at all — is what is **not** presumed. **These are two different
exclusivities and only one of them has to be bargained for.**

### ★ ART. 33 — SILENCE IS ACCEPTANCE, and a foreign buyer buys 120 days

> **"Art. 33.** Não sendo previstos, no contrato de representação, os prazos para recusa das propostas ou
> pedidos, que hajam sido entregues pelo representante, acompanhados dos requisitos exigíveis, ficará o
> representado **obrigado a creditar-lhe a respectiva comissão, se não manifestar a recusa, por escrito**, nos
> prazos de **15, 30, 60 ou 120 dias**, conforme se trate de comprador domiciliado, respectivamente, na mesma
> praça, em outra do mesmo Estado, em outro Estado ou **no estrangeiro**."

**An order transmitted in writing, with the required particulars, converts into a commission entitlement unless
the principal refuses IN WRITING inside the window.** This is not a minor procedural rule — **it makes the
entire order flow a written, dated, self-proving process**, which is exactly the shape C1 requires.

### ★ ART. 27(j) — the termination indemnity ACCRUES OVER THE WHOLE LIFE OF THE CONTRACT

> **"j)** indenização devida ao representante pela rescisão do contrato fora dos casos previstos no art. 35,
> cujo montante **não poderá ser inferior a 1/12 (um doze avos) do total da retribuição auferida durante o
> tempo em que exerceu a representação.**"

**Not one twelfth of a year's commission — one twelfth of EVERYTHING ever earned under that contract.** A
representation running eight years at R$40.000/month has built an indemnity floor of roughly R$320.000 that the
principal must pay to terminate without cause. **That is a compounding asset created by statute, and it is the
closest thing to equity available inside these constraints.**

**Two anti-avoidance rules protect it:**
> **"§ 2°** O contrato com prazo determinado, uma vez prorrogado o prazo inicial, tácita ou expressamente,
> torna-se a prazo indeterminado."
> **"§ 3°** Considera-se por prazo indeterminado todo contrato que suceder, dentro de seis meses, a outro
> contrato, com ou sem determinação de prazo."

**A principal cannot defeat the indemnity by rolling fixed terms.**

## 2. ⚠ THE DUTY THAT CUTS AGAINST C1 — and it must not be glossed over

> **"Art. 28.** O representante comercial fica obrigado a fornecer ao representado… informações detalhadas
> sobre o andamento dos negócios a seu cargo, **devendo dedicar-se à representação, de modo a EXPANDIR os
> negócios do representado e PROMOVER os seus produtos.**"

**This is a statutory duty of active promotion, not passive order-taking.** Art. 35 makes breach of obligations
a just cause for termination without indemnity — so the art. 27(j) asset above is contingent on actually
working the territory.

> **The honest question for any agency candidate is therefore not "can she collect on art. 31?" but "can she
> discharge art. 28's duty to EXPAND and PROMOTE entirely in writing?"** If a category's orders are generated
> by relationship selling, art. 28 and C1 are in direct conflict and the candidate is dead. **This is the screen
> that any agency idea has to clear first.**

## 3. THE OTHER OPERATIVE TERMS

| Article | Effect |
|---|---|
| **27(d), (e)** | The contract must state the **zone**, and whether zone exclusivity is guaranteed. **Get (e) in writing — do not rely on art. 31's silence default when it is free to make it express** |
| **27(f)** | Retribution depends on actual realisation of business **"e recebimento, ou não, pelo representado, dos valores respectivos"** — ★ **so it is expressly contractible that she is paid whether or not the principal has been paid.** Negotiate this |
| **27(i)** | Whether she represents that principal exclusively — i.e. whether she may carry other lines. **Keep this non-exclusive: multiple principals is how the arithmetic reaches the target** |
| **32** | The right to commission arises **on payment of the orders**; commissions payable **by the 15th of the following month** |
| **33 §1** | ⚠ **No commission at all if non-payment results from the buyer's insolvency**, or if the buyer unwinds the deal. **She carries customer credit risk she cannot assess** |
| **33 §2** | Commissions paid monthly, the principal issuing the account **against copies of the invoices sent to buyers** — so she receives documentary evidence monthly without asking |
| **29** | She may not grant discounts or concessions without express authorisation — **which is protective: it removes negotiation from her role, and negotiation is the part that requires a conversation** |

## 4. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SETTLE

**Settled:** the anti-disintermediation right is real, is the statutory default, survives the principal selling
direct, and generates an indemnity that compounds over the whole relationship. Combined with **RIR/2018 art. 162
§2º III** (agents who do not trade on their own account are not *equiparados à pessoa jurídica*) and
**STJ REsp 1.678.551/DF** (no CORE registration needed), **the legal vehicle is clean.**

**NOT settled, and these are the live questions:**
1. **Art. 28's promotion duty versus C1.** The whole thing turns on finding a category where orders are
   generated by written specification, catalogue, RFQ or reorder rather than by relationship.
2. **Why a principal appoints her at all.** *"I will email people"* is not an answer.
3. **Commission rates.** Every figure found so far (3–15% general, 3–5% industrial, 5–10% machinery) comes from
   **Brazilian accountancy blogs — SECONDARY, and the evidence standard says vendor and SEO content is not
   evidence a number exists.** **UNVERIFIED. An actual representation contract, a trade-association schedule or
   a tender document is needed.**
4. **Customer credit risk under art. 33 §1** — she is unpaid if the buyer goes insolvent, and she has no way to
   assess that.

**Arithmetic, for reference:** US$11.100/month at 5% commission requires **~US$222.000/month of territory
volume**; at 10%, **~US$111.000/month.** *(~US$1,3–2,7M/year — a plausible single industrial territory, which is
why this shape survives the production ceiling that killed the S1 family: her hours never make the units.)*
