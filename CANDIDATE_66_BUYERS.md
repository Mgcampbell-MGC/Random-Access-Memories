## 3. The channel answer — and it is not what I had planned

**Almost every dense community forbids exactly what a faceless newcomer needs to do:**

- **r/ecommerce and r/dropship** — self-promotion removed outright.
- **r/AmazonSeller** — confined to one pinned Community Promotion Post; violation = **account and site ban**.
- **Shopify Community and Amazon Seller Central** — support forums. *Pitching into a thread where
  someone just lost 1.500 inventory values reads as predatory.*
- **eCommerceFuel** — the highest-quality buyers, **gated by revenue: $1M+/yr, or $2.5M+ if
  Amazon-only, plus $199–299/month.** Identity-gated, not merely paywalled. **She cannot buy in.**
- **Trade shows and associations** (K5, NRF, IMRG, BEVH, FEVAD) — pay-to-play as an exhibitor.

**So the seeding plan I wrote — post before-and-afters in seller groups, Kaching-style — is largely
dead.** That is a real correction and it was the plan's weakest link.

### What replaces it

> community's own search traffic looks for the answer.**

> **Every quote in §1 is a permanently indexed, Google-crawled thread describing a live, unresolved,
> technical failure in the buyer's exact words. That is free query data, harvested from the buyer's
