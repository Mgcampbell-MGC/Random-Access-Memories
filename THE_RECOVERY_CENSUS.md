# THE RECOVERY-INDUSTRY INVERSION — 24 industries censused, 20 Aug 2026

**The inverted hunt: start from every contingency-fee recovery industry (proven demand) and ask what half of
its evidence a third party could hold. Result: A NULL THAT STRENGTHENS CANDIDATE #1. Across 24 industries,
exactly ONE needs an input that is a transient third-party public fact nobody archives — and it is the seed.**

> ### **The working-day ledger's structure — refund-side buyers + a schedule whose POSTER is not the INVOICE ISSUER + a bounded capture universe — occurred exactly once in 24 industries. The next hunt should not look for another recovery industry; it should look for other POSTER ≠ INVOICE-ISSUER splits inside industries that are already refund-side.**

---

## 1. ★★ THE THREE NEW LAWS

> ### ★★ THE GO-FORWARD SPLIT — run FIRST on any "audit/recovery" industry.
> **Most audit industries are not recovery industries: their fee is a percentage of FUTURE SAVINGS, not past
> refunds** — verified on the firms' own pages (Merchant Cost Consulting: *"our fee is a percentage of the
> savings we generate"*; O'Connor: *"half the property tax savings for the current year"*; waste/utility
> auditors: 12–60-month savings shares). **A firm paid on the future buys no history.** Only refund-side
> industries can ever want an archive — and D&D recovery (25–35% of money clawed back) is pure refund-side.
> **That is not incidental to why the seed survived; it is why.**

> ### ★★ THE INCORPORATED-SCHEDULE COROLLARY — the other side of the mandatory-field self-supply law.
> **When a counterparty's own invoice incorporates its own posted schedule by reference, the POSTER archives
> the schedule — it has to, to defend its own invoices.** Three instances in one day:
> **(1) WM publishes its own 52-week historical fuel/environmental surcharge table** — PDF extracted and read,
> weekly rows back a full year. **(2) AT&T runs its own Service Guide Archive**, and its state guidebooks are
> revision-numbered dated filings (*"7th Revised Sheet 25… Effective: June 12, 2024"*, PDF read).
> **(3) 47 CFR 42.10 makes carriers post rates; 42.11 — SAME PART — makes them retain 2,5 years, producible in
> 10 business days.** The retention-duty corollary, exactly.
> **The only escape is the seed's: the gate schedule is an input to the CARRIER'S invoice, but is posted by the
> TERMINAL — the party that would need to archive it to defend an invoice is a different company from the party
> that posts it.**

> ### ★ THE DSAR FRONT DOOR — no third-party evidence market can exist under free statutory discovery.
> In any consumer-vs-firm dispute, **GDPR art. 15 makes the defendant hand the claimant the records free within
> a month.** Kills the entire consumer-redress evidence family in one line.

## 2. THE CENSUS — 24 industries, causes of death only

**Refund-side industries (the only ones that could buy history), and why each still dies:**
duty drawback — claimant's own entries, CBP holds the record (claimant-evidence + platform-arbiter) ·
securities class-action filing — trade history is the claimant's; ISS SCAS has tracked **13.000+ cases since
1996** (head start); pool ≈ 8–10 firms worldwide · unclaimed property — a **continuing present-tense state**
(two-dates) + the state is holder AND judge + **CA CCP §1582 caps fees at 10%** · EU261 — **AirHelp 35%+15%
verified**, but Cirium holds 20 years (the named head-start case) · Amazon FBA — **GETIDA 25% verified**, and
Amazon is judge AND record-keeper (the platform-arbiter archetype) + C4 · medical underpayment — 20–35%
refund-side, but **both numbers of an underpayment are in the claimant's own contract and 835 remittance**
(two-dates), MRF universe is terabytes (speculative capture), Turquoise tracks 219 payers (head start).

**Savings-side industries (go-forward split — never buyers of history):** telecom/TEM · utility audit · waste
audit · property tax appeals · merchant fee audit.

**Killed by the incorporated-schedule corollary specifically:** waste surcharges (WM's own 52-week table) ·
telecom rates (AT&T's own archive + 47 CFR 42.11).

**Others:** R&D/ERC — the product IS manufacturing claimant conduct evidence, and the IRS attacks the fee model ·
royalty audit — counterparty-private books via contractual audit clause + expert credential · WC premium —
rates filed with bureaus (gazette) · cargo claims — delivery-receipt exceptions are claimant conduct (the port
kill's twin) · VAT reclaim — claimant paperwork, mature industry · federal grants — **2 CFR 200 restricts the
fee model itself** · CAM/lease audit — counterparty books · chargeback representment — claimant conduct +
card-network arbiter · consumer CMCs — the DSAR front door · auto diminished value — millions of listings/day
(speculative capture) + Marketcheck/vAuto (head start) · AP recovery — client ERP access (C4) · pharmacy MAC —
PBM is arbiter and record-keeper; CMS archives every weekly NADAC file.

**The near-miss worth recording:** the waste-surcharge ledger was the closest structural rhyme — ~5 pages/week,
perfect capture arithmetic — and died three independent ways (poster publishes its own history · the surcharge
is a formula on the DOE diesel index, gazetted forever · the buyers are savings-side). **Its residual test, if
anyone re-opens it: read WM's standard service agreement dispute window; if ≤12 months, the published 52 weeks
covers every claimable period and the idea is zero.**
