## ★★ KILL 2 — THE DISPUTE-IS-THE-PRODUCT LAW *(new, and reusable)*

**In any pay-per-lead or pay-per-outcome model, the operator's core recurring workload is not matching — it
is ARBITRATING WHETHER THE LEAD WAS REAL.** Measured on the category leader: GetNinjas, Brazil's largest
pay-per-lead marketplace, whose public complaint record is about **money, not match quality** — *"cobrança
por lead inválido e falta de reembolso"*, *"leads desbloqueados sem retorno"*, *"dados de contato
falsos/inexistentes… o suporte se recusou a realizar o estorno."*

**Every one of those is a conversation with a small business about money it believes was taken from it.
C1 therefore fails not at the edges but at the exact moment revenue begins.** *(Generalises
`THE ONBOARDING LAW`: written-only is compatible with SELLING, and not with BEING INVOICED AGAINST.)*

**★ THE CHEAP TEST IT GIVES YOU: before proposing any marketplace or success-fee model, read the SELLER-SIDE
complaint record of the category leader. It measures the disputes-per-transaction workload for free.**

## ★★ KILL 3 — THE SELF-REPORTED-CONVERSION TRAP *(new)*

**A success fee on an outcome only the counterparty can observe is uncollectable.** The R$300–500 "verified
purchase" tier is verified by whom? Her options are **trust** (they underreport, and the incentive is
permanent and one-directional) or **standing access to their systems (C4)**. There is no third.

> **Corollary that VALIDATES the board:** this is why `THE_WORKING_DAY_LEDGER` must stay priced at a **fixed**
> $150–350/month + $49–99 per extract and must **never** convert to a share of recovered demurrage — despite
> the buyers themselves working on 25–35% success fees. **Sell the input at a fixed price; never take a share
> of an outcome you cannot see.**

## KILL 4 — settled law, applied

- **`THE SOURCING/REPUTATION SPLIT`.** COROA is definitionally *"which vendor should I use"* — the half she
  loses permanently. Her half is *"what is the answer."* **Settled; not re-litigated.**
- **`THE WHATSAPP LAW`.** Only *escritórios de contabilidade* are structurally written-only in Brazil. **Hair
  studios are maximally WhatsApp-native.** The 30-day test's first step — five providers signing a written
  per-lead contract — is also its first casualty.
- **The expert veto.** *"Returns up to three APPROPRIATE verified providers"* is a judgment about a
  medical-adjacent condition, for chemotherapy patients: the highest-trust category that exists.
- **C5.** *"Recruit 30 genuine customers through search ads."* C5 explicitly does not buy paid acquisition.
- **`THE ATTENTION TRILEMMA`.** Stated acquisition is a public directory plus a newsletter — exit 2
  (affiliate/referral), closed this morning.
- **Two-sided cold start.** Marketplaces are cold-started with capital, a sales force, or the founder's
  network. **C5, C3 and C7 remove all three.**
