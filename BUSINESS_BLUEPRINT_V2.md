## 2. The Airtight Niche & Offer

### The exact micro-niche

TITLE FORGE sells **finished, liability-bearing land-title work product** — certified patent-to-present runsheets, mineral-severance reports, heirship research, and counsel-supervised curative programs — to the buyers racing through the US energy, transmission, and data-center land rush. It is not software and not day-rate labor: fixed-price, SLA-backed, E&O-insured deliverables from a vision-LLM pipeline with 100% instrument-level human verification.

### The buyer and the quantified pain

Utility-scale solar/storage/transmission developers (2,600+ GW queued in interconnection queues), data-center site teams, and E&P/mineral buyers all need proven chain of title on rural tracts on the **critical path to site control and financing**. Today that comes from landmen billing $400–600/day and producing 1–3 tracts/week — **$300k–600k and 3–6 months per 200-tract project**, against deadlines costing $10k–300k/day of delay. The abstractor workforce is aging out exactly as demand peaks. The budget already exists and already flows to day-rate brokerages that structurally cannot automate without destroying their own billable-day P&L; TITLE FORGE redirects it. The buyer universe is publicly enumerable: **~500 named developers + ~200 mineral funds/E&P land departments + ~40 energy project law firms**, rammed in trust-barrier order — funds/E&P first (2–6 week cycles, months 4–14), developers second (6–12 month MSA cycles, months 10–24), law firms as channel and curative fulfillment partner (month 18+). All work is **Texas-county only at launch**; OK/NM orders are declined-and-waitlisted until certificates/licenses are held.

### The Flood-Test Moats

Honestly labeled, with time-to-replicate stated:

1. **The compounding own-capture plant — the core moat.** Three layers with different replication economics: **(i)** raw image capture is *not* a moat — a $10M copycat can blanket-scan 100 counties in months. **(ii)** **Adjudicated chain graphs plus per-county frozen gold sets** are the defensible layer: they are produced *only* by delivering paid certified work — ~150–200 delivered tracts per county before survey-overlap yields the 60–80% reuse rate that cuts repeat COGS by 60–80%. 4. **Certification standing — relabeled honestly.** In year 1 this is an operational prerequisite (disclosed-AI E&O, credentialed signers), *not* a moat — a funded copycat binds comparable coverage in weeks. The true regulatory barriers (OK/ND/NE abstract-plant certificates) are deliberately staged year-2/3 capital projects; the flood-test defense stands without them.

## 3. The AI Infrastructure Stack

**Design principle:** certification correctness is conjunctive — at 99% per-instrument accuracy, a 120-instrument chain is fully correct only ~30% of the time (0.99^120) — and the killer error class is **omission** (the reservation the model never extracted), to which a diff UI is structurally blind. Verification is therefore redesigned per instrument class:

- **Typed/digitized instruments:** field diff *plus* a model-generated clause inventory (reservations, exceptions, liens, habendum qualifiers) that the reviewer affirmatively checks against highlighted source-image regions — ~1.5 min/instrument.
- **Handwritten pre-1970 instruments:** mandatory **full read** with model transcription overlaid on the image — 4–8 min each (5 min budgeted), never 45 seconds.
- **Cross-vendor ensemble** (Anthropic + Google vision stacks) so disagreement correlates with genuine error rather than shared misreads.
- **Canary seeding:** known-defect instruments injected into every reviewer's queue; per-reviewer catch-rate SLAs gate hiring, retention, and per-shift throughput.
- **Omission recall is the gold-set metric**, measured against full-manual re-abstraction of a random 5% of delivered tracts per county.
- **Cached-tier certification defined:** cache entries are verified artifacts with named signer, date, and pinned model version; repeat certifications re-verify all new instruments plus a salted re-review sample, with written carrier sign-off on the cache-reliance model as part of the month-1 E&O gate.

Pipeline: capture manifests with SHA-256 hashing and deterministic index-coverage manifests; small-model triage; structured extraction; Soundex/nickname name-variant recall; **deterministic chain-graph and interest math in code, never an LLM**. Capture is physically capped at 30–40 tracts/mo per courthouse, so the 500 scanned tracts/mo ladder requires 14–16 producing counties. Runner ops are formalized (paired first visits, salted re-pull audits, per-page pay plus completion bonus, clerk-relations protocol, state-archive/GLO microfilm backup). **Data provenance rule, audited:** nothing enters the plant except own-capture images and direct-from-county copies at statutory fees; Enverus/Tracts/Landman.ai are lead-targeting and per-order cross-checks only, never stored.

**Fulfillment cost arithmetic — per-tract COGS at $70/hr loaded QC labor:**

| Tier | Compute (±3x band) | Acquisition | QC labor | Rework/OH | **COGS** | Price (mo 36) | GM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scanned first touch | $12–18 | $300 expensed (remainder of ~$540/tract capture capitalized into county plant seed) | 6.0 hr = $420 | $96 | **$830** | $1,950 | 57% |
| Digitized first touch | $8–12 | $85 blended | 2.0 hr = $140 | $50 | **$285** | $385 | 26% |
| Plant-cached repeat | $3–6 | $10 | 1.0 hr = $70 | $26 | **$110** | $450 | 76% |

Review budgets: scanned ~6.0 hr/tract (≈40 handwritten × 5 min + 80 typed × 1.5 min), digitized 2.0 hr, cached 1.0 hr. Compute runs ~0.8–2% of revenue (~$17k/mo at month 36; even a 3x miss stays under 3%). The durable cost saving is **chain reuse** — fewer instruments processed per repeat order — not prompt-cache pricing, which is fragile. Eval upkeep is a budgeted 0.5 FTE running a challenger model continuously against every frozen county suite.

**Abstractor supply program (the real rate limiter, treated as one):** a two-tier workforce — reviewers verify, senior signers certify, signer premium in COGS. A 90-day paid apprenticeship converts title-company escrow/paralegal staff and landmen into reviewers; remote review draws OK/LA/NM pools; equity/retention packages for the first five signers; 1.5 reviewers recruited per county launched on a 3-month lead. Catch-rate and time-per-instrument are first-class ops metrics from month 4.
