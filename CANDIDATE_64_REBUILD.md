# CANDIDATE 64 — GROUND-UP REBUILD

**"Antes de Você Chegar" → "BEFORE YOU"**
**14 Aug 2026. Rebuilt from the mechanism up, not patched.**

**Verdict: DO NOT KILL. But the version that survives is not the version that was proposed.**
**The clinic goes. Brazil goes. The book stays, and it gets 9× more valuable.**

---

## 1. What the rebuild found

The proposed business needs **411 books a month** to reach R$30.000. The rebuilt business needs
**45**. Same object, same labour, same founder. The difference is entirely **who is buying and in
what currency**, and it was found by asking one question the original never asked:

> **What is the proven form of "prompted questions → written book → printed hardcover"?**

It has a name, it is a large business, and it has never been pointed at pregnancy.

### StoryWorth is the proof of model, and it is the thing nobody mentioned

[StoryWorth](https://memoirji.com/blog/storyworth-pricing-2026-buyer-guide/) sells **$59 / $109 /
$199 a year**: weekly prompted questions by email, answers compiled, hardcover book delivered.
[Remento](https://help.remento.co/en/articles/8365892-remento-s-pricing-guide) is **$99/year** with
a book credit. [Memoirji's "Remember"](https://memoirji.com/blog/remento-vs-storyworth-vs-memoirji/)
is **$79/year with weekly prompts over WhatsApp**, voice or text, hardcover with QR codes.

Three things follow, and each one is load-bearing:

1. **The mechanic is proven at scale in English.** People pay $59–199 for "answer some questions,
   receive a beautiful printed book." It is not a hypothesis.
2. **It is bought as a gift.** StoryWorth's entire business is children buying for parents. The
   giver never writes a word — the recipient does. **That solves the hardest problem in the
   original design.**
3. **Every single one is aimed at elders and legacy. Not one has a pregnancy edition.** The vertical
   is empty, and the search for a done-for-you *written* pregnancy book returned nothing in English
   either — [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/market/pregnancy_keepsakes) and
   [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Hard-Cover-Pregnancy-Journal-Personalized/dp/B087PPTF8Y) are wall
   to wall **blank prompted journals the mother fills in herself**, exactly as in Brazil.

**The category is proven. The vertical is empty. That combination has not appeared once in 64
candidates.**

## 2. The three facts that made me move the business out of Brazil

### a) The same book is worth 2–3× more in English, for identical labour

R$349 ≈ **US$60**. That is StoryWorth's *entry* price. The US market pays **$99–199** for this
exact mechanic — R$580–1.160.

She writes English fluently. **In this business the English writing IS the product**, not a sales
channel bolted onto it. It is the one candidate in 64 where her strongest skill is the deliverable.

### b) Fulfilment is completely solved, and it is free — verified

The Brazilian version rested on an unverified lab, an unknown reseller discount, and an unanswered
drop-ship question. [Lulu's Print API](https://www.lulu.com/sell/sell-on-your-site/print-api)
answers all of it, in writing, on the record:

> *"white-label services"* · *"no minimum print runs"* · *"the API is completely free to set up and
> use, and you only pay for printing and fulfillment"* · *"drop shipping to customers worldwide"*

- **No inventory. No packing. No post office. No lab negotiation.** The physical-goods veto is
  satisfied properly, not conditionally.
- **The print order is an API call**, which deletes 5 minutes of manual work per book forever.
- **Verified cost anchor:** a **100-page** full-colour premium hardcover runs
  [**~$32.50**](https://podvector.ai/articles/print-on-demand/costs-and-suppliers/lulu-print-on-demand-pricing),
  plus a **$1.75 per-order fulfilment fee**. A 40-page book is materially less — binding base plus
  ~40 pages of premium stock puts it around **$17–22**, which is the **one number still to confirm
  on Lulu's own calculator** before anything is priced.

Compare with Brazil, where the print cost swung from R$80 to R$194 across the same lab's price list
and took the margin negative at the top of the range.

### c) The clinic channel was never going to hold

R$70 × 5 orders = **R$350/month to a clinic**, and **zero** to the receptionist who actually hands
over the QR code. That is not a channel, it is a favour, and favours decay by month three. The
original design's one genuine asset was structurally weak — so losing it in the move costs less
than it appears.

## 3. THE REBUILT BUSINESS

### The object

**A 40-page premium-colour hardcover that arrives already written.** Not a journal. Not an album.
A short book about one specific pregnancy, in the mother's own voice, that she did not have to
write.

### The buyer, and the mechanic that makes it work

**Bought as a baby-shower gift by the mother's mother, sister, or closest friend. Redeemed by the
expectant mother.**

This is StoryWorth's mechanic, and it resolves three problems at once:

| Problem in the original | How the gift mechanic kills it |
|---|---|
| Price anchored against R$96 blank journals | Gift budgets are a different mental account. $149 is an ordinary close-family baby-shower gift; $149 is an extravagant self-purchase |
| The giver can't answer the questionnaire | She doesn't. She buys a card. **The mother redeems and answers** |
| No warm moment of purchase without a clinic | **The baby shower is the moment**, and it needs no partner, no QR code, and nobody's permission |

The gift card is the product on the shelf. The book is what it becomes.

### The flow — where every minute goes

1. **Giver buys** a gift card on the site or Etsy. *Sol's time: zero.*
2. **Mother redeems**, uploads her own cropped ultrasound and bump photos, answers **28 questions**
   in a form. *Sol's time: zero.*
3. **AI drafts** the book from her answers, in her register. *Sol's time: ~6 min supervising and
   editing.*
4. **Mother approves the TEXT ONLY** — a plain page, no layout. **One approval gate. One revision
   round included.** *Sol's time: ~6 min.*
5. **Layout is deterministic** — fixed template, her images, approved text. **No layout revisions
   are offered.** *Sol's time: ~5 min.*
6. **Lulu API prints and ships direct** in white-label packaging. *Sol's time: ~1 min.*

> **~18–20 minutes per book, and the two time sinks are structurally capped rather than hoped
> away.** Text-only approval is the single most important design decision in this document — it is
> what stops a keepsake business from becoming an unpaid design service.

### The questionnaire is the actual intellectual property

Not the printing, not the software, not the AI. **28 questions that make an exhausted person
produce publishable material in twenty minutes of typing.** That is a writing-and-structure problem,
which is precisely the work she asked for.

Eleven sections: *Before you* (the parents' story) · *The day we found out* · *The secret months* ·
*Your name* — what was chosen, what was rejected, what it means · *Seeing you* — the scan moments ·
*Carrying you* — cravings, fears, the body · *The people waiting* · *Letters* from each parent ·
*The room* · *The last weeks* · **a blank final spread for the birth photo.**

**The AI rule, absolute: every sentence traceable to an answer.** No invention, no medical reading,
no generated faces. It shapes what she wrote; it does not add.

### The design decision that makes it look expensive

Ultrasound scans are thermal paper photographed on a phone. They are ugly at full-bleed and no
amount of print quality fixes that.

> **Scans are placed small, framed, in a single duotone treatment — never full-bleed. The hero
> image on the scan spread is a clean line-trace of her actual scan.** Bump photos carry the
> full-bleed pages.

Traced from her real image, never generated. This turns the object's biggest production weakness
into its signature look, and it is a decision only someone doing the design work would make.

## 4. The arithmetic, rebuilt

**Price: $149** core edition, 40pp premium-colour hardcover.

| Line | |
|---|---|
| Price | **$149** |
| Print + fulfilment fee + US shipping | **−$28** *(to confirm; anchored on $32,50 at 100pp)* |
| Payment processing (2,9% + $0,30) | **−$4,60** |
| **Contribution per book** | **≈ $116 ≈ R$673** |

### The second copy is the best margin in the business

**The grandmother wants one too.** A duplicate is **zero extra labour** and ~$25 of print.
Sold at **$69**, it adds **$44**. At a 40% attach rate that is **+$17,60 average**, lifting
contribution to **≈$133 ≈ R$770.**

### What that does to the ceiling — the whole point of the rebuild

| | Books for R$12.000/mo | Books for R$30.000/mo | Her hours/month at R$30.000 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Brazil, R$349, clinic** | 164 | **411** | **171 h — impossible solo** |
| **English, $149 + attach** | **16** | **39** | **~13 h** |

**Ten times fewer units. Thirteen hours a month at the three-year target.**

The ceiling that killed the Brazilian version does not exist here. R$30.000 leaves **~117 hours a
month** for marketing, product and a second SKU — and the second SKU is obvious: **the first-year
book, sold to a customer who already loves the first one.** That is LTV, which nothing in this
record has ever had.

## 5. The honest problem: Brazil had a channel and this does not

**This is the real risk and it should not be dressed up.** The clinic QR was weak, but it was
*something*. The rebuilt business starts cold, and the seeding law from the marketplace case
studies applies directly:

> *Zero documented cases of a cold listing reaching $1k MRR on in-marketplace search alone. Every
> winner was seeded by hand through written, faceless channels.*

So the seed has to be designed, not assumed. Four channels, all written, all faceless, all
near-zero cost:

1. **Pinterest first.** It is a search engine, not a feed; pins compound for years; pregnancy and
   baby-shower gifting is one of its largest categories; and it needs no face, no video and no
   audience. **Making beautiful pins is writing, designing and organising** — her exact list.
2. **Etsy as a second storefront**, not the strategy. It is where "custom keepsake" intent already
   lives, listings cost $0,20, and faceless shops are the norm.
3. **Baby-shower gift-guide outreach.** Cold written email to gift-guide bloggers and listicle
   writers with photographs of the sample. Faceless, free, and evergreen once placed.
4. **Group seeding, Kaching-style** — bump groups and forums, where rules permit, answering rather
   than advertising.

**She cannot buy her way in.** At a few hundred dollars total she gets no meaningful paid
acquisition. Month 3 at zero is expected and fine. **Month 8 is the real exam**, against her own
bar of R$3.000 being a failure — and at this contribution R$3.000 is only **4–5 books a month.**

## 7. What can still kill it

Ranked by how likely they are to actually fire.

1. **The cold start.** No channel exists on day one and Pinterest compounds slowly. This is the
   single most likely cause of death, and it is a *distribution* death, not a product death.
2. **Print quality at 40pp.** Lulu is a book printer, not a photo lab. If a bump photo prints
   flat and muddy, the object does not justify $149. **Testable for $30.** Blurb is the fallback.
3. **The voice.** She is writing intimate American English about an experience she has not had.
   AI plus a strong questionnaire gets most of the way; the last 10% is craft. **Testable with
   three sample books.**
4. **Revision creep.** If mothers average three rounds instead of one, 20 minutes becomes 60 and
   the ceiling returns. The text-only gate is the defence; it has to hold.
5. **StoryWorth or Remento adds a pregnancy edition.** A feature for them, a business for her.
   Real, but they are branded around elders and legacy, and the timing is not imminent.
6. **$149 is a considered purchase with a two-week production time.** Not impulse. The gift
   framing carries it, but conversion will be slower than a $30 keepsake.

## 8. THE TEST — under $200, about three weeks

**Not 40 clinics. Not an app. Three books and a seed.**

| # | Action | Cost | What it settles |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Build one complete book** for a real volunteer, recruited in writing, and order it from Lulu | ~$30 | **Print quality, true print cost, production time, and her real minutes-per-book — all four in one motion** |
| 2 | Run Lulu's own price calculator at 40pp and 60pp premium colour hardcover | $0 | The last unverified number in the model |
| 3 | Build two more books from different volunteers | ~$60 | Whether the questionnaire produces good writing from *strangers*, and whether 20 min/book holds on repetition |
| 4 | Photograph the samples; Etsy listing + a one-page storefront | ~$20 | The object photographs well enough to sell |
| 5 | 40–60 Pinterest pins over three weeks | $0 | Whether the seed moves |
| 6 | Cold-write 20 baby-shower gift-guide bloggers | $0 | Whether the written faceless channel opens |

### Kill criteria

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Print quality unacceptable at 40pp, and Blurb no better | **Kill.** The object is the business |
| Fully-loaded time per book still >45 min at the third book | **Reprice to $249 or kill.** The ceiling is back |
| Confirmed print cost pushes contribution under $70 | **Reprice, don't kill** |
| Average revisions >2 rounds across three books | **Restructure the gate, then re-test once** |
| Zero sales by week 10 with the storefront live and pins compounding | **Kill.** No channel, and she cannot buy one |
| Any buyer requires a call | Will not happen in this market. If it does, **kill** |

## 9. Recommendation

**Do not kill it. Kill the Brazilian clinic version, and build this one.**

It is the first candidate in 64 to clear every hard constraint, the first with a proven model behind
it, the first with solved fulfilment, the first with a plausible second product and real LTV, and
the first that reaches R$30.000 without a single employee.

**What it does not have is a channel** — which is exactly the same gap as the other 63, with one
difference: at $116–133 a book, **the seed only has to produce four sales a month to clear the
month-8 bar**, instead of forty-one.

The next move is one book, one volunteer, $30. It answers four questions at once, and until it is
printed and in a hand, everything above is design rather than evidence.

### One open naming item

*Antes de Você Chegar* does not travel. The English edition needs a name — **BEFORE YOU** is the
strongest candidate as a cover title, but it is generic and hard to protect. Worth 30 minutes and a
domain check before the storefront goes up, and not worth blocking the sample book on.
