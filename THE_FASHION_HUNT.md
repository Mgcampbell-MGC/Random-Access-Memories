**Fourteen candidates in her own industry. NOTHING SURVIVES. But the causes are unusually clean, and one
finding reframes how her background should be used at all.**

## 1. ★★ THE FINDING THAT MATTERS MOST

> ### **HER MODELLING KNOWLEDGE IS DIAGNOSTIC, NOT COMMERCIAL.**
>
> It was genuinely load-bearing in this hunt — it is what identifies usage-rights overrun, agency payment
> mechanics and sample-size economics as real, unwatched problems that an outsider would never see.
>
> **And every one of them turns out to have a buyer pool of ~75 firms, no published email, and an incentive not
> to act.** The knowledge tells her where the gaps are; the gaps sit in a market too small and too relational to
> sell into without speaking.
>
> **That is not a failure of the knowledge. It is the honest shape of the industry she came from — and it argues
> for using her domain affinity as a SCREEN ON CANDIDATES FOUND ELSEWHERE, rather than as the hunting ground.**

## 2. ★★ THE THREE STRUCTURAL LAWS THIS HUNT PRODUCED

> ### ★ THE 75-FIRM CEILING
> New York's **Fashion Workers Act** registry — a genuine clock-start, registration opened 21 Dec 2025, deadline
> 19 Jun 2026 — is public at `data.ny.gov/resource/hder-iq9y.json`. **Pulled and counted: 75 rows, all Active**
> (71 model management companies, 3 groups, 1 blank). First row read in full: *1 Model Management, LLC / dba ONE
> Management / phone 2125055545 / 42 Bond Street / issue 2026-07-29 / expires 2028-07-29.*
> **The schema has `phone`. It has NO email column.** NYC's older register: 353 employment-agency premises
> licences, `contact_phone`, **no email either**.
>
> **At US$11.100/month, 75 firms means $148/month from every single one, zero churn, 100% penetration. Any
> product whose buyer is "model agencies" is dead on arithmetic before a single constraint applies.**
> *(And `THE GATE/CONTACT ANTI-CORRELATION` predicted it exactly: the FWA register carries the heaviest money
> gate in the domain — $500–700 fee plus a **$50.000 surety bond** — and publishes the least contact data. For a
> founder who can never make a phone call, a phone-only register is not a buyer list. It is a wall.)*

> ### ★★ THE DISCOVERY/DETAIL SPLIT — a marketplace can be readable at the ITEM and unreadable at the INDEX
> Measured today with a browser UA: **Amazon product detail page → HTTP 200, 1.087.788 bytes, real content, no
> CAPTCHA. Amazon search page → HTTP 200, 2,6 MB, and ZERO `data-asin` tokens.** eBay 403 · Walmart *"Robot or
> human?"* · Mercari 403 · DHgate Cloudflare 403 · AliExpress a 2.371-byte shell. **Only Poshmark returned real
> listings** (5,9 MB, 144 listing links, 48 sellers, 48 prices).
>
> **This is a third state, neither open nor blocked, and it is fatal: AN ARCHIVE NEEDS ENUMERATION, NOT
> RETRIEVAL. Test discovery, never detail, before believing a marketplace is accessible.**

> ### ★ THE TALENT-SIDE INCENTIVE INVERSION — generalising `THE KNOWLEDGE-LIABILITY TEST`
> The party holding the contract (the agency) sells to the party breaching it (the brand). **A detector that
> works costs the agency its client.**
> ### **So ask not only *"does the BUYER get money or a problem"* but *"does the BUYER'S CUSTOMER get a problem?"* — the second suppresses demand just as hard.**
