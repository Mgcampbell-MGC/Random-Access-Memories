# THE GROUND HUNT — the lens that was never opened, 2 Sep 2026
**Founder: *"YES SHE IS WILLING TO LEAVE THE HOUSE."* That reverses a line this file has carried since the
beginning — *"verification is closed to her: a physical asset needs a body in a place… she can have neither."*
**Half of that just became false.** `RESURRECTION_AUDIT` named "Brazilian eyes on the ground" as **the single
most reopenable item in the record** and it has never been hunted. This is that hunt.**

---

## 1. THE LENS — and it is the inverse of every hunt before it

Every previous hunt asked *"what business is permitted by her constraints?"* and then checked whether she could
reach a buyer. **That search is structurally doomed**, because six of the eight sources of commercial advantage
are switched off for her: expertise, credential, audience, capital, proprietary data, and scale.

> ### **THIS HUNT ASKS THE OPPOSITE QUESTION: what can ONLY be done by a body standing in São Paulo — and who abroad is compelled to pay hard currency for it?**
>
> **Physical presence is the one advantage she holds that cannot be copied by a better prompt, undercut by a
> contractor in Manila, or outspent by a funded competitor.** It is also the only one of her assets that every
> previous design deliberately engineered away, by insisting she never leave her desk.

**A body provides exactly six things.** The hunt runs each against *"who is compelled to buy it, in hard
currency, on a schedule they do not control?"*

| | What a body supplies | The foreign buyer who needs it |
|---|---|---|
| **WITNESS** | establishing that something is true at a place and time | anyone whose only informant is the party being evaluated |
| **ACQUIRE** | obtaining a physical thing | anyone who must hold the object to know the truth |
| **CAPTURE** | making a dated record of a physical fact | anyone who must prove a state of affairs later |
| **RECEIVE** | being an address | anyone shipping into Brazil |
| **ATTEND** | being present at an event | anyone bidding, inspecting or sourcing remotely |
| **INTRODUCE** | local relationships | *(closed — `THE MARGIN LAW` caps goods commission at 3–6%)* |

## 2. ★★★ THE FINDING — the first three all have the SAME buyer, and it is a buyer she understands

**A foreign consumer brand with Brazilian exposure and no Brazilian body.** It cannot see its own market, cannot
hold its own product, and cannot prove its own facts. Three separate compelled needs, one customer, one trip
into the city.

### ★ G1 — SHELF TRUTH · *the strongest candidate this hunt produced*
**A foreign brand sells into Brazil through an importer or distributor. Its only source of truth about the
Brazilian market IS that distributor** — the party being evaluated. Practitioner guidance on international
sales says the quiet part out loud: *"it should be agreed with your importer how often **his team** record the
prices and in which format this needs to be reported to you."*

> ### **`THE SPLIT TEST` PASSES CLEANLY, AND ALMOST NOTHING EVER DOES.** The party on the hook for Brazilian performance is the **brand**. The party generating the input is the **distributor**, who is being marked on it. **Two different companies, opposed interests, and the fact is only obtainable by standing in the shop.**

**What she does:** walks a defined route of São Paulo retailers each month — is the product there, at what
price, in what condition, facing which competitor, is the promotional material current, is stock rotating, is
it appearing in channels the contract forbids. Photographs, timestamps, a structured report.
**No opinion is offered. She reports what she saw.** *(Which is why the expert veto does not bite: witnessing
is the one professional activity that requires no credential and cannot be done remotely.)*
**Her fit is the best of anything in this file**: consumer brands, beauty and retail, visual judgement, out of
the house, in good shops, a written deliverable, and roughly two days a month of walking.
**Open:** the price. Commodity mystery-shopping runs US$15–50 a visit; a brand-level monthly audit with a
written report is a different product and its price is quoted, never published — **which `THE
LEGIBILITY–MARGIN ANTI-CORRELATION` says is exactly where margin lives.**

### ★ G2 — PROOF OF USE · *the compelled one, and it is measured*
A Brazilian trademark can be cancelled for non-use, and the owner must **prove use in Brazil** or lose the
mark. **Measured in four weeks of the gazette: 79 *caducidade* notifications against foreign holders — roughly
a thousand a year, each with a deadline and a registration at stake.**
**The proof is physical**: the product on a Brazilian shelf, photographed and dated; packaging in Portuguese;
local invoices; advertising in market. **Gathering it is not legal work** — a law firm bills professional rates
for what is an errand with a camera. **She gathers; their lawyer argues.**

### ★ G3 — TEST PURCHASE DESK · *the one that completes GUARDA*
**This is precisely what VERA needed and could not do, and what GUARDA cannot do from a desk.** Brand
protection vendors sell test purchases as a named product across 140+ countries; every one of them needs a
person with a Brazilian address and a Brazilian card. She buys the suspect item, documents the seller, receives
it, photographs batch codes and packaging, keeps chain of custody, and ships it to the brand or its counsel.
**Sold wholesale to brand-protection firms and IP boutiques — few relationships, foreign payer.**
⚠ **Regulated goods (medicines, injectables) need a licensed partner. Consumer goods do not.**

### The three are one business
> **"Your eyes and hands in Brazil."** One buyer, one city, one trip. **G2 is compelled and episodic, G3 is
> event-driven, G1 is recurring** — and G1 is what turns the other two from projects into a retainer.

## 5. ⇒ THE NEXT STEP, and this time it is not "go and test a weak lead"
**The lens is new, the buyer is one buyer, and the only missing input is a price. That is a buyer-side quote
probe, not a hunt** — and it is the same instrument that has never once been run in five weeks:

> **Email fifteen brand-protection firms and IP boutiques, and fifteen foreign consumer brands with Brazilian
> distribution, as a prospective BUYER of these three services. Ask what they currently pay someone in Brazil
> to walk into a shop, buy a product, or photograph a shelf — and who does it for them today.**

**If the answer is "our distributor tells us" or "our law firm sends a trainee," the gap is real and priced by
nobody. If it is "we use [named vendor] at [price]," the ceiling is known in an afternoon** — and either way it
is the first number in this file that comes from the demand side of a market she can actually enter.
