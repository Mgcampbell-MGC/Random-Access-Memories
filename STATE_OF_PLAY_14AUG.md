## 3. THE LAWS THAT NOW DO THE WORK

These kill candidates in minutes that used to take days.

1. **THE ARBITRAGE LAW** *(newest, biggest)* — a Brazilian cost base is only an advantage against a
   buyer who does not have one. **Both survivors sell in hard currency; almost every corpse sold in
   reais.**
2. **THE B2B BUYER LAW** — a business buyer brings a negotiation, a diligence, an incumbent and a
   $5–50/month price anchor. A consumer brings none. **28 B2B models produced zero survivors.**
3. **THE VERIFIABILITY LAW** — a faceless seller can only sell work whose correctness the buyer can
   check faster than they could do it. *This generated candidate 67.*
4. **THE INTEGRATION TAX** — every AI-department product is sold on a call because it must be wired
   in. The only version she can sell is the one needing no wiring.
5. **LAW 1** — content costs nothing to produce, so incumbents give it away. **Stronger in English
   markets, not weaker.**
6. **THE TRANSPOSITION LAW** — a gap exists where two mature industries sit adjacent and neither
   crosses. *This generated candidate 64.*
7. **NEVER SELL THE AGENT, SELL THE OUTPUT** — any agent worth selling is free on GitHub within a
   year.
8. **CORRECTED LAW A1** — an incumbent kills only when free, or above ~20% measured adoption in the
   target segment.
