IDEA HUNT — PORTABLE BRIEF V9
A self-contained instruction set for a frontier LLM with sub-agent capability.
Written 19 Aug 2026, after seven parallel research agents reported. Supersedes V1-V8.
================================================================================

HOW TO USE THIS FILE
--------------------
Paste the whole thing as your opening message to a frontier model that can spawn
parallel sub-agents. It assumes the model knows NOTHING about this project.

Do not summarise it before pasting. The specificity IS the value. Two weeks,
seventy-two dead candidates and seven primary-source research agents produced
Sections 5 and 6; an agent that has not read them will burn its budget
re-deriving conclusions that are already settled, or repeating a premise that has
already been falsified.

Three of the corrections in Section 6 overturned things the PRINCIPAL believed
and briefed agents on. Expect this file to be wrong somewhere too. Say so.

SECTION 1 — THE MISSION
================================================================================

Find a business for one specific person. Not a category, not a "space" — a
business with a named buyer, an observed price, and a route to that buyer.

THE FOUNDER
  A 26-year-old Brazilian woman in Sao Paulo. She works alone and always will.
  Intelligent, literate in English and Portuguese, capable of sustained careful
  work. No professional credential, no industry background, no track record, no
  reputation, and no intention of acquiring a public one.

THE TARGET
  US$10,000/month NET TAKE-HOME — in her hands, after Brazilian tax.
  Reached within 12-18 MONTHS, with revenue beginning by roughly month 6.

CAPITAL
  US$500-2,500. ONE TIME. Not monthly.
  Buys: a company entity, tooling, domains, data acquisition, one small test.
  Does NOT buy: paid customer acquisition as a channel, a legal or regulatory
  opinion, or the purchase of an existing cash-flowing asset.
  If a candidate needs more, SAY SO EXPLICITLY rather than quietly assuming it.

MARKETS
  Brazil domestic, USA, UK, EU, Canada, Australia. All open.
  Brazil domestic is a FIRST-CLASS option and was under-weighted for seventy-two
  candidates. Read Section 7.

SECTION 2 — THE GOVERNING ARITHMETIC. READ BEFORE PROPOSING ANYTHING.
================================================================================

  Target, net, in her hands            US$10,000/month
  Brazilian burden — VERIFIED          8.1% tax; ~10% incl. payment rail and
                                       accountant, as a Lucro Presumido company
                                       selling SERVICES to buyers OUTSIDE Brazil
  GROSS REVENUE REQUIRED               ~US$11,100/month  (~US$133,200/year)
                                       = R$57,800/month at PTAX 5.2037
  Her time                             30 h/week = ~130 h/month
  Of which actual delivery             ~78 h/month (60% — generous for a solo)
  REQUIRED REALISATION                 ~US$142 PER HOUR OF DELIVERY TIME

  >>> THE PRODUCTION CEILING <<<
  At this target, ANY business in which she personally produces each unit of
  output is arithmetically dead. Not hard — dead. Her 78 delivery hours must
  BUILD OR OPERATE AN ASSET. They must never MAKE THE UNITS.

  The proof, so you need not take it on faith: the best-designed hand-production
  business this project ever received priced a professional written deliverable
  at US$325/unit covering up to 150 source pages, one clarification round and one
  revision. To gross US$11,100/month she must deliver THIRTY-FOUR of them inside
  78 delivery hours — about 2h20 each, including intake, reading, drafting and
  revision. Not credible. Meanwhile OBSERVED live freelance listings for written
  deliverables sit at US$15, US$30 and US$45, realising perhaps US$15-30/hour.
  The gap to US$142 is five to nine times. No pricing discipline closes it.

REVENUE MUST THEREFORE COME FROM ONE OF FOUR PLACES. State which one.
  A. THE ASSET PRODUCES THE UNITS — built once, serves customer 2 through 400 at
     near-zero marginal cost. (Software, a dataset, a watcher.)
  B. A SLICE OF SOMEONE ELSE'S FLOW — commission or take-rate on transactions she
     introduces but does not perform.
  C. A SPREAD SHE OWNS — closed for physical goods (Section 5).
  D. RENT ON SOMETHING SHE OWNS — a sub-case of A. Beware Section 6's SOURCING
     LAW before pointing any of it at the United States.
  If a candidate is none of these, it is a job, and it does not reach the target.

TWO ARITHMETIC RULES THAT KEEP GETTING FORGOTTEN
  * FIXED COST vs PER-CUSTOMER COST. A watcher that reads one public file weekly
    and serves it to every customer has fixed cost. A monitor that must issue
    paid API queries per customer, forever, has variable cost that scales with
    customers — and a solo operator entering last always loses that race. ASK OF
    EVERY CANDIDATE: is the source read ONCE and sold MANY times, or read once
    PER CUSTOMER?
  * HOW THE MONEY ARRIVES IS PART OF THE SCREEN. A bank-transfer invoice costs
    ~0.5%. A card checkout costs ~8% all-in — MORE THAN HER ENTIRE TAX BILL.
    INVOICE, DON'T CHECKOUT.

SECTION 3 — HARD CONSTRAINTS
================================================================================

Real, not preferences. Two were recently relaxed; the relaxations are stated
precisely. Do not extend them further.

C1  NEVER SPEAKS IN ONGOING OPERATIONS.
    No customer calls, video, meetings, voice notes, webinars or podcasts, in any
    language, ever. Written only.
    RELAXED: a small, bounded number of ONE-OFF SETUP calls at launch is
    acceptable — a supplier account, a payment-processor risk review, instructing
    counsel, signing a partner. Ongoing customer contact stays written forever.

C2  PERMANENTLY FACELESS.
    No camera, no photograph, no personal brand, no audience-building, no
    name-based reputation. Forbids the entire build-in-public playbook, which is
    how most solo software is actually sold. Private KYC to a payment processor
    is fine.
    ON PERSONAS: a company brand or pen name is available ON ARGUMENT, but the
    default is anonymous, and a persona must NEVER be the thing that rescues an
    otherwise weak idea.

C3  NO EMPLOYEES, NO CONTRACTORS, EVER.
    Vendors, suppliers, software and APIs are unlimited.
    Note the asymmetry: CAPACITY IS PURCHASABLE AS MACHINES, NOT AS PEOPLE.

C4  NEVER TAKES CUSTODY OF A CUSTOMER CREDENTIAL, and never holds standing access
    to a customer's systems. Work arrives as a file the customer sends.
    This closes most B2B SaaS (OAuth refresh tokens ARE standing access), all
    bookkeeping and ops work, and all monitoring that needs access.
    What survives: products that process an uploaded FILE, and products that
    watch something PUBLIC. See also the platform-enforced exception in §5.

C5  CAPITAL: US$500-2,500, ONE TIME.
C6  ~30 HOURS PER WEEK.
C7  HER PERSONAL SAO PAULO NETWORK IS NOT A SALES CHANNEL.

VETOES — absolute:
  * Anything requiring her to be BELIEVED AS AN EXPERT. Highest body count of any
    rule here. Kills advisory, interpretation, opinion, assurance, and anything
    priced on credibility.
  * Construction as a buyer segment.
  * Labour-compliance document chasing.
  * Physical goods and inventory, unless made-to-order.
  * Anything built around death. It is her life, not an argument. Do not
    relitigate it.

THE FOUR KNOWN MECHANISMS THAT SURVIVE THE EXPERT VETO:
  1. THE ARTEFACT PROVES ITSELF. Ship a proof sheet: every material statement
     paired with its source file and page; contradictions surfaced rather than
     silently resolved; unsupported claims listed in a "not used" section.
  2. THE BUYER SUPPLIES THE DOMAIN, SHE SUPPLIES THE THROUGHPUT.
  3. THE BUYER SUPPLIES THE "BEFORE." She never claims a baseline. They send work
     they already did with their own hours attached; she returns the after; the
     two objects sit side by side and the buyer does the comparing.
  4. THE RESELLER SUPPLIES THE FACE. See THE WHOLESALE ESCAPE in Section 6.

SECTION 4 — THE EVIDENCE STANDARD. NON-NEGOTIABLE.
================================================================================

FOUR FABRICATED STATISTICS HAVE ALREADY BEEN CAUGHT IN THIS PROJECT. Assume you
will be audited.

  * EVERY NUMBER NEEDS A PRIMARY-SOURCE URL. Acceptable: the company's own
    pricing page or documentation; a statute; a regulator or government
    publication; a trade body's own survey; a live listing you actually opened; a
    peer-reviewed or arXiv paper; a court or regulatory filing.
  * WRITE "UNVERIFIED" EXPLICITLY wherever you cannot meet that bar. A flagged
    gap beats a plausible figure. "There is no reliable public data on this" is a
    valuable sentence, not a failure.
  * NEVER CITE SEO CONTENT FARMS, LISTICLES, ROUNDUPS OR VENDOR MARKETING as
    evidence that a requirement, a market or a price exists. Whole genres of this
    material are fabricated end to end and cite each other in circles. The worst
    offenders encountered so far: "top 10 micro-SaaS success stories" posts, "GEO
    statistics 2026" roundups, "average cold email reply rate" agency blogs, and
    any accounting-firm blog quoting a tax rate. Use them ONLY to locate a
    primary source, then read the primary source and cite that.
  * OBSERVED LISTINGS BEAT PUBLISHED CLAIMS. Freelance price bands quoted at
    US$500-1,500 in blogs were US$15/US$30/US$45 when the live gigs were opened.
  * FIELD-INSPECT, DO NOT ASSUME. "Publicly available" and "obtainable at scale"
    are different properties. Open the record layout before claiming a list
    exists. And BEWARE MIRRORS: for the Brazilian company register, two of the
    four obvious API mirrors silently return null for the email field while two
    return the real address. One agent's first run of 80 records returned 0/80
    and was pure artefact.
  * PREFER A CENSUS TO A SAMPLE OF WINNERS. See THE VERIFICATION-IS-THE-BRAND LAW
    in Section 6 — it is the most important methodological finding in this file.
  * WHEN A CORRECTION LANDS, PROPAGATE IT to every conclusion that depends on it.
  * A CLEAN NEGATIVE WITH A NAMED CAUSE OF DEATH IS A GOOD RESULT. You are not
    scored on finding something. You are scored on being right.

SECTION 5 — ALREADY SETTLED. DO NOT RE-DERIVE. DO NOT PROPOSE.
================================================================================

DEAD BUSINESS FAMILIES
  * Digital assets on marketplaces — templates, stock, fonts, presets, prompt
    packs, mockups, icon sets. AI is either banned there (her skill is worthless)
    or allowed (price goes to zero). No third door.
  * Written deliverables sold on freelance marketplaces. Observed US$15-45,
    sampled three times across unrelated categories.
  * Verification of anything. A physical asset needs a body in a place; a digital
    asset needs a credential into the system that hosts it. She can have neither.
  * Lost-knowledge and documentation products. Where no third party holds a veto
    there is no buyer; where a veto exists, the veto-holder or a funded vendor
    publishes the artefact free.
  * Reselling physical goods / merchant of record. Closed by Brazilian tax law
    (a natural person habitually trading GOODS is deemed a company by operation
    of law, and non-creditable turnover taxes then stack).
    *** BUT SEE THE AGENT EXEMPTION IN SECTION 6 — the same decree expressly
    exempts agents and representatives who never trade on their own account, AND
    non-commercial services, AND creative works. The kill is narrower than this
    file believed for two weeks. ***
  * CONSUMER mobile app stores. 12-18 months, organic discovery ~zero.
  * B2B SOFTWARE MARKETPLACES — NEWLY CLOSED, on a full census rather than a
    guess. All 4,192 paid cloud apps in the best-fitting marketplace were pulled
    from its public API: MEDIAN 13 INSTALLS; median VENDOR 8 installs across its
    entire catalogue; 14.9% have zero installs; 49.7% have zero reviews; only 12
    of 1,384 single-app vendors exceed 1,000 installs and one of those twelve is
    the platform itself. The four with clean version histories took 5.8-7 YEARS.
    Median price US$450/year at 50 seats, and 79% are free at ten seats.
    >>> B2B marketplaces are not a different animal from consumer app stores.
    They are the same animal with a better-dressed buyer. <<<
  * AI-VISIBILITY MONITORING ("am I cited in AI answers?") — NEWLY KILLED. Four
    independent causes: free tiers from large incumbents; a venture-funded
    incumbent at a US$1bn valuation publishing competitive teardowns of the long
    tail; API cost per observation at or ABOVE the incumbents' retail price per
    observation; every relevant robots.txt disallows the consumer surfaces, so a
    compliant monitor measures a proxy and an accurate one scrapes in breach; and
    a variance decomposition over 12,933 responses finding BRAND IDENTITY IS 1.5%
    OF THE VARIANCE against 34.8% resampling noise and 26.5% query language.
  * Anything whose buyer segment is construction, or whose subject is death.

DEAD OR HEAVILY DISCOUNTED CHANNELS
  * PAID ADVERTISING — dead on C2, not on budget. The major ad platform publicly
    displays the advertiser's NAME AND LOCATION, permanently, linked from every
    ad. "Location: Brazil" against a US professional-services pitch is a trust
    discount on every impression she pays for. Separately: NO ad platform
    publishes a CPC benchmark, so every CPC figure in circulation is UNVERIFIED
    by construction.
  * Content, community, audience, personal brand — C2.
  * Her personal network — C7.
  * ROYALTY AND LICENSING INCOME AIMED AT THE UNITED STATES — see THE SOURCING
    LAW in Section 6. This is a hard, recent, and frequently missed kill.
  * Affiliate networks as a PRIMARY channel. They are a payment rail, not demand;
    affiliates promote only what already converts, so it returns zero times zero.
    Keep the mechanism; do not treat it as a channel.
  * Organic SEO as a PRIMARY channel — too slow for 18 months. BUT SEE THE
    INDEXATION/RANKING SPLIT in Section 6; "SEO is dead" is too strong and the
    two halves must not be collapsed.

ONE OPERATIONAL FACT
  * Several sites are simply unreadable to automated agents and any conclusion
    about them needs a human with a browser: one large craft marketplace (four
    agents, every method, bot-detection blocked all), the main indie-founder
    revenue community (interactive challenge on every path including
    robots.txt), and various vendor pricing pages that 403 selectively.

================================================================================
SECTION 6 — THE LAWS. THE REUSABLE HALF OF SEVENTY-TWO FAILURES.
================================================================================

Use these as tests. If a candidate fails one, say so and explain why it survives
anyway — or drop it.

--- ON WHETHER ANYONE WILL PAY ---

THE CONSTRAINT-SIDE LAW
  "This saves you four hours" only sells to a buyer SHORT OF HOURS. Ask: what
  breaks if their volume doubled next month? DEMAND-CONSTRAINED (short of
  customers — will NOT buy time back; they have spare hours and would rather keep
  them); SUPPLY-CONSTRAINED (turning work away — an hour saved is revenue);
  MARGIN-CONSTRAINED (volume fixed by something outside their control, cost is
  their own hours — an hour saved is margin). Most sole traders are
  demand-constrained. Worked example: 118,804 marriages in Australia in 2025
  divided by 10,832 registered celebrants is 11.0 ceremonies each per year even
  if celebrants performed every marriage in the country. Realistically ~9. A
  person with nine jobs a year is not short of hours.

THE EXOGENOUS-VOLUME SPEC
  Hunt a buyer whose VOLUME IS SET BY SOMETHING THEY DO NOT CONTROL and whose
  COST IS THEIR OWN HOURS. Renewal dates, statutory deadlines, publication-
  triggered legal clocks, settlement dates, pay cycles, filing seasons.

THE INVERSION CHECK
  Not "does the buyer possess this data?" but "COULD THE BUYER PRODUCE THIS
  ANSWER IN AN AFTERNOON?" If yes, there is no business.

THE FREE FRONT DOOR
  "Knowing which one" is usually the customer-acquisition cost of whoever holds
  the stock or the paying work — so they give it away. Before selling
  identification, matching, crossing or lookup, NAME WHO IS ALREADY PAID TO GIVE
  IT AWAY FREE.

THE MOAT-IS-A-CONTRACT TEST
  Name who is being PAID to eliminate the fragmentation you are calling a moat.
  Two candidates died on this in one day: a municipal-gazette unification product
  (a philanthropically funded nonprofit ships an open-source crawler fleet and a
  free public API doing exactly that, and every city it adds deletes part of your
  inventory) and an EU disclosure corpus (a funded EU body is building the free
  machine-readable version, with dates already set).

THE EMPTY-NICHE WARNING
  Zero competitors is WORSE evidence than a crowded market. A crowded market with
  visible prices proves demand; an empty one usually proves someone already tried.

--- ON PRICE ---

THE CEILING-HOLDER LAW
  Regulatory or audit exposure raises a category's CEILING but never creates a
  FLOOR, and the ceiling is held with a credential and a name. A faceless seller
  lands at the floor of every category she enters.

THE MONITORING PINCER
  Self-serve monitoring clusters at US$0-100/month because the raw signal is free
  and a competent solo builder always undercuts. Above US$300/month it is sold by
  a sales force. The rare products that are BOTH self-serve AND above US$300
  share exactly one property: THE BUYER'S OWN REVENUE IS A DIRECT FUNCTION OF THE
  WATCH — the investor buys the house, the retailer reprices the stock, the
  expired certificate takes the site down.
  >>> The test is not "is this watchable?" It is: DOES ONE ALERT, ACTED ON, PAY
  FOR A YEAR OF SUBSCRIPTION? <<<

THE PRICE-HIDING TELL
  If the nearest comparable does not publish a price, the category is SALES-LED
  and she cannot enter it. This is a ten-second screen and it killed several
  candidates outright.

THE REPEATABILITY TRAP
  The property granting her leverage (it is repeatable, so a machine can do it)
  is the same property destroying pricing power (it is repeatable, so anyone's
  machine can do it).

THE UNCERTAINTY-PREMIUM LAW
  A business whose price is supported by the buyer's IGNORANCE of a new channel
  is priced on a decaying asset. Ask: does the platform being measured have both
  the data and the incentive to publish this itself? If yes, you are renting
  time, not building a business.

THE NOISE-FLOOR FILTER
  A product whose measured construct is a small fraction of its own variance
  cannot be sold honestly. The tell: the honest version of the product — "your
  number is mostly noise, here is the decomposition" — talks its own customer out
  of buying.

--- ON DISTRIBUTION: THE ACTUAL BINDING CONSTRAINT ---

Seventy-two candidates died at the same place: where a stranger has to decide to
give her money. She has unlimited production and zero distribution.

THE DISTRIBUTION SCISSORS
  Being FACELESS pushes toward MANY SMALL self-serve customers (a relationship
  needs a person). Having NO AUDIENCE AND NO BUDGET pushes toward FEW LARGE
  customers (reaching 400 strangers needs a following or money). THE TWO POINT IN
  OPPOSITE DIRECTIONS. This tension is the real strategic problem.

*** THE WHOLESALE ESCAPE — the most important finding in this file ***
  There is exactly ONE channel in which a faceless supplier is PREFERRED rather
  than merely tolerated: SELLING THE MACHINE TO A FIRM THAT RESELLS IT UNDER ITS
  OWN NAME. The reseller supplies the face, the credential, the interpretation
  and the liability. She supplies the throughput. Price is then set by the
  RESELLER'S CLIENT REVENUE, not by the self-serve comparable.
  Arithmetic: 17 firms at US$650/month reaches the target. Seventeen, not three
  hundred. Positioning that tested well: "You own the relationship. We are your
  invisible department."

*** THE CONVERGENCE — arrived at independently by two agents on unrelated
    questions, and it dissolves the Scissors ***
  FOR A COMPOUNDING DATASET, THE PRODUCT AND THE DISTRIBUTION ARE THE SAME
  ARTEFACT. The pages that constitute the dataset are the pages that get cited by
  AI answer engines and search. Publishing IS the marketing. It is the only
  structure found in seventy-two candidates that NEUTRALISES C2 rather than
  merely tolerating it — because what makes her invisible as a person is
  irrelevant to an object that answers a question with a number.

THE DISCLOSED-DEPARTMENT LAW
  The invisible department survives, but only half of it. In regulated
  professions the firm's own code of conduct typically requires it to INFORM ITS
  CLIENT that a third-party provider may be used, while a confidentiality
  contract with the provider removes the need for client CONSENT.
  >>> Her IDENTITY can stay invisible. Her EXISTENCE cannot. Any pitch promising
  the buyer that nobody will ever know is asking a professional to breach their
  own code, and sophisticated buyers know it. <<<
  The escape worth mining: such codes typically carve out ADMINISTRATIVE SUPPORT
  SERVICES (record storage, application hosting, transmittal) from the disclosure
  rule entirely. Positioning as administrative support rather than
  professional-service assistance moves the work outside the rule. That is a
  product-shaping constraint, not a legal quibble.

THE ONBOARDING LAW
  Written-only is compatible with SELLING. It is NOT compatible with BEING
  ONBOARDED. Reseller setup, acquirer risk review, vendor security diligence,
  fiscal-representative appointment and dispute escalation are all conversations.
  ASK OF EVERY CANDIDATE: how many separate parties must onboard her, and can
  each be done in writing?

THE POSTAL LIST LAW, and its exception
  A public register gives name, street address, phone and an expiry date. It does
  not give email. Of NINE registers now field-inspected across six countries,
  exactly TWO publish email at scale. ENUMERABLE AND CONTACTABLE ARE DIFFERENT
  PROPERTIES. Field-inspect before believing — and beware mirrors that silently
  null the field.
  THE EXCEPTION IS LARGE: Brazil's national company register publishes an email
  field for every establishment, alongside industry code, size, capital, tax
  regime and start date, free and in bulk. Measured fill rate 62% of a random
  sample of 32 (small N, measured through a mirror, needs confirming at source).
  In Brazil, enumerable IS contactable.

THE CONSPICUOUS-PUBLICATION LAW
  All five non-Brazilian target jurisdictions permit unsolicited commercial email
  to a business address the recipient PUBLISHED THEMSELVES, where the message is
  relevant to their role and carries an opt-out. They differ in who carries the
  BURDEN OF PROOF, not in whether the door is open. (Germany is stricter and was
  not checked — treat DACH as closed.) Brazil has NO anti-spam statute at all;
  what governs is the data-protection statute's legitimate-interest basis, plus a
  requirement to identify the controller and honour objections — which requires
  an identifiable controller, NOT a face.

THE PROVIDER-AUP LAW
  Cold email is legal under the statutes of every market here and SIMULTANEOUSLY
  PROHIBITED by the terms of the mailbox provider used to send it. The regulator
  is not the risk; the VENDOR is. She is not one complaint away from a fine — she
  is one complaint away from losing the sending infrastructure, and re-warming
  costs weeks.

THE INDEXATION / RANKING SPLIT
  AI answer engines cite by LIVE RETRIEVAL, not training — the providers run
  separate crawler fleets and document them separately, and a site that blocks
  every major TRAINING crawler was observed being cited FIRST. The lag is days,
  not years. BUT one major engine is index-gated: the page must already be
  indexed in ordinary search, though it need not RANK (measured: 29.8% of its AI
  citations come from domains absent from the corresponding first page).
  >>> Being INDEXED is free, fast, written-only and a hard prerequisite. RANKING
  is what stays out of reach. Do not collapse the two. <<<
  Scale check before betting on it: AI referrals were measured at roughly 0.6% of
  the incumbent search engine's referral volume, growing ~357%/yr. A channel
  growing tenfold off 0.6% is at 6% in eighteen months. SECONDARY CHANNEL, NEVER
  PRIMARY. And llms.txt is noise — the largest engine's own documentation says it
  does not use such files, and 97% of published ones received zero requests.

THE SOURCING/REPUTATION SPLIT
  Two studies appear to contradict each other on whether small unknown sites get
  cited. They measured different question types. "What is the answer to this
  question" is a SOURCING problem, won by whoever holds the data — citation there
  is measurably flatter than search ranking, with over half of citation hosts
  appearing exactly once. "Which vendor should I use" is a REPUTATION problem,
  won by stature — small brands appear in 11% of such answers against 73% for
  large ones.
  >>> She loses the reputation query PERMANENTLY — it requires exactly what her
  veto forbids. She can win the sourcing query, because IT DOES NOT ASK WHO SHE
  IS. <<<
  And what wins once retrieved is measured: adding QUOTATIONS and STATISTICS
  scores highest; KEYWORD STUFFING scores BELOW baseline — classic SEO actively
  fails. Where specific data does not exist in retrievable form, the slot is
  filled with generic vendor content marketing. Those slots are UNOCCUPIED.

THE VERIFICATION-IS-THE-BRAND LAW — the key methodological finding
  Publicly verifiable success in a channel is NOT a neutral sample of that
  channel. It is a sample of operators who CHOSE TO BE VISIBLE, and visibility is
  itself their business model. The largest verified-revenue feed literally
  requires a public social post as its verification rule.
  >>> Asking any agent to "find verified examples" of a faceless business
  GUARANTEES an audience-driven answer set. USE PLATFORM CENSUSES INSTEAD — they
  count everyone, including the silent. <<<
  Corollary: much of what looks like solo-software success is audience
  monetisation wearing software clothing — courses, boilerplates and tools sold
  to other founders. Closed to her twice over.

THE MERCHANT-OF-RECORD SPLIT — a ten-second platform screen
  GROUP A: the platform bills the customer, remits their taxes and pays her a net
  share. This converts her from a merchant selling to thousands of foreign
  consumers into a SUPPLIER INVOICING ONE CORPORATE COUNTERPARTY — an ordinary
  service export, which sidesteps the tax structure that closed the resale shape.
  GROUP B: the platform is a directory only; she builds her own billing and IS
  her own merchant of record.
  >>> Group B's "0% revenue share" is not generosity. It is the ABSENCE OF THE
  ONLY THING that made the platform useful to her. <<<

--- ON DATA AND ASSETS ---

THE HEAD-START ASYMMETRY — screen for this FIRST, before buyer, price or law
  The property making a dataset defensible (it compounds) is the SAME property
  making it un-enterable. If a dataset is worth accumulating, someone started
  accumulating it, and their moat points AT HER. The law says so out loud: in the
  jurisdictions with a database right, accumulation of successive additions
  qualifies the database for a FRESH term of protection — a continuously-updated
  database is effectively perpetually protected.

THE CLOCK-START RULE
  The only moment a solo entrant and an incumbent are LEVEL is when the recording
  opportunity COMES INTO EXISTENCE — a register that opened this year, a regime
  that just took effect, a mandate whose first filings are landing now.
  HUNT CLOCK-STARTS, NOT DATASETS.

THE STATE KEEPS STATE, NOT HISTORY
  A register publishes what it needs to OPERATE. It has no operational need for
  last year's snapshot, so it overwrites. The CHANGE-LOG of a current-state-only
  register is the one artefact the register-holder will never produce, and it
  CANNOT BE BACK-FILLED BY ANYONE — including an AI agent — because the prior
  state is gone. The only genuinely non-re-assemblable class found.

THE SCRAPING PERIMETER
  The computer-misuse statutes are NOT the barrier — the leading case ended with
  the scraper winning on the statute and then LOSING ON CONTRACT, settling with a
  six-figure judgment and an injunction to destroy the data and derived
  algorithms. PUBLIC + NO LOGIN does not mean permitted. A scraper that never
  registers an account is in a very different position from one that does.
  Government open data is the only raw material simultaneously free, licensed for
  commercial re-use, and outside the terms-of-service trap — but CHECK THE
  LICENCE: at least one major national procurement portal publishes under a
  NO-DERIVATIVES licence, which forbids a derived data product outright.

--- ON WHERE SHE IS ---

THE SOURCING LAW — a distribution constraint disguised as a tax one
  There is NO US-Brazil income tax treaty. Personal-service income is sourced
  where the services are PERFORMED; royalties are sourced where the property is
  USED. Therefore: work performed in Brazil is foreign-source and reaches her
  whole. A ROYALTY FROM A US PAYER IS US-SOURCE AND REACHES HER AT 70 CENTS ON
  THE DOLLAR, with no treaty relief available.
  >>> SELL SERVICES. NEVER LICENSE IP INTO THE UNITED STATES. <<<
  It compounds with a Brazilian rule: passive royalties falling outside the
  company's REGISTERED OBJECT are taxed on a 100% base rather than a 32% presumed
  base — roughly 34% instead of 7.7%. A US royalty stream can be hit at 30%
  American withholding AND ~34% Brazilian tax. If a licensing stream is ever
  built, the company's registered object must be the exploitation of that right.
  This is a one-line drafting decision at incorporation worth a quarter of the
  revenue.

THE AGENT EXEMPTION — the resale kill is narrower than believed
  The decree that deems a habitual goods-trader to be a company EXPRESSLY EXEMPTS
  (a) agents and representatives who do not trade on their own account,
  (b) non-commercial services, and (c) the exploitation of artistic, didactic and
  scientific works. Two agents found these independently while working on
  unrelated questions. The kill applies to the GOODS LEG ONLY and must not be
  cited against a services, creative-works or commission-agency candidate.

THE ANTI-DISINTERMEDIATION STATUTE
  The reason two-sided businesses fail is that both sides transact directly once
  introduced. In Brazil, the UK and the EU, an EXCLUSIVE-TERRITORY COMMERCIAL
  AGENT is owed commission on business concluded in her territory THAT SHE NEVER
  TOUCHED. Statutory, non-derogable in the UK/EU, and the default rule in Brazil.
  >>> And the distinction is one word in one contract: the AGENCY form carries
  this protection; the BROKERAGE form has the OPPOSITE default, denying any
  commission where the parties concluded directly. Every contract must be an
  agency, never a brokerage. <<<
  Note also that in Brazil the apparent registration gate on commission agency
  was struck down as unconstitutional — commission is earnable and suable
  unregistered, and registration buys a statutory package (a termination
  indemnity, a prohibition on credit-risk clauses, priority in the principal's
  insolvency) which may itself deter principals from appointing her. NOT
  registering can be the better move.

THE CURRENCY DIRECTION LAW
  Earn hard currency, spend soft. The property is in the BILLING, not the cargo.

THE ASYMMETRIC PAIR
  Put the SOURCE in Brazil and the BUYER abroad. Brazilian raw material is cheap,
  voluminous, badly formatted and protected by language — her arbitrage, and free
  to her. Foreign buyers pay hard currency. The configuration satisfying both:
  SOMETHING OBTAINABLE ONLY IN BRAZIL, SOLD TO SOMEONE WHO IS NOT IN BRAZIL.
  (Weigh it against THE SOURCING LAW: services, not licences, if the buyer is US.)

--- ON METHOD ---

FILTER LAST, NOT FIRST
  A constraint applied early deletes the COMPONENT along with the candidate, and
  the component was the valuable half. Hold parts loose, recombine, THEN filter.

NAME THE SHAPE OUT LOUD
  Before proposing a candidate, state its shape (Section 8). If it is the same
  shape as the last one, STOP and change shape first. Optimising for her
  constraints drives everything toward "questionnaire, she writes prose,
  deliverable," because that is the most constraint-compatible activity in
  existence. IT IS A LOCAL OPTIMUM AND THE SEARCH ROLLS BACK INTO IT EVERY TIME
  UNLESS SOMETHING FORBIDS IT. Three consecutive "new candidates" were once one
  idea with three different buyers.

PORT THE WHOLE MODEL, NOT ONLY THE PARTS
  When an outside business model is supplied, ask what HER VERSION OF THE WHOLE
  MODEL is — not merely which components it donates.

THE TEST DESIGN THAT WORKS
  Twenty real instances, and COUNT HOW MANY CLEAR A NUMERIC BAR. Measure the
  RATE, because the rate predicts everything after it. Prefer the test that costs
  nothing and needs no phone. State the bar IN ADVANCE and what result kills the
  candidate. A test that cannot fail is not a test.

SECTION 7 — BRAZIL DOMESTIC, AND WHAT CHANGED THIS YEAR
================================================================================

Selling domestically REMOVES: the cross-border tax pincer that closed the resale
shape; the foreign-insurance bar (a natural person is statutorily barred from
buying the foreign cover trade customers require); most of the onboarding-by-
voice problem (the domestic instant-payment rail has no acquirer risk review and
no chargeback arbitration conducted by telephone); and the foreign-anonymous-
vendor trust penalty. It ACTIVATES local access — the only genuine arbitrage
identified in the entire project.

It COSTS: lower price points, and earning the soft currency.

TWO THINGS CHANGED IN 2026 THAT THIS FILE MISSED FOR MONTHS:
  * THE EU ADOPTED A DATA ADEQUACY DECISION FOR BRAZIL, 26 JANUARY 2026. A
    European firm may now send client files to a processor in Sao Paulo with NO
    standard contractual clauses, NO transfer impact assessment, NO Article 46
    safeguard. This overturned a structural kill recorded by another agent on the
    same day. EVERY EU-FACING WHITE-LABEL CANDIDATE PREVIOUSLY KILLED OR
    DISCOUNTED ON DATA-TRANSFER GROUNDS MUST BE RE-EXAMINED. The UK did NOT
    follow — plan for EU does not equal UK.
  * The domestic instant-payment rail now supports native recurring subscription
    collection under a 2025 central-bank resolution. BUT IT IS NOT FREE: the
    regulation expressly permits charging a legal entity for receipt, and
    charging a natural person for "receiving funds for the purpose of
    purchasing" — which is exactly what she would be doing. What survives is
    still valuable: no acquirer risk review, no chargeback dispute by telephone,
    settlement in seconds.

THE PRICE PROBLEM, STATED HONESTLY. R$57,800/month from Brazilian buyers, against
observed Brazilian self-serve B2B software list prices of roughly R$60-650/month,
implies 89-963 customers. Not serviceable at 30 h/week.
  >>> She must clear R$1,000+/month per customer, which is ABOVE the entire
  self-serve Brazilian band. THE BUYER'S COMPARISON MUST BE A SALARY, NOT A
  SUBSCRIPTION. Under THE CONSTRAINT-SIDE LAW that points at buyers whose volume
  is set by the Brazilian fiscal and statutory calendar and whose cost is their
  own hours. <<<

SECTION 8 — THE SIX SHAPES
================================================================================

Label every candidate.

  S1  WRITTEN WORK to order. DEAD — over-explored AND arithmetically dead at this
      target. If you believe you have an exception you must show the per-hour

  S2  REPLENISHMENT / MERCHANT OF RECORD. CLOSED for goods by Brazilian tax law.

  S3  CONTINUOUS OPERATION — the product is that something was watched. The

  S4  A COMPOUNDING DATASET. Arithmetically ideal, and it is the shape THE

  S5  SOFTWARE SOLD AS A PRODUCT. Consumer app stores dead; B2B marketplaces now
      also dead on a full census. What survives is software sold DIRECT, through
      one of the live channels.
  S6  MATCHING TWO ENUMERABLE POPULATIONS. Live, and protected by THE
      ANTI-DISINTERMEDIATION STATUTE.

SECTION 9 — THE THREE LIVE CHANNELS, AND WHAT SURVIVED THE HUNT
================================================================================

Given 12-18 months and US$500-2,500, three channels are cheap enough, fast enough
and compatible with having no face:

  1. COLD EMAIL — and specifically, IN PORTUGUESE, INTO BRAZIL, off the national
     company register, segmented by industry code. It is the only channel that
     clears every constraint at once: written both directions; requires an
     identifiable controller but not a face; free list; under US$100/month all
     in; no foreign-sender signal; no FX, no withholding, no merchant-of-record
     problem, no foreign-insurance bar. Its binding constraint is PRICE, not
     reach — see Section 7.
  2. WHITE-LABEL / WHOLESALE PARTNERS — 15-25 relationships. Opened by the C1
     relaxation and by the EU adequacy decision. See THE WHOLESALE ESCAPE and THE
     DISCLOSED-DEPARTMENT LAW.
  3. THE DATASET AS ITS OWN DISTRIBUTION — see THE CONVERGENCE. Slower and
     smaller in absolute terms, but it compounds and it is the only one that
     neutralises C2 outright.

  Also live but secondary: DIRECT MAIL, the one channel with no consent regime,
  no spam filter, no platform terms and no identity disclosure — the natural
  partner to the seven registers that publish street addresses but not email.
  Also under-explored: BUYING AN EXISTING DISTRIBUTION ASSET rather than building
  one. It converts the unsolved problem (acquisition) into a solved one (capital
  plus written diligence), and diligence is document review — exactly C4's
  permitted shape. It needs the top capital tier and is therefore currently out
  of budget; say so rather than pretending otherwise.

THREE CANDIDATES SURVIVED THE SEVEN-AGENT HUNT. Treat them as the incumbents to
beat, not as answers:
  * WHOLESALE MULTI-OFFICE IP-GAZETTE WATCH, white-labelled to small IP
    boutiques. 17 firms at US$650/month. Statutory deadlines with malpractice
    consequences; buyers enumerable and email-contactable from the same free
    data. Fails on: an incumbent anchor at US$30-95/month for a single-office
    version, and THE ONBOARDING LAW.
  * EXCLUSIVE ADVERTISING REPRESENTATION for niche B2B newsletters, structured as
    an exclusive-territory AGENCY. Observed 30-40% intermediation rate from the
    incumbent's own published schedule; ~7-8 placements/month needed; under
    US$500; no licence in any market; chicken-and-egg solved because unsold
    inventory is worth zero once the send goes out. Fails on: about 10 hours per
    closed placement against 78 delivery hours, so IT ONLY WORKS IF ADVERTISERS
    REBOOK — and that rate is the first thing to measure.
  * A CLOCK-START DATASET on a statutory regime whose first filings have not yet
    happened, sold to software vendors whose product breaks without it. Fails on:
    an unknowable-today deviation rate that decides whether the dataset is rich
    or thin, and a funded public body that will publish the current-state version
    free (the residue is that it will never publish the HISTORY).

SECTION 10 — HOW PREVIOUS HUNTS FAILED. DO NOT REPEAT THESE.
================================================================================

  1. GENERATING BUYERS INSTEAD OF IDEAS. Three consecutive "new candidates" were
     one idea with three buyers. Name the shape out loud.
  2. FILTERING TOO EARLY, deleting valuable components with the candidates.
  3. MINING A SUPPLIED MODEL FOR PARTS and applying them back onto the old
     business instead of asking what HER version of that whole model would be.
  4. ACCEPTING SECONDARY SOURCES. Four fabricated statistics, and two research
     premises sourced only to the marketing of the firms that benefited.
  5. ASSUMING A REGISTER IS A MAILING LIST. Field-inspect the record layout, and
     beware mirrors that silently null the field you care about.
  6. CONFUSING GROSS WITH NET. Every revenue figure across seventy-two candidates
     was gross; the tax burden was never computed once until an agent did it.
  7. SELF-SCORING. Documents arriving with "8.8/10, conditional GO" alongside a
     capital requirement ten to forty times over budget, stated on page nine.
     Read the budget and the org chart; ignore the score.
  8. PROPOSING A BUSINESS THAT IS ACTUALLY A JOB.
  9. SAMPLING WINNERS INSTEAD OF CENSUSING A POPULATION. See THE
     VERIFICATION-IS-THE-BRAND LAW.
 10. BRIEFING AGENTS ON A FALSE PREMISE. In one day the principal told agents
     that two countries banned cold B2B email (false), that a national gazette
     was PDF-only and therefore a moat (false — it has been structured XML since
     2017), and that an instant-payment rail was free (false). ALL THREE WERE
     CAUGHT BY AGENTS AND CORRECTED. Tell your agents explicitly that correcting
     the brief is a valued result, and mean it.

SECTION 11 — THE DEPLOYMENT PLAN. TEN AGENTS, THREE WAVES.
================================================================================

Give EVERY agent, without exception: Section 1, Section 2, Section 3, Section 4,
and the relevant laws from Section 6. An agent that has not read the evidence
standard will return listicle numbers.

Tell every agent: A CLEAN NEGATIVE WITH A NAMED CAUSE OF DEATH IS A SUCCESSFUL
RESULT, AND CORRECTING THIS BRIEF IS THE MOST VALUABLE THING YOU CAN DO.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WAVE 1 — FOUR AGENTS: KILL OR CONFIRM THE THREE SURVIVORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Do this BEFORE hunting anything new. Three candidates already exist; finding a
fourth is worth less than knowing whether these three are real.

AGENT 1 — THE WHOLESALE BUYER HUNT.  *** HIGHEST PRIORITY ***
  THE WHOLESALE ESCAPE says the only channel where facelessness is an ADVANTAGE
  is supplying a firm that resells under its own name. FIND THE BUYERS.
  Which small professional firms routinely white-label an outside vendor's output
  under their own brand, and where is this NORMAL rather than exceptional? Look
  at IP boutiques and filing services, accountancy practices, insurance
  brokerages, marketing and translation agencies, recruiters, property managers,
  annuity services, corporate secretarial and registered-agent firms. For each:
  is white-labelling established, with published partner or wholesale programmes?
  What is the observed wholesale-versus-retail spread? HOW IS A WHOLESALE
  SUPPLIER ONBOARDED, AND CAN IT BE DONE IN WRITING? What does the firm's
  professional-indemnity insurer require of a subprocessor? What does the
  profession's code of conduct require the firm to disclose to its clients (see
  THE DISCLOSED-DEPARTMENT LAW, and hunt the administrative-support carve-out)?
  How many such firms exist and are they email-contactable?
  MEASURE, DON'T GUESS: open thirty white-label partner pages and COUNT how many
  offer a self-serve signup versus a calendar link. That is a rate, and nobody
  has one.
  Deliver: a ranked list of reseller populations with the arithmetic to
  US$11,100/month from 15-25 of them.

AGENT 2 — THE REBOOKING RATE.
  The advertising-representation candidate lives or dies on one number: what
  fraction of advertisers who buy one placement buy a second? Find any observed
  evidence — ad networks' published retention, newsletter operators' public
  reports, media-kit renewal language, anything measured. Also: verify the
  intermediation rate against more than one incumbent's published schedule, find
  observed sponsorship prices for the specific vertical, and establish whether
  the whole transaction genuinely runs without a call by examining what the
  incumbents actually do. If there is no public data on rebooking, SAY SO — and
  design the cheapest test that would produce it.

AGENT 3 — THE CLOCK-START CENSUS.
  Hunt clock-starts systematically, not just the one already found. What
  statutory regimes, registers, mandates or reporting obligations COME INTO
  EXISTENCE in the next 24 months, in any of the six markets, such that first
  filings have not yet happened and no incumbent has a head start? For each:
  what gets published, in what form, by whom, on what calendar; who breaks if
  they do not have it; is the publishing body funded to produce the machine-
  readable version itself (THE MOAT-IS-A-CONTRACT TEST); and crucially, WILL THE
  HISTORY BE KEPT OR OVERWRITTEN (THE STATE KEEPS STATE, NOT HISTORY)?
  Check licences: a no-derivatives licence forbids the product outright.

AGENT 4 — THE RED TEAM.  *** DEFAULT TO KILLING ***
  Take the three survivors in Section 9 and try to destroy them. Mandatory
  checks, each answered explicitly:
    - Who is already paid to give this away free?
    - Could the buyer produce this answer in an afternoon?
    - Is the buyer demand-, supply- or margin-constrained?
    - How many separate parties must onboard her, and can each be done in
      writing?
    - Does one unit, acted on, pay for a year?
    - Is the source read ONCE and sold MANY times, or once PER CUSTOMER?
    - Does the property granting leverage also destroy pricing power?
    - Is the list actually contactable? Open the record layout.
    - What is the cheapest existing substitute, with a URL and a price?
    - What does this look like in a global downturn in 2027-2028?
  A survivor of a genuine attempt to kill it is worth ten unexamined candidates.

WAVE 2 — FOUR AGENTS: THE REMAINING GAPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AGENT 5 — THE EXOGENOUS-VOLUME BUYER HUNT.
  Enumerate populations whose volume is set by something they do not control and
  whose cost is their own hours, EXCLUDING death and construction. Renewal and
  expiry calendars, statutory filing deadlines, publication-triggered legal
  clocks, settlement dates, payroll cycles, licence renewal, tax seasons,
  insurance renewal, IP annuities, grant acquittal, admissions cycles, customs
  windows. For each: what sets the volume; population size; IS IT ENUMERABLE AND
  EMAIL-CONTACTABLE (field-inspect); what do they pay now; does ONE person
  decide? Rank by wallet times contactability. Bias toward breadth — this
  produces the target list the other agents aim at.
  FOR BRAZIL SPECIFICALLY: the buyer must clear R$1,000+/month, so the comparison
  must be a salary. Start from the fiscal and statutory calendar.

AGENT 6 — THE EU REOPENING.
  The EU granted Brazil data adequacy on 26 January 2026 and this project has not
  absorbed it. Re-examine every EU-facing possibility that was previously killed
  or discounted on data-transfer grounds: white-label subcontracting into EU
  professional firms, screening and compliance processing, document and file
  work. What remains after adequacy — sector rules, professional codes,
  procurement policies, insurer requirements? Confirm the UK position separately
  and do not assume it followed.

AGENT 7 — THE COMMISSION-AGENCY DEEPENING.
  Commission agency in an industry with no referral-fee rule is protected by THE
  ANTI-DISINTERMEDIATION STATUTE and passes the onboarding law. Go deeper: which
  specific industries pay commission to an unlicensed intermediary, at what
  PUBLISHED rate, and can the whole transaction run in writing? Map the licence
  gates negatively first. Compute required monthly flow at every rate you can
  source. Note that no current primary-source commission survey exists for
  industrial sales agency — if you find one, that alone is worth the leg.

AGENT 8 — THE FREE-TEST DESIGNER.
  For every survivor, design a test that COSTS NOTHING, NEEDS NO PHONE AND NO
  CUSTOMER, and measures a RATE. The best ones produced so far: pull twenty
  firms' published price lists and count how many contain a specific line item;
  email thirty organisations one written question and count replies CONTAINING A
  NUMBER; write the same deliverable twice and time both (second under half the
  first means it is a machine; the same means it is a job); parse four weeks of a
  public gazette and count how many events touched each of twenty named firms;
  publish twenty structured factual pages on one indexed domain and count how
  many are cited within sixty days. Each test states its numeric bar IN ADVANCE
  and what result kills the candidate.

WAVE 3 — TWO AGENTS: THE THINGS THAT NEED A HUMAN OR A RETRY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AGENT 9 — THE UNVERIFIED LEDGER.
  Collect every UNVERIFIED item from all prior reports into one list, sort by how
  much rests on it, and attempt each one again by a different method. Mark clearly
  which ones genuinely need a human with a browser. In this project that list has
  included: bot-blocked marketplaces and pricing pages, a JavaScript-only
  government file host, a public API returning gateway timeouts, PDFs that are
  image scans with no text layer, and legislation sites that refuse automated
  clients. A retry from a different network often succeeds where the first failed.

AGENT 10 — THE COMPLETENESS CRITIC.
  Read everything the other agents produced and answer one question: WHAT IS
  MISSING? Which shape was not hunted, which market was not checked, which claim
  is load-bearing and unverified, which two reports contradict each other, and
  which conclusion rests on a premise nobody tested? What it finds becomes the
  next wave.

SECTION 12 — SYNTHESIS
================================================================================

Do NOT concatenate the reports. Do this:

  1. LIST THE CONTRADICTIONS FIRST. Where two agents disagree, name it and
     resolve it on the evidence, saying which you believe and why. In this
     project, agents correcting each other AND correcting the principal has
     produced more value than agreement. On one day, one agent's structural kill
     was overturned by another agent's finding within hours.
  2. SEPARATE THE LAWS FROM THE CANDIDATES. The reusable finding outlives the
     business. Write down every new structural rule with the evidence that
     produced it, whether or not any candidate survived.
  3. BUILD ONE NUMBERS LEDGER with three columns: VERIFIED PRIMARY /
     SECONDARY-FLAGGED / UNVERIFIED-AND-NOT-USED. Anything unverified goes in the
     third column BY NAME rather than being quietly dropped.
  4. FOR EACH SURVIVOR state: the shape (S1-S6); which revenue structure (A-D);
     the named buyer; what breaks for that buyer without it; the observed
     comparable price with a URL; customers needed for US$11,100/month; the
     channel that reaches them; the capital tier; the onboarding count; and THE
     HONEST REASON IT MIGHT FAIL.
  5. RANK BY WHAT IS LEAST LIKELY TO BE WRONG, not by what is most exciting.

SECTION 13 — WHAT A GOOD ANSWER LOOKS LIKE
================================================================================

  * It names a buyer, not a market.
  * It has an observed price with a URL, not an estimate.
  * It states the arithmetic to US$11,100/month explicitly.
  * It says which revenue structure it is, and therefore why her 78 delivery
    hours are not the bottleneck.
  * It answers THE ONBOARDING LAW with a count.
  * It says whether the source is read once and sold many times, or once per
    customer.
  * It says how the money arrives, and what that costs.
  * It has a free test with a numeric bar set in advance.
  * It flags its own weakest link before anyone else has to.
  * IT CORRECTS SOMETHING IN THIS BRIEF.

  A bad answer, received many times: a well-written blueprint with a
  self-assessed score, a capital requirement ten times over budget stated on page
  nine, a market size from a content farm, and a buyer who is short of customers
  rather than short of hours.
