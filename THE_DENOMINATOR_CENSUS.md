## 1. ★★ THE FOUR LAWS — the real output

> ### ★★ LAW 1 — THE MANDATORY-FIELD SELF-SUPPLY LAW *(a sharpening of `THE SUBSET LAW`, and the reason this pattern is rare)*
>
> **A regulator makes a field mandatory on the party that already holds the input. Regulators do not compel a
> party to state a number it cannot obtain.**
>
> All eleven mandatory fields in **49 CFR 1333.4** (rail demurrage invoices) are the railroad's own operating
> records — waybill date, actual placement, constructive placement, notification, release — and **1333.5** then
> compels the railroad to hand them over machine-readable.
>
> **★ THE SEED IS THE EXCEPTION, NOT THE TYPE SPECIMEN. 46 CFR 541.6 puts the free-time end date on the OCEAN
> CARRIER, but the gate calendar is generated by the MARINE TERMINAL. The rule-maker split the field from its
> input across two companies.**
>
> ### **THE SPLIT TEST, thirty seconds, run it FIRST: is the party legally on the hook for the field the same party that generates the input? If yes, stop.** Killed rail, SEC Rules 605/606/614, ETF 6c-11, parcel money-back guarantees and CBAM in ten seconds each.

> ### ★★ LAW 2 — THE RETENTION-DUTY COROLLARY. **A short posting window is evidence of a LONG retention duty, not of erasure. READ THE WHOLE PART BEFORE BELIEVING A WINDOW.**
>
> - **18 CFR 284.13(b)** posts 90 days — **284.12(b)(3)(v)** retains **3 years** at a reasonable fee.
> - **18 CFR 37.7(b)**, in ONE SENTENCE: *"Audit data must remain available for download on the OASIS for 90
>   days, except ATC/TTC postings that must remain available… for 20 days. The audit data are to be **retained
>   and made available upon request for download for five years**."*
> - **18 CFR 37.6(b)(2)(ii)** — ATC/TTC inputs *"retained for six months after the applicable posting period."*
>
> **Of nine machine-readable regulator-capped windows found, FOUR are paired with an explicit retain-and-produce
> duty in the same part.** This is `46 CFR 520.10` generalised: **the tighter the posting window, the more
> likely the regulator wrote the archive duty next to it.**

> ### ★★ LAW 3 — THE OUTCOME-IS-RETAINED LAW. **A publisher that overwrites keeps the OUTCOME and discards the RUNNING COMMENTARY — and a mandatory field almost always needs the outcome.**
>
> CBP posts only the four most recent weekly quota Commodity Status Reports, but retains year-end snapshots and
> **fill-rate tables giving the date and time each quota filled** *(SECONDARY — cbp.gov 403s)*. The moment of
> fill is the legally load-bearing fact; the running weekly percentage is not. **The erased half and the
> load-bearing half are different halves.**
>
> **★ THE SCREEN: ask what the ANNUAL or FINAL version of this data is. If one exists, the publisher keeps it
> and you are archiving noise.**
>
> ### ★ AND THE SEED PASSES THIS TEST UNIQUELY — MEASURED BY ME TODAY.
> **A gate calendar has no outcome. Every day IS its own outcome and nothing supersedes it.** Tested against the
> obvious objection — *do terminals publish an annual holiday calendar, making closures knowable in advance?*
> **YTI's gate schedule, read today, is a rolling ~6-day window with per-shift values that vary week to week:**
> **20 Aug 2nd CLOSED · 21 Aug 2nd CLOSED · 22 Aug BOTH CLOSED**, against 17–19 Aug all OPEN.
> **Those are operational closures, not statutory holidays. No annual calendar contains them.**

> ### ★ LAW 4 — THE GAZETTE LAW. **Any input that is a RATE, PRICE or THRESHOLD set by a public authority is published in an official gazette and archived by statute. RATES ARE NEVER ERASABLE. Only OPERATIONAL STATES are.**
>
> This one sentence deletes four of the seven hunting grounds before a single fetch — tax and customs rates,
> statutory payroll rates, index-linked construction adjustment, regulated benchmark rates, and the Brazilian
> ICMS-ST MVA/PMPF branch. **The live space is not "published numbers." It is "the operating condition of a
> private facility on a named day."**

## 2. THE THREE STRONGEST, ALL DEAD

**① PAYER TRANSPARENCY-IN-COVERAGE FILES — the only instance passing all four properties.**
**45 CFR 147.212(b)(2)(ii)**: files must be *"free of charge and without conditions, such as establishment of a
user account, password, or other credentials"*; (b)(3): *"must **update the machine-readable files… monthly**."*
**No retention duty anywhere in the section** — property 4 satisfied by the rule's own text, the cleanest in the
census. **Dead twice:** Serif Health holds the archive back to **January 2023** and Turquoise tracks 219 payers
*(SECONDARY)* — so `THE HEAD-START ASYMMETRY`, which `CLAUDE.md` says to screen FIRST. **Plus THE VOLUME WALL:**
national payer MRFs run to hundreds of GB per payer per month, which turns the seed's $0,45/month storage into
thousands, blowing C5 on day one.

**② MAJOR NON-INTERSTATE GAS PIPELINE POSTINGS — the cleanest uncoupled window found.**
**18 CFR 284.14(a)(4)**: point-level capacity *"must remain posted for at least a period of one year"* — and the
three-year archive duty in 284.12 binds **interstate** pipelines only, so these carry **no archive duty at
all.** **Dead on three:** no mandatory field consumes it *(property 1 absent — it is world-state data for
traders, i.e. `THE PORT_EVIDENCE_KILL`'s "corroboration nobody asked for")* · occupied by Criterion Research via
ICE and NatGasHub *(SECONDARY)* · and the buyer is a commodity trading desk, the sales-force-gated end of
`THE MONITORING PINCER`.

**③ CBAM ARTICLE 9 FOREIGN CARBON-PRICE DOSSIER.** Reg (EU) 2023/956 art. 6(2)(c) makes the certificate count
after the art. 9 reduction a mandatory declaration field, and art. 9(1) requires *"any rebate or other form of
compensation available in that country"* to be taken into account — scattered across ~80 jurisdictions in local
languages. **Dead on LAW 4** (carbon-price legislation is gazetted and permanently reconstructible) **and on
★ THE CERTIFICATION TRAPDOOR:** art. 9(2) requires the documentation be *"**certified by a person that is
independent** from the authorised CBAM declarant… The name and contact information of that independent person
shall appear on the documentation."* **The rule does not want the fact supplied — it wants somebody's name
attached to it being right. That is the expert veto in statutory form.**
