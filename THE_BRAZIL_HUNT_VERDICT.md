## 2. Head-to-head: CONFORMIDADE against itself

**What I verified today, from primary text, not from vendor blogs:**

**ANVISA RDC nº 1.002, 15/12/2025** (DOU 16/12/2025, Edição 239, Seção 1, p. 191) — Boas Práticas de Funcionamento for dental assistance services and prosthesis labs. Load-bearing articles, read verbatim:

- **Art. 2º** — applies to **all** services providing dental assistance in Brazil, PF or PJ, public, private, philanthropic, civil or military.
- **Art. 6º / Art. 110** — it is **the RT's** responsibility to elaborate and implement the **Série de Documentos de Boas Práticas de Funcionamento (SDBPF)**.
- **Art. 111 III** — the pack must be *"formalmente aprovada e assinada pelo responsável legal do serviço de saúde."*
- **Art. 113** — the 17-item minimum content list.
- **Art. 122 §2º** — *"A elaboração, a implantação e o monitoramento do PGRSS são atividades que podem ser terceirizadas, mediante contratação formal."*
- **Art. 182** — **360 days** from publication to comply. **Art. 185** — non-compliance is an *infração sanitária* under Lei 6.437/1977. **Art. 186** — in force on publication.

**ANVISA Nota Técnica nº 2/2024/SEI/GGTES/DIRE3/ANVISA** (Processo SEI 25351.921737/2023-11, doc. SEI 2790101) — the regulator's own statement that aesthetics establishments are in scope: *"A ausência de regulamento federal específico para estabelecimentos que oferecem serviços de estética classificados como serviços de saúde não os isenta da obrigatoriedade de cumprir as normas sanitárias aplicáveis a todos os serviços de saúde"*, then enumerates RDC 63/2011, 222/2018, 509/2021, 36/2013, 15/2012, 50/2002 as applying to them, and states that for waste *"devem ser apresentados o PGRSS e as cópias do contrato… e da licença ambiental."* It also quotes **RDC 222/2018 art. 10 parágrafo único**: *"A elaboração, a implantação e o monitoramento do PGRSS pode ser terceirizada."*

**Three findings, and one of them is a correction to the brief:**

1. **The mandate is real, is federal, and the regulator explicitly authorises the exact commercial act the business performs.** Outsourcing the plan is written into both the 2018 and the 2025 rules. This is the opposite of `THE COMPLIANCE COSTUME` — it is a mandate that names its own outsourcing.

2. **★ CORRECTION — the rented credential appears not to be required, and that is a demotion, not a promotion.** The brief and `THREE_BRAZIL_IDEAS.md` treat "a contracted credentialed professional signs" as the unlock that C3's amendment bought. But the RDC 222/2018 articles quoted *inside ANVISA's own nota técnica* (arts. 4, 5, 6, 9, 10) contain **no conselho-de-classe or ART requirement** — art. 10 puts responsibility on the generator and permits outsourcing. RDC 1.002 art. 111 III has the pack signed by **the establishment's own responsável legal**. So: cheaper for her (no credential to rent, no professional liability), **but the barrier she assumed was protecting her margin is not there for anyone else either.** The ART requirement, where it exists, is state or municipal. **Do not build the pricing on a credential gate that the federal text does not impose.**

3. **The dental wedge dominates the aesthetics wedge on every axis except her stated interest.** Aesthetics has no dedicated federal norm — only a scatter of general ones, which is a harder sell and a vaguer deliverable. Dental has a dated deadline, a numbered checklist, a named sanction and a biennial revision clock. **`THE FREE-VALIDATOR SCREEN` inverted: sell where the rule is scattered — and RDC 1.002 art. 113 has already done the scattering-into-a-list for you, which is what makes it machine-shaped.**

**Verdict: CONFORMIDADE is not demoted — its wedge is.** The shape (compelled document pack → questionnaire → deterministic generator → file → one-off price → free register distribution) is the strongest thing on the board and is now supported by regulator text I read myself rather than by consultancy blogs. **The v1 spec is the weaker half of its own idea.**

**Two honest scars, named:**
- **Distribution is cold email at volume.** The founder's amended rule bars a business whose *engine* is high-volume cold outbound/SDR closing. Self-serve checkout off a compelled list with a statutory deadline is direct marketing, not SDR — but it is still outbound volume. **The engine must become the channel** (contabilidade firms, dental-supply distributors, franchise networks): one relationship, dozens of packs. Cold email seeds it; it must not remain the engine.
- **`THE KNOWLEDGE-LIABILITY TEST` fires, weakly.** The intake will surface non-compliances the clinic must pay to fix. Turn it into the reason to buy now: **sell the pack plus the gap list**, and make the contract state what the rule already states — the RT elaborates and signs, she prepares.

## 3. Recommendation, and the one test

**Build CONFORMIDADE, pointed at RDC 1.002/2025 dental compliance, priced at a one-off R$1.500 — but do not write a line of code until the probe below returns.**

**Precondition — 10 minutes, before the emails, non-negotiable.** Confirm on ANVISA's own legislation page that **art. 182's 360-day term has not been postponed or amended during 2026.** This is `THE VENDOR-URGENCY LAW` exactly: the reforma tributária play died because a regulator deferred a rule nineteen days before the vendor content was still describing it as live. **If art. 182 has been deferred, the dental arm loses its deadline and everything above reverts to v1.**

### THE TEST — the two-arm deadline probe

**Cost: one domain (~R$40) + a free sending tier. No phone. Five days.**

Pull two lists from the Receita Federal CNPJ open data via `publica.cnpj.ws` — **30 dental (CNAE 8630-5/04)** and **30 aesthetics (CNAE 9602-5/02)** — both filtered to `porte ≠ MEI` with a non-empty `CORREIO ELETRÔNICO`. Send one plain-text email, no pitch, no attachment, three questions:

> *(1) Vocês já montaram a Série de Documentos de Boas Práticas exigida pela RDC Anvisa 1.002/2025 (prazo de 360 dias, art. 182)?* — aesthetics arm: substitute the PGRSS under RDC 222/2018.
> *(2) Quem está preparando — o próprio RT, uma assessoria, ou um modelo pronto?*
> *(3) Se contrataram, qual foi a faixa de preço?*

**Count, do not interpret. Four bars:**

| | Bar | If it fails |
|---|---|---|
| **Reach** | **≥5 replies in at least one arm (≥17%)** | <3 in both arms ⇒ the free register is not a channel for this buyer. Stop; the whole distribution premise goes with it. |
| **Demand** | **≥60% of dental repliers say "not started" or "not sure what that is"** | If most are already done, the wave has passed you. |
| **Price** | **≥3 replies naming ≥R$800** | If the modal number is R$100–300, the catalogue scissors have already closed and R$1.500 is fiction — revert to a channel/wholesale price, not a direct one. |
| **Free front door** | **<25% say a supplier, collector, association or CRO gave it to them free** | ≥25% ⇒ `THE FREE FRONT DOOR` is live and CONFORMIDADE dies where every candidate in this file dies. |

**The arm that clears is the arm you build.** If dental clears and aesthetics does not, build dental and accept that her stated interest is served adjacently — note that **harmonização orofacial is a CFO-registered specialty**, so the dental buyer and the facial-aesthetics buyer are frequently the same person. If neither arm clears, **the board is genuinely empty** and that is the finding to report, not a reason to reprice.
