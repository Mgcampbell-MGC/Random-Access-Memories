# CANDIDATE 64 — CORRECTIONS ADOPTED

**14 Aug 2026. External critique received, verified, and largely upheld.**
**Four of my claims were wrong. The candidate survives, narrowed and repriced.**

## 1. The four errors, verified against sources

### ERROR 1 — "The pregnancy vertical is empty." **FALSE. The critique is right.**

I searched for the *done-for-you written* product and correctly found none. **I never searched the
prompted-app-to-printed-book category in English**, and it is occupied:

| Product | Mechanic | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **[Qeepsake](https://babynotebookapp.com/qeepsake-vs-the-short-years-vs-baby-notebook/)** | Questions **by text message**, answers stored, printed into a book. Starts in pregnancy | **Free lite** (1 question/week, no book) · Plus **$47,88/yr** · Premium **$95,88/yr** + book |
| **[Baby Notebook](https://babynotebookapp.com/pregnancy-memory-books/)** | App with **Pregnancy, Family and Home chapters**, prompts, automated layout | Free app · Premium **$49,99/yr** · book **$99 + shipping** (subscriber), ~$165 full |
| **[The Short Years](https://www.theshortyearsbooks.com/shop/the-short-years-baby-book)** | Prompted binder pages shipped through the year, no subscription | **from $129** |

There is even a published comparison article titled *"Qeepsake vs. The Short Years vs. Baby
Notebook."* This is a **category**, not a gap.

**What survives, and it is narrower than I claimed:** none of the three *writes*. All three are a
structured place for the mother to put her own words. The distinction is exactly one thing —
**editorial transformation** — and the critique states it better than I did:

> *"We interview the mother, rewrite her answers into polished first-person narrative prose, and
> deliver a finite pregnancy memoir she never has to journal or design."*

**And there is a stronger frame available than "empty vertical," which the incumbents' own pricing
hands us.** Qeepsake cannot quote a book price up front *because it depends on how much you
completed*. That is the category confessing its dominant failure mode:

> **THE COMPLETION FRAME — the baby-memory category's product is a container, and most people never
> fill it. The done-for-you version does not sell a nicer container. It sells the finished book.**

### ERROR 2 — "39 books a month." **WRONG, on an exchange rate I did not check.**

I used ~R$5,79/USD. The actual rate on 13 Aug 2026 was **1 USD = 5,1907 BRL**. R$30.000 is
**≈US$5.780**, not US$5.180.

**This propagates to every dollar figure in the rebuild and to the genealogy candidate.** Every
R$ conversion in `CANDIDATE_64_REBUILD.md` and `HUNT_14AUG_TRANSPOSITION.md` is ~11% optimistic.

### ERROR 3 — "LGPD Art. 11 dissolves." **WRONG, and this is the one I should have known.**

**LGPD applies to processing operations carried out in Brazilian territory, regardless of where the
company is domiciled or where the data subject lives.** Sol processing ultrasound images, due dates
and pregnancy narratives *from São Paulo* is processing in Brazil. **LGPD applies. Health data
remains Art. 11 sensitive data.**

What actually dissolved was narrower and I should have said only that: **the clinic-mediated
problem** — she is no longer receiving patient data from a healthcare provider about *their*
patients, which was the hard part. **Her own controller obligations do not dissolve.** The critique's
privacy design is correct and adopted in full: separate explicit consent, optional uploads, cropping
instructions, no medical interpretation, disclosed processors including Lulu, encryption, deletion
of raw uploads ~30 days after delivery, no training or marketing use without separate consent, and
fictionalised prototypes in all public samples.

### ERROR 4 — "Fourteen for fourteen." **Overstated.**

LGPD was wrong, so it is not fourteen. Several of the remaining passes are **provisional, not
established** — print cost, minutes per book, and distribution are all unmeasured. The correct
statement is: **it clears every constraint that can be checked from a desk, and three that matter
most cannot be.**

**The core differentiation stands, and the critique agrees with it** — it calls editorial
transformation *"potentially defensible"* and the production constraints *"the strongest design
decisions in the entire rebuild."* Kept unchanged: the gift/redemption split, the text-only

Short Years are funded competitors **already working Pinterest and baby-shower gift guides.** My
seeding plan is not entering an empty channel — it is entering a contested one. **That raises the
distribution risk beyond what either document said.**

## 3. The corrected model

**Price rises to $179.** The visible category band is $99–165, so the premium must be legible in the
first photograph: a written page, not a filled-in form.

| | |
|---|---|
| Standard hardcover pregnancy memoir | **$179** |
| Founding-customer test price | $149 |
| Second identical copy | +$69 |
| Two-copy gift bundle | $229–239 |
| Digital PDF fallback | $89 |

| Channel | Working cost | Contribution before CAC |
|---|---|---|
| Direct site, $179 | $34–49 | **$130–145** |
| Etsy, $149 | $44–59 *(6,5% + 3% + $0,25 ≈ $14,41)* | $90–105 |

> **R$30.000/month ≈ US$5.780 ≈ 40–45 books.** *(was: 39)*
> **R$12.000/month ≈ US$2.312 ≈ 17 books.**

**Against the Brazilian clinic version's 411 books, the reframe survives completely intact.**

### Labour, staged rather than assumed

Measured **order received → shipment confirmed**, not editing time. Book 1: no limit. **Book 3: ≤45
min. Book 10: ≤30 min. Mature: 20–25 min.** My flat 20 minutes was a maturity figure presented as a
starting figure.

### Build order, corrected

**Place the first 10–20 orders in Lulu manually. Do not integrate the API until the product sells
and print files are reliably accepted.** This is straightforwardly better against *"build yes, bugs
and such after the build."*

## 4. The test, adopted from the critique

**Stage 1 — production.** Three complete books: a cooperative friend, a **disorganised stranger**,
and a mother with **poor images and short answers**. Pass only if landed cost is acceptable, quality
supports $179, book three is under 45 minutes, one correction round suffices, and the prose does
not sound generic.

**Stage 2 — paid demand.** Checkout page at the $149 founding price, 30 Pinterest assets, three
Etsy listings on different gift intents, 30 gift-guide publishers contacted. **Sell five copies to
strangers. Volunteers do not count.**

**Stage 3 — the gift mechanic.** On the first 10 codes: **redemption rate, days to redemption,
intake completion rate, revision rate, gift-buyer support burden.** This is the sharpest thing in
the critique — **the mechanic fails if buyers buy and mothers never redeem**, and it is the same
completion failure the incumbents have. Her odds are better (8 short sessions over weeks, not nine
months of journaling), but better is not measured.

### Kill conditions

- Book three still over 45 minutes of human work
- Landed production plus replacement reserve over $40/book
- Customers will not pay $149
- Fewer than 3 of the first 5 paying customers complete the intake
- More than 20% need a second correction round
- **Zero paid orders after 300 qualified product-page visits**
- **Gift-code redemption under ~60%**
- Customers compare it to a $99 Baby Notebook rather than seeing the ghostwritten distinction

---

## 5. Verdict

**7,8/10 before paid validation is a fair score and I accept it.** The critique did not find a
different business; it found four overstatements in mine and one better sentence for the front page.

**Decision unchanged: ADVANCE. Three prototypes, then five paid orders from strangers. No software,
no API, no permanent price above the founding tier until the prototypes exist.**

The positioning line, taken from the critique verbatim because it is better than mine:

> **A baby-shower gift that turns the mother's voice notes, photographs and memories into a
> beautifully written pregnancy memoir — without asking her to journal, design, or write a book.**
