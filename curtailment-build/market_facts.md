## *** THE CLASSIFICATION IS A BLACK BOX — THIS IS THE PRODUCT ***
- Volt tested MME's **SOSIN formula** and found it produces **143% MORE sobreoferta** than ONS's own calculation.
- Donato da Silva Filho (CEO, Volt Robotics), MegaWhat 23 Jul 2026: *"Não basta informar que um corte foi
  energético. É necessário publicar os dados, as premissas e os critérios que permitam reproduzir e auditar
  essa conclusão."*
- Termo Cl. 4ª makes the generator accept CCEE's volumes AND CLASSIFICATIONS "de modo irretratável e incondicional".
- BUT Art. 5º §6º gives a **contestation window of 10 days** after ONS's consistency analysis (~Mar 2027).
=> **THE SERVICE: re-run the classification of the client's own curtailed hours, hour by hour, and contest
   mis-classified sobreoferta before they irrevocably accept it.** Each percentage point moved from
   sobreoferta to confiabilidade is cash. This is auditable, defensible, repeatable, and nobody is selling it.

## *** THE VETO — LIVE, UNVOTED, AND IT CHANGES EVERYTHING ***
Lei 15.269/2025 sanctioned 24 Nov 2025 (pub. 25 Nov) with 20–22 vetoes.
**VETOED — proposed art. 1º-A**: compensation for ALL production-reduction events caused by factors external
to the plant, regardless of contracting modality or cut origin, EXCEPT sobreoferta, WITH RETROACTIVITY.
**SURVIVED — art. 1º-B**: the narrow version now being implemented by Portaria 140.
Government's stated rationale: the provision could raise tariffs by up to **R$6bn** (press rendering; the
literal razões de veto could not be retrieved — Planalto 503). Sector counter-estimate: **R$2.8bn**.
**OVERRIDE STATUS: removed from the Congressional agenda of 18 June 2026 and NOT voted. As of 26 Jul 2026
the veto has been neither maintained nor overridden.** (Canal Solar 17 Jun 2026)
=> A generator signing the Termo waives, irrevocably, claims on events to 25 Nov 2025 — while Congress may
   still enlarge the underlying right retroactively. THE central advisory question.

## CCEE SETTLEMENT — VERIFIED NEGATIVE
Constrained-off does NOT transit the MCP settlement. CCEE's judicial-adjustments annex (SUM001 Mar/2026)
contains zero curtailment items — all entries are recuperação judicial, contract-registration disputes, or MRE/GSF.
The R$1.09bn / R$310m / R$10bn "retained by liminares" figures circulating are **GSF (hydrological risk)**,
a different dispute. Do not cite them as curtailment.
Apr/26 unpaid MCP R$441.86m, of which only **R$13.67m (3.2%) is court-driven**; R$328.07m is ANEEL
Despacho 413/2026 (suspension of disconnection for Criuva, Serrana, Boa Fé — not curtailment).
