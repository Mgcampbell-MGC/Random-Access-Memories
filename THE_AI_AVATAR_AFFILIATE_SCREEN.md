   - Views and conversions collapsed for *"a solid week"* when Instagram restricted her DM automations
     (`THE PLATFORM-ARBITER LAW`: one account is the whole asset, and the platform can throttle it).
   - `THE PORTFOLIO REQUIREMENT` applies: the visible winners are survivors.
3. **The video sells the community** (`THE CONTENT-ECONOMY SELECTION BIAS`).
   - The pinned CTA is a sales call.
   - A comment says there were *"no time slots for July"*.
4. **★ In the US, an AI character speaking as a customer is a fake testimonial — verified at source 29 Sep 2026.**
   - **16 CFR 465.2(a)** (FTC Trade Regulation Rule on Consumer Reviews and Testimonials): *"It is an unfair or
     deceptive act or practice… for a business to write, create, or sell a consumer review, consumer testimonial… that
     materially misrepresents, expressly or by implication: (1) That the reviewer or testimonialist exists; (2) That
     the reviewer or testimonialist used or otherwise had experience with the product…"*
   - **§465.1(f)** defines a consumer testimonial to include *"depictions of the… likeness… of an individual"* that
     consumers are likely to believe reflect a real user's experience.
   - **§465.2(b)** reaches the brand that disseminates it.
   - ⇒ An AI "dog owner" whose dog got better is squarely inside the rule unless it is disclosed and implies no real
     experience.
   - Brazil already has the same line: CONAR's May 2026 influencer guide §1.3.1 bars misleading AI *"simulações,
     endossos ou testemunhais"* (`O_LANCAMENTO_AIRTIGHT.md` §4.4).
5. **Running US pages from Brazil is a terms risk.**
   - A comment asks how non-US students upload: *"a US proxy with an antidetect browser like Dolphin… separate
     device… multiple accounts."*
   - Hiding location and running multiple accounts is exactly what platforms ban accounts for.
6. **It was already hunted in Brazil:** the TikTok affiliate agency died in the sixth hunt (*"the platform squeezes
   the spread it funds"*, −2 points on 23 Sep 2026).
