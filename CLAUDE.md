# Working rules for this repo

**This repo is a business search for Sol — a solo founder in São Paulo. Seventy-two candidates, one
graveyard, a parts bin, and roughly twenty derived laws. Read this before proposing anything.**

---

## THE SHAPE CHECK — run this before proposing any candidate

**On 17 Aug 2026 the founder caught a failure that had been running for days:** three consecutive
"new candidates" — 70, 71 and 72 — were **one idea with three different buyers.** All three were
*questionnaire → she writes prose → deliverable.* What was being generated was buyers, not ideas.

**The cause was diagnosable.** Optimising for her constraints drives everything toward
prose-from-a-form, because that is the single most constraint-compatible activity available. **It is a
local optimum and the search rolls back into it every time unless something forbids it.**

> ### RULE 1 — Before proposing a candidate, name its SHAPE out loud. If it is the same shape as the last one proposed, STOP and change shape first.

**The shapes:**

| | Shape | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| **S1** | **Written work** | She writes prose. *(70, 71, 72, 64, the whole book family)* |
| **S2** | **Replenishment / merchant of record** | **CLOSED 17 Aug by seven agents — see below** |
| **S3** | **Continuous operation** | The product is that something was watched for ninety days |
| **S4** | **A compounding dataset** | She accumulates what nobody else has |
| **S5** | **Software or a system, sold as a product** | Built once, licensed. Partly tested — app stores and Fiverr |
| **S6** | **Matching two enumerable populations** | She connects, never produces |

**S1 is over-explored and holds the only live candidates. S2 is closed. S3, S4 and S6 have NEVER been
hunted.** If a proposal is S1, it must be justified as a *deliberate* return, not presented as a new idea.

### S2 is closed — do not re-open it without new facts

Seven agents, primary statutory and supplier sources, several correcting each other and two correcting me:

- **The rail works** — Global O-Ring has a self-serve blind-drop-ship checkbox, a $5 minimum, and **card-pay
  that bypasses credit approval**. Copylite's reseller form lists Brazil. **Plumbing was never the problem.**
- **THE FREE FRONT DOOR** — "knowing which part" is the customer-acquisition cost of whoever holds the stock.
  Grainger sells it as a KeepStock service line; CrossFilters gives away *manual* crossing in one business day
  and advertises *"Real inventory, not just data."* **Zero part-identification gigs exist on Fiverr or Upwork.**
- **MERCHANT OF RECORD IS FALSE IN BRAZIL.** RIR/2018 art. 162 §1 makes her *equiparada à pessoa jurídica* by
  operation of law, registered or not. **Two non-creditable turnover taxes stack in opposite directions** —
  Brazilian PIS/COFINS on her gross selling price (not an export, no input credits: SC COSIT 306/2017 and
  31/2023) and US state sales tax on her cost (no resale certificate). **LC 126/2007 art. 20** bars a *pessoa
  física* from the foreign insurance her customers contractually require.
- **THE ONBOARDING LAW** — written-only is compatible with *selling*; it is **not** compatible with *being
  onboarded*. Reseller setup, acquirer risk review, fiscal-rep appointment and dispute escalation are all
  conversations. **Ask this of every future candidate.**
- **THE COMMISSION-AGENT RESOLUTION** — resale is closed by Brazilian tax law; brokerage is closed by
  professional referral-fee rules. **The surviving intersection is commission agency in an industry with no
  referral-fee rule.** *(This corrects parts-bin G7, which said the escape was to resell rather than broker.)*
- **THE RENEWAL SCISSORS** — a regulator needing a filing from 100.000 entities makes the artefact cheap by
  design. **G9 does not transpose to documents: no statute can force an annual DOCUMENT to be expensive.**
- **THE ASSURANCE COUPLING** — enforcement teeth and the absence of a stamp are anti-correlated by design.
  And **THE DOWNSTREAM STAMP**: Companies Act 2006 **s.496** makes the auditor opine on the strategic report,
  so every document nested inside it already has a mandatory stamp downstream.
- **THE COMPLIANCE COSTUME** — six widely-sold "mandatory" replacement cycles are not in the rule text.
  OSHA's own position: ANSI standards bind only if OSHA adopts them, and **Z358.1 and Z308.1 were not
  adopted.** Treat fake mandates as a kill list, never an opportunity list.
- **THE POSTAL LIST LAW** — of eight registers field-inspected, **one publishes email.** Enumerable ≠
  contactable.

> ### RULE 2 — Filter LAST, not first.

A constraint applied early deletes the **component** along with the candidate, and the component was the
valuable half. *"Brazilian eyes on the ground"* was filtered out as a C3 violation, and underneath it sat
the only idea in the file that passes the inversion check — that **local access is genuine arbitrage.**
Hold parts loose, recombine, *then* filter. See `THE_PARTS_BIN.md` and `PARTS_BIN_02_replenishment.md`.

> ### RULE 3 — When an outside model is supplied, ask what HER VERSION OF THE WHOLE MODEL is, not only what parts it donates.

**This is the miss the founder named.** Three industrial replenishment models were supplied. They were
mined for eighteen parts, and the parts were then applied **back onto the existing writing business.**
Nobody asked *what is her replenishment business.* **Extract the parts AND port the model. Both.**

## Evidence standard — non-negotiable

**Four fabricated statistics have been caught in this file.** Two more were caught same-day by agents
auditing their own sources.

- **Every number needs a primary-source URL.** The company's own pricing page, its own docs, a statute, a
  government or trade-body publication.
- **Write "UNVERIFIED" explicitly** where a number cannot be confirmed. A flagged gap is worth more than a
  plausible figure.
- **Never cite SEO content farms or vendor marketing as evidence a requirement exists.** Vendors overstate
  requirements to sell things. *(A research premise supplied to an agent — "brokers discount undocumented
  businesses" — turned out to be sourced only to brokers' own marketing.)*
- **Observed listings beat published claims.** Fiverr's *stated* pricing bands came from SEO blogs at
  $500–1.500; the *observed* live gigs were $15, $30, $45.
- **When a correction lands, propagate it to every law that depends on it.** The graveyard once corrected
  a claim sixty lines below where it was made and never fixed the original.

## Founder constraints — hard, and they do not bend

| | |
|---|---|
| **C1** | **NEVER speaks to anyone.** No calls, video, meetings, voice notes, in any language, ever. Written only |
| **C2** | **Permanently faceless.** No camera, no personal brand, no audience-building. *(Governs PUBLIC facing, not private KYC to a payment processor)* |
| **C3** | **No employees, no contractors, ever.** Vendors and suppliers are allowed; hiring a person is not |
| **C4** | **Never takes custody of a customer credential** or standing access to their system. Work arrives as a file they send |
| **C5** | **A few hundred US dollars, ONE TIME.** Not monthly |
| **C6** | **~30 h/week** |
| **C7** | Personal São Paulo network is not a sales channel |

**Vetoes:** construction as a buyer segment · labour-compliance document chasing · physical goods and
inventory unless made-to-order · **anything requiring her to be believed as an expert** · **anything built
around death** *(added 17 Aug — she is uneasy about it, and it is her life, not an argument)*.

**Not constraints:** the USA is not the only market — UK, EU, Canada and Australia are open, and
**Australian and Northern-Hemisphere seasons are opposite, which fills each other's trough.**

## Things that are settled — do not re-derive them

- **AI on asset marketplaces:** either banned (her skill is worthless) or allowed (price → 0). No third
  door. Closes templates, stock, fonts, SVGs, presets, prompt packs, mockups, icon sets.
- **Written deliverables on Fiverr price at $5–45.** Sampled three times across unrelated categories. Audit
  exposure raises the ceiling but never creates a floor, and the ceiling is held with a credential and a
  name — so she gets the floor of every category she enters.
- **Verification is closed to her.** A physical asset needs a body in a place; a digital asset needs a
  credential into the system that hosts it. She can have neither.
- **Lost-knowledge and documentation products are closed.** Where no third party holds a veto there is no
  buyer; where a veto exists, the veto-holder or a funded vendor publishes the artefact free.
- **App stores take 12–18 months** to a top-quartile solo outcome. Organic discovery for a new indie app is
  effectively zero.
- **Etsy could not be read all day, by four separate agents.** DataDome blocks every method available.
  Zero Etsy prices, sales counts or review counts have ever been obtained here. Any Etsy conclusion needs a
  human with a browser.

## The test design that works

**Twenty real instances, and count how many clear a numeric bar.** Not "get three customers" — measure the
*rate*, because the rate predicts everything after it. Three separate agents independently recommended this
shape for three different verticals.

**And prefer the test that costs nothing and needs no phone.** The best ones in this file: pull a General
Price List under the FTC Funeral Rule and look for a line item · email thirty franchisors one question and
count the answers containing a number · write the same deliverable twice and time both.

## Repo conventions

- Develop on `claude/volta-architecture-design-txe8r5`. Push with `git push -u origin <branch>`.
- **Do not open a pull request unless explicitly asked.**
- **Never put a model identifier in a commit message, a PR, a code comment, or any pushed artefact.**

  part; the candidate is not.**
