# LIBERA — killed 2 Sep 2026. It is PASTA LIBERADA's twin, and its incumbent is already in this file.

**Proposal:** a monthly invoice-release desk. Brazilian cleaning/portaria/facilities suppliers must submit a
documentation folder to each condominium before the invoice is approved. Sol matches documents to employees,
checks them against the client's checklist, splits, renames, uploads through an authorised account, fixes
rejections and returns proof of acceptance. **R$149 per accepted packet, R$1.490/month minimum, 6 suppliers ×
20 packets = R$17.880/month.** Self-rated 7,2/10.

> ### FOUR KILLS, ANY ONE SUFFICIENT. Two of them were available before the proposal was written.

## KILL 1 — IT IS ON THE PERMANENT VETO LIST, BY NAME
`CLAUDE.md`, founder's vetoes: *"construction as a buyer segment · **labour-compliance document chasing** ·
physical goods and inventory…"* — set 13 Aug 2026, and the record states why: *"the latter after the gauntlet
converged on it three times in three different segments."* **LIBERA is labour-compliance document chasing:
payroll evidence, FGTS, INSS, timesheets, benefit receipts, monthly, against a checklist.** It is the fourth
convergence on a category the founder closed permanently.

## KILL 2 — THE HOURS FAIL BY 2,5×, COMPUTED FROM ITS OWN NUMBERS
The proposal states **45 minutes per packet** and **"approximately 25 hours/week"**.

| | |
|---|---|
| Its own model | 6 suppliers × 20 packets = **120 packets/month × 45 min = 90 h**, stated as ~25 h/week ≈ **108 h/month** |
| **Her constraint** | **~43 h/month (10 h/week)** — hard, and the reason three earlier candidates died |
| **At her actual limit** | 34 productive hours after selling ÷ 0,75 h = **45 packets = R$6.705/month** |
| The domestic bar | **R$12.300–15.400/month gross** |
| **Result** | **44–55% of the bar, with zero hours left for selling, QA or exceptions** |

**This is the ten-second kill this file says to run before any research: units × her minutes against 43 hours.**
The proposal appears not to have had the hours constraint, or not to have applied it.

## KILL 3 — IT IS PASTA LIBERADA, ON THE OTHER SIDE OF THE TABLE
**PASTA LIBERADA was the highest-scoring candidate ever recorded here (72/100), survivor of five separate kill
attempts, killed 13 Aug 2026.** Its description: *"cobrança e conferência da documentação mensal do empreiteiro
antes da liberação da medição"* — the monthly labour-document packet that gates a supplier's payment.
**Identical mechanism, identical artefact, identical monthly clock. Even the name is a permutation.**

**★ In fairness, LIBERA picks the better side.** PASTA LIBERADA sold to the *buyer* (the construtora receiving
documents); LIBERA sells to the *supplier* producing them. That is a genuine and smarter difference — the
supplier is the party actually in pain, because its invoice is frozen. **It does not survive kill 4.**

## KILL 5 — THE PRICE IS 4–127× WHAT THE SAME WALLET ALREADY PAYS
Sertras publishes what it charges the **fornecedor** for document analysis — *"O valor é pago pela análise
documental… independente do resultado"*: **Simplificado R$141/ano · Prata R$633–1.030 · Ouro R$1.109–3.055 ·
Platinum R$2.495–4.857.**

**LIBERA asks R$1.490/month = R$17.880/year from that same supplier.** And `THE R$1.200 CEILING` stands: a
**complete** outsourced Brazilian back office — apuração, guias, escrituração, eSocial, folha — sells at
**R$450–1.200/month.** LIBERA prices one sub-task above the whole.

## What is worth keeping
1. **★ The side-of-the-table insight is real and reusable.** PASTA LIBERADA sold to the party holding the
   money; LIBERA sells to the party whose money is held. **The frozen invoice is a sharper pain than the
   compliance risk.** Where a document gate exists, sell to the side that is not being paid.
2. **The recurrence engine is genuinely well-constructed** — monthly by contract, tied to a payment release
   rather than to hypothetical savings, and acceptance is binary and observable. That is a better recurrence
   argument than anything currently on the board, and it is the one thing to steal.
3. **The Receita Federal procurement evidence is real** (R$314–898 per contract per month for buyer-side
   contract-monitoring support) and the proposal is honest that it prices a different service.

## The law it confirms
> **`RULE 1`, fifth occurrence: the same idea with a different buyer is not a new idea.** 70/71/72 were one
> idea with three buyers. PASTA LIBERADA and LIBERA are one idea with two sides of one table. **Before
> proposing a document-packet business, grep this repository for the artefact, not for the buyer.**
