# THE REBUILD — 19 Aug 2026. Ground up, from the money backwards.

**The founder changed two things in one sentence: the target went from R$12.000–15.000/month
(≈US$2.300–2.900) to US$10.000/month NET TAKE-HOME, and the constraints stopped being walls and became
LEVERS WITH PRICES.**

> **This document does not propose a candidate. It works out what the new number makes arithmetically
> possible, and therefore where to point the search. Seventy-two candidates were generated by asking "what
> could she do?" This asks "what must be true of ANY business that pays US$10.000 a month to one person who
> cannot speak, cannot be seen, and cannot hire?" — and then goes looking for things with that property.**

---

## 1. THE GOVERNING ARITHMETIC — one number that reorganises everything

| | |
|---|---|
| Target, net, in her hands | **US$10.000/month** |
| Brazilian burden on a solo operator | **UNDER RESEARCH.** Somewhere between ~6% (Simples Anexo III, best case) and ~33% (Anexo V plus pró-labore INSS and IRPF). **Working figure ~25%, flagged, pending the tax leg** |
| **Gross revenue required** | **≈ US$13.000/month · US$156.000/year** |
| Her time | 30 h/week × 4,33 = **130 h/month** |
| Of which actual delivery, after selling, admin, support | **~78 h/month** *(60% — generous for a solo operator)* |
| **Required realisation** | **≈ US$100 per hour of TOTAL time · ≈ US$167 per hour of DELIVERY time** |

### What that does to the existing board — and it is not a re-ranking this time, it is a kill

**RISKSTORY is the best-constructed hand-production business this file has ever received.** Its own numbers:
**$325 per submission**, one insured, ≤5 locations, ≤3 losses, **≤150 source pages**, one clarification round,
one revision.

> **To gross $13.000/month at $325 a unit she must deliver 40 units a month. In 78 delivery hours. That is
> UNDER TWO HOURS PER UNIT** — intake, reading up to 150 pages of applications and loss runs, drafting,
> building the proof sheet, and handling the revision.
>
> **It is not credible, and it is the best-designed version of the shape.** The weaker versions are worse:
> observed Fiverr written deliverables at **$15 · $30 · $45** realise somewhere between $15 and $30 an hour.
> **She needs five to eleven times that.**

> ### THE PRODUCTION CEILING — at US$10.000/month net, ANY business in which she personally produces each unit of output is arithmetically dead. Not "hard". Dead. The gap is 5–11× and no amount of pricing discipline, proof sheets or slot models closes it. **Her 78 hours must go into BUILDING OR OPERATING AN ASSET, never into producing units.**

**This retires S1 (written work to order) as a target-compatible shape.** It was already the over-explored
local optimum that RULE 1 exists to prevent. It is now also too small. **Both reasons now point the same way,
and the second one is arithmetic rather than judgement.**

---

## 2. WHAT SURVIVES THE ARITHMETIC — four structures, and all four are unhunted

If her hours cannot be the unit of production, revenue must come from somewhere else. There are only four
somewheres:

| | Structure | Where revenue comes from | Shape | Hunted? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **The asset produces the units** | She builds a machine once; it serves customer 2 through 400 at near-zero marginal cost | **S5 software · S4 dataset · S3 watcher** | S5 partly (consumer app stores only) · **S3 and S4 never** |
| **B** | **A slice of someone else's flow** | Commission or take-rate on transactions she introduces but does not perform. Her hours per unit are trivial against the value transferred | **S6 matching · commission agency** | **Never** |
| **C** | **A spread she owns** | Buy at one price, sell at another | **S2** | **CLOSED** — RIR/2018 art. 162 §1 makes her *equiparada à pessoa jurídica*; PIS/COFINS on gross with no input credits |
| **D** | **Rent on something she owns** | Licensing a work, a system, a name, a right | Sub-case of A | Barely |

> **Three of the six shapes have never been hunted — S3, S4 and S6 — and they are exactly the three that
> survive the new arithmetic. That is not a coincidence: they were never hunted BECAUSE the old target was
> small enough that hand-production looked viable. Raising the target has done the search a favour.**

**All six agents currently in the field are pointed at A and B.**

---

## 3. THE REAL BINDING CONSTRAINT — and it is not the one we have been optimising

**Seventy-two candidates. Read the causes of death and they collapse into one sentence:**

> **She has unlimited production and zero distribution. Every candidate has died at the point where a
> stranger has to decide to give her money.**

The veto law, the free front door, the postal list law, the ceiling-holder law, the empty-niche warning, the
app-store finding, the Fiverr floor — **each of those is a distribution failure wearing a different costume.**
Production has never once been the problem.

> ### THE INVERSION — stop searching for a business and then asking how it sells. Find the CHANNELS that demonstrably work for a faceless, voiceless, anonymous foreign operator, and fit a product to the channel. **Channel first. Product second.**

### And the target now tells us which channels are even eligible

$13.000/month decomposes into price × customers, and each row demands a different channel:

| Price | Customers needed | The only channels that deliver that many |
|---|---|---|
| **$29/mo** | **448** | High-volume self-serve only — SEO, marketplace, paid ads |
| **$99/mo** | **131** | Self-serve, marketplace, or sustained cold email |
| **$299/mo** | **43** | Cold email · marketplace · partner |
| **$999/mo** | **13** | **Partner / white-label — thirteen relationships** |
| **$2.500/mo** | **5** | Five relationships, each of them a negotiation |

> **THE DISTRIBUTION SCISSORS — being FACELESS pushes her toward many small self-serve customers, because a
> relationship needs a person. Being unable to BUILD AN AUDIENCE or run a sales motion pushes her toward few
> large customers, because reaching 448 strangers requires either a following or a budget. The two constraints
> point in OPPOSITE DIRECTIONS. That tension is the actual strategic problem of this business, and every
> candidate so far has ignored it.**

**There are exactly two resolutions, and both are now open:**

**DOOR 1 — BORROWED INTENT.** Sell self-serve at $99–299 inside a marketplace that already has buyers with
their wallets out. This solves facelessness and volume simultaneously, because the marketplace supplies the
trust she cannot. *The file's "app stores are dead" finding was about CONSUMER MOBILE app stores. B2B
software marketplaces — Shopify, Xero, QuickBooks, Atlassian, HubSpot, Monday, RapidAPI — are a different
animal with buyer intent and a business budget. That distinction has never been drawn here and is now under
research.*

**DOOR 2 — THE INVISIBLE DEPARTMENT.** Ten to thirteen white-label partners at $999–2.500/month, each one
reselling her capacity to their own clients under their own name. *"You own the relationship. We're your
invisible department."* **This door was CLOSED yesterday by THE ONBOARDING LAW — being onboarded as a vendor
is a conversation. The founder opened it today by allowing setup calls. It is the single largest change in
this file's history.**

## 4. THE LEVER TABLE — every constraint priced, not just listed

**The founder's instruction: these are levers, not a checklist. So here is what each one costs to hold.**

| | Constraint | Status today | **What holding it COSTS** | What moving it would buy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C1** | Never speaks | **MOVED — setup calls now allowed, ongoing operations still written-only** | Held fully, it closed every professional onboarding, every vendor security review, every partner agreement | **Already banked: DOOR 2 reopens.** Highest-value move available and it has been made |
| **C2** | Permanently faceless | **HELD** | **Now the most expensive constraint in the file.** Almost all solo software is sold on a founder's audience. It closes content, community, build-in-public, podcasts, YouTube, and reputation pricing | Identity, not economics. Not proposed for moving — but the price should be known |
| **C3** | No people, ever | **HELD** | Caps output at ~78 delivery hours forever. **This is what makes §1 arithmetic binding** | Note the asymmetry: she may buy MACHINES and VENDORS without limit. Capacity is purchasable; labour is not |
| **C4** | No customer credentials | **HELD** | Closes most B2B SaaS (OAuth to the customer's account), all bookkeeping and ops work, all monitoring that needs access | What survives: products that process **a file the customer sends**, or watch something **public** |
| **C5** | Capital | **MOVED — tiered** | At <$500: no entity, no counsel, no data acquisition, no paid test | **$500–2.500** buys an entity and tooling. **$2.500–10.000** buys a real paid-acquisition test or a licensing opinion. *The exact figure is the open question below* |
| **C6** | ~30 h/week | **HELD** | The 78-hour delivery budget in §1 | — |
| **C7** | SP network not a channel | **HELD** | Removes the one warm channel she has | — |
| **V1** | Must never be believed as an expert | **HELD** | **The highest body count of any rule here.** Kills advisory, interpretation, opinion, assurance, and anything priced on credibility | What survives: **the artefact proves itself** (the proof sheet) · **the buyer supplies the domain and she supplies throughput** · **the buyer supplies the "before" and she returns the after** |
| **V2** | Nothing built around death | **HELD** | Removes the best-evidenced buyer on the board (funeral homes: exogenous volume, cost is their own hours) | Not proposed for moving. It is her life, not an argument |

## 6. BRAZIL DOMESTIC IS A FIRST-CLASS MARKET — and it may be the best one

**Re-stated by the founder, 19 Aug: the business does not have to sell to the USA.** This has been treated
as a footnote for seventy-two candidates and it should not have been.

**What selling domestically REMOVES — and these are the exact things that killed candidates here:**

| Killer | Domestic status |
|---|---|
| The cross-border tax pincer that closed **S2** — PIS/COFINS on gross with no input credits *(SC COSIT 306/2017, 31/2023)* stacked against US state sales tax on her cost | **Does not arise** |
| **LC 126/2007 art. 20** barring a *pessoa física* from foreign insurance her customers require | **Does not arise** |
| **THE ONBOARDING LAW** — acquirer risk review, fiscal-rep appointment, vendor security diligence, all by voice | **Much thinner.** Pix settles in seconds, is effectively free, has no acquirer risk review and no chargeback arbitration to be argued by telephone |
| The foreign-anonymous-vendor trust penalty — a São Paulo stranger handling a US broker's confidential client files | **Does not arise** |
| **THE POSTAL LIST LAW** — of eight foreign registers, one published email | **Under field inspection now.** The Receita Federal public CNPJ dataset is a free bulk download of every registered company in Brazil; whether it carries email is being verified rather than assumed |

**And it activates the one genuine arbitrage this file has ever identified.** RULE 2 records that *"Brazilian
eyes on the ground"* was filtered out early as a constraint violation, and that underneath it sat **the only
idea in the file that passes the inversion check — local access is real arbitrage.** Domestically she has
local access for free: native Portuguese, local context, local time zone, local data.

**What it COSTS, stated honestly:**

- **Price points are lower.** ~US$13.000/month ≈ **R$70.000/month** from Brazilian SMEs implies materially
  more customers than the same revenue from US or UK buyers. This is being quantified with observed prices.
- **THE CURRENCY DIRECTION LAW says earn hard, spend soft.** Earning BRL domestically earns the *spending*
  currency — worse for resilience, and worse still under the assumed 2027–28 crash.

### The configuration that gets both — and this is the strongest structural idea in the rebuild

> ### THE ASYMMETRIC PAIR — put the SOURCE in Brazil and the BUYER abroad.
>
> **Brazilian raw material is cheap, published in enormous volume, badly formatted, and protected by
> language — which is her arbitrage and costs her nothing. Foreign buyers pay in hard currency — which is
> THE CURRENCY DIRECTION LAW. The configuration that satisfies both at once is: SOMETHING OBTAINABLE ONLY IN
> BRAZIL, SOLD TO SOMEONE WHO IS NOT IN BRAZIL.**

**It survives every constraint by construction:** the data proves itself, so she is never believed as an
expert · the source is public, so she never holds a customer credential · the asset compounds, so her hours
do not produce the units · the buyer is foreign, so she is paid in dollars · and being anonymous is normal
for a data vendor in a way it is not for a writer.

**Concrete seed, now under research on legs 4 and 6:** foreign parties who must understand or screen a
Brazilian counterparty — exporters and importers, compliance and KYC vendors, trade finance, foreign counsel,
and market-entry work — against material that exists only in Portuguese and mostly only in PDF: the CNPJ
register and *situação cadastral* changes, CEIS/CNEP debarment, PNCP procurement, INPI's weekly RPI, the
Diário Oficial, DataJud. **Whether anyone actually pays for this is the open question, and it is the right
kind of open question: a demand test, not a legal one.**

## 7. What the founder settled today, and what it rules out

| Question | Answer | **Consequence** |
|---|---|---|
| **Is $10.000 a destination or a requirement?** | **Destination, 12–18 months** | **Rules OUT anything with a two-year discovery curve** — organic SEO as a primary channel, and any dataset that must compound for a year before the first sale. **Requires revenue to start by roughly month 6**, so the business must be sellable while still incomplete |
| **Capital, one time?** | **US$500–2.500** | Buys an entity, tooling, domains, data acquisition and a small acquisition test. **Rules OUT paid acquisition as a CHANNEL** (it buys a test, not a customer base), a licensing or regulatory opinion, and **acquiring an existing cash-flowing asset** |
| **Where is the line on faceless?** | ***"Open to it if it makes sense, but the idea needs to make sense"*** | **The default stays anonymous. A company brand or a pen name is a lever available ON ARGUMENT — it must never be the thing that rescues a weak idea.** No candidate may depend on a persona for its viability |

### Which leaves exactly three channels alive

**Cheap enough for $500–2.500, fast enough for 12–18 months, and compatible with having no face:**

1. **Cold email** — the only channel that is simultaneously cheap, fast and faceless. Its constraint is
   legality and deliverability for a foreign sender, which is why leg 3 is critical path. *(Note: CASL and
   Australia's Spam Act require consent and effectively bar cold B2B mail; the US permits it under
   conditions; **Brazil's LGPD position is being checked and may be the most permissive of all** — that would
   be a significant finding.)*
2. **Borrowed intent — a marketplace that already has buyers.** Costs nothing, supplies the trust she cannot.
3. **White-label partners — thirteen relationships at ~$999.** Opened today by the C1 relaxation.

**Everything else — SEO, ads, content, community, audience — is either too slow, too expensive, or forbidden.
The search is now for a product that fits one of those three channels, not for a business that happens to be
possible.**

## 8. What is now assumed, and should be corrected if wrong

1. **US$10.000/month is NET, in her hands, after Brazilian tax** — not gross revenue, not company profit.
2. **C1 is relaxed at SETUP ONLY** — a bounded number of one-off calls to open a supplier account, pass a
   payment-processor review, instruct counsel, or sign a partner. **Ongoing customer contact stays written.**
3. **C5 is now tiered rather than "a few hundred, once."** Every agent is reporting cost at
   **<$500 / $500–2.500 / $2.500–10.000** so the answer does not depend on guessing the figure.
4. **C2, C3, C4, C6, C7 and both vetoes are unchanged and are being enforced.**

> **The old file asked "what can she do given these constraints?" and kept arriving at prose-from-a-form,
> because that is the most constraint-compatible activity in existence. The new question is "what pays
> $13.000 a month to a machine and a channel?" — and her constraints are then applied as the LAST filter, not
> the first, exactly as RULE 2 requires.**
