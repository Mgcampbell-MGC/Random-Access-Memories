# COUNCIL — THE MERCHANT'S SEAT

**UK homewares brand, £1,2M/year, Shopify + Amazon UK, 3.400 SKUs, two staff, Amazon DE launch this
quarter.** Chose that seat because *"if they can't sell me, they can't sell anyone."*

> **VERDICT: BUY AT A DIFFERENT PRICE.** Would risk **$149 on the sample this week**. Will not climb
> to $899–4.999.

---

## 1. The objection is not the one I have been trying to solve

> ***"My product catalogue is 95% public — it's literally on my website and my Amazon listings.
> Anyone can scrape it today.***… *The only sensitive columns in a Shopify export are cost-per-item
> and the vendor field… I can delete two columns before exporting. GDPR is a non-issue — there's no
> personal data in a product file.* **So data security is not my real objection, and if the founder
> fixes 'trust' they've fixed nothing."**

**I have spent this whole assessment treating facelessness as the core risk. The buyer says it is
not.** What he actually wants is *accountability machinery*:

> *"When Matrixify breaks, I have support and 1.000 reviews. When a Fiverr seller botches it, I have
> escrow. When this company botches 2.500 adjudication calls, **I have a Stripe receipt and a
> contact form.**"*

**His stated fix, which is buildable and cheap:**

1. **A diff report of every change, with a stated reason for each.**
2. **A "flagged for your decision" pile — never silent adjudication of anything ambiguous.**
3. **A redo-or-refund term in writing.**

> *"**Accountability mechanisms, not biography.**"*

## 2. Where it actually dies: the import

> *"The reason my catalogue is a mess is a bulk import in 2023 that duplicated 400 products and
> unlinked half my variant images… Handing me a 'better file' hands me back exactly the part of the
> job I'm scared of, and now the file contains **2.500 changes I didn't make and can't fully
> review.** **A better file is maybe 40% of my problem.**"*

And the sentence to sit with:

> *"**LitExtension charges from $129 and a named human performs the migration for me. They're
> cheaper than this AND they press the button.**"*

**But he also handed over the workaround that does not require a credential:**

> deliver the file **pre-formatted for Matrixify** (which supports partial imports and **generates a
> rollback export first**) · a **change log so he can spot-check the 50 riskiest edits** ·
> instructions to **run it against a duplicate dev store first** · and *"if the import corrupts, we
> fix the file free within 48h."*

**That is a way to sell the import safety without ever holding a login.**

## 3. The 48-hour promise is worth nothing — and is actively harmful

> *"I don't care. This is a once-every-few-years job that has waited three years already… **48 hours
> slightly worries me, because it says 'automated pipeline', and I can get automated for $9.**
> Speed is worth nothing here. **Certainty is worth everything.**"*

**Delete the 48-hour promise. It signals the wrong thing to the only buyer who would pay.**

## 4. Price

> *"$899 for 500 products — my gut said **'that's agency money from a vendor with no agency behind
> it.'** I'd have guessed **$200–400**."*

- **Buy-without-thinking: $149.** *"That's exactly where they put the sample, which is well judged."*
- **Next rung he'd pay without agonising: ~$399 for 500.**
- At 3.400 SKUs he lands on the **$2.999–4.999 rungs, where "Acelerar costs the same and comes with
  humans I can shout at."**

**And the sharpest line of the day:**

> *"**Anonymous-plus-premium is the one combination that doesn't work on me. I'll pay premium to the
> accountable, or pennies to the anonymous. Premium to the anonymous is asking me to fund their
> reputation with my catalogue.**"*

Asked to choose between a **$120 Fiverr gig with 200 reviews** and **her page at $899**: *"Fiverr —
and it's not close, and it's not really about the $779."*

## 5. The trigger — and it confirms the red team exactly

> *"The week I connect Amazon DE and run **Build International Listings**… and it chokes, because
> BIL faithfully exports my existing mess into a new language, and **the DE flat file bounces with
> 600 validation errors five weeks before my planned launch.**"*
>
> *"**Without a channel-expansion event or a platform migration, this stays on my someday list
> forever** — a messy catalogue that's already selling £1,2M is, day to day, a tolerable disease."*

**The red team independently proposed "merchants opening a new marketplace" as the surviving
segment. The buyer just named the same moment, unprompted, with a date and a failure mode.** That
is the strongest convergence of the day.

## 6. His first question — and the answer that closes the tab

> *"Which number am I paying on — 3.400 SKUs or 1.100 parent products? And when you say 'categories
> mapped for each channel': do I get back **an actual Amazon.de flat file with valid product-type
> and browse-node values that passes Amazon's file validation**, or a CSV with a 'suggested
> category' text column that I still have to transpose myself?"*
>
> **Tab-closing answer:** *"we return one enriched master CSV and you adapt it to each channel."*
> → *"**this is a $9 product wearing an $899 price.**"*

**The unit of sale must be defined, and the deliverable must be a per-channel importable file — not
a suggestion column.**

## 7. What he would buy that is not on offer — all three worth more than the core

| Want | His price |
|---|---|
| **Repair + press the import** — dev store, verified, then production, with rollback | **£2.500–3.000** — *more than her top rung*, because it removes the part he fears instead of returning it |
| **German localisation better than BIL's machine translation** — real DE titles, keywords, attribute values for 3.400 SKUs | **£1.500+** — *"the expensive problem in my quarter"*, adjacent to what she already does, **absent from the page** |
| **Catalogue-health monitor**, emails when a supplier feed or staff edit re-breaks something | **£50/month** — *"the disease is chronic and they're only selling the surgery"* |

## 8. What this changes

1. **Stop solving anonymity. Start building accountability** — diff report, flagged-decisions pile,
   written redo-or-refund. Cheap, concrete, and it is what the buyer actually asked for.
2. **Kill the 48-hour promise.** Replace with certainty language and a booked delivery date.
3. **Reprice: $149 sample, ~$399 at 500.** $899 loses in four comparison tabs.
4. **Sell to the channel-expansion moment**, not to "merchants with messy catalogues."
5. **The deliverable is a per-channel importable file that passes the platform's own validator**, or
   it is a $9 product.
6. **Solve the import fear without a credential** — Matrixify-ready output, rollback-first
   instructions, dev-store rehearsal, change log, free fix if it corrupts.
7. **Localisation is a bigger, unserved, adjacent problem** — and it is squarely within
   "writing and organising with AI doing the heavy lifting."
