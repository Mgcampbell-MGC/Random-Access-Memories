## 4. Writing-dominant or design-dominant? The answer is the worst possible split

> **The value proposition is writing-LED. The pricing power and the moat are OBJECT-and-BRAND.**

**Writing is genuinely what is sold.** Every leader counts it — *"150 questions and wildcards"*, *"170
prompts"* — and TableTopics prints sample questions in the listing. WNRS is purely typographic, no
illustration. So this is *not* an artist's deck.

**But the writing does not command the price. Look where the money sits inside the same ladders:**

- **WNRS Ick $9 and Healing $12 versus Couples $20** — same author, same writing quality, **thin object,
  one-third the price**
- **WNRS 365 at $25** — the value is *"365 scratch off cards"* plus *"1 heart-shaped scratch tool."* The
  mechanism is the product
- **WNRS XXX at $30**, the top of the range, adds a consent-form notepad and transparent cards
- **WNRS Kids at $25**, above the $20 flagships, adds coloured pencils and a notepad
- **TableTopics: identical question-writing sells at $25 in an acrylic block and $7,20 in a "To Go" pack —
  a 3,5× differential created entirely by the object**
- BestSelf's **$39** deck is the one described as *"thoughtfully **designed**… five thoughtfully
  **designed** phases"*

**And distribution is the part she structurally cannot copy:** WNRS was built on a **founder-fronted
Instagram audience** — closed to a permanently faceless, voiceless operator.

**And the writing is not defensible.** Questions are short, unprotectable in any practical sense, trivially
transcribed from a photograph of the deck, and now generatable in bulk at zero marginal cost. **There is no
version of this where the writing is the moat.**

## 5. THE FINDING THAT GENERALISES — the market solved fulfilment by not fulfilling

Every legacy, family-history and grief question product the agent surfaced is a **digital download**:

*"Grandparent Conversation Starter Cards (**printable PDF Download**)"* · *"Grandparent Interview Cards
(**digital Download**)"* · *"Life-story Interview Kit | **Printable** Legacy & Memory Journal | Instant
Download"* · *"Grief Question Cards… **A4 printable PDF**, 9 cards per page"* · *"Family & Ancestry: 122
Genealogy Questions… **12 printable pages**"*

> **"The market has already solved fulfilment by not fulfilling. Your direct substitute is a PDF of the
> same writing with zero COGS, zero shipping, zero border, and instant delivery. That is who sets the
> price the buyer will accept for the WRITING, because the writing is all you are both selling."**
>
> **"If any version of this idea survives, it is the PDF, not the deck. The deck is the part that loses
> money."**

**This is the second independent agent to reach that conclusion by a different route.** Agent 9 found the
$200–600 band occupied by short written pieces delivered as documents; agent 1 found the object is where
the margin dies. **Two agents, two methods, one answer: when the writing is the value, the winning form
has no object in it.**

**That is precisely candidate 70's shape** — and candidate 70 was derived independently, from the
funeral-home channel.

## 7. Two more disqualifiers, either sufficient alone

- **She cannot see her own product.** The one compliant manufacturer states **"Brazil and Belarus currently
  unavailable"** for shipping. She would ship a physical object she has never touched, to strangers, sight
  unseen. *(TGC's white-label status — packing slips, invoices in the box, logo on packaging — is also
  **UNVERIFIED**, and the agent flagged it as something to settle by email.)*
- **MakePlayingCards is China-based**, so goods cross a border into a market where the **de minimis
  exemption was indefinitely suspended** (Federal Register, 24 June 2026, codifying E.O. 14324, effective
  29 Aug 2025). MPC's own shipping page claims *"no import tax and customs duty issues for shipments to US
  addresses"* — but that same page still references *"annual factory holidays… resume back to normal on 15
  February 2015*," so the page is partly unmaintained. **UNVERIFIED in practice.**

## 9. Where the sweep leaves the board

**ASSEMBLY 1 — "the object doesn't have to be a book" — FULLY DEAD.** Card decks die on inverted unit
economics. Puzzles, fabric, wallpaper, 3D printing, engraving and board games are design- or
engineering-dominant. Newspapers cost $74 a copy at quantity one. Text prints need 5× their observed
ceiling. **The premise was right and worth testing — the object genuinely did not have to be a book — and
the answer came back that it should not be an object at all.**

**ASSEMBLY 2 — dead** on the verification pincer plus zero observed demand.

**ASSEMBLY 3 — dead** on the Veto Law. **But its death-admin half produced CANDIDATE 70.**

**ASSEMBLY 4 — half falsified.** The recession is not an acquisition channel, and sentiment survives a
downturn only at low ticket.

**ONE SURVIVOR FROM ELEVEN AGENTS: CANDIDATE 70** — the human-written memorial narrative, sold wholesale to
funeral homes, delivered as a document, from a written questionnaire, on a deadline.

**And it is the only candidate that every independent finding points toward rather than away from:** short
not book-length (agent 9) · deadline-bound, where urgency carries a premium (agent 9) · written-only intake
as a normal priced tier (agent 9) · **no object, because the object is where the margin dies (agents 1
and 3)** · a channel that already buys third-party vendors and already sells written life stories, with no
vendor built (agent 6) · actuarial demand with a cremation tailwind (agent 8) · and none of the eight Etsy
mechanics that killed the book (agent 10).
