# ★★★ #1 — CONFORMIDADE · the compelled-document machine
**Shape (Rule 1): S5, a system sold as a product, distributed by COMPULSION.**

**The mechanism.** Every aesthetics clinic, dental clinic, veterinary consultório and medical practice in
Brazil needs a **licença sanitária**, and the **PGRSS** (Plano de Gerenciamento de Resíduos de Serviços de
Saúde, mandatory under **RDC 222/2018**) gates it. The industry's own phrasing: ***"Sem ele, sua licença não
sai."*** A structured web questionnaire feeds a deterministic generator; **a contracted credentialed
professional reviews and signs**; the pack is delivered as a file.

**Two clocks on the same buyer — this is what makes it a business rather than a product:**
1. **ANNUAL** — PGRSS + licence renewal pack. Documented annual revision is the recommended practice.
2. **PER-PROCEDURE** — the **termo de consentimento** and **prontuário** are mandatory on every procedure and
   are the clinic's only defence when a patient sues, which in Brazilian aesthetics is common.

**Arithmetic — comfortable, not tight:**
- R$700/pack ⇒ **89 packs/month** (1.072/year).
- Population: ~45.000 aesthetics clinics alone (**R$40bn sector, +12%/yr, avg clinic R$70.000/month at
  20–40% net margin** — these are NOT the R$700/month SMEs the `R$1.200 CEILING` was derived from), plus
  dental, vet and medical.
- At 200.000 establishments × R$700 ⇒ **R$140M/year addressable**; target needs **0,54% penetration.**
- `MARKET-MULTIPLE RULE` (need 3–5×): requires R$2,25–3,75M reachable. Satisfied by two orders of magnitude.

**Distribution — free, and already verified in this file:**
- **Receita Federal CNPJ open data**: free, monthly, publishes `CORREIO ELETRÔNICO`, filterable by CNAE
  (9602-5/02 estética · 8630-5/04 odonto · 7500-1/00 veterinária), porte and capital social. **~76% email
  fill measured.** ⚠ Use `publica.cnpj.ws` — `minhareceita.org` and `brasilapi` **silently redact email**.
- **The accountant channel (`X5`)**: *escritórios de contabilidade* are the ONE structurally written-only
  Brazilian SME segment, and each already serves dozens of these clinics. One firm = dozens of packs.
- **Franchisors**: beauty/wellness franchising grew 16,5% in 2024. One sale = 20–100 units.

**⚠ Honest weaknesses.** Occupied by environmental consultancies (Alleanza, MKAPLUS, Seven Resíduos, Inguz)
and undercut by template sellers at R$100–300. **No consultancy publishes a price — every one says "depends,
contact us" — so the R$700 price point is UNVERIFIED and is the first thing to test.** And whether a
machine-generated pack is accepted by a given municipal vigilância sanitária is unknown.

**The test, cheap and written-only:** email 30 clinics from the CNPJ file asking what they paid for their last
PGRSS and who signed it. **Count the answers containing a number.** Separately, ask 3 environmental
consultancies for a quote as a clinic. Cost: R$0.

# ★★ #2 — SINAL · recovering the quantified no-show
**Shape: S3, continuous operation, priced on an outcome she can now observe.**

**The mechanism.** No-shows are the measured #1 operational loss: **one R$350 procedure lost per day =
R$7.700/month per clinic.** The fix is known — WhatsApp reminders + active waitlist + **a Pix deposit taken at
booking** — and reportedly cuts no-shows **40–60%**. Pix makes the deposit free and instant, which is why this
is newly possible in Brazil specifically.

**Why it isn't just another clinic system.** It sits as a **layer on top of whatever they already run**.
Clinics will not switch systems; they will bolt something on. **And C4's relaxation is what makes the pricing
work:** she can now see the recovered bookings, so a share-of-recovery price is collectable — which
`THE SELF-REPORTED-CONVERSION TRAP` previously forbade.

**Arithmetic:** R$600/month ⇒ **104 clients**. If a clinic recovers even R$2.500/month of R$7.700, R$600 is an
easy yes.

**⚠ Honest weaknesses.** The category is crowded — Belle, Clinicorp, ByDoctor, Quark, Iter, Versatilis — and
**Clinicorp prices at R$159,90 (Standard) / R$369,90 (Premium)**, which anchors the whole market low. 104
paying clients is a real sales operation even with free lists. **The 40–60% reduction figure is a software
vendor's own marketing — SECONDARY, and it flatters.**

**The test:** ask 20 clinics one question — *"how many no-shows did you have last week, and do you take a
sinal?"* If most already take a deposit, the wedge is gone.

# ★ #3 — PLANO PET · subscription infrastructure for the fragmented half
**Shape: S5 sold through a channel — deliberately a different buyer from #1 and #2.**

**The mechanism.** Brazil is the **world's #3 pet market: R$77,96bn in 2025 → R$81,24bn projected 2026.**
**Small and medium petshops are 48,1% of sector revenue** — Petz+Cobasi merged holds only **11% share**. The
named 2026 trend is *"planos mensais de banho e tosa e assinaturas de ração"* to create predictable cash.
Small shops want recurring revenue and have no infrastructure to run it: recurring Pix/card billing, plan
credits, scheduling, no-show handling.

**Why it is here:** biggest market of the three, most fragmented, a genuine consumption clock forming, and it
is female-friendly and emotionally warm.

**⚠ Honest weakness — and it is the reason this ranks third:** the wallet is likely the smallest of the three,
the buyer is the most price-sensitive, and **I have no evidence of what a Brazilian petshop pays for software.
This is the least-verified of the three and I am not going to pretend otherwise.**
