### Studio production rules as they apply here (non-negotiable)

1. **The pack is never drawn by a generator.** Every product pixel is a Blender Cycles render of real geometry
   wearing the code-built master texture, or a code composite of such a render. Generated scenes contain only a flat
   matte chroma-blue stand-in (§D.9). **No AI edit is ever applied after the pack is in the frame.**
2. **All ad type is set by code beside the pack, never on it.** No generator writes a single letter, anywhere.
3. **People are fictional adults, always disclosed.**
   - **Disclosure:**
     - *imagem ilustrativa criada com IA* on every presenter or people frame;
     - *render 3D ilustrativo* on every product render.
   - **Speech:**
     - no testimonial lines;
     - the presenter speaks to *você* about *ela* and never claims a mother of her or his own.
   - **The pack:** never in a presenter's hand. A presenter scene contains no product at all.
4. **Claim discipline.** Until each master view passes the one-time unwrap check with a planted error (§D.7), every
   file and caption says only ***render 3D, textura do arquivo-mestre***, never *verificado*.
5. **Fire safety is a per-frame rule.** §D.8 runs as a checklist on every delivered frame.

### A.5 Insight

**She was your first fan. You have never been hers.** (*Ela foi sua primeira fã. Você nunca foi fã dela.*)

1. **Her side: the origin story.**
   - Every Brazilian's first stage was the school Dia das Mães show: a sunflower costume, a TNT backdrop, a forgotten
     step, a wave at the wrong mother.
   - She sat in the front row and filmed it with her finger over the lens.
2. **Your side: the master metaphor.** Gen Z is Brazil's most fan-literate generation. They queue for 48 hours outside
   the Allianz Parque, know the setlist and fight for the *grade*.
3. **The language.** Portuguese describes her whole life with the idiom *longe dos holofotes*. **HOLOFOTE hands Gen Z a
   role they have rehearsed for years and gives it a new headliner.**

### A.6 Personality

**The stadium announcer with a lump in his throat.** Loud, deadpan and ceremonial, then suddenly tender.

- **Generous**: the joke is always on the child.
- **Exact**: names a real thing every time.
- **Never sarcastic about her.**

It has three voices, each with its own typeface. The voice map is a rule.

| Voice | Who | Register | Typeface | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **O LOCUTOR** | The announcer: the brand at full volume | Capitals, short declaratives, ceremonial deadpan | Special Gothic (Expanded One / wght 700) | *A ATRAÇÃO É ELA.* |
| **A PRODUÇÃO** | The crew: practical, backstage | Lower case or small caps, terse, numbers and times. **Safety, prices and instructions live here.** | Special Gothic Condensed One | *sessões de até 4 h.* |
| **A FÃ** | You, the child: affectionate | Lower case, ≤6 words, one punctuation mark | Shantell Sans | *(girassol, 2007)* |

**Two exceptions:**
- The tagline ***holofote nela.*** is the Locutor in lower case.
- The only exclamation mark in the brand is inside the name ***MAIS UM!***.

### A.7 Tone of voice rules

**DO**
1. **Land the joke on the child.**
   - Always *o seu pior show*, *abertura: você*.
   - Never her age, body, cooking, tech skills or sacrifice.
   - *Zoeira com respeito.*
2. **Speak show, not slang.** The brand dictionary:
   - *deixa, camarim, coxia, primeira fila, sinal, bis, temporada, ingresso, setlist, abertura, atração, turnê, grade,
     passagem de som, equipe técnica*.
   - *A grade* is the only Gen Z fandom word, and it appears once (Ad 3A).
3. **Lead with a Brazilian specific in every piece:**
   - *girassol, TNT, prézinho, festa junina, bigode de lápis de olho, feira de ciências, pronto-socorro às 3h,
     monobloco branco, dedo na lente*.
   - Specificity is what stops it reading as a US "mom tour tee" bootleg.
4. **Applaud her; never thank her.**
5. **Speak to *você* about *ela*.** The presenter never says *minha mãe*.
6. **Keep sentences short.** One idea per line. Full stop.

**DON'T**
1. *obrigada, mãe*; *rainha do lar*; *guerreira*; *melhor mãe do mundo*; *Mãe nota 10*; *ser mãe é padecer no
   paraíso* played straight.
2. **"Mãe/mother" as diva slang**, *serviu*, *entregou*, *a mãe tá on* (blocked in every personalisation field), drag
   or ballroom codes.
3. **Testimonials or claims of experience:**
   - *eu uso*, *comprei*, *minha mãe amou*, *me salvou*, *não vivo sem*;
   - any result or calm claim (*relaxa*, *acalma*, *equilibra*, *aromaterapia*, *purifica*);
   - anything *para gestantes* or *para o quarto do bebê*.
4. **Scolding.** *Seja fã. Pelo menos no domingo.* is cut. A roast is fine; a lecture is not.
5. **Memorial phrasing** in any ad: *acende a luz pra ela*, *pra quem partiu*, a lone candle captioned in the dark
   about her absence.
6. **Real artists, tours, festivals, venues or ticketing brands**: no Taylor/eras, friendship bracelets, K-pop
   lightsticks, *Acústico MTV* styling. **No WhatsApp or Pix marks**; the word *Pix* appears once (C07).
7. **Millennial tics:** *rsrsrs*, Title Case, emoji in brand copy, *gente*, *oi gente*.

## B. Product: **A TURNÊ**, the collection

Four candles, four moments of **her** show: before, during, the encore, the quiet version. They are numbered as album
tracks (*FAIXA 01–04*). A row of them reads ***MÃE MÃE MÃE MÃE***.

**Common to every candle:**
- **Wax:** *cera vegetal de coco e soja*, natural and undyed, render colour CERA `#F3E9D6`. It must contain no paraffin,
  so the CandleScience "coconut apricot" blend is excluded.
- **Fragrance load:** 6–8%, a direction to the candle maker, **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Wick:** **one wood wick** with a booster ply, 12,7 × 0,7 mm on 200 g and 9,5 × 0,6 mm on 80 g, trimmed 5 mm proud.
  Sizing needs a candle-maker test (tunnelling, soot): **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Burn:** 200 g ÷ ~5 g/h ⇒ ***aprox. 40 h***; 80 g ⇒ ***aprox. 16 h***. Always written "aprox.".
- **Claim on pack:** only *Perfuma o ambiente com aroma agradável* (IN 474/2026 group 2).
- **Formula:**
  - no lilial (prohibited by RDC 1.029/2026);
  - allergens declared above 0,001% from the supplier's certificate.
  - The per-SKU allergens below are **probable, UNVERIFIED**.

### D.5 The system's seven rules

1. **Ela é sempre a maior palavra; você, a menor.**
2. **Toda linha ocupa a largura inteira.** Fill by size, by `wdth` or by an L/R split. Ragged lines never appear.
3. **A luz tem borda.**
   - One source and one hard pool.
   - **Type never enters the pool.**
   - No soft glow, bokeh or haze, except a faint physical cone (density ≤0,002).
4. **Cada fonte é uma voz** (§A.6).
5. **Tudo é papel de verdade.** Paste wrinkles, tape, perforations, misregistration. Never a glossy app UI.
6. **Duas horas do dia:**
   - **O MURO** (day, hard flash, the poster wall: the announcement);
   - **O PALCO** (night, one hard spot: the show).
   - Never both lights in one frame. A third, neutral state, **LOJA**, is for e-commerce only.
7. **A segurança está no roteiro.** Every lit candle obeys §D.8.

**The one show:**
- **The pop tour** is the master metaphor: tour, setlist, wristband, merch, encore, acoustic set, *grade*, *coxia*.
- **The school recital** is the origin story: the back panel, C02, the presenter set, Ad 1.
- **The theatre's three bells** (*primeiro, segundo, terceiro sinal*) are the one theatre loan.
- **Lambe-lambe** is how the tour is announced. It is a graphic grammar, not a story.

### D.7 Label fidelity: the method this kit must pass before any "verificado"

1. **One-time unwrap check per master view** (front 0°, ¾ front 20°, back 180°, base):
   - render a **label AOV pass** (an emission-only pass of the label texture through the same camera);
   - unwrap it through the same UV map;
   - compare it with the flat master **with a planted error** (a mirrored lettered patch);
   - a pass counts only if the planted error is caught.
2. **Every film frame and every still** is checked against its view's AOV pass.
   - **Bars:** worst tile ≥0,80 and 5th percentile ≥0,93 in film (≥0,95 for stills).
   - **Colour:** a calibrated median hue + chroma shift ≤2,0, measured on the **Standard-view neutral-light render** of
     the same view. **The bar is never relaxed.**
3. **Flame-lit frames** (F15 blackout f204–221, C06, the lit KV-45):
   - letters and geometry are checked on the **Cycles Diffuse Color (albedo) pass**, with a planted error;
   - colour is measured on the neutral-light render of the same view.
4. **Personalised masters:**
   - each master is checked flat against its `label_copy.json` strings with a planted error before printing;
   - the AOV check runs on the rendered sample units (DONA CIDA, MAINHA, MÃE ♥).
5. **Packs below 20% of frame height are graphic thumbnails** (C03, C07). They are rendered from the same master but
   **carry no fidelity claim.**

### D.8 Fire safety: the per-frame checklist (CONAR art. 33)

Run on every delivered frame. Any NO ⇒ the frame is not delivered.

1. **Movement:** the pack moves only while **unlit**. It is never carried or moved lit.
2. **Clearance:** every lit candle is **≥30 cm** from paper, fabric, a chair or a person.
   - Setlist paper is 60 cm away.
   - The rotunda is 4 m away.
   - The front-row chairs are ≥2 m away.
3. **Debris:** no confetti, streamers or paper particles in any lit shot.
4. **Surface:** the candle stands on a firm, flat surface: the stage floor, a concrete ledge, the lid on a table, or the
   seamless.
5. **Attendance:** no lit candle in a generated scene. No lit candle alone in a domestic room. The stage is the show.
6. **Copy:** the safety micro-line ***Nunca deixe a vela acesa sem supervisão.*** is on every end card and on every
   lit-product post.

### E.5 Sound direction

**Sonic logo: *a ovação de uma pessoa só* (2,0 s).**
1. **0,0 s:** **CLAC** (a theatre lamp switching on).
2. **0,1–0,9 s:** stadium roar.
3. **Hard cut.** Then **three claps** from one pair of hands in a small, dry room, at 1,1, 1,4 and 1,7 s.

A whole stadium, delivered by one person: that is the gift.

**Music:**
- An **AI-generated track assigned to Sol** (studio rule).
- **Style:** a **school *fanfarra* / drumline** at **120 BPM**: snare roll, bass drum, B♭ brass stabs.
- No melody that quotes a real song, chant or artist.
- Two stems: drums and brass.

**Foley** (recorded by the studio or from a licensed library):
- **wood-wick crackle**, recorded from a real wood-wick candle: this is the brand's quiet applause;
- match strike *fsst*;
- case magnet *clac*;
- wristband clasp click;
- paper seal *rrrip*;
- theatre bell;
- one audience cough;
- small-room tone.

**The crackle-to-applause morph:** the wick's crackle crossfades over **1,5 s** into distant applause. **The candle
applauds her.**

**Data, not decoration:**
- **What is keyed:** every type slam and every CLAC warm-up is keyed to **onset detection on the actual recorded
  transients**, not eyeballed.
- **How:** run `librosa.onset` on the final mix and snap the slams to the onsets within ±1 frame.

**Mix:**
- **Loudness:** −14 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP true peak.
- **Voice:** VO and presenter voice sit 6 dB over the music bed.
- **Silent versions:** captions plus all type, with no reliance on sound.

## G. Ten things that make HOLOFOTE one up on AVELUNE, FAÍSCA and VOLTA

1. **An idea, not a mood line.**
   - **The previous kits:** each tagline could sign any brand in its category.
   - **HOLOFOTE:** it is built on a national memory with a date (the school Dia das Mães show) and turned into a role
     reversal anyone can say in two words, ***holofote nela.***
2. **The product is designed by the idea.**
   - The glass is a tour poster on the front and a tour T-shirt on the back.
   - The lid is the stage mark, the seal is a festival wristband, the box is a road case with a dressing-room mirror,
     the insert is the setlist and the base answers *"não precisava"*.
   - **Every component carries the concept, including the legal copy.**
3. **The pack moves, label-exact.**
   - **The previous kits:** they wrote that a pack in motion *"exigiria outra rota"*.
   - **HOLOFOTE:** the candle rises on a stage lift and lights in a blackout. It is real geometry wearing the master
     texture, checked per frame on a label AOV pass, with the flame-lit frames checked on the albedo pass.
4. **Personalisation is the pipeline's showcase.**
   - The **ESTREIA** birth line, *como ela tá salva no seu celular?* and *abertura: [você]* make every unit a different,
     exact label.
   - The fit ladder was measured on the font files, and every master passes a planted-error check before printing. **A
     generator cannot do this, and the sample proves it.**
5. **A complete campaign with zero generated pixels.**
   - The KV in four formats, ten campaign images, eight daily cards, seven store images, five films, stickers and
     digital assets are all **local** (Blender + code type).
   - The generated people layer is a bonus, not a crutch.
6. **A sonic identity.**
   - **CLAC → one person's ovation**, a sonic logo that means something.
   - The wood wick's **crackle morphs into applause.**
   - A fanfarra bed, and type slams keyed to the recorded onsets.
   - **The previous kits:** room tone.
7. **A distribution mechanic aimed at both generations.**
   - **BOLETIM DA TURNÊ:** mothers forward the brand's 06:03 countdown to their own children.
   - **Salva como** turns personalisation into comment bait.
   - **O CARTAZ DELA** turns every 9 May story into a branded poster.
   - **The SINAL cuts** hit the 59% who buy in the final week.
8. **Compliance as design.**
   - The setlist **is** the instructions, the *Manifesto de Carga* **is** the legal panel, and the rider is the joke on
     the side.
   - Fire safety is a per-frame checklist, contrast is measured, and the legal type height was measured on the font.
   - **The disclosure is split and honest:** *render 3D* on the renders, *criada com IA* on the people.
9. **A brand system that generates without a designer.**
   - Seven rules, two light states, three voices mapped to two type families, eleven devices (six data-driven).
   - **Re-set, never cropped.**
   - A full store voice, plus **CORTINA FECHADA**, the grief opt-out as a gesture in the brand's own world.
10. **Funny, then moving, and built to last.**
    - It is the register no previous kit attempted: loud joke, then lump in the throat.
    - **Built to last:**
      - a refill (**NOVA TEMPORADA**) turns *o copo fica* into a repeat purchase;
      - an inclusive headliner field (*VÓ, MADRINHA, PAI*) outlives Mother's Day;
      - a son in the cast;
      - the platform extends to Dia dos Avós, graduation season and Dia dos Pais (*O SHOW DELE*).
