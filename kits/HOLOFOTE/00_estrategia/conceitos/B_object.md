# B_object — **pode.**

### *Não é pra visita.*

Concept B, object-led. SOL Estúdio sample case for O LANÇAMENTO + O ANÚNCIO.
Fictional demo brand. *Caso demonstrativo · marca fictícia.* Names are working names; trademark, domain and
regulatory route are **not verified**. Written 6 Oct 2026 for Dia das Mães, **Sunday 9 May 2027**.

> **The pitch in one breath.** Every Brazilian mother runs a museu in her living room: the guest soap nobody may
> use, the decorative towel nobody may dry a hand on, the good glasses, and the candle on the shelf that has
> never been lit. All of it is *pra visita*. **pode.** makes the first object in that cabinet that is for her.
> The brand name is the answer to the word she said most: *não pode*.

## 1. Brand name

### Three options

| Option | What it carries | Why not / why |
|---|---|---|
| **1. pode.** | The opposite of *não pode*, the word every Brazilian mother said most. It is permission, the brand's whole job. It turns every product name into a sentence (*pode. sabonete de visita*). Short, native, sayable (PÓ-dji), and it works on any occasion after Mother's Day (*pode. domingo*). | **Chosen.** Risks: it is a common verb, so trademark distinctiveness is weak (UNVERIFIED), and it can be misread as the past tense *pôde*. The lower-case setting plus the cherry full stop make it a mark rather than a word. |
| **2. DONA** | *Dona* is both the comic title Gen Z gives mum (*a Dona Cida*) and "owner". | Rejected. It reads as *dona de casa* (housewife), and domestic-labour framing is on the cringe list (`culture.md` §6). It also lacks a verb, so it cannot build a system of permissions. |
| **3. CRISTALEIRA** | It names the brand's world exactly: the glass cabinet where the good stuff lives. | Rejected. It names the place where things go *not* to be used, so it is the opposite of the idea. It is also five syllables and nostalgic rather than fun. |

Also considered: *Não Mexe* (too negative, and it fights the act of lighting), *Visita* (the ironic reading is
lost on the shelf), *Gomo* (a lovely word for the ribbed dome, but it carries no meaning).

**None of these collides with a Brazilian candle brand in the research sample** (Granado, Phebo, L'Occitane au
Brésil, Bendita Vela, Naturel, Herô, Mels Brushes, Lucci, Océane, Glow Vibes, Hello Hello, Tok&Stok, Perfuma &
Decora, Antik, L'odorat, Imaginarium, Pirilampa, Elemento Mineral, Orgânica, La Florentina, Nesti Dante:
`category.md` §1). **Availability not checked.**

### Why "pode." wins

Mum's vocabulary is the brand's raw material (`culture.md` §0.7), and *não pode* sits under all of it: *você não
é todo mundo*, *não mexe*, *isso é pra visita*. A brand that answers with one word is a reversal the audience
completes in its own head. That is what W+K-grade naming does: the audience does the last 10% of the joke and
remembers it for that reason.

## 2. Tagline, brand idea and insight

**Tagline:** **pode acender.**
It works three ways at once: literally (it is a candle), as permission (it is not a decoration), and as a
counter to mum's *apaga essa luz!* (`culture.md` #13).

**Brand idea:** *pode.* makes the good things in the house for her to use, not to keep for guests.

**Campaign platform (Dia das Mães 2027):** **não é pra visita.**

### The insight: a Gen Z truth about mothers

> **A vida inteira ela disse "não mexe, isso é pra visita". O sabonete bom, a toalha bordada, o copo bom, o bolo
> do domingo, a vela da estante: tudo guardado pros outros. Agora você cresceu, talvez até saiu de casa — e quando
> volta no domingo, ela tira o copo bom. Pra você. Você virou visita. E o melhor da casa continua não sendo pra
> ela.**

The truth has three layers. Each is funny on its own, and together they are moving:

1. **The comedy layer.** Every Gen Z Brazilian grew up in a house with objects they were forbidden to touch:
   *sabonete de visita, toalha de enfeite, a louça boa, o sofá com plástico.* This is shared, specific and
   affectionate. The joke lands on the house rule, never on her age, body or tech skills (the *zoeira com
   respeito* rule, `culture.md` §6).
2. **The tender layer.** Saving the best for others is a form of self-erasure. Mothers put themselves last in
   their own living room, and the gift fixes exactly that.
3. **The turn.** Once the child grows up, **the child becomes the visit**. The good glass comes out for *them*.
   That is the line the AD3 presenter delivers, and it is the one that makes people send the ad to a sibling.

**Why the insight is right for a candle specifically:** the candle is the most famous never-used object in the
cabinet. Decorative candles sit unlit for years. *pode.* is designed to be lit, so the brand's whole design
answers the insight: a refill means burning it is not a loss, and the tagline gives the permission.

> ⚠ **Evidence status: UNVERIFIED (cultural observation, very widely known).** The phrases *sabonete de visita*,
> *toalha de enfeite*, *louça de visita* and *é pra visita* could not be fetched at a source today. The US-only
> search tool returned nothing relevant, and DuckDuckGo and Bing were blocked or off-target (attempts are in
> `research/raw_object/`).
>
> **R$0 check before any client use:** a person with a browser counts the TikTok and Instagram results for
> *#sabonetedevisita* and *"é pra visita"*, then sends one written question to 20 people aged 18–28: *"Na casa da
> sua mãe tem alguma coisa que é 'pra visita'? O quê?"*
> - **Bar, committed in advance:** at least 14 of 20 name an object without being prompted.
> - **Below 10 of 20:** fall back to the light/arrival insight (`culture.md` #2/#13), which this object system
>   also supports (§7.4).

## 3. The launch product: Coleção *As Coisas Boas* (Dia das Mães 2027)

### 3.1 The four scents

Every scent is named after something mum keeps *pra visita*. On the label, brand plus name reads as a permission.

| # | Full label line | The object it frees | Saída (top) | Coração (heart) | Fundo (base) | Pot / dome / ink |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · HERO** | **pode. sabonete de visita** | The rose-shaped soap in the downstairs toilet, untouched since 2009 | bergamota, aldeídos, pimenta-rosa | rosa, íris (pó de arroz), flor de laranjeira | almíscar branco, baunilha, sândalo | **Rosa** pot · **Cereja** dome · **Lápis** ink |
| 2 | **pode. toalha de enfeite** | The embroidered towel folded in three that nobody has ever dried a hand on | limão-siciliano, aldeídos | acorde de flor de algodão, lavanda, acorde de lírio | almíscar limpo, cedro | **Lápis** pot · **Céu** dome · **Nuvem** ink |
| 3 | **pode. bolo da visita** | The cake that only came out of the oven when the doorbell rang | erva-doce, raspas de laranja | acorde de bolo de fubá (milho tostado, manteiga), leite | baunilha, fava tonka, açúcar queimado | **Manteiga** pot · **Âmbar** dome · **Lápis** ink |
| 4 | **pode. perfume de festa** | The perfume kept for the cousin's wedding, in its original box since 2014 | aldeídos, tangerina | jasmim, tuberosa, rosa | âmbar, patchouli, almíscar | **Cereja** pot · **Rosa** dome · **Nuvem** ink |

**Why these four:**
- **Two are on-trend at the shelf.** Floral is Gen Z's favourite profile: 74%, per a trade-press survey with no
  stated sample (`category.md` §6, secondary). The gourmand of Brazilian home food is empty white space: no *bolo
  de fubá* among ~100 Brazilian candle names (`category.md` §3.6).
- **Two do something else.** *Toalha* gives the range a clean/fresh note, and *Perfume de festa* gives it an
  evening note.
- **Four colours make an Otherland-style wall**, recognisable at thumbnail size.

**Fragrance compliance notes:**
- **Formulate without Lilial** (butylphenyl methylpropional, now prohibited by RDC 1.029/2026). The *acorde de
  lírio* in #2 is a classic place it used to hide.
- **Declare allergens** from the fragrance house's certificate on the box: coumarin from tonka (#3), and likely
  linalool, limonene, citronellol and geraniol in #1 and #4 (`label_rules.md` §7).
- **Never claim effects.** No *relaxa*, *acalma* or *aromaterapia*; IN 474 group 2 allows only *perfuma o
  ambiente, confere aroma agradável*.

**Name risks and fallbacks (regulatory review needed):**
- ***sabonete*** in a candle name could be argued to mislead about the product's nature (RDC 907 art. 12 I).
  - Mitigation: *vela aromática* always sits directly under the name on the pack, and the name sits in the scent
    slot of the lockup.
  - Fallback name: **pode. lavabo de visita** (the guest bathroom), which keeps the joke.
- ***bolo*** is a food name. The candle does not look like food and is not shaped like food, which is the actual
  trap (`label_rules.md` §8).
  - Fallback name: **pode. domingo de visita**.

## 4. The vessel and pack: *O pote bom*

**Design principle: everything is borrowed from her own cabinet, then handed back to her.**
Each part of the object quotes one *pra visita* object a Brazilian mum owns:

| Part | Quotes | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Glossy coloured **porcelain** pot | *a louça boa* (the good china) | The material is literally the good stuff, in a candy colour no china set ever came in |
| **Gold rim line** (*filete*) | the gold edge of the visit cups | The universal "this is the nice one" code, reduced to one 1,8 mm line |
| Translucent **ribbed glass dome** with a ball knob | *a compoteira* (the cut-glass sweet dish with a lid) | **Gives the brand a silhouette nobody on the shelf has**: every Brazilian candle has a flat lid or none. Seen from above, the dome is a 12-petal flower, which becomes the logo |
| **Masking-tape strip** with handwriting | how mums label things (*fita crepe e caneta BIC*) | The object claims an owner: *é da mãe.* |
| **Paper doily** in the box | *a toalhinha de crochê* under every object in the living room | Pinterest *Laced Up* (*lace doily* +105%, `codes.md` §2a, secondary): grandma-core read as Gen Z |

### 4.1 Geometry (for the Blender build)

All dimensions are concept direction, not industrial design.

**Pot**
- Porcelain, outer **Ø 82 mm × H 78 mm**, wall 4 mm, base 12 mm.
- Fillet 1,5 mm at the rim and 3 mm at the foot.
- 1,5 mm recessed foot ring with **"pode." embossed 0,4 mm** inside it.
- **Squat on purpose**: it reads as *pote*, not tumbler.

**Capsule (refill insert)**
- Aluminium, Ø 74 × 64 mm.
- Its 1 mm lip shows at the rim, **anodised gold**, so it reads as a second gold line.
- Wax top sits **14 mm below the rim**, with a 1 mm concave meniscus.
- 200 g ÷ ~0,9 g/cm³ ≈ 222 cm³ ≈ 52 mm of wax in Ø 74 mm.

**Wick and flame**
- Wick 9 mm proud, slightly off-vertical, carbon tip.
- Flame 26 mm tall.

**Dome**
- Pressed soda-lime glass, **Ø 86 mm skirt**, with an internal locating ring (Ø 73 mm × 6 mm) that sits inside the
  rim.
- Dome rise 40 mm, **12 vertical gomos** (ribs).
- **Ball knob Ø 22 mm** on a 6 mm neck.
- Height closed **≈136 mm**.
- Tinted in four transparent jewel tones (Cereja, Céu, Âmbar, Rosa). These are material colours, never text.

**Materials (Cycles)**

| Part | Settings |
|---|---|
| Glaze | Principled, base colour per SKU, roughness 0,08, coat 1,0 / coat roughness 0,03, a faint orange-peel normal (noise 0,4 mm, strength 0,02) |
| Filete | Metallic #C9A24A, roughness 0,25, slight wear mask |
| Wax | Ivory #F3EBDD with subsurface (radius ~3 mm) and roughness 0,35. Lit variant: melt pool plus sink ring |
| Dome | IOR 1,52, transmission 1, roughness 0,02, tint via volume absorption, with micro-variation in rib thickness so caustics are uneven like real pressed glass |
| Flame | Emission gradient (blue base → ~1900 K core → orange tip) plus a small 1900 K point light inside, so the dome glows red when it rests beside the lit pot |

### 4.2 Label layout: cylinder wraps only (rule 4)

**Front panel**, a ceramic decal printed under the glaze coat, so it has no paper edge.
It spans about 126° of the 257,6 mm circumference (x −45 to +45 mm). Measurements are from the rim.

| From rim | Element | Type and colour |
|---|---|---|
| 3,0–4,8 mm | **filete** (gold rule, full circumference) | — |
| 12–24 mm | **pode.** | Fraunces 900, SOFT 100, WONK 1, opsz 144, cap ≈10 mm. Lápis. The full stop is replaced by a **Cereja circle at 115%**, the "knob seen from above" |
| 29–45 mm | **sabonete** / **de visita** | Fraunces 700, SOFT 100, opsz 72, cap 6 mm, 8,5 mm leading. Lápis |
| 49 mm | **vela aromática** | Fraunces 500, opsz 9, cap 2,4 mm |
| 54–58 mm | **PESO LÍQUIDO 200 g** | **Figures 4,0 mm**, "g" 2,8 mm (≥⅔). Lápis on Rosa **5,76:1**. Portaria Inmetro 249/2021 requires ≥3 mm for >50–200 g; 4 mm is the house rule |
| 62–74 mm | **the fita** (see below) | Paper sticker on top of the glaze |

**The fita (ownership strip)**
- A paper sticker, **18 × 64 mm**, rotated −3°, torn ends (alpha), with paper-fibre bump.
- Applied over the glaze, left of centre, **never over the legal lines**.
- Text in **Playwrite BR**, Caneta blue #1F3FBF on Fita #EADFC6 (**6,29:1**).
- **Default:** *é da mãe.*
- **Personalised by code at checkout:**
  - *é da mainha.* · *é da mamis.* · *é da dona cida.* · *é da vó.*
  - **for the 16% who buy for themselves** (Sebrae MT): *é minha.*
  - The name field takes 2–14 characters. The digital-print sticker is the only per-order part; the pot stays
    generic.

**Back panel (decal)**, the jar block from `label_rules.md` §11.2:
- *ODORIZANTE DE AMBIENTE · VELA AROMÁTICA*
- *Perfuma o ambiente com aroma agradável.*
- *ATENÇÃO* with the four fire lines
- *Advertências completas e modo de uso: ver embalagem externa.*

**Base sticker (Ø 40 mm, not on the dome):**
`LOTE A0000 · FAB MM/AAAA · VAL MM/AAAA · Indústria Brasileira`. Every value is a FICTÍCIO placeholder. Lot and
validity sit on the primary pack, never on a removable part (`label_rules.md` §6).

**Easter egg, inside the pot floor** (fired enamel, seen only once the capsule comes out):
*agora é seu pote. vaso, porta-joias, porta-tudo. só não é pra visita.*
⚠ This is a reuse message, which is fine on Route A (cosmetic Grau 1) and **forbidden on Route B** (saneante:
*"Não reutilize a embalagem vazia"*). Under Route A the reuse suggested is non-food only: no drinking, no food
storage.

### 4.3 Gift box: *a caixa pode rasgar*

- **Body:** rigid two-piece box, **100 × 100 × 150 mm**.
  - Outside **Lápis**, inside **Rosa**, so opening reveals the colour.
  - Lid top **Nuvem**, with a write-on line: **pode ______________.** The child completes it in their own pen.
    This is the bentô-cake behaviour (a gift whose top carries a written line, `culture.md` §5) given a brand.
- **The band (*cinta*):**
  - A 40 mm Fita-cream paper band wraps the box vertically.
  - It has a perforated tear strip printed **PODE RASGAR.** (Cereja), with *ela abre devagarinho pra guardar o
    papel. esse pode.* in Playwrite BR beside it.
  - **This is the unboxing meme:** mum about to unpick the tape to save the paper, and the box telling her not to.
- **Inside:**
  1. A die-cut Nuvem paper **toalhinha** (24-petal lace) cradles the pot.
  2. **The *vales* pad**: five perforated 90 × 45 mm slips, plus one blank. This is the school Mother's Day coupon
     book, now written in the child's voice.
     - *vale: um áudio de 7 minutos, ouvido até o fim.*
     - *vale: avisar quando chegar. sem você pedir.*
     - *vale: levar o casaquinho. e vestir.*
     - *vale: um domingo sem celular na mesa.*
     - *vale: você escolhe o filme. eu não reclamo.*
     - *vale: ______________*
  3. **A card** with a handwriting field.
- **Base panel:** the full Route A legal block (`label_rules.md` §11.4), with FICTÍCIO placeholders.
- **No matches or lighter in the box** (Inmetro status unverified, `label_rules.md` §8).

### 4.4 The rituals: the object is the meme

| Ritual | What happens | Why it travels |
|---|---|---|
| **rasga** | She hesitates at the band, then tears it. | Gen Z will film mum hesitating. That is the unboxing video, and the band has scripted it. |
| **tlin** | The glass dome lifts off the porcelain with a clink. | **The sonic mnemonic.** The sound of the good china, recognisable with the screen off. |
| **fsst** | Match strike; the flame blooms. | The candle category's universal sound, attached to *pode.* by sitting second in the two-note logo. |
| **pode gastar** | The capsule runs out and a refill drops in. | It removes the reason to save the candle. **The object answers the insight mechanically, not only in copy.** |
| **vira** | The empty pot becomes a vase or a porta-joias; with the dome on, a jewellery cloche. | The thing she will keep anyway, designed to be kept. |

⚠ **The dome is a dust cover, not a snuffer**, until a candle maker tests it. Pack copy: *apague com um
abafador; recoloque a cúpula com a vela apagada.* A pressed-glass dome over a live flame risks heat stress and
soot.

## 5. Visual identity: the world of *A Cristaleira Aberta*

**The world in one line:** mum's display cabinet with its glass door open, shot like a deadpan product catalogue.
Everything in the house that used to be *pra visita*, re-issued at full colour for her.

**The Collins test:** the system has to be able to produce the 100th execution without a designer inventing
anything. It does this because every execution is one sentence, *pode. [coisa boa]*, set on one object, in
one of two lighting modes.

### 5.2 Two typefaces, both OFL on Google Fonts

| Family | Role | Settings | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fraunces** (variable: opsz, wght, **SOFT**, **WONK**) | Wordmark, product names, headlines, and small text down to legal | Wordmark: wght 900 · SOFT 100 · WONK 1 · opsz 144. Names: 700 · SOFT 100 · opsz 72. Legal: 500 · SOFT 50 · opsz 9–12 | At SOFT 100 and Black weight it lands on the warm, gooey 70s serif of mum's era: the Cooper-Black feel, but ours. **Animate SOFT 0→100 and the letters melt like warm wax** (`codes.md` §1b). | **Playwrite BR** | Mum's hand and the child's notes: the fita, the box lid, the vales, *pra visita* tags | wght 300–400, **2–8 words per use, never body copy** | The vertical cursive taught in Brazilian schools: *the* hand of the fridge note, by construction. ### 5.3 Graphic devices: the modular parts

1. **The permission sentence.** **pode. + [a concrete thing she saves]**
   - Lower-case. The full stop is always the Cereja circle.
   - The *thing* is always an object or an act, never a feeling, an effect or a product claim.
   - **This is the generator:** any *pra visita* object produces a line (*pode. o copo bom* · *pode. a toalha
     bordada* · *pode. o sofá sem plástico*).
2. **The fita.** One masking-tape strip per layout, Playwrite BR, −3°, torn ends. It only ever says ***é da
   ___.*** Never a sales line.
3. **The carimbo.**
   - A round rubber stamp in Cereja: outer ring *AUTORIZADO PELA FAMÍLIA · 09.05.27*, centre ***PODE.*** in
     Fraunces 900.
   - Misregistered 0,4 mm, ink starving at one edge.

4. **The *pra visita* tag.** A small kraft card tag on string, *pra visita.* in Playwrite BR.

6. **A flor da tampa.**

     and a ceiling rose at once.
   - **The product generates the logo.** Uses: favicon, seal on the base, sticker, loading spinner.
7. **The toalhinha.** A 24-petal paper lace circle. **Only under unlit products, never under a lit candle**
   (CONAR art. 33, and keep 30 cm from flammables).
8. **The vale.** A perforated coupon, *vale:* in Fraunces, the promise in Playwrite BR. Always the child's voice.

**Layout grammar:**
- One permission sentence per layout.
- Type never touches the product, the face or the flame.
- Generous Nuvem or flat-colour field, one hard shadow, centre-weighted symmetry (deadpan, never chaotic).

**Motion:**
- Reveals use the **SOFT melt** (Fraunces SOFT 0→100 over 12 frames).
- The stamp lands in 2 frames, with a 1-frame camera bump.
- Cuts land on the *thunk*.
- **No dissolves, no particles, no lens flares.**

**Sonic identity:**
- **tlin → fsst**: glass dome off porcelain, then match strike. Every film ends on it.
- A soft *thunk* (rubber stamp) is the punctuation inside films.

### 5.4 Render style: two lighting modes, both Blender Cycles

**Mode A, *Luz de cristaleira*:**
- Lápis lacquered cabinet with a **mirrored back**, glass shelves, a warm 2700 K strip practical inside the top.
- Soft 4500 K key from camera-left; a hard rim from back-right to draw the dome's edge.
- 85–100 mm-equivalent lens for product, 40–50 mm for the room.
- **The cabinet's door is always open.**

**Mode B, *Flash de visita*:**
- On-axis hard flash on a flat Rosa / Lápis / Manteiga / Nuvem seamless, with a crisp drop shadow.
- Products lined up like a police line-up or a museum catalogue.
- This is the Gen Z party-photo look made precise (TikTok asks for *"not overly polished"*, `codes.md` §0.1).
  The craft is in looking effortless.

**Imperfection rules (anti-slop):**
- One **fingerprint on the dome**. It has been *used*, which is the whole point.
- A wick slightly off-vertical, and a faint sink ring on the lit variant.
- The doily a few degrees off-centre.
- **On the cabinet shelf, the doily keeps a pressed circle where the candle stood for years.** Never a dust ring:
  mum's house is spotless, and a dust joke lands on her.
- Micro-dust on glass shelves only in close-ups; uneven caustics through the gomos.

**What it must never look like:**
- The frosted tumbler with a bamboo lid and a whisper label (the Brazilian cliché, `category.md` §2).
- The amber jar with a kraft label.
- Beige spa or "ritual de bem-estar".
- Mother's Day clip art: hearts, roses, *rainha do lar*, *mãe guerreira*, gold script.
- Glitter, or the *bom dia* image (that is another concept's code).
- Bokeh orbs, fairy lights, smoke wisps; teal-and-orange grading.
- AI gloss: skin like wax, wax like plastic.
- A *Melhor Mãe do Mundo* mug.

## 9. Why this beats AVELUNE, FAÍSCA and VOLTA, and why it is not AI slop

### 9.1 What the previous kits were

All three are excellent beauty campaigns in one shape:
- a single product, with a beautiful fictional or real model;
- a pastel or sunlit editorial world;
- 2–4-word lines (*Chegue em cor.*, *O cuidado percorre o fio.*, *O movimento é seu.*);
- a pack that can only appear as a frozen layer from 9 to 15 s.

They prove craft and fidelity. **They do not prove an idea.** Remove the model, and the brand has no
point of view left.

### 9.2 What this one adds, point by point

| Dimension | Previous kits | *pode.* |
|---|---|---|
| **Idea** | A mood | **A cultural truth turned into a rallying line** (*não é pra visita*) that people can repeat about their own home |
| **Name** | A word | **A naming system**: brand + product = one sentence of permission |
| **Object** | A label on a stock-shaped pack | **An object designed from the culture**: porcelain = *louça boa*, dome = *compoteira*, fita = how mums label things. Its top view is the logo |
| **Ritual** | None | **rasga → tlin → fsst → refil**, each one filmable, one of them a sonic logo |
| **Product in motion** | Flat KV layer only | **The 3D product moves** (dome lifts, flame lights) with the label exact by construction |
| **People** | Required; the campaign depends on generated faces | **Optional.** The campaign is complete as type + object; the presenter is a bonus layer |
| **System** | A palette and a font | **8 modular devices, motion and sound rules, a voice, personalisation by code, a vales template, a sticker pack.** The 100th execution needs no new invention |
| **Audience care** | — | **A grief opt-out written in the brand's own grammar** (*pode pular essa data*) |

### 9.3 Why it is not AI slop

1. **No pixel of product or type is generated.**
   - The pot, dome, wax, flame and label are Blender geometry and shaders.
   - Every letter is set by code from two OFL families.
   - The generator only ever sees a blue stand-in, and only in the optional people layer.
2. **The specificity is human.** *Sabonete de visita, toalha de enfeite, a vela nunca acesa, o copo bom no
   domingo* are details nobody writes from a prompt. Slop is generic: *"ilumine momentos especiais"*.
3. **Imperfection is designed in:**
   - a fingerprint on the dome;
   - the pressed circle on the doily;
   - uneven caustics;
   - a wick off-vertical;
   - stamp misregistration.
   No glow halos, particles, smoke or teal-orange.
4. **The joke is earned and kind.** It lands on the house rule and on the child, and it turns into tenderness
   (*você virou visita*). It never mocks her. That is the difference between Droga5-grade humour and a meme
   template.
