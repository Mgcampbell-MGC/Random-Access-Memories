## The pitch in sixty seconds

In May 2021 WhatsApp added a button that changed how Brazil hears its mothers: **1,5x. 2x.**
([MacMagazine, 21 May 2021](https://macmagazine.com.br/post/2021/05/21/whatsapp-comeca-a-liberar-reproducao-acelerada-de-audios/):
*"usuários poderão ajustar a velocidade do áudio para 1,5x ou 2x"*). Brazil sends voice notes at four times the
rate of any other country (`culture.md` #8). And the longest voice notes in the country come from mothers.

So a generation that talks to its mother every day mostly listens at double speed: seven minutes in three and a
half, the family group muted, a sticker sent back instead of a reply. That is not a lack of love. It is how the
phone works. **And one object in the house cannot be sped up: a candle.**

**sinal** is a candle brand that speaks fluent phone and sells the one thing a phone cannot accelerate. Each
candle is a message in the chat between you and her, made in ceramic. The pot is a chat bubble. The lid lifts by its
red notification badge. The hero's label is a voice note: ▶, a waveform, **40:00:00**, and a speed button that only
says **1x**.

For Dia das Mães the brand makes one deadpan promise: **vela não tem 2x.**

Everything in the launch can be made locally: Blender renders, code-set type, a sonic logo and kinetic film. The
people layer is a bonus, not a crutch.

### Options

| | Name | Read | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **sinal** | *Signal*: the bars on your phone; *dar sinal*, to let someone know; and the oldest signal of all, a light left on. | It holds the whole idea in five letters: **the candle is the original notification**. Before phones, a lit window meant "I'm home" or "I'm waiting up". Portuguese, sayable everywhere (*"uma vela da sinal"*), no accent in the URL, and it builds a symbol for free (the dot of the **i** becomes the flame, §5.3). No candle brand called *sinal* turned up in the category sample or a web search (§10). | A common noun is a weak trademark alone. It needs the wordmark and the device to be registrable. **INPI class 4 search not done: UNVERIFIED.** |
| **B** | **visto** | The read receipt: *visto por último*, *visualizou*. | Very digital. The double tick becomes the logo. | It carries the guilt of *visualizou e não respondeu* (being left "no vácuo"), which is a negative state to build a gift brand on. It also means "visa". |
| **C** | **1x** (*um xis*) | The speed button. | It *is* the platform. | It is unsayable as a brand (*"comprei uma um xis"*), a number cannot be owned on a shelf, and it is too narrow to outlive one campaign. **Kept as the brand's signature device and tagline, not its name.** |

**Rejected, with the reasons worth keeping:**
- ***"dá sinal de vida"* as the tagline.** It was the first line on the wall: mum's eternal complaint, a living
  flame, and the brand name in one phrase. **It was killed by a search.** The phrase's web footprint in Brazil is
  dominated by families of missing people. One 2026 report counts 84.760 disappearances in 2025, in a piece about
  mothers asking for visibility ([Sul21, via search](https://sul21.com.br/?p=293142); secondary). A Mother's Day
  campaign cannot hand that sentence to those mothers. The name survives because *sinal* alone does not carry the
  shadow; *de vida* does. **Never write "sinal de vida" anywhere in the brand.**
- **digitando…** Lovely as a device, awkward as a name (*"a vela da Digitando"*). It is used for the mini (§3).
- **online.** English, generic, and unownable.
- **chegou?** Mum's most-sent message. It belongs to the language route (Concept A); this route owns the medium,
  not the phrases.

### Pick: **sinal**

Always lowercase, set in dot-matrix with the **i**'s dot replaced by a flame (§5.3). Handle and URL: `sinal`
(availability **UNVERIFIED**; fall back to `sinal.velas` / `usesinal`).

**Why it wins.** W+K would say the name has to be the strategy, and here it is: *a candle is the oldest
notification there is.* Collins would say the identity has to be found in the material, and it is: the dot of the
**i** is already a light.

## 2. Tagline, brand idea, insight

### Tagline (permanent, every piece)

# **vela não tem 2x.**

**Why this line.** It passes the swap test that the previous kits' lines fail (§9). *"Chegue em cor"* works for any
lipstick; *"vela não tem 2x"* only works for a candle, only in a country that sends voice notes at 4×, and only
with a generation that taps the speed button by reflex. It is **literally true**: 200 g burns at about 5 g an hour
whatever you press (`category.md` §5.1). It is **dry**, not a lecture about phones. And it is a **template** that
outlives May: *"domingo não tem 2x"*, *"aniversário não tem 2x"*, *"natal não tem 2x"*.

The **1x pill** (§5.3) is the tagline drawn: one speed button that always reads **1x**.

### Brand idea in one sentence

> **PT:** sinal fala a língua do celular e vende a única coisa que ele não acelera.
>
> **EN:** sinal speaks fluent phone and sells the one thing your phone can't speed up.

### Dia das Mães 2027 campaign line

# **Ela te manda áudio de 7 minutos. Devolve um de 40 horas.**

The campaign name is **MÃE EM 1X**. The hero product is the line: ***áudio de 40 horas***, a candle that is a
voice note from the child, finally the long one, and not skippable.

### The insight (the Gen Z truth about mothers)

> **The generation that talks to its mother most is the one that listens to her at 2x.**
> **A geração que mais fala com a mãe é a que ouve ela em 2x.**

**What holds it up:**

1. **The medium is real and measured.** WhatsApp shipped 1,5x and 2x on iOS from 10 May 2021 and on the Web
   ([MacMagazine](https://macmagazine.com.br/post/2021/05/21/whatsapp-comeca-a-liberar-reproducao-acelerada-de-audios/);
   [Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/apps/whatsapp-comeca-a-liberar-reproducao-acelerada-de-mensagens-de-audio-180943/)),
   explicitly for *"quem recebe gravações longas"*. Brazil sends voice notes at 4× any other country, and frequency
   (+7%) and duration (+8%) are still rising in 2026 (`culture.md` #8). **Mum's long audio meets Gen Z's 2x button
   every day.** *That Gen Z specifically listens to its mother at 2x is a cultural observation, not a statistic:
   **UNVERIFIED** as a measured behaviour. It is never stated as a number in copy.*
2. **The child's side of the chat is short.** Mum sends the *bom dia* at 6h, the 7-minute audio, "chegou?". The
   child answers with a sticker, *"ss"* or nothing (*deixar no vácuo*, the Brazilian name for leaving someone on
   read; [definition](https://www.auditorioibirapuera.com.br/o-que-e-ficar-no-vacuo-no-whatsapp/), weak source).
   **The joke lands on the child, as `culture.md` §6 requires,** never on mum's age or tech skills. In this route
   mum is the **power user**: the most consistent creator in Brazil, with a *bom dia* every morning for years.
3. **Gen Z already knows a Pix is not enough.** 47% think mum would prefer cash or Pix, led by Gen Z, yet 54%
   still want a physical gift for the emotion (CNDL/SPC, `culture.md` §5). **A Pix arrives in 3 seconds. A candle
   stays 40 hours.** That contrast is the same insight in money.
4. **The white space is exact.** No Brazilian candle in the sample is built for the date. In 2026 every campaign
   spoke about mum or as mum; none spoke from the child's phone (`culture.md` §2, `category.md` §3).
5. **The platforms reward it.** TikTok asks for the proposition in 3 s and a native, not-over-polished feel
   (`codes.md` §0). A brand whose visual language *is* the phone reads as native without pretending to be a creator.

### Positioning against the shelf

| The Brazilian candle shelf (`category.md` §2) | sinal |
|---|---|
| Frosted or clear glass tumbler, bamboo lid, small white label | A **chat-bubble ceramic pot** in candy gloss; the lid lifts by a red **notification badge** |

## 4. The vessel and the pack

### 4.1 O BALÃO (the pot is a chat bubble)

**The object.** Look straight down at a phone and every message is a rounded rectangle with a small tail. **Extrude
that shape 74 mm and fill it with wax.** From above it is a message. From the front it is a soft, glossy block with
one flat face, which carries the label.

**Geometry (for the Blender build and the potter):**
- **Plan:** a rounded rectangle 88 × 66 mm, corner radius 16 mm.
  - The **tail** grows from the **front-right corner**, where a "sent" bubble's tail sits. It is a vertical fin,
    full height, protruding 8 mm on the diagonal, its tip rounded to 2 mm.
  - From the front the fin reads as a small spur on the right edge; from above it completes the bubble.
- **Walls 4 mm, base 10 mm.** The base underside is recessed 1 mm, with the **i-flame symbol** embossed in it (§5.3),
  read when she turns it over.
- **Front face:** flat, **56 mm wide × 74 mm tall**. **The label is a flat decal on a flat face.** That is the easiest
  label geometry this studio has ever had to verify: the checker fits a plane, not a cylinder (`CLAUDE.md`, *fit
  the model the placement used*).
- **Back face:** flat, carries the back decal (§4.2).
- **Material:** **grés esmaltado** (glazed stoneware), opaque, **high-gloss candy glaze** with a faint orange-peel
  texture. Interior glazed in the same colour, so the wax line is clean.
  - Why ceramic, not glass: the shape needs a slip-cast mould, which is cheap in ceramic and expensive in pressed
    glass, and an opaque glaze gives the label maximum contrast.
  - Why not clear glass: a coloured translucent cylinder with a flame inside reads in Brazil as a **devotional
    *vela de sete dias***. The squat, opaque bubble cannot be mistaken for one.
- **Second life:** a pen and *recado* pot on her desk, the shape still saying "message". Allowed on Route A
  (cosmetic) only; Route B (saneante) forbids reuse claims (`label_rules.md` §1.1). **Do not print a reuse claim;
  show it in lifestyle images only.**

**O BADGE (the lid).**
- A **10 mm glazed ceramic slab in the same bubble outline, tail included**, in the pot's glaze. A food-safe
  silicone ring underneath gives a dust seal.
- **The knob is a notification badge:** a **Ø 20 × 7 mm cylinder in Vermelho Badge glaze**, inset 6 mm from the
  rear-right corner of the lid, where a badge sits on an app icon. **The number is a fired white decal** on its
  flat top, in Gabarito Black.
- **You lift the lid by the badge.** Opening the candle is opening the notification. Lit, the lid goes underneath
  as a coaster: the bubble under the bubble.
- **The lid top is printed.** Seen from above, the candle is a message preview (§4.2).

### 4.3 A CAIXA DE LANÇAMENTO (the phone box)

**The joke.** Gen Z's most-filmed ritual is unboxing a phone: the slow lift, the suction, the device in its tray,
the tiny quick-start card. **We make the box of a device with no screen.**

- **Construction.** A rigid two-piece telescope box, **112 × 90 × 104 mm**, 1,5 mm greyboard wrapped in uncoated
  paper in **Modo Escuro**.
  - The tolerance is tight, so the lid takes about 3 seconds to slide off under its own weight: the sound and
    timing of a phone box. **Prototype the fit; UNVERIFIED.**
- **Lid top.** A full-bleed **render of the pot, front 3/4, lit**, printed the way phone boxes print the device,
  with the wordmark in Bolha. **No Apple or Samsung trade dress:** not white, no device photography conventions
  beyond "object on the lid".
- **Seal.** A round **Vermelho Badge** sticker reading **1** bridges lid and base. **To open the box she breaks the
  notification.**
- **Long side: the spec sheet**, deadpan, in Doto:

```
ESPECIFICAÇÕES
modelo ............ áudio de 40 horas
bateria ........... aprox. 40 h (não recarrega)
velocidade ........ 1x (não tem 2x)
tela .............. nenhuma
câmera ............ nenhuma. ela já tem foto sua demais.
notificações ...... 1
conexão ........... não precisa
peso líquido ...... 200 g
```

- **Back and base:** the full Route A regulatory panel (`label_rules.md` §10–11): INCI with declared allergens,
  Portuguese composition, warnings, use instructions, *razão social*, CNPJ, AFE, Processo ANVISA, SAC, VAL, address
  and **Indústria Brasileira** "com destaque". Every number is a placeholder marked **FICTÍCIO**. PESO LÍQUIDO is
  also on the main panel.
- **Inside the lid** (printed): ***configurando… isso leva umas 40 horas.***
- **Tray:** natural grey moulded pulp, honest and recyclable, shaped to the bubble so the pot sits like a device.
- **No matches or lighter** in the box (Inmetro certification unclear, `label_rules.md` §8).

### 4.5 Regulatory fit (Route A, cosmetic Grau 1; `label_rules.md`)

- **Classification:** *odorizante de ambiente*, RDC 907/2024. The model label follows Route A; Routes B and C are
  shown in `label_rules.md` §1.1 for a real client. **Which route ANVISA prefers today is UNVERIFIED.**
- **Front panel:** brand, product name, "vela aromática" and PESO LÍQUIDO at 4 mm figures in contrasting Tinta.
  Tinta on every glaze passes ≥7:1 (§5.1).
- **Shape:** a chat bubble is not a food, drink, toy or child motif (RDC 989 art. 29, cautious reading). The candy
  glaze is the closest thing to a risk, so **no candy, sweets or confectionery props in any image.**
- **Lot** on the base sticker, never the lid. **No therapeutic, pregnancy, infant, insect or air-purifying claim.**
  *"cera vegetal"* only with proof (CONAR Anexo U, CDC art. 37).

### 4.6 Fallback if the ceramic fails on cost

Keep every graphic idea and change only the substrate. Use a **stock straight-walled opaque glass tumbler** in the
same glazes, with the bubble moved to a **flat bubble-shaped lid** (laser-cut, powder-coated aluminium, red badge
knob kept) and the front label as a **flat rectangular decal**.
- From above it still reads as a message; from the front it loses the tail.
- **A downgrade, not a different brand.** It also lets the four SKUs launch before a mould exists.

### 5.3 Graphic devices: the modular kit

Every device is a rule with fixed proportions, so code can draw it.

1. **O i aceso (the wordmark and symbol).** *sinal* in Doto, ROND 100, wght 700, tracking 0.
   - **The dot of the i is replaced by a flame** drawn on the same 6×10 grid: one cell high, 1,6× the dot's width,
     Chama, with a Bolha core.
   - **Symbol (app icon / avatar):** a Modo Escuro squircle holding one giant dot-matrix **i** with its flame. A red
     badge **1** sits on the top-right corner.
   - The same symbol is embossed under every pot.
2. **O balão (the bubble).** The master shape, used for headlines, cards, labels, lids and the pot itself.
   - **Corner radius = 28% of the bubble's height. One tail per bubble.**
   - **Sent** (the child, the brand) = right tail, glaze colour, Tinta type.
   - **Received** (her) = left tail, Bolha, Tinta type.
   - **≤7 words per bubble.**
   - No green bubbles, ever (§5.7).
3. **O áudio (the voice-note row).** Play disc · waveform · duration in Doto · pill.
   - Waveform: **32 bars, bar width = gap width, rounded caps, heights computed from a real recording.**
   - The played part of the progress fills in Rosa Play.
4. **A pílula 1x (the speed pill).** A rounded rectangle, height : width = 1 : 1,9, Gabarito 800, reading **1x**.
   - It is the tagline as an object. **It may appear as "2x" only struck through or greyed, and only in the act of
     returning to 1x.**
5. **O badge (the notification).** A red circle, white Gabarito 900 numerals. **The number always means something:**
   - **1** = from you;
   - **247** = the family group;
   - **7** = missed calls;
   - **3** = days to go.
   - Never decorative, never a random number. One per frame.
6. **O horário (the timestamp).** Doto in Cinza Visto. **Every image carries one time, and it tells the story:**
   - **06:03** the *bom dia*;
   - **12:40** lunch;
   - **23:47** her "chegou?";
   - **02:17** the reply that finally came;
   - **09/05 · 08:00** the brand's official time.
7. **O visto (✓✓).** Our own two ticks, drawn as vectors. **Cinza Visto = sent; Chama = lit/read.** The double
   tick turns warm when the candle is lit: *acesa = lida*.
8. **O digitando (• • •).** Three Bolha dots. The loading and transition device, and the mini's module.
9. **Salva como (the contact header).** The personalisation slot (§7.5). A single line above a bubble, Gabarito 700,
   ≤18 characters, with our own heart vector allowed.
10. **As barras (signal bars).** Four rounded bars. They appear **only** as the intensity meter on the box spec
    sheet (*sinal: cheio*). One device, one job.
11. **A Chaminha (the flame character).** The flame with two Tinta dot-eyes, after TikTok's own Portuguese advice to
    give a product *"olhos e lábios expressivos"* (`codes.md` §0.12).
    - **Stickers, the end-card wink and the *bom dia* system only. Never on the pack label**, and never a face on
      the real flame in a product render.
12. **Silenciar (the mute bell).** A bell with a slash. Used **only** for the opt-out (§7.5), because the system
    should have a gentle way to say "not this year".

**O gerador (the five-slot generator).** Every execution is:

> **[1 interface module] + [1 object] + [1 line, lowercase, ≤7 words, one full stop] + [1 timestamp] + [the
> wordmark]**

Example: [badge **247**] + [nº 04, lid off, lit] + *"saiu do silencioso. ela vai saber."* + **23:12** + *sinal*.

**Layout rules:**
- The flame is the brightest point.
- Type never touches the pot.
- One red per frame.
- Bubbles align to a 12-column grid like a chat thread: **sent right, received left.**

### 5.4 Render style: two modes, both Blender Cycles

**Mode 1 · *Modo Escuro* (the brand's home; KV, film, most social).**
- **The set:** a seamless backdrop and floor in Modo Escuro with a soft centre lift to `#241C2C`.
- **The interface lives in the room as physical props**, built (in 3D) the way a set designer would build them:
  - giant **inflatable vinyl bubbles** with heat-welded seams and a little valve;
  - a **gummy silicone 1x pill**;
  - waveform bars as **foam-cored cylinders**;
  - a **lacquered red badge** like a beach ball.
  - Real-world scale (a bubble 1,2 m wide), real materials, real dust.
- **The two lights:**
  1. **the candle**: the motivated warm key, 1.900 K, plus a low, large, warm bounce;
  2. **the screen**: a cool 6.500 K rectangle **off-frame right**, the glow of a phone nobody is looking at, giving a
     thin cool rim.
  - **Rule: the warm light always wins the frame.** That is the whole idea, in lighting.
- **Camera:** 85–100 mm equivalent for product, 35–50 mm for room scale, an f/4–5,6 look. Parallel verticals on
  the pot. No wide-angle distortion.

**Mode 2 · *Modo Claro* (e-commerce, the *bom dia* system, daytime).**
- The set: a Bolha seamless with **hard on-camera flash** (the Gen Z flash-photo look) plus a soft fill.
- Crisp shadow and specular highlights on the glaze; the candle lid on, or lit with the flame still the hottest
  point.

**Imperfection rules (anti-slop, `codes.md` §8):**
- Fingerprints on gloss in close-ups.
- One speck of dust on the floor.
- The wick leans 2°.
- The wax dome is never perfectly flat.
- A soot freckle on the rim in "second burn" shots.
- Bubble props show seams and slight under-inflation wrinkles.
- **No** floating particles, bokeh orbs, smoke wisps or lens flares; **no** teal-and-orange grade; **no** glow halo
  added in post.

### 5.5 Motion grammar (the system moves; every move is rendered or coded, none generated)

| Move | Spec | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Pop** | A bubble scales 0 → 108 → 100% with a spring, 260 ms, 8% overshoot | A message arrives |
| **Digitando** | Three dots bounce in sequence, a 900 ms loop, 4 px amplitude | Waiting, loading, a beat |
| **Conectando** | The Doto wordmark lights dot by dot in reading order, 40 ms per dot; the i-flame ignites last with a 3-frame flicker | The logo sting |
| **Trava 1x** | The pill tries 1,5x and 2x (each tap 120 ms), then snaps back to **1x** and locks with a 2 px settle | The tagline |
| **Visto** | ✓✓ turn from Cinza Visto to Chama over 300 ms | Lit = read |
| **Match cut** | A 2D interface bubble seen flat **becomes the top of the 3D pot** in the same outline and position, then the camera tilts to 3/4 | Interface → object (§7.3) |
| **Apagada** | Snuffed: the flame goes out, a thin smoke line rises for 1 s (real, not stylised), and a received-bubble line appears: ***esta vela foi apagada.*** | The care beat (*apagar* means both "delete" and "put out") |

### 5.6 Sound

- **Sonic logo:** **a match strike plus the flame's first breath** ("tsk-fff"), 0,8 s. It always marks the moment
  something turns to 1x. Recorded for real; never a stock "magic" whoosh.
- **The message tone:** our own two-note *plim* (a soft marimba, a perfect fourth up). **Never WhatsApp's sounds**,
  which are part of its brand.
- **The 2x voice:** a warm synthetic voice mumbling non-words, sped up chipmunk-fast. Funny, never mocking: it is
  the *speed* that is ridiculous, not her.
- **Bed:** room tone and a quiet flame. Music, if any, is AI-made and assigned to Sol (the studio rule), so one track
  works on both platforms.
- **Each SKU's label waveform** is computed from its own recorded sound: the strike (nº 01), the first coffee
  pour (nº 02), a pin click (nº 03), a phone buzzing in a drawer (nº 04).

### 5.7 What it must never look like

- **WhatsApp:** no green bubbles, no #25D366, no blue ticks, no doodle wallpaper, no logo, no name, no sounds
  (Meta brand rules, `codes.md` §3).
- **iOS or Android chrome:** no SF Pro, no exact iOS badge red `#FF3B30`, no status-bar clock, no Apple-box white.
- **A real Pix screen or receipt,** or the Pix logo. We may write the word "Pix" in copy only (`codes.md` §3).
- **A devotional candle:** red translucent glass, a tall cylinder, saints or religious motifs (the *vela de sete
  dias* reading).
- **The Brazilian spa shelf:** frosted glass, a bamboo lid, a tiny kraft label, eucalyptus sprigs.
- **Cyberpunk "tech":** neon grids, holograms, glitch, blue-purple gradients. Our tech is domestic: a kitchen at
  night.
- **The *bom dia* image played straight:** Comic Sans, glitter text, clip-art roses. We design *bom dia*, we never
  become it.
- **A toy or a sweet:** no candy props, no sprinkles, nothing edible near the pots.
- **AI-smooth plastic:** the props are vinyl, ceramic and wax, with seams, dust and fingerprints.

### 7.5 The three mechanics, specified

**1 · *Salva como* (conversion).**
- **The question every Brazilian Gen Z has an answer to: how is she saved in your phone?** *Mãe ♥*, *Mainha*,
  *Dona Cida*, *Mamis*, *Mommy*, *Mãe (celular novo)*.
- At checkout, one field: ***como ela tá salva no seu celular?*** (≤18 characters, our heart vector allowed, no
  emoji font).
- The answer is set **by code** on the lid's contact line (§4.2) and on the bubble card. **It stretches the date
  naturally** to *vó Neném*, *madrinha*, *tia Rô*, or a mother figure, without the brand having to say so.
- **Social:** *"comenta como sua mãe tá salva no seu celular"* is comment bait with a purchase at the end.
- **UNVERIFIED** as a named Brazilian trend; it rests on a universal behaviour. The adjacent proof is TikTok's
  2025 *"emergency contact"* trend, which started with *"Realizing I changed my emergency contact from my mom to this
  man"* (29M views; [Dexerto, 12 Feb 2025](https://www.dexerto.com/tiktok/what-is-tiktoks-emergency-contact-trend-3086867),
  secondary). **How we save the people we love is already content.**
- **Production:** personalised lids are a **decal batch** (the lid print is a separate decal), so personalisation is
  a print-run question, not a hand-lettering job. Cut-off **5 May**.

**2 · *Bom dia oficial* (earned media: mum is the channel).**
- **The insight:** mothers are Brazil's most consistent publishers and the family group is their network. **So
  sinal designs the *bom dia* images and lets mums do the distribution, aimed at their own children.**
- **2 to 9 May, a countdown, posted at 06:03 daily:**
  - square 1080 × 1080 images, Mode 2 (Bolha);
  - one **3D rose or sparkle prop** (ours);
  - one pot;
  - one *bom dia* line;
  - a Doto timestamp;
  - a small wordmark.
- **Downloadable free from the brand's profile and site, plus a sticker pack**, with no use of any messaging
  platform's marks.
- **Copy (each one a phrase mums will forward to their children with pleasure):**

| Day | Line |
|---|---|
| **2/5** | ***bom dia ✿ faltam 7 dias. pode encaminhar.*** |
| **4/5** | ***bom dia ✿ leva um casaquinho. e um presente.*** |
| **6/5** | ***bom dia ✿ faltam 3 dias. vou contar até três.*** |
| **7/5** | ***bom dia ✿ visualizou? então responde.*** |

| **9/5** | ***bom dia ✿ feliz dia das mães. de todas as mães, pra todas.*** |

- **Why this is the Droga5 move:** the target's own mother becomes the reminder engine, in her own medium, for
  free, and she will enjoy every forward.
- **Rule:** the images quote the *bom dia* genre with design discipline. **Not religious, never Comic Sans, never
  glitter text.**

**3 · *Silenciar* (care).**
- Mother's Day hurts people who have lost a mother, are estranged, or are trying to conceive (`culture.md` §6).
  **The brand's own interface already has the right gesture: mute.**
- On the site, in every email and in the newsletter sign-up, one toggle with the mute-bell device: ***silenciar dia
  das mães · a gente não toca no assunto até junho. sem pergunta.***
- One tap, honoured instantly, no survey. Confirmation: ***silenciado ✓. até junho.***
- US brands do this at scale (DoorDash: 80.000 opt-outs in 2023, `culture.md` §6). **No Brazilian precedent was
  found, so it is UNVERIFIED as a local practice, which is also why it will be noticed.**

**Who she is** (a fictional adult; no civil name, nationality, biography or contract is attributed):

- **She must not resemble any real person or celebrity** (CONAR art. 34; art. 27 §9 on *sósias*).

**Styling (one look, held across all three ads):**
- **Her mother's lilac crochet vest** (*colete de crochê*), worn over a **white ribbed baby tee**. *Laced Up*
  (`codes.md` §2) and the *casaquinho* in one garment.
- Thin gold hoops, one plain ring.
- Short glossy nails in **cherry**: the one red in her frame, and never blue.
- Makeup: skin first, a touch of blush, balm. No contour, no lashes.

**Set:**

**Camera:** 50 mm equivalent, chest-up, eye level, **a locked-off tripod**.

**Energy and voice:**
- **She is the deadpan newsreader of her own family:** dry face, warm eyes. One eyebrow does every punchline, after

**Rules:**
- **She never holds or mentions using the product.** No *eu uso*, *comprei*, *minha mãe amou*, *me salvou*, *não

- **The disclosure is fixed throughout**, top-left, y 280–330, on a Bolha sticker-shaped plate, TikTok Sans 700, 34 px:

**Fixed structure, every ad:** **0–6 s** she talks to camera, no product · **6–9 s** a silent gesture or reaction,

## 9. Why this beats the previous kits, and why it is not AI slop

### What the previous kits were

| Kit | Line | Model |
|---|---|---|
| **AVELUNE** (hair mask) | *O cuidado percorre o fio.* | A fictional model with the pack in hand |
| **FAÍSCA** (gloss) | *Chegue em cor.* | A fictional model with the pack in hand |
| **VOLTA** (curl cream) | *O movimento é seu.* | A real model with the pack in hand |

All three are **beauty campaigns built from a look**: a tagline, a casting, a palette, the pack in hand. They are
well made, and **every one depends on the people layer.** Remove the model and what is left is a packshot.

### Where sinal goes further

1. **An idea, not a look.** *vela não tem 2x* is a cultural observation with a verified mechanism under it (the
   2021 speed button, Brazil's 4× voice notes). **The swap test:**
   - put *"Chegue em cor"* on any lipstick and it still works;
   - put *"vela não tem 2x"* on anything that is not a candle and it means nothing.
2. **The pack is the medium.** In the earlier kits the pack is a container for a label. Here the **pot is a chat
   bubble, the lid lifts by a notification badge, the label is a voice note whose waveform is the sound of lighting
   it, and the box is a device box with no screen.** The product is the campaign's first ad.
3. **A system, not a palette.** Twelve devices with numeric rules, a **five-slot generator**, a motion grammar, a
   sonic logo and a store voice. **It can make the 100th execution without a designer, which is what Collins
   sells.**
4. **It is produced in-house, start to finish.** Every hero asset is a Blender render plus code-set type: the KV, the
   six images, the film and both cuts. **The AI people are a bonus layer.** If generation fails or a client refuses
   synthetic people, the campaign stands. That is the studio's method promoted from a safeguard to a strategy.
5. **The label checker's job gets easier, not harder.** The front of the pot is **flat**, so verification is a
   plane fit, the case the checker handles best. The previous kits put labels on tubes and bottles.
6. **Participation and care are designed in.** *Salva como* turns personalisation into comment bait. *Bom dia
   oficial* makes mothers the media plan. *Silenciar* gives the grieving a one-tap exit in the brand's own
   interface. The previous kits had captions.
7. **Compliance is turned into craft.** The warnings open in her voice. The disclosure is a designed sticker. The
   AI presenter's lines are scripted so no testimonial is possible. The scam joke ends with real advice.
8. **One-up, made literal:**
   - the film's **match cut** from a flat interface to a rendered object;
   - a **real-time timer** that is the punchline;
   - **generative waveforms** from real recordings;
   - a presenter who speaks in **one unbroken take** on a platform of jump cuts.

### Why it is not AI slop

- **No generator ever draws the product, the label or a letter.** The pot, wax, flame, props and type are
  deterministic: Blender geometry and code-set Gabarito and Doto. **The generator's only job is a fictional person
  at a kitchen table**, and she never touches the pack.
- **Slop is generic; this is specific.** A Brazilian voice-note habit, a Brazilian scam, a Brazilian *bom dia*
  image, a Brazilian school typeface. It only works here, now, for this generation.
- **The craft rules ban the tells:** no particles, no glow halos, no teal-and-orange grade, no plastic skin. The props
  carry fingerprints, seams and dust; the wick leans; the wax is not perfect.
- **The tone refuses the slop register.** No *"ilumine momentos especiais"*, no tears, no *guerreira*. One dry
  line, one true promise.
- **Its most important frame was argued, not generated.** The warm light beats the cold light, every time.
