# What a scented candle sold in Brazil must carry: label, box and ad rules

**Researched 6 Oct 2026, from primary sources where they could be reached.** Copies of the sources are in
`research/src/`. Anything not confirmed at the source is marked **UNVERIFIED**. A source that is not primary is
marked *(secondary)*.

This covers a **fictional demo brand**. Every registration number, CNPJ and address on the model label is a
placeholder and is marked FICTÍCIO. A real client must have its own regulatory adviser (RT) confirm the
regulatory route before printing.

---

## 0. Summary for the creative team

1. **A scented candle IS an ANVISA-regulated product.** It is an *odorizante de ambiente*. The label needs ANVISA
   data, and claims are tightly limited. You cannot say "aromaterapia que acalma", "purifica o ar" or "para
   gestantes".
2. **Three regulatory routes exist.**
   - **(A) Industrial cosmetic, Grau 1, notified.** This is the route the model label uses.
   - **(B) Saneante, risco 1, notified.**
   - **(C) The new artisanal regime.** It was published **5 Oct 2026** and is in force from about **4 Dec 2026**,
     before Dia das Mães 2027.
   - The route changes the mandatory phrases. For a fun Gen Z brand, **route B is the worst**. It requires *"Não
     reutilize a embalagem vazia para outros fins"*, which kills any "reuse the jar" idea. It also bans packaging
     that resembles food, drinks, toys or child motifs.
3. **Net content (INMETRO Portaria 249/2021).**
   - Use "PESO LÍQUIDO 180 g" on the front panel of both the jar label and the box, in a contrasting colour.
   - Figures must be **≥3 mm tall for >50–200 g** and **≥4 mm tall for >200–1.000 g**.
   - Unit symbol ≥⅔ of the figure height.
   - **Design every pack at 4 mm figure height.** It is safe for anything from 150 to 250 g.
4. **Fire warnings are not listed in any ANVISA candle rule, but CDC art. 9 makes them compulsory in substance.**
   Use the warning block in §11.
5. **Fragrance allergens:**
   - Brazil now has an EU-style list: RDC 1.029/2026, from Mercosur GMC 06/25. Limonene, linalool, citral,
     coumarin, geraniol, citronellol, hexyl cinnamal and others must be named in the ingredient list above
     **0,001%** (leave-on).
   - Whether the leave-on threshold applies to a candle is **UNVERIFIED**; declare them anyway.
   - **Butylphenyl methylpropional (lilial) is now prohibited.**
6. **AI presenter in the ad:**
   - **No Brazilian statute in force requires AI disclosure in commercial ads**, as of 6 Oct 2026. PL 2338/2023 is
     still a bill.
   - CONAR's May 2026 influencer guide says expressly that it creates **no new AI-disclosure duty**. It also says
     every CONAR rule applies in full to AI content, especially testimonials (art. 27 §9, Anexo Q).
   - The studio's line *"imagem ilustrativa criada com IA"* plus *"no testimonial lines"* is therefore
     **best practice, not a legal requirement**, and it is exactly the right practice.
7. **The ad must not show dangerous use (CONAR art. 33):**
   - no lit candle left alone or near curtains or bedding;
   - no children or pets near the flame;
   - no one asleep with a candle burning.
   - A Mother's Day "breakfast in bed with a candle" scene is the obvious trap.

## 1. Is a scented candle regulated by ANVISA? YES. Which category is ambiguous, and it matters.

**Evidence that it is regulated:**

- **ANVISA enforcement, 2025.** Resolução-RE nº 762/2025 (GGFIS/ANVISA), Anexo, item 1: *"Empresa: CASA CALMA
  LTDA … Produto: VELAS AROMÁTICAS E AROMATIZANTES DE AMBIENTE CASA CALMA (TODOS); **Tipo de Produto:
  Saneantes**"*. The measures were seizure and a ban on sale, manufacture and advertising.
  [anvisalegis RE 762/2025](https://anvisalegis.datalegis.net/action/ActionDatalegis.php?acao=abrirTextoAto&link=S&tipo=RES&numeroAto=00000762&seqAto=000&valorAno=2025&orgao=RE/GGFIS/ANVISA/MS&cod_modulo=134&cod_menu=9451)
  The Diretoria Colegiada's Voto nº 148/2025 confirms it. The grounds were *"comercialização de velas aromáticas e
  aromatizadores de ambiente sem registro ou notificação, realizada por empresa sem Autorização de Funcionamento
  (AFE) para fabricar saneante"*, breaching **Lei 6.360/1976 arts. 2º and 12**.
  [Voto 148/2025](https://www.gov.br/anvisa/pt-br/composicao/diretoria-colegiada/reunioes-da-diretoria/votos-dos-circuitos-deliberativos-1/2025/cd-0635-2025-voto.pdf/@@download/file)
- **Earlier precedent.** In 2016, ANVISA banned Voluspa and Michel Design Works *"aromatizantes de ambientes e
  velas aromatizantes"* because they had no registration, notification or cadastro (RE nº 1.917/2016).
  [ANVISA news](https://www.gov.br/anvisa/pt-br/assuntos/noticias-anvisa/2016/aromatizantes-de-ambiente-e-velas-sao-proibidos)

**Evidence that it can be a COSMETIC:**

- **RDC 907/2024 art. 4º** (in force, amended by RDC 949/2024): *"Os produtos com a finalidade de odorizantes de
  ambientes são classificados como produtos de higiene pessoal, cosméticos e perfume Grau 1."* Anexo I, Grau 1
  list, item 35: *"Odorizante de Ambiente"*. Grau 1 means notification (*comunicação prévia*), not registration
  (arts. 34–35).
  [RDC 907/2024](https://anvisalegis.datalegis.net/action/ActionDatalegis.php?acao=abrirTextoAto&tipo=RDC&numeroAto=00000907&seqAto=000&valorAno=2024&orgao=RDC/DC/ANVISA/MS&codTipo=&desItem=&desItemFim=&cod_menu=1696&cod_modulo=134&pesquisa=true)
- **IN 474/2026, Anexo, group 2 "Odorizantes de ambiente"** (published 5 Oct 2026) lists ***"Velas aromáticas,
  sachês perfumados, difusores de varetas, sprays"*** as a **cosmetic** group. This is the most recent and the
  most explicit ANVISA text that names candles.
  [IN 474/2026](https://anvisalegis.datalegis.net/action/ActionDatalegis.php?acao=abrirTextoAto&tipo=INM&numeroAto=00000474&seqAto=000&valorAno=2026&orgao=DC/ANVISA/MS&codTipo=&desItem=&desItemFim=&cod_menu=1696&cod_modulo=134&pesquisa=true)

**Evidence that it can be a SANEANTE:**

- **RDC 694/2022 art. 3º VII** (in force) lists *"odorizantes/aromatizantes de ambientes"* as a category of
  *produtos de limpeza e afins*. Art. 4º XXI defines it: *"produto que tem em sua composição substâncias capazes de
  mascarar os odores desagradáveis"*.
  [RDC 694/2022](https://anvisalegis.datalegis.net/action/ActionDatalegis.php?acao=abrirTextoAto&tipo=RDC&numeroAto=00000694&seqAto=000&valorAno=2022&orgao=RDC/DC/ANVISA/MS&codTipo=&desItem=&desItemFim=&cod_menu=1696&cod_modulo=134&pesquisa=true)
- **IN 394/2025 Anexo I** (the saneante category list) includes "Odorizante".
  [IN 394/2025](https://anvisalegis.datalegis.net/action/ActionDatalegis.php?acao=abrirTextoAto&tipo=INM&numeroAto=00000394&seqAto=000&valorAno=2025&orgao=DC/ANVISA/MS&codTipo=&desItem=&desItemFim=&cod_menu=1696&cod_modulo=134&pesquisa=true)
- **RDC 989/2025** (DOU 20 Aug 2025, amended by RDC 1.040/2026) is the new general saneante rule. It **revoked RDC
  59/2010**. [RDC 989/2025](https://anvisalegis.datalegis.net/action/ActionDatalegis.php?acao=abrirTextoAto&tipo=RDC&numeroAto=00000989&seqAto=000&valorAno=2025&orgao=RDC/DC/ANVISA/MS&codTipo=&desItem=&desItemFim=&cod_menu=1696&cod_modulo=134&pesquisa=true)

**How practitioners split it** *(secondary, a regulatory consultancy)*: perfuming *environments in general* is a
cosmetic, Grau 1. Perfuming *objects or specific spaces* (drawers, clothes, cars) is a saneante, risco 1.
[Stone Okamont](https://www.stoneokamont.com.br/como-regularizar-aromatizantes)

> **UNVERIFIED:** which route ANVISA's technical area currently *prefers* for a candle. The 2025 enforcement
> called candles "saneantes". The 2024 cosmetics RDC and the Oct 2026 artisanal IN put them under cosmetics.
> **For the demo, model the label on Route A, the cosmetic Grau 1 route.** It is the more current text, it names
> candles explicitly, and it is the one most compatible with a fun, reusable-jar design. Show the Route B and C
> differences in the packet so a prospect sees we know them.

### 1.2 Claims: what the copy may and may not say on pack

- **IN 474/2026 group 2, odorizantes de ambiente.**
  - **Allowed:** *"Perfuma o ambiente, confere aroma agradável, mascara odores."*
  - **Not allowed:** *"Ação terapêutica, purifica o ar, elimina bactérias, repele insetos."*
  - This is written for the artisanal regime, but it is ANVISA's clearest statement of candle claims, so use it
    as the standard for all routes.
- **RDC 907/2024 art. 12.** No names, marks, images, links or text that:
  - mislead about properties, nature, composition, use or safety (I);
  - make therapeutic claims (II);
  - claim to kill or repel insects (III);
  - claim to disinfect (IV).
- **RDC 907/2024 Anexo I item 1(a).** A Grau 1 product that declares an indication *"para gestantes ou público
  infantil"* **becomes Grau 2**, which requires registration.
  ⇒ **No "segura para grávidas", no "para o quarto do bebê".** This matters for Mother's Day copy.
- **Repellent and citronella candles** need **registration**: RDC 907 art. 34 VIII, or the saneante desinfestante
  route. Stay away from them.
- **Saneante route only.** RDC 989/2025 art. 39 bans on-label words such as *"seguro", "ecológico", "contém todos
  os ingredientes naturais", "não tóxico"*. Art. 40 II bans health-protection claims.
- **Sustainability words** (e.g. "vegana", "cera de soja sustentável", "eco") need proof under **CONAR Anexo U**
  (see §9) and **CDC art. 37**.

## 2. INMETRO: declaring net content ("Peso líquido")

**Current rule: Portaria Inmetro nº 249, de 9 de junho de 2021.** It is the consolidated Regulamento Técnico
Metrológico on how to express the quantity of pre-packaged goods.

- **Confirmed current, from primary sources.** RDC 989/2025 art. 45 §1º (consolidated text, Aug 2026) refers to
  *"Portaria Inmetro nº 249/2021 e suas atualizações"*. Inmetro's metrology regulatory agenda for 2026–27
  (**Portaria Inmetro nº 246, de 22/04/2026**) contains **no project revising it**.
  [Agenda 2026–27](https://www.gov.br/inmetro/pt-br/assuntos/regulamentacao/agenda-regulatoria/RTAC003139.pdf)
- **UNVERIFIED at the primary PDF.** `inmetro.gov.br/legislacao/rtac/pdf/RTAC002775.pdf` and the ALADI mirror
  returned 503 or a connection reset today. The values below are cross-checked against:
  - the ABRE summary of Portaria 249/2021 *(secondary)*:
    [ABRE](https://abre.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/inmetro.pdf)
  - the primary text of the predecessor it consolidated, **Portaria Inmetro 157/2002**, which has the same
    Tabela II values: [legisweb 157/2002](https://www.legisweb.com.br/legislacao/?id=184078)
  - Item numbers inside Portaria 249 are therefore **UNVERIFIED**.

**Checklist:**

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Where | On the **vista principal** (main panel), on the label or the product body (157/2002 item 3.1; ABRE summary of 249) |
| Colour | **Contrasting** with the background. With transparent packaging, contrasting with the product |
| Wording before the number (mass) | **PESO LÍQUIDO** or **CONTEÚDO LÍQUIDO** or **PESO LÍQ.** or **Peso Líq.** or **Peso Líquido** |
| Unit | SI: **g** (grams). Write **"180 g"**: number, a space, lowercase g, no full stop, no plural, never "gr" or "grs". **UNVERIFIED at source today**; this is the SI writing convention Inmetro adopts |
| Figure height, Tabela II (mass/volume) | ≤50 g: **2 mm** · >50 to ≤200 g: **3 mm** · >200 to ≤1.000 g: **4 mm** · >1.000 g: **6 mm** |
| Unit symbol height | **≥ ⅔ of the figure height** (157/2002 item 4.3; same in 249 per ABRE) |
| Not on the main panel | Characters **at least 2×** the table values |
| Moulded or embossed on the product body without contrast | **+2 mm** over the table value |
| Grouped products | Transparent group packs are exempt if the individual packs are readable. Packs of *different* products state each item's quantity, preceded by "contém", "conteúdo" or "cont." |

**What the net weight measures:** the **wax (product) mass only**. The glass, lid, wick tab and box are packaging
(*tara*). This is the general metrological principle. Fill tolerances are set by a separate Inmetro portaria on
pre-packaged goods: **UNVERIFIED which number is current**.

**For 150–250 g:**

- 150–200 g ⇒ figures ≥3 mm, "g" ≥2 mm.
- >200–250 g ⇒ figures ≥4 mm, "g" ≥2,7 mm.
- **House rule: set the figures at 4,0 mm cap height on both the jar and the box front.** As a rough guide, lining
  figures are about 0,70 em, so 4 mm needs about 16 pt type and 3 mm about 12 pt. **Measure on the real typeface
  before release.**
- **Put the net weight on the box front too** if the box is opaque. The box is what the buyer sees at sale.

## 3. CDC (Lei 8.078/1990): information duties, quoted

Source: [Planalto, compiled text](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l8078compilado.htm)

- **Art. 31**: *"A oferta e apresentação de produtos ou serviços devem assegurar informações corretas, claras,
  precisas, ostensivas e **em língua portuguesa** sobre suas características, qualidades, quantidade, composição,
  preço, garantia, prazos de validade e origem, entre outros dados, bem como sobre os **riscos que apresentam à
  saúde e segurança** dos consumidores."*
- **Art. 9º**: *"O fornecedor de produtos e serviços potencialmente nocivos ou perigosos à saúde ou segurança
  deverá informar, de maneira ostensiva e adequada, a respeito da sua nocividade ou periculosidade…"*
  ⇒ **This is the legal hook for candle fire warnings.**
- **Art. 8º, §1º**: for an industrial product, the manufacturer gives the safety information *"através de impressos
  apropriados que devam acompanhar o produto"*. An insert card is a legitimate home for the full instructions.
- **Art. 6º III**: the right to clear information including quantity, composition and risks.
- **Art. 10, §1º**: the duty to recall publicly if a danger is found later. That is a reason to print a **lot
  code**.
- **Art. 12**: strict liability, including *"informações insuficientes ou inadequadas sobre sua utilização e
  riscos"*.
- **Art. 36**: an advert must be immediately identifiable as an advert. The supplier must keep the data that
  supports the message.
- **Art. 37 §1º**: misleading advertising includes misleading by omission.

## 4. Required and customary warnings

**Legally required texts, by route:**

- **Route C, artisanal: IN 474/2026, group 2 mandatory minimum warnings.** These are the most candle-specific
  ANVISA warnings in existence:
  - *"Produto destinado exclusivamente à odorização de ambientes. Não aplicar sobre a pele."*
  - *"Evite contato com os olhos e mucosas. Em caso de contato, lave com água em abundância."*
  - *"Manter fora do alcance de crianças e animais."*
  - *"Inflamável. Manter afastado do fogo e do calor."* Applies only *"quando o produto apresentar característica de
    inflamabilidade, especialmente em formulações com alta concentração de álcool ou apresentação em spray"*. It
    does **not** fit a candle, which is meant to burn.
- **Route B, saneante: RDC 989/2025 arts. 44–45.**
  - Main panel: **"CONSERVE FORA DO ALCANCE DE CRIANÇAS E ANIMAIS DOMÉSTICOS"** and **"ANTES DE USAR LEIA AS
    INSTRUÇÕES DO RÓTULO"**, in capitals and bold (art. 44 III–IV, §1º).
  - Main or secondary panel:
    - *"Mantenha o produto em sua embalagem original"*;
    - *"Não reutilize a embalagem vazia para outros fins."*;
    - *"Em caso de contato com os olhos e/ou pele, lave imediatamente com água em abundância."*;
    - *"Em caso de ingestão, não provoque vômitos e consulte imediatamente o Centro de Informação e Assistência
      Toxicológica (Ciat) ou um médico levando a embalagem ou o rótulo do produto."*;
    - the phone number of at least one **Ciat**. ANVISA's national line is **Disque-Intoxicação 0800-722-6001**.
      [ANVISA](https://www.gov.br/anvisa/pt-br/assuntos/agrotoxicos/disque-intoxicacao)
  - RDC 694/2022 art. 23 adds lot number and use instructions.
- **Route A, cosmetic: RDC 907/2024 art. 13.** Requires *"advertências e restrições de uso (se for o caso)"* on
  both packs. It has no candle-specific list; art. 24 lists aerosols, dyes and so on, not candles. ⇒ The content
  comes from **CDC art. 9º**, plus the IN 474 group-2 phrases adopted voluntarily.

**Customary candle fire-safety content.** It is not written into Brazilian law, but CDC art. 9 requires it in
substance. Sources:

- **US National Candle Association** (trade body).
  [NCA Candle Safety Tips](https://candles.org/candle-safety-tips/), fetched:
  - *"Trim the wick to ¼ inch before each use"* (≈6 mm);
  - *"Never leave a burning candle unattended"*;
  - *"Keep candles out of reach of children and pets"*;
  - keep 12 in (≈30 cm) from curtains, bedding and books;
  - avoid drafts; don't move a candle while the wax is liquid;
  - snuff, don't use water;
  - *"stop when ½ inch of wax remains"* (≈1 cm).
  - The **4-hour maximum burn** is on [NCA FAQs](https://candles.org/faqs/). Seen only as a search snippet:
    **UNVERIFIED at page**.
- **EU EN 15494:2019 (candle safety labels).** The standard itself is paywalled; summaries are *(secondary)*.
  Its mandatory core is a warning triangle plus "never leave a burning candle unattended", "keep away from
  children and pets" and "keep away from things that catch fire". This is **not** a Brazilian requirement.
  [BSI listing](https://knowledge.bsigroup.com/products/candles-product-safety-labels) ·
  [summary (secondary)](https://candle-shack.co.uk/blogs/all-blogs/candle-safety-labels-general-product-safety-pictograms)
- **ASTM F2417** (US candle fire-safety labelling) and **F2058** (holders) are referenced by NCA. They are voluntary
  and US-only.
- **No ABNT or INMETRO standard or compulsory programme for candles was found.** **UNVERIFIED** that none exists.

## 5. Manufacturer identification and origin

- **Route A, cosmetic: RDC 907/2024 art. 13 II.** The secondary packaging, or the primary if there is no box
  (art. 14), must carry:
  - nome (razão social) do titular;
  - **CNPJ**;
  - **AFE** number for the cosmetic class;
  - **número do processo de regularização**;
  - **país de origem**;
  - customer-service contact (*"telefone, e-mail, página web ou outro meio"*);
  - marca and nome do produto;
  - ingredients in INCI;
  - prazo de validade;
  - conteúdo.
  - **Art. 20:** information in Brazilian Portuguese, except INCI codes, names and brands.
- **Route B, saneante: RDC 989/2025 art. 45:**
  - AFE number;
  - manufacturer identification;
  - notification number;
  - **name, address and phone of the holder**;
  - country of origin.
- **Route C, artisanal: RDC 1.045/2026 art. 20:**
  - producer identification, including the *licença sanitária* when there is one;
  - contact (phone, e-mail or social network);
  - **endereço de produção**.
- **Tax rule: RIPI, Decreto 7.212/2010.**
  [Planalto](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2007-2010/2010/decreto/d7212.htm)

    - II. the establishment's **CNPJ**;

    - IV. the expression **"Indústria Brasileira"**.
  - **Art. 274:** *"A expressão Indústria Brasileira será inscrita com destaque e em caracteres bem visíveis."*

## 6. Lot, manufacture date and shelf life

- **Route A:** **lote** on the **primary** pack, i.e. the jar (RDC 907 art. 13 I c). **Prazo de validade** on
  the secondary pack (art. 13 II m). For a kit, the box shows the **shortest remaining shelf life** of its
  components (art. 19).
- **Route B:** lot and shelf life with manufacture date, or an expiry date (RDC 989 art. 45 VIII–IX). These must
  **not** be printed on removable parts such as **lids** (art. 32).
- **Route C:** manufacture date plus shelf life, **12 months maximum** (RDC 1.045 arts. 9º and 20 V).
- **Practical rule for every route:** print **LOTE + FAB + VAL on a base sticker on the jar**, never on the lid,
  and repeat VAL on the box.

## 7. Fragrance allergens: now required in Brazil for cosmetics

- **RDC 1.029/2026** (adopted 11 Jun 2026; in force on publication; it internalises **Mercosur GMC 06/25** and
  revokes RDC 530/2021). Fragrance ingredients must be named **in INCI in the ingredient list** above **0,001% in
  leave-on** and **0,01% in rinse-off** products. Confirmed in the annex for:
  - **LIMONENE**, **LINALOOL**, **CITRAL**, **CITRONELLOL**, **COUMARIN**, **GERANIOL**, **HEXYL CINNAMAL**;
  - HYDROXYCITRONELLAL, ISOEUGENOL, ALPHA-ISOMETHYL IONONE, AMYL CINNAMAL, CINNAMYL ALCOHOL;
  - METHYL 2-OCTYNOATE, 6-METHYL COUMARIN;
  - "Rose ketones".
  - **BUTYLPHENYL METHYLPROPIONAL (lilial) was transferred to the PROHIBITED list.**
  - Products already regularised get **24 months** to comply (art. 2º); new products comply from the start.
  [RDC 1.029/2026](https://anvisalegis.datalegis.net/action/ActionDatalegis.php?acao=abrirTextoAto&tipo=RDC&numeroAto=00001029&seqAto=000&valorAno=2026&orgao=RDC/DC/ANVISA/MS&codTipo=&desItem=&desItemFim=&cod_menu=1696&cod_modulo=134&pesquisa=true)
- **RDC 898/2024**: composition in **Portuguese** as well as INCI. Either in brackets after each INCI name, or as a
  second list headed *"Ingredientes (português):"*, or via a QR code preceded by that phrase.
  [RDC 898/2024](https://anvisalegis.datalegis.net/action/ActionDatalegis.php?acao=abrirTextoAto&tipo=RDC&numeroAto=00000898&seqAto=000&valorAno=2024&orgao=RDC/DC/ANVISA/MS&codTipo=&desItem=&desItemFim=&cod_menu=1696&cod_modulo=134&pesquisa=true)
- **UNVERIFIED:** whether a candle is "sem enxágue" for this list. It is not applied to skin, but it is never
  rinsed, so the cautious reading is 0,001%. **Recommendation: declare every listed allergen the fragrance house's
  IFRA or allergen certificate shows above 0,001% of the finished candle.**
- **Saneante route:** composition shows the active and toxicologically relevant components by technical name, and
  the rest by function (RDC 989 art. 45 §3º).
- **Not found as a Brazilian requirement:**
  - EU CLP-style hazard labelling of candles (EUH208, *"Contém X. Pode provocar uma reação alérgica"*) or GHS
    pictograms on consumer candles. ABNT NBR 14725:2023 is referenced only for professional-use saneantes' safety
    data sheets (RDC 989 art. 26).
  - **IFRA** standards are voluntary industry self-regulation. The IFRA category number for candles is
    **UNVERIFIED**.

## 8. Recycling and packaging

- **No mandatory recycling symbol for glass or paperboard was found in federal law.** **UNVERIFIED** that none
  exists. **ABNT NBR 16182:2013** (symbols for selective-disposal guidance and material identification;
  reconfirmed 2017 and 2022 per the catalogue) is **voluntary**.
  [normas.com.br (catalogue)](https://normas.com.br/visualizar/abnt-nbr-nm/33130/abnt-nbr16182-embalagem-e-acondicionamento-simbologia-de-orientacao-de-descarte-seletivo-e-de-identificacaode-materiais)
  ABNT NBR 13230 covers **plastics** only (e.g. a PP lid).
- **Route B creative traps:**
  - RDC 989/2025 **art. 29 par. único**: saneante packaging *"não deve simular, nem ter apresentação ou semelhança
    com produtos alimentícios, bebidas, cosméticos, medicamentos e brinquedos ou qualquer motivo infantil que
    possa ser atraente para as crianças"*.
  - **Art. 45 XIV**: *"Não reutilize a embalagem vazia para outros fins."*
  - ⇒ **No candle that looks like a brigadeiro, a latte or a cake; no "reuse me as a cup" under Route B.**
  - Under Route A this is softer (RDC 907 art. 12 I: nothing that misleads about nature or safety). Even so, **a
    candle that imitates food is a CDC art. 8–10 safety risk**. Avoid it on every route.
- **Gift box and kit:**
  - A transparent outer wrap is exempt if the mandatory labelling stays visible (RDC 907 art. 17).
  - Kit shelf life is the shortest (art. 19).
  - Under Route C, *"simples montagem de kits"* is not artisanal.
  - **Do not put matches or a lighter in the box** unless they are Inmetro-certified. Lighters appear in Inmetro's
    regulatory programme [AIR isqueiros 2025](https://www.gov.br/inmetro/pt-br/assuntos/regulamentacao/analise-de-impacto-regulatorio/dispensas-de-air/2025/isqueiros-a-gas/relatorio).

## 9. Advertising: CONAR, AI presenter and testimonials

Sources:

- **Código Brasileiro de Autorregulamentação Publicitária**, 2026 edition:
  [Codigo_CONAR_2026.pdf](https://conar.wpenginepowered.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Codigo_CONAR_2026.pdf)
- **Guia de Marketing e Publicidade por Influenciadores Digitais**, published 12 May 2026, in effect from
  1 Jun 2026:
  [Guia 2026](https://conar.wpenginepowered.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/260525_GUIA_INFLUENCIADORES_CONAR_v6.pdf)

**What binds the O ANÚNCIO with an AI presenter:**

- **Art. 27 §9º, Testemunhais:**
  - *"a. O anúncio abrigará apenas depoimentos personalizados e genuínos, ligados à experiência passada ou presente
    de quem presta o depoimento…; b. o testemunho utilizado deve ser sempre comprovável; c. quando se usam modelos
    sem personalização, permite-se o depoimento como 'licença publicitária' que, em nenhuma hipótese, se procurará
    confundir com um testemunhal"*.
  - ⇒ An AI presenter who has used nothing **cannot give a testimonial**. They can only speak as an unpersonalised
    model ("licença publicitária"), never as a customer. **This is the legal basis of the studio's no-testimonial
    rule.**
- **Anexo Q, item 3(b):** *"Os modelos profissionais… não deverão se fazer passar por Consumidor comum."*
  **Item 5(a):** the advertiser must be able to prove any testimonial.
- **Art. 28:** the ad must be clearly identifiable as an ad, whatever the format. With CDC art. 36, an organic-style
  vertical needs the "publi" or brand identification visible.
- **Art. 33** (very relevant to candles): condemns ads that *"b. estimulem o uso perigoso do produto oferecido; c.
  deixem de mencionar cuidados especiais para a prevenção de acidentes, quando tais cuidados forem essenciais ao
  uso do produto"*.
  - ⇒ **No lit candle unattended, near fabric, beside a bed, near children or pets, or being carried.**
  - Add a micro-line on the end card: **"Nunca deixe a vela acesa sem supervisão."**
- **Art. 34(a):** condemns using images of living persons without prior express authorisation. ⇒ **The AI
  presenter must not resemble a real person**, including a celebrity look-alike; see also art. 27 §9(e) on
  *sósias*.
- **Anexo U, sustainability claims:**
  - "vegana", "natural", "sustentável", "eco" and "cera de soja" sustainability claims must be true, verifiable
    and specific;
  - greenwashing is condemned;
  - the annex applies with the CDC.
- **Guia 2026 §1.3.1 "Inteligência Artificial":** CBAP and the guide *"aplicam-se integralmente aos conteúdos
  publicitários, independentemente das tecnologias… sendo aplicável… aos conteúdos gerados, editados ou
  segmentados com o uso de sistemas ou ferramentas de Inteligência Artificial"*. Everyone in the chain stays
  responsible and must ensure the ad has no *"apresentação visual ou sonora, simulações, endossos ou testemunhais
  que possam induzir a erro"*.
- **Guia 2026 footnote 6:** *"Este texto não cuida de diretrizes ou deveres novos acerca dos conteúdos gerados com o
  uso de tais sistemas de IA… não versando em particular sobre avisos (disclosure) acerca do uso de tais técnicas
  de produção."*
  - ⇒ **CONAR imposes no AI-disclosure duty, as of May 2026.**
  - **Footnote 2** notes international best practice of transparency so consumers know *"que não está interagindo
    com um ser humano real"*. It also defines virtual influencers as including computer-generated "persons" and
    avatars.
- **Decreto 12.880/2026, art. 11** (ECA Digital regulation): transparency about *"caráter sintético e
  automatizado"* applies to **interactive generative systems directed at or likely accessed by children and
  adolescents** (chatbots and LLM interfaces). It does **not** apply to a pre-rendered ad.
  [Planalto](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2026/decreto/D12880.htm)
  ⇒ **Target paid media at 18+.**
- **PL 2338/2023 (the AI framework):**
  - approved by the Senate on 10 Dec 2024;
  - in the Câmara special committee; vote reported postponed to after the Oct 2026 elections *(secondary)*;
    [CASRAI](https://casrai.org/guides/brazil-ai-bill-pl-2338-status) ·
    [Câmara text](https://www.camara.leg.br/proposicoesWeb/prop_mostrarintegra?codteor=3106629&filename=Avulso+PL+2338%2F2023)
  - **not law**;
  - it would require provenance markers on synthetic content.
- **Electoral rules (TSE)** require AI labelling in *electoral* ads only *(secondary; out of scope)*.
  [Barbieri](https://www.barbieriadvogados.com/conteudo-gerado-por-ia-marcacao-e-rotulagem/)
- **Precedent:** CONAR archived the 2023 Volkswagen/Elis Regina AI case and acknowledged a lack of rules
  *(secondary, headline only, not fetched)*.
  [Folha Vitória](https://www.folhavitoria.com.br/cultura/conar-diz-que-anuncio-em-ia-nao-desrespeitou-elis-arquiva-caso-mas-admite-falta-de-regras/)
- **Saneante route only:** RDC 989/2025 art. 24. A product may be advertised only once it is regularised, and
  ads must match the approved conditions.

**Studio rule set, confirmed as compliant best practice:**

1. On-screen *"Imagem ilustrativa criada com IA."*, plus the brand handle.
2. The presenter never says "eu uso", "comprei", "minha mãe amou", "me salvou", results or therapeutic effects.
3. The presenter never holds the labelled pack in motion. This is also why the composited key visual carries the
   label.
4. No dangerous use on screen; the safety micro-line goes on the end card.
5. Claims stay inside IN 474 group 2: perfuma, aroma agradável, mascara odores.

## 10. Exact checklist: Route A, cosmetic Grau 1, as used for the demo

**JAR (embalagem primária: a label on a cylinder, plus a base sticker)**

- [ ] Brand (marca) *(RDC 907 art. 13 I d)*
- [ ] Product name *(I e)*, plus the group if not implicit: "Vela aromática" / "Odorizante de ambiente" *(I b)*
- [ ] Warnings and use restrictions *(I a)*. The full block, or *"Advertências: ver embalagem externa"* if space is
      short *(art. 16)*. **Recommended: keep the 4 core fire lines on the jar anyway** (CDC art. 9)
- [ ] **LOTE** on the jar base sticker, not on the lid *(I c; RDC 989 art. 32 by analogy)*
- [ ] **PESO LÍQUIDO 180 g** on the front panel, figures ≥3 mm (≥4 mm house rule), "g" ≥⅔ *(Portaria Inmetro
      249/2021)*
- [ ] Contrasting colour for the net weight
- [ ] "Indústria Brasileira" *(RIPI art. 274)*, if the jar is sold without a box

**GIFT BOX (embalagem secundária)**

- [ ] Brand and product name; group *(RDC 907 art. 13 II d, j, k)*
- [ ] **PESO LÍQUIDO** on the front panel *(Inmetro 249)*
- [ ] Full ingredients in **INCI**, with declared allergens, plus **Portuguese composition** *(II i; RDC 1.029/2026;
      RDC 898/2024)*
- [ ] Warnings and use restrictions *(II a)*, plus use instructions *(III a)*
- [ ] **Razão social**, **CNPJ**, **AFE nº**, **Processo ANVISA nº** *(II e–h)*
- [ ] **País de origem**: "Indústria Brasileira", *"com destaque"* *(II l; RIPI arts. 273–274)*, plus the
      manufacturer's address *(RIPI art. 273 III)*
- [ ] Customer service (SAC): phone, e-mail or site *(II c)*
- [ ] **Validade** (and FAB) *(II m)*
- [ ] Optional: recycling and disposal symbols per ABNT NBR 16182 (voluntary); the PP-lid symbol per NBR 13230
- [ ] Nothing therapeutic, nothing "para gestantes" or "infantil", nothing that kills or repels insects, nothing
      that disinfects *(RDC 907 art. 12; Anexo I item 1)*
