# Culture research: Dia das Mães × Gen Z × candles (Brazil)

Research date: 6 Oct 2026. Purpose: the raw cultural material for a fictional Gen Z candle brand launching for **Dia das Mães, Sunday 9 May 2027**.
Evidence rule used here: every number has a URL. Where a phrase or behaviour is common knowledge but I could not fetch a source, it is marked **UNVERIFIED (cultural observation)**. Do not quote those in client-facing copy as facts; they are fine as creative material.

---

## 0. The ten findings that matter most

1. **The date is Sunday 9 May 2027.** The second Sunday of May has been fixed by Decreto 21.366 of 5 May 1932 ([Câmara, original text](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/decret/1930-1939/decreto-21366-5-maio-1932-559485-publicacaooriginal-81718-pe.html)). Computed: 2026 = 10 May, 2027 = 9 May.
2. **It is retail's second-biggest date, the "Christmas of the first half".** CNC projected R$14,47 bi in 2026 (+1,5%) ([Diário do Comércio, 7 May 2026](https://diariodocomercio.com.br/economia/dia-maes-deve-movimentar-r-144-bilhoes-2026/)).
3. **Perfume and cosmetics are the #2 gift category everywhere**, at 28–50% depending on the survey. Home items run 12–22%. A candle sits between "beauty" and "home", the two categories it can steal from.
4. **Buying is last-minute.** In Sebrae-MT's survey, 59% planned to buy in the week before the date ([Sebrae MT PDF](https://datasebrae.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Pesquisa-Intencao-de-Consumo-para-o-Dia-das-Maes-2026.pdf)). A DOOH vendor reports shopping-centre sales up 78% on the Saturday before, against the previous Saturday ([helloo, 6 May 2026](https://static.poder360.com.br/2026/05/Pesquisa-Dia-das-Maes-helloo-6mai2026.pdf)).
5. **Gen Z knows Mum would rather have a Pix.** CNDL/SPC: "47% dos consumidores entendem que essa seria a preferência das mães" (cash or Pix, led by Gen Z), but 54% still prioritise a physical gift for its emotional value ([Central do Varejo](https://centraldovarejo.com.br/dia-das-maes-2026-varejo-espera-movimentacao-de-r-3791-bilhoes-com-127-milhoes-de-consumidores/?amp=1)). **That tension is a ready-made headline:** a candle's job is to be the thing a Pix cannot be.
6. **The 2026 ad landscape moved to "menos idealização e mais realidade"** ([Meio & Mensagem, 7 May 2026](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/womentowatch/o-que-marca-as-campanhas-de-dia-das-maes-em-2026)). It split into tearjerkers (O Boticário "Despedidas", 40M+ organic views in 5 days, per [Propmark](https://propmark.com.br/mercado/marcas-travam-disputa-pelas-campanhas-que-mais-emocionam-no-dia-das-maes/)) and gentle humour (Tim, Natura, Rede Bom Lugar). **Nobody has taken the child's-eye, meme-literate, affectionate point of view, and no candle brand owns the date.**
7. **Mum's love language is a set of repeated phrases**: *leva um casaquinho*, *me avisa quando chegar*, *já comeu?*, *na volta a gente compra*, *você não é todo mundo*. Gen Z turns them into memes, and the press frames them as a shared "dialect" ([Jovem Pan](https://jovempan.com.br/noticias/brasil/entenda-a-logica-por-tras-do-vocabulario-das-maes-brasileiras.html)). **The brand's raw material is that vocabulary, not "mother" as an abstraction.**
8. **WhatsApp is the stage.** Brazil sends voice notes at 4× the rate of any other country (Zuckerberg, June 2024, via [TecMundo](https://www.estadao.com.br/tecmundo/produto-e-review/286513-por-que-brasileiros-enviam-tantos-audios-whatsapp/)), and frequency rose 7% and duration 8% against 2024 (Meta, cited in [ABC da Comunicação, 2026](https://www.abcdacomunicacao.com.br/usaflex-transforma-audio-de-whatsapp-em-acao-de-dia-das-maes/)). The *bom dia* glitter image, the 5-minute voice note and the family group are mum's medium. Gen Z now reuses the *bom dia* aesthetic post-ironically ([nss magazine, Italian buongiorno equivalent](https://test5.nssmag.com/en/lifestyle/38293/buongiorno-immagini-2024)).
9. **Memes work with Gen Z, but forcing them does not.** 7 in 10 Gen Z approve of brands using memes ([Propmark / Instituto Z, May 2024](https://propmark.com.br/mercado/sete-a-cada-dez-pessoas-da-geracao-z-aprovam-o-uso-de-memes-em-estrategias-de-marketing/)). A teacher quoted in a May 2026 piece on Gen Z slang and mothers: *"se o adulto forçar, vira 'cringe' na hora"* (paraphrased in [ES Hoje](https://eshoje.com.br/?p=866978)). **Use mum's vocabulary, not Gen Z slang.** Mum's lines are evergreen; Gen Z slang expires in months.
10. **The candle category is small-maker-led and cheap.** MEIs making candles rose from 570 (2021) to 1.685 (2025), +195,6% (Sebrae data via [O Hoje, 11 May 2026](https://ohoje.com/2026/05/11/mercado-de-velas-aromaticas-cresce-quase-200-no-brasil/)). Artisanal candles sell online at R$16–55, and personalised kits exceed R$100. Decorative candles run R$17–200, most under R$60 ([Elle Brasil, 2021](https://elle.com.br/lifestyle/velas-decorativas)). **The opening is a brand with an idea in a category of anonymous makers.**

**Do not add these together.** They measure different things: retail volume, purchase intention, and commerce plus services. **What this means for a candle:**
- Price the hero at or under the ~R$262–294 average ticket. Better still, sit inside the R$100–200 band where Gen Z budgets live (41% of one 2025 sample planned R$100–200, per the [ACP/Bem Paraná summary](https://www.bemparana.com.br/publicacao/blogs/simonebello/pesquisa-da-acp-indica-comportamento-do-consumidor-para-o-dia-das-maes-2025/), secondary).
- A kit or gift box matches Sebrae's explicit advice ("kits prontos… embalagens especiais… cartão personalizado").
- **Recipients go beyond "mum":** grandmother, sister, wife, a mother figure, herself (16%), and pet mums (searches for "dia das mães pet" rising since 2021, per [Jornal Contábil](https://jornalcontabil.com.br/noticia/dia-das-maes-tendencias-de-pesquisa-revelam-aumento-nas-buscas-e-preferencias-dos-consumidores/), secondary). Copy should leave room for *a mãe que você tem*.

**White space:**
- Every campaign above speaks about mum, or as mum. **None speaks as the Gen Z child, in the child's own register:** affectionate zoeira and mum's catchphrases quoted back to her with love.
- The tearjerker lane is crowded and expensive (Boticário). The humour lane belongs to telecoms and retailers, not gift objects.
- A candle brand that turns mum's phrases into the product is unclaimed territory.

## 3. Gen Z: memes, slang and cringe (facts)

- Brazil's Gen Z (13–27) is over 50 million people. **80% say memes make it easier to connect with people.** 98% use Instagram for memes; TikTok is second at 55% ([Forbes Brasil, Jun 2024, Instituto Z/Trope](https://forbes.com.br/forbes-tech/2024/06/80-da-geracao-z-acredita-que-os-memes-facilitam-a-conexao-entre-pessoas/)).
- **70% approve of brands using memes. 75% say memes signal youth and modernity**, but they still want *"produtos de qualidade e com conexão com a GenZ"* ([Propmark, May 2024](https://propmark.com.br/mercado/sete-a-cada-dez-pessoas-da-geracao-z-aprovam-o-uso-de-memes-em-estrategias-de-marketing/)).
- **Slang now comes from X and TikTok, not TV** (linguist Luciana Migliaccio). And when adults force it, it turns cringe at once (teacher Adriana Fontes) ([ES Hoje, 9 May 2026](https://eshoje.com.br/?p=866978)).
- **The mother–child slang gap is itself viral content.** On 24 Apr 2026 Ingrid Guimarães posted WhatsApp screenshots with her 16-year-old (*cpa*, *rz*, *nn*, *amnh*, *várzea*, *-1000 aura*), got 195.000+ likes, and replied *"Você está perdendo o português, minha filha"* ([Diário do Nordeste](https://diariodonordeste.verdesmares.com.br/entretenimento/zoeira/conversa-entre-ingrid-guimaraes-e-filha-diverte-web-com-girias-da-geracao-z-1.3760519)).
- **The 2021 "cringe war" list of what Gen Z calls cringe:** yellow emojis used on purpose; Disney, Friends and Harry Potter fandom; endless talk of coffee and *boletos*; side partings and skinny jeans; calling beer *breja* or *litrão*; perfect grammar with capitalised sentences; laughing with *rsrsrs* ([Tribuna de Jundiaí, 25 Jun 2021](https://tribunadejundiai.com.br/mais/brasil/batalha-de-geracoes-o-que-e-cringe-termo-que-viralizou-na-internet-essa-semana/)). **A brand that writes like a millennial copywriter is dead on arrival.** Lower-case, dry, short.
- **Gen Z emoji codes:** 🥀 replaces 💔; 🪑 and 🚡 replace 😂. They work as secret codes that confuse older people ([Canaltech, 27 Jun 2025](https://canaltech.com.br/comportamento/geracao-z-esta-usando-tres-emoji-sem-sentido-e-confunde-os-mais-velhos/)). Mum's emoji set (👍 🌹 🙏 😘 ☕ in a *bom dia*) is the opposite pole, and that contrast is usable. **UNVERIFIED (cultural observation)** for mum's set.
- **Brands that adopt a meme literally win.** Havaianas renamed its cream "Brasil Logo" flip-flop **"Encardida"** on its own e-commerce after users mocked the colour (15 Jul 2025) ([Exame](https://exame.com/marketing/havaianas-encardida-marca-renomeia-chinelo-apos-viralizar-como-meme/)). **Naming the product after the joke is the move.**

## 5. How Gen Z actually gifts on Dia das Mães

- **Cash vs object:** Gen Z leads the "mum would prefer a Pix" view (47%), but 54% hold that she won't give up the emotion of a physical gift ([CNDL/SPC](https://centraldovarejo.com.br/dia-das-maes-2026-varejo-espera-movimentacao-de-r-3791-bilhoes-com-127-milhoes-de-consumidores/?amp=1)). **So the product must deliver what a transfer cannot: a smell, a light, an unboxing, a line she will repeat.**
- **Meme-gift culture already exists: the bentô cake.** A mini cake in a lunchbox, custom-written with meme phrases and Flork drawings, sold on Instagram at about R$30–49 ([Diário do Nordeste, Dec 2021](https://diariodonordeste.verdesmares.com.br/verso/conheca-os-bento-cakes-bolinhos-na-marmita-com-frases-e-desenhos-engracados-1.3173496)). It became a standard gift for birthdays, graduations and Mother's/Father's Day; [TudoGostoso](https://www.tudogostoso.com.br/noticias/o-que-escrever-no-bento-cake-de-dia-dos-pais-veja-frases-divertidas-para-o-bolo-e-arrase-no-presente-a6709.htm) publishes phrase lists for Father's Day. **The behaviour to borrow: a gift whose top surface carries a funny written line.** A candle lid or a label can do this, set by code and personalisable.
- **Personalised is rising:** Ecommerce na Prática ([source](https://ecommercenapratica.com/blog/o-que-vender-no-dia-das-maes/), secondary) reports mugs and themed baskets. Sebrae MT lists *cestas personalizadas*.
- **Timing:** research starts up to two months ahead (Opinion Box via [Ecommerce na Prática](https://ecommercenapratica.com/blog/o-que-vender-no-dia-das-maes/)). 73% research 15+ days ahead and 43% buy in the first week of May (CNDL). 59% buy in the final week (Sebrae MT). **Seed late April, convert 1–8 May; there is a last-48-hours "presente de última hora" opportunity.**
- **The Instagram dump and the unboxing video** are the share formats: 98% of Gen Z meme interaction happens on Instagram (Forbes/Instituto Z). The gift box must photograph flat-lay and play as a 10-second unboxing.
- **Self-gift:** 16% buy for themselves (Sebrae MT). The mother may buy the candle for herself. Copy should let her: *"Pra você, que sempre lembrou do casaquinho."*

## 6. The cringe list: what this brand must never do

1. **Force Gen Z slang into brand copy.** "Se o adulto forçar, vira cringe" ([ES Hoje](https://eshoje.com.br/?p=866978)). Quote mum's phrases (timeless); keep Gen Z slang to a wink at most.
2. **The perfect-mother / rainha-do-lar / guerreira tearjerker.** 2026 moved away from idealisation ([M&M](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/womentowatch/o-que-marca-as-campanhas-de-dia-das-maes-em-2026)), and Boticário owns the tears.
3. **Millennial copywriting tics:** coffee-and-boletos jokes, *rsrsrs*, yellow emoji faces, Title Case Every Word, exclamation marks everywhere (2021 cringe list, [Tribuna de Jundiaí](https://tribunadejundiai.com.br/mais/brasil/batalha-de-geracoes-o-que-e-cringe-termo-que-viralizou-na-internet-essa-semana/)).
4. **Mocking mum.** The approved register is *zoeira com respeito*. On the "mãe bonita, pai feio" trend the top-style comment was *"estou rindo… mas eu juro que é com respeito"* ([DOL](https://dol.com.br/tuedoide/viral/800060/trend-mae-bonita-pai-feio-bomba-nas-redes-veja-os-videos)). **The joke must always land on the child,** never on her age, body or tech skills.
5. **Domestic-labour gifts and jokes** (iron, mop, "a rainha da cozinha"). Durable goods are shrinking anyway (CNC −4,4%).
6. **Physical-punishment winks** (chinelada) and *"engole o choro"*.
7. **Dead memes** (Nazaré Confusa, the 2016 "Sou rica" era): UNV., but by definition *old*. Use evergreen mum-isms instead.
8. **Fake testimonials** ("minha mãe amou!"). These are also forbidden by the studio rules.
9. **Health or therapy claims for scent** (*"acalma a ansiedade"*, *"aromaterapia que cura"*). Keep it to mood and memory. *(Regulatory detail not researched: UNVERIFIED; general caution.)*
10. **"A mãe tá on"** (see #40).

**Sensitivity:**
- Mother's Day hurts people who lost a mother, are estranged, or are trying to conceive (Boticário's 2025 campaign was about *tentantes*, per [Nosso Meio](https://nossomeio.com.br/entre-afeto-e-estrategia-como-as-marcas-estao-falando-sobre-maes-em-2025/)).
- US brands let customers opt out of Mother's Day emails; DoorDash reported 80.000 opt-outs in 2023 ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2023/04/30/mothers-day-2023-marketing-holiday-email-opt-out)).
- **Recommendation:** one line of graceful opt-out in the CRM flow, plus a scent or card for *"pra quem a gente quer lembrar"*. Done quietly, it is a brand-love moment Gen Z notices. **No Brazilian precedent was found: UNVERIFIED as a BR practice.**

## 7. Creative implications for a candle (raw material for the concept team)

**The core insight, in one line:** *mum's love speaks in repeated phrases, and Gen Z only realises they were love after leaving home.* A candle is literally **the light she leaves on** (#2, #13) and **a smell that becomes a memory**. Phrases plus light plus smell is a three-way fit no other gift category has.

**Headlines that fall straight out of the research (drafts, for the concept tournament):**
- **"A única luz que ela não vai mandar apagar."** (#13; the strongest)
- **"Ela ia preferir um Pix. Pix não tem cheiro."** (CNDL 47% / 54%)
- **"Acende quando chegar."** / **"Me avisa quando acender."** (#2)
- **"Você não é todo mundo. Ela também não."** (#10)
- **"Na volta a gente compra. Voltou."** (#6)
- **"Leva um casaquinho."**, as a scent name and as a hug (#1)
- **"Bom dia ✨🌹"**: the *bom dia* image, made gorgeous (#23)
- **"Beijos, mãe."** as the sign-off (#26)

**Scent-name system, one mum-ism per scent:**

| Scent name | Notes |
|---|---|
| *Leva um Casaquinho* | cashmere, warm vanilla, wool |
| *Me Avisa Quando Chegar* | clean cotton, lamp glow, orange blossom |
| *Já Comeu?* | bolo de fubá, cinnamon, coffee |
| *Bom Dia, Família* | rose, glitter-pink pepper, morning coffee |
| *Áudio de 7 Minutos* | chamomile, long burn |
| *Você Não É Todo Mundo* | the signature, unique |

Names stay short enough to set large on a label.

**Graphic codes owned by mum's world**, to be executed with Madison-Avenue restraint: glitter script, roses, sparkles, the *bom dia* layout, the WhatsApp voice-note waveform, the typing bubble, *"Encaminhado com frequência"*, 👍.
**The trick:** take mum's kitsch codes and render them with luxury discipline (a big serif, flat colour, hard-flash product). Gen Z gets the irony; mum just sees a beautiful candle. **Both readings are true, so neither audience is excluded.**

**Personalisation by code, cheap at scale:** *Mainha / Mamis / Mãezinha / Dona ____* name field on the label (#31–34). Regional editions use the utility name in the *"sócia da ___"* joke (Light RJ, Enel SP, Coelba BA, Cemig MG). Check that naming utilities is acceptable to counsel: **UNVERIFIED** trademark risk; safer to keep it generic, *"sócia da companhia de luz"*.
