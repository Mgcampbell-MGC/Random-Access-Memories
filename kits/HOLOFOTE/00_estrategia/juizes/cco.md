# CCO verdict: the Mother's Day candle tournament (concepts A–E)

**Judge:** Chief Creative Officer, judging at W+K / Droga5 / Collins calibre. 6 Oct 2026.
**Lens:** idea strength · the brand system (a Collins brand world, not a logo) · ownability · craft potential
inside the studio's rules · fit to the brief (*Gen Z, fun, appealing*) · would it place at Cannes Lions or CCSP ·
does it read as Madison Avenue rather than AI-generic.

## 2. Concept by concept

### A · MÃENÊS: *Tradução: eu te amo.* (6,5)

- **Best single thing:** the lid replies (***levei. · comi. · cheguei. · nem você.***). The glass carries her nag and the
  lid answers it twenty years late. *levei.* is the best two-syllable piece of copy in the tournament.
  - *Tradução: eu te amo.* is a true template, and it attaches to any phrase forever.
  - The base emboss, *SABIA QUE VOCÊ IA VIRAR.*, is a perfect button.
- **Worst single thing:** the territory is the most worn ground in Brazilian Mother's Day.
  - Claro already ran the *"você não é todo mundo"* inversion on national TV in 2022, and Pão de Açúcar ran
    *"mãezices"*.
  - The object is a phrase printed on a drinking glass, which is the novelty-mug genre the concept itself bans.
  - The "-ês dictionary" is the format of regional souvenir items (*mineirês*, *baianês*). That is my observation;
    a search today did not confirm it, so it is **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Other notes:**
  - The system is excellent: the *verbete* module, two reading distances, *"the last definition is always eu te amo"*,
    and the best store microcopy of the five.
  - But it is a **copywriter's brand, not a designer's brand**. Fraunces SOFT is the 2023 startup serif, and the
    palette is cream, BIC blue and candy colours.
  - Its own risk note is right: clever reads cold.
  - The brand ink is blue, while the studio's generated scenes use a chroma-blue stand-in. That friction is handled by
    a rule but stays on every production day.

### B · pode.: *não é pra visita.* (7,5)

- **Best single thing:** the turn in AD3. *"Domingo, ela tira o copo bom. Pra você. Você virou visita."* It is
  funny, then tender, then the line people forward to a sibling. Of the five, it is the insight with the most layers.
- **Worst single thing:** the name.
  - *pode.* is a bare common verb that means nothing on a shelf until someone tells you the joke, and it can be
    misread as *pôde*.
  - It cannot be protected as a trademark.
  - It shares Fraunces and Playwrite BR with A, so the type is not its own either.
- **Other notes:**
  - This is the best object design from culture. The compoteira dome, the gold *filete* and the masking-tape strip
    (*é da mãe.*) give it a silhouette no Brazilian candle has, and the top view becomes the logo.
  - The refill makes the insight mechanically true (*pode gastar. tem refil.*).
  - The tear band (*pode rasgar. ela abre devagarinho pra guardar o papel. esse pode.*) scripts the unboxing video.
  - **But the build is the heaviest in the tournament:** porcelain, pressed glass, an aluminium capsule, and a full
    cristaleira set with mirrored back and glass shelves. The ribbed dome's caustics are a noisy, slow render.
  - It is tender rather than *fun*.

### C · cheguei: *Você nunca chegou numa casa apagada.* (8,0)

- **Best single thing:** the *fresta*, a clear band at the base of a dark glass. One detail does three jobs:
  - it is the brand symbol, the light under her door;
  - it is the burn-down stop line;
  - it brightens as the candle burns (*"Quanto mais tarde, mais acesa. (Igual a ela.)"*).

  Idea, product truth and safety in one detail is Design Lions material. The headline is also the best line in the
  tournament: nobody can argue with it.
- **Worst single thing:** it is the least fun answer to a brief that asked for fun.
  - A near-black glass, one flame in the dark, and "a light left on for her" sit one step from a *vela de sétimo dia*.
    The concept names memorial as its own #1 risk and has to defuse it with parenthetical jokes.
  - Dark glossy glass with a colour accent is also next to Boy Smells' trade dress, which concept E itself flags.
- **Other notes:**
  - The *Uma luz só* rule is real Collins discipline.
  - The key → switch → match sonic logo is the most specific sound in the five.
  - *A PRIMEIRA VEZ* prints a birth-time label per buyer. That shows off the label pipeline better than anything else
    in the tournament.
  - The Ad 1 gag (the light under her door switching off one frame after the key turns) is great film craft.
  - Doto is trending and is shared with D.

### D · sinal: *vela não tem 2x.* (5,5)

- **Best single thing:** *Bom dia oficial*. Mothers become the media plan, forwarding the brand's 06:03 countdown
  images to their own children in the family group. It is the only distribution idea in the tournament, and it is
  Droga5-grade.
- **Worst single thing:** the territory is taken and the look is generic.
  - Usaflex owned WhatsApp voice notes for Dia das Mães 2026. Pão de Açúcar did mothers' audios before that.
  - Chat bubbles, notification badges, dot-matrix type and inflatable 3D props on a dark seamless are the most
    over-used DTC and Cinema 4D look of the decade. This is the concept most likely to read as AI slop even with every
    rule obeyed.
- **Other notes:**
  - The tagline is sharp and true.
  - The phone-box spec sheet is the funniest single page in the tournament (*"câmera: nenhuma. ela já tem foto sua
    demais."*).
  - But the brand is about the phone, not about her, and its emotional core is the thinnest of the five.
  - The chat-bubble pot is a novelty object. Gimmick undercuts premium.

### E · HOLOFOTE: *Ela aplaudiu de pé o seu pior show.* (8,5)

- **Best single thing:** the back panel. ***ELA ESTEVE EM TODAS.*** is followed by the tour dates she sat through
  (*PRONTO-SOCORRO, 3H ........ SEM INGRESSO. ENTROU.*). Turning the glass takes you from the joke on the front to a
  lump in the throat on the back. It is the best long-form copy in the tournament, and it lives on the object.
- **Worst single thing:** overload.
  - **Four show genres:** street poster, pop tour, theatre bells and school recital.
  - **Seven gift objects:** road case, mirror, PVC fan-club card, setlist, wristband, foam and the stage lid.
  - All of that sits on one 200 g candle. Until it is edited, it is a props department, not a brand.
  - **The core metaphor has a seam the concept never addresses:** a candle is the smallest, softest light there is,
    and a spotlight is the hardest.
- **Other notes:**
  - It is the only concept that leaves her house, the only loud candle on a sedative shelf, and the inversion of the
    most-awarded mother campaign of the last fifteen years.
  - The system is the richest:
    - two states (O MURO, O PALCO);
    - seven rules;
    - three voices mapped to three faces;
    - ***"MÃE is always the biggest word, você always the smallest"***, a hierarchy rule that makes every execution
      the joke.
  - **Unboxing:** the dressing-room mirror (*olha a atração.*) is the best unboxing beat in the five, because she
    sees herself first. The woven wristband turns the seal into something she wears.
  - **Production:** the hero frame reads at 40 px (one yellow glass on a black stage under a hard spot, *MÃE AO VIVO*
    above). The pack rising on a stage lift is the studio's capability shown, label-exact by construction.
  - **C02 (the empty front row and the handbag saving a seat)** is the most moving image in the tournament with no
    person in it.

## 3. Winner: **E · HOLOFOTE**, conditional on the edits in §4

**Why E and not C.**
- C has the best line and the best single product detail. But it answers *"Gen Z, fun and appealing"* with a dark,
  tender, nocturnal brand whose own #1 risk is reading as a memorial.
- E is the only concept that is loud, funny and moving in that order, which is exactly the brief.
- Its brand world produces the most executions without a designer.
- **It demonstrates more of what Sol actually sells than any other concept:** label-exact 3D packs in motion, a 360°
  printed object, type set by code and personalised per buyer, and a campaign that is complete with no generated
  people.

## 4. Required edits to E before anything is built

1. **One show, not four.**
   - The **pop tour** is the master metaphor, because it is Gen Z's own world.
   - The **school recital** stays as the origin story, on the back panel and in the ad set.
   - ***Terceiro sinal*** is the single theatre loan. It is kept because every Brazilian knows it means *"it's
     starting"*.
   - **Street-poster lambe-lambe is the graphic language, not a fifth story.**
2. **Edit the box to three load-bearing objects.**
   - Keep:
     - the **wristband** (the seal becomes something she wears);
     - the **mirror** (*olha a atração.*);
     - the **X lid** (her mark);
     - the setlist, because it carries the legally required instructions.
   - Cut the PVC *carteirinha* as a physical object. It is a third metaphor and a cost line. Move it to a digital and
     social asset.
3. **Close the metaphor seam with one line,** and make the candle's smallness the joke:
   ***O menor holofote do Brasil. Pra maior atração.***
4. **ACÚSTICO is blue (`#2F4BFF`).** That puts a second blue next to the studio's chroma-blue stand-in.
   - **Recommended:** swap it to a day-glo **UV / black-light violet**, which is also a real stage-light colour.
     Recompute the WCAG pairs.
   - **Minimum:** ACÚSTICO never appears in any generated scene.
5. **Cycles does not simulate fluorescence.** For renders, use the concept's own fallback: saturated near-neon
   coatings.
   - Do not fake day-glo with emission. It reads as plastic, which is an AI tell.
6. **Render a dedicated 9–15 s KV variant** for O ANÚNCIO and the film's product block, with the candle at **45–50% of
   frame height** (crane closer, lineup type above).
   - The 20%-height KV is right for feed and stories.
   - At 20%, the 9–15 s block fails the studio's film rule, and its fine print will not survive the fidelity check.
7. **Keep the wood wick and the crackle-to-applause morph.** It is the one sound in the five that carries the idea.
   - Make sure the hero flame reads as a light at thumbnail size.
   - Test-render the wide, low flame against a taller one before locking it.

## 5. Grafts from the other concepts (ranked)

Each graft is restated in E's own vocabulary. Nothing below imports another concept's type, palette or world.

1. **ESTREIA (from C, *A PRIMEIRA VEZ*).** The back-panel tour list opens with the buyer's birth, set by code:
   - the line: ***ESTREIA · 03.08.2003 · 14:32 ........ QUEM FEZ O SHOW FOI ELA***;
   - the product-page instruction, kept from C: ***Pergunta pra ela que horas você nasceu. Ela sabe de cor.***

   It gives an exact, different label per buyer, which is the studio's pipeline as the emotional peak.
2. **BOLETIM DA TURNÊ (from D, *Bom dia oficial*).** A daily 06:03 countdown poster, 2 to 9 May, in lambe-lambe
   grammar, built for mothers to forward to their children in the family group.
   - Example: *faltam 3 dias pro show. ingressos com os filhos.*
   - Free download plus a sticker pack. She becomes the media plan aimed at her own kids.
   - E has no distribution mechanic on the mother's side; this is it.
3. **NOVA TEMPORADA (from B, the refill).** A 200 g refill capsule that drops into the *copo do show* (reuse B's
   capsule spec).
   - Line: ***O copo fica. A turnê continua.***
   - It turns *"o copo fica"* from a sentiment into a repeat purchase.
4. **The base gag (from A, *SABIA QUE VOCÊ IA VIRAR.*).** Replace E's base emboss with the answer to her line *"não
   precisava"*: ***PRECISAVA.***
   - She finds it when she turns the glass over to see the price.
5. **Three-bell countdown cuts (from A, *vou contar até três*).** Three 6 s cuts, with the date as a code variable and
   the number of bells as the days left, aimed at the 59% who buy in the final week:
   - ***PRIMEIRO SINAL*** on 7 May;
   - ***SEGUNDO SINAL*** on 8 May;
   - ***TERCEIRO SINAL*** on 9 May.
6. **Store voice in show vocabulary (from A's microcopy discipline).**

   | Store moment | Line |
   |---|---|
   | Add to cart | *ingresso reservado.* |
   | Checkout | *confirmar presença.* |
   | Shipped | *saiu da coxia.* |
   | Delivered | *a atração chegou.* |
   | Out of stock | *esgotado. bis em breve.* |
   | 404 | *essa página saiu de cena.* |
   | Abandoned cart | ***você reservou e não veio. ela nunca fez isso.*** |

7. **CORTINA FECHADA (from D, *Silenciar*).** The grief and estrangement opt-out, as a gesture in the brand's own
   world: one toggle, ***esse domingo pesa? a gente fecha a cortina pra você até junho.*** Honoured instantly, no
   survey.
8. **RIDER DA ATRAÇÃO (from D's phone-box spec sheet).** A deadpan backstage rider on one side of the case, in E's
   *Produção* voice:
   - *camarim: o sofá dela*
   - *exigências: nenhuma (ela vai dizer)*
   - *bis: obrigatório*
   - *água: ela vai perguntar se você bebeu*
9. **The seal that predicts her (from B, *pode rasgar.*).** The wristband's paper seal reads ***pode rasgar. o que se
   guarda é a pulseira.***
10. ***Ribalta* (optional, from C's *fresta*; gated on a supplier test).**
    - **Device:** an unpainted 2 mm clear ring at the 1 cm stop-line height in the opaque coating. These are the
      footlights.
    - **What it does:** it glows as the flame nears the end. Line: ***quando a ribalta acender, fim de temporada.***
    - **Why it matters:** it gives E the one product truth it lacks.
    - **Conditions:** drop it if the coating edge or a hot spot fails the supplier test.

**Do not graft:**
- C's dark NOITE world (E already has O PALCO);
- Doto from C and D (trending, and it would muddy Special Gothic);
- D's chat UI;
- Fraunces and Playwrite BR from A and B;
- A's lid reply (E's X lid is its own and better for this world).

## 6. What would make it win at CCSP (and what would lose it)

**It wins on four things:**
- The back panel, read in hand.
- The mirror reveal, filmed by real families.
- C02, the empty front row with the handbag.
- The KV at 40 px.

Enter **Design (packaging)** for the wristband seal, the mirror case and the X lid, and **Film Craft** for the stage-lift
film with the crackle-to-applause morph.

**It loses** if the poster wall turns into generic Fidalga pastiche, or if the box ships with all seven objects.
- The owned element is the **hierarchy rule** (*MÃE* biggest, *você* smallest), not the woodtype.
- Edit until every object is doing the idea's work.
