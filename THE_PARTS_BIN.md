# THE PARTS BIN — components, not candidates

**Created 17 Aug 2026 at the founder's instruction, and the instruction was a correction to method.**

> *"i need you to think like a human .. some parts from here some parts from there .. thats how
> ideation with humans works .. not just full does this meet checklist. when we learn we are pulling
> from everywhere .. to then once we get it we are like oh parts of it is like this and that and what
> makes a great idea"*

**He is right, and the correction is structural.** Every assessment in this file runs a candidate
through C1–C7 and twenty laws and reports what survives. **A filter can only subtract.** It produced a
graveyard with two survivors, because subtraction cannot invent. Assembly is additive: hold the parts
loose, recombine them, and *then* filter.

**So this file holds PARTS, not candidates.** A part is a mechanism that worked, extracted from
whatever died around it. Parts are reusable. Candidates are not.

## Assemblies — parts combined, honestly rated

### ASSEMBLY 1 — **The object does not have to be a book** · C2 + C4 + E3 + D1

**This is the most valuable unassembled combination in the bin, and it appeared an hour ago.**

**C2** says a made-to-order object manufactured in the destination country crosses no border: no tariff,
no customs, no freight, no inventory. **C4** says growth comes from copies per act of work. **E3** says
the engine is input → artefact. **D1** says Etsy bills the ads after the sale.

**None of that is specific to books.** The current candidate treats "book" as the product when the
verified asset is actually *print-on-demand manufacturing in the buyer's own country, from a file, where
the writing is the value.* Objects that fit the same structure and have never been examined here:

- **Card and question decks** — content is entirely written, print cost far below a book, giftable, and
  one deck yields many cards from one act of writing
- **Prints, posters, canvas** — Prodigi, Gelato, Printful, all with destination-country plants
- **Fabric and wallpaper** — Spoonflower prints on demand
- **Puzzles and custom games** — The Game Crafter manufactures POD in the US
- **Stationery, invitations, engraved and laser goods**
- **3D-printed objects** — Craftcloud, Shapeways; far less saturated than paper

**Why it rates highest:** it costs nothing to explore, it reuses every verified asset (Lulu pricing
method, Etsy channel, the writing engine, the partner channel), and it directly attacks the one failing
key — **a deck of cards is one act of writing sold as fifty-two objects.**

**Where it could die:** most POD object categories are saturated and design-dominant, which is the
failure that killed the recipe book. It only works where **the writing is the product** and the object
is merely the carrier.

### ASSEMBLY 2 — **The trust layer without a body** · A1 + A3 + A4 + D2 + C5, resolving F3

STONEPROOF's structure was right and died on bodies. **Remove the body: make the evidence digital.**

Buyer submits acceptance criteria and files. She produces an **observed-condition report** with
three-state honesty (verified / not visible / access denied), no warranty, sold at middle-tier price on
a zero-CAC channel. **She never holds a credential — they send files.** F3 dissolves, C1/C3 survive.

**Where it dies, and it is one specific place:** naming a digital object worth $200+ to verify, that
the buyer cannot verify themselves, and that a chat window cannot verify either. **That object is not
yet identified.** Structurally elegant, currently hollow.

### ASSEMBLY 3 — **The handoff catastrophe, relocated** · F8 solved with B1 + D3

The volunteer-org finding — *nobody sells surviving the turnover of the person who runs everything* —
was real and died on **adverse buyer**. The fix is to move it somewhere the sufferer pays **in
advance**, on an **actuarial** trigger, through a **partner** who already owns them.

**Where it dies:** Everplans and Empathy are funded and sitting in the obvious version of this.
Needs a non-obvious version.

### ASSEMBLY 4 — **Substitution-up as standing positioning** · B4 + C1

Not a business — a **repositioning that applies to whatever she sells.** Price and describe every
product as the affordable replacement for a specific expensive thing people will abandon in 2027–28,
and let the downturn do the acquiring. Combined with **C1**, a crash then improves her margin *and* her
demand simultaneously.

**Free to apply. Should be applied regardless of which product wins.**

## The method note, kept

> **Filter last, not first.** Run the parts bin before the constraint list. A constraint applied early
> deletes the component along with the candidate — and the component was the valuable half.

**F3 is the example.** "Brazilian eyes on the ground" was filtered out as a C3 violation, and the part
worth keeping was underneath it: **local access is genuine arbitrage that passes the inversion check.**
The question was never *can she inspect factories* — it was *what local access does not require a
body.* That question only appears if the part is separated from the candidate first.
