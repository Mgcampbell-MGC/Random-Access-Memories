## 1. THE CASH-CYCLE MODEL — why this question decides the business

Target US$2.000–2.500/mo net = R$10.400–13.000; Simples Anexo I at 7,3% on gross; 60% COGS. Cycle: d0 win +
empenho · d2 order · d8 delivered · d12 attested; government pays d12+P; she pays the supplier d2+T.
**Days her own money is tied up = (12+P) − (2+T).**

**What her R$15.600 alone carries, in monthly billings** (bar = R$24.400–39.800):

| | gov pays 30d | 45d | 60d |
|---|---|---|---|
| **no terms** | R$19.500 *short* | R$14.182 *short* | R$11.143 *short* |
| **28-day terms** | **R$65.000** | **R$28.889** | R$18.571 *short* |
| **30-day terms** | **R$78.000** | **R$31.200** | R$19.500 *short* |

⇒ **Terms roughly TRIPLE her capacity, and the §8 figure of "R$40–60k needed" is the NO-TERMS case.** Two
variables decide it, and both are findable without research: the term a distributor gives a new CNPJ, and how
fast the specific buyer pays.

## 2. SUPPLIER TERMS — the founder's premise is right, and the gate excluding her is PUBLISHED

Ladders of 7/14/21/28/35 days are the Brazilian default, out to 10/30/60/120 at BZ Tech and 30–180 with a finance
partner. **She is not in it yet, by written rule:**

- **Vitória:** *"No primeiro envio, será necessário o pagamento antecipado de **50% do valor do pedido**."*
- **FCW's cadastro form** has a block headed *"REFERÊNCIAS DE FORNECEDORES"* with **five blank slots** — the
  credit file is built from the exact asset she does not have.
- **★ THE PERMISSIVE END, and it is the lever: Flora Saúde — *"O CNPJ é avaliado pelo setor financeiro **a partir
  da segunda compra**… sendo o **prazo inicial 21 dias**."*** ⇒ **TWO clean purchases, not twelve months.**

⇒ **The operational move is SUPPLIER SELECTION BY CREDIT GATE, not negotiation.** A mid-size regional distributor

## 3. ⚰ TRADE CREDIT INSURANCE — the founder's specific idea, and it BACKFIRES

**She cannot buy it.** SUSEP: *"As partes deste seguro são o **segurado (credor da operação de crédito)** e a
seguradora. **O devedor… não é parte integrante do seguro.**"* The distributor buys it, at **under 0,5% of
turnover** (Allianz Trade).

**And a distributor that holds it is MORE constrained about her, not less.** Chubb's SUSEP-registered wording
(Processo 15414.902173/2013-01, in force 23/06/2025) has exactly two doors and both are shut:
- **Named limit** — the insurer underwrites her. Nothing to underwrite.
- **Discretionary limit** — cl. 3: the insured *"obriga-se a seguir a Política de Gerenciamento de Crédito… **a fim
  de garantir que um Cliente tenha a capacidade de cumprir com as suas obrigações de pagamento e um histórico de
  bom pagamento**"*; cl. 9.2: *"**devendo comprovar, no caso de Perda, que cumpriu com as determinações de referida
  política quando da reclamação de Sinistro.**"*
⇒ **Selling to a no-history buyer under the discretionary limit converts an insured receivable into an uninsured
one. Insurance does not create history; it hard-codes the requirement for it and attaches a penalty for skipping
it.** ⚠ And **Junto Seguros — named by the founder — does not sell this product**; its sitemap carries only
seguro garantia (surety). Surety is useful to her later for bid and performance bonds, useless for buying stock.

## 4. ⚰ THE DISTRIBUTOR-SIDE FINTECH — it exists, and it multiplies a limit of zero

## 6. ★★ THE BIGGER LEVER, AND IT IS FREE: THE PAYMENT LAG IS A PROPERTY OF THE BUYER, NOT THE LAW

**The statutory clock is far faster than §8 assumed.** IN SEGES/ME 77/2022 art. 7: *"I – **10 dias úteis para a
liquidação**… II – **10 dias úteis para pagamento**"*, and **§2º: for purchases within the dispensa-by-value limit
(R$65.492,11) "os prazos… serão reduzidos pela metade"** ⇒ **5 + 5 business days ≈ 14 calendar days.** Teeth:
art. 5 §7 exposes the paying agent to Decreto-Lei 2.848 art. 337-H.

**What actually happens, measured on 2.276 small goods invoices** at three federal units, via the free
unauthenticated `contratos.comprasnet.gov.br/api/contrato/{id}/faturas` (which publishes `emissao`, `ateste` and
`data_liquidacao` per invoice):

| buyer | n | median emissão→liquidação | ≤14d | ≤30d |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **IF de São Paulo (158154)** | 618 | **15 days** | 48,9% | 78,5% |
| FUNASA (255000) | 50 | 48 days | 24,0% | 40,0% |
| **SAA-MS / Min. Agricultura (250110)** | 1.608 | **67 days** | 3,0% | 6,2% |

**And the delay is in the ATESTE, not the payment.** `ateste→liquidação` median 1 day (IFSP) and 6 days (SAA-MS);
`emissão→ateste` median **14 days vs 60 days**. Same law, same year, same instrument, **4× apart**. Corroborated
on the last leg by TJDFT's own published queue (n=452): authorisation→payment order median **0 days**, p90 2.

**⇒ THE REUSABLE TOOL, thirty minutes per órgão and free:** `GET /api/contrato/ug/{UASG}` → contract ids →
`GET /api/contrato/{id}/faturas` → median `data_liquidacao − emissao` on `FORNECIMENTO DE BENS`. **Bid only where
the median is under ~30 days.** The same call returns `utiliza_antecipagov`.

| buyer class | cycle | monthly shipping on R$12k | capital to ship R$55k/mo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **fast (IFSP-class)** | **~27 days** | R$13.300 | **~R$50.000** |
| slow (SAA-MS-class) | ~79 days | R$4.600 | ~R$145.000 |

⇒ **Buyer selection is worth ~3× her capacity at the same capital, and costs nothing. It outranks every credit

## 7. ⚠⚠ TWO CORRECTIONS TO `THE_FIELDTAG_BRASIL.md` §8, ONE IN EACH DIRECTION

**(1) §8 SAID ANTECIPAGOV ONLY REACHES THE POST-DELIVERY LEG. WRONG.** Serpro's integration manual publishes the
formula: *"**Margem contábil = (70% (A − B)) − C**, onde A: Valor Total do Contrato; B: Valor Pago Siafi…
**O valor empenhado será utilizado, quando for maior do que o valor atual do contrato**"* — the base is COMMITTED
minus PAID, and the help page confirms *"Valores a liquidar: referem-se a serviços ainda não executados"* sits
inside the margin. The instrument is the empenho itself (*"empenhos com força de contrato… Não é necessário
anexar documentos"*), and **Lei 14.133 art. 95 I** makes the nota de empenho the contract instrument for
*"dispensa de licitação em razão de valor"* — her exact case.

**(2) §8 SAID A NEW COMPANY QUALIFIES BECAUSE THE COUNTERPARTY IS THE TREASURY. ALSO WRONG, AND THIS ONE HURTS.**
The portal's own FAQ 9: ***"O Governo Federal será solidário (ou garantidor)…? **Não.** A operação… não representa
garantia da União."*** It publishes the underwriting method — the 6 Cs, including *"**Capacidade**: Avalia se o
faturamento e os gastos da organização… possibilitam o cumprimento da dívida"* and *"**Capital**: Análise do
relatório ou demonstrativo financeiro"*. Worse for a pre-delivery draw: **IN 82/2025 Anexo 2.1(a)** makes the UASG
report *"se a execução está suspensa ou sequer foi iniciada"* — an undelivered empenho always answers "not begun".

**And three more limits, measured:** the first operation takes **~40 business days** (10+15+5+20 úteis);
**only 50,7% of federal units are enabled** — `utiliza_antecipagov` = Sim on **149 of 294** units sampled from the
3.774 with active contracts (±2,9pp at 95%); and the programme runs **~500 operations a year at a ~R$250k average
ticket** (SECONDARY) — not a retail product for a R$5.000 dispensa line.
⚠ **A methodological catch worth recording, made by the agent against its own output:** an earlier 120-unit draw
returned 51,3% and the 300-unit draw returned 50,7%, and they were **presented as mutual corroboration until the
seeds were checked — both used the same random seed, so the smaller sample is a PREFIX SUBSET of the larger.**
Their union is 294, not 414. **There is one measurement, not two.** This is `CHECK WHETHER YOUR TWO CORROBORATING
NUMBERS ARE ONE NUMBER` firing on a fresh run, and it is the first time in this file that the agent caught it
rather than the reviewer. ★ **The SISG shortcut is 99,3%, not exact: 148 Sim/Sim · 144 Não/Não · one exception each
way — so read `utiliza_antecipagov` itself, which the same API call returns, rather than inferring it from SISG.** ⇒ **§8's conclusion that it is the
SECOND-YEAR instrument for atas de registro de preços was right for the wrong reason.**

## 8. WHAT IS DEAD, AND WHY — so nobody re-opens it

- **⚰ CARTÃO BNDES.** *"**Capital de giro não é item financiável pelo Cartão BNDES.**"* Regulamento cl. 15.3
  confines it to *"ITENS AUTORIZADOS constantes dos Catálogos de Produtos expostos no PORTAL"*, and cl. 6.1 to
  new goods, *insumos integrantes da atividade produtiva*, and parts for industrialização. She would buy at
  catalogue price, destroying the only margin she has. *(Rate 1,73% a.m., Sep 2026; 48 instalments; ≤R$2M.)*
- **⚰ PRONAMPE IS CAPPED BY CAPITAL SOCIAL, NOT REVENUE — and raising capital to chase it is self-defeating.**
  Lei 13.999/2020 art. 3 §1º admits sub-1-year firms at *"50% do seu capital social"*, **but BNB's own page adds:
  *"o capital social da empresa deve ser de, pelo menos, **150%** do valor do empréstimo concedido"*** (Sicoob
  demands personal guarantee to 150%). At R$15.600 of capital that is a **R$10.400 loan**; to borrow R$60k she
  would have to put in R$90k. Rate ceiling Selic + 6% ≈ **20% a.a. ≈ 1,53% a.m.** (Selic meta 14,00%, since 6 Aug
  2026). *(⚠ 30% vs 50% under MP 1.382/2026 is UNVERIFIED — Planalto's consolidated text says 30%, BNB and Sicoob
  publish 50%.)*
- **⚰ SECURED PJ CARDS RESHUFFLE HER CAPITAL, THEY DO NOT CREATE CREDIT.** Cora's Limite Garantido: *"reservar o
  saldo da sua conta… **sem necessidade de análise de crédito**"*, **hard cap R$5.000**. Value is the ~30–40 days
  of float and, more importantly, the **Cadastro Positivo record** — *"pode contribuir para o histórico de crédito
  da sua empresa na Cora **e no mercado**"*. **Treat as a history-manufacturing device, not working capital.**
- **⚰ CESSÃO DE CRÉDITO OUTSIDE ANTECIPAGOV IS CONTESTED.** Lei 14.133 has **no article on it** (grepped). PGDF
  Parecer 525/2022: *"CESSÃO FIDUCIÁRIA DE CRÉDITOS DECORRENTES DE CONTRATOS ADMINISTRATIVOS. **IMPOSSIBILIDADE**"*
  … *"não vincula de qualquer modo o Poder Público contratante"* … *"Pode a Administração se negar a anuir? Sim."*
  That is exactly why AntecipaGov exists (IN 82 art. 12 obliges payment into the linked account).
- **⚰ SEBRAE AND STATE PROGRAMMES: NOTHING.** SEBRAE does not lend (advisory only). Desenvolve SP requires
  *"Balanço patrimonial e DRE"*. **No federal or state programme finances public-sector suppliers as such** — the
  state route IS FGI PEAC wearing a local badge (BDMG, Banrisul, Banestes, BRDE are all agents).
- **VENDA À ORDEM changes who ships, not when she pays** — SEFAZ-SP RC 18807/2018: the supplier invoices HER with
  ICMS at each shipment (CFOP 5.118/5.119) while the goods go straight to the órgão. Real operational gain
  (no freight, no storage, no double handling), **zero cash-timing effect**. **CONSIGNAÇÃO MERCANTIL** (RICMS-SP
  arts. 465–469) genuinely moves the payable — the *"Venda"* NF is issued only when she sells — but the
  distributor is then financing unsold stock in a stranger's warehouse, a bigger credit decision, and it
  reintroduces the inventory veto. Worth asking for; do not plan on it.
- **★ ONE FREE GREP WORTH DOING ON EVERY EDITAL: `pagamento antecipado`.** Lei 14.133 **art. 145** forbids advance
  payment *"salvo… se representar condição indispensável"*, **and only where "expressamente prevista no edital"**.
  Rare — but where it appears the working-capital problem is zero.

## ★ The laws this produced

- **★★★ WHEN A CONSTRAINT LOOKS LIKE A MONEY PROBLEM, CHECK WHETHER IT IS A COUNTERPARTY-SELECTION PROBLEM FIRST.**
  Identical purchases under the identical statute clear in **15 days at one federal buyer and 67 at another**, and
  the spread is published free in an open API nobody here had opened. **Buyer selection bought 3× the capacity
  that the entire credit market could not.** Ask of any cash-cycle constraint: *is the lag a property of the rule,
  or of the counterparty?* If the counterparty, the fix is a filter, not a loan.
- **★★★ EVERY CREDIT INSTRUMENT PRICES OFF HER BALANCE SHEET, NOT THE GOVERNMENT'S — INCLUDING THE ONE DESIGNED
  FOR GOVERNMENT SUPPLIERS.** AntecipaGov looked like the exception; its own FAQ says *"não representa garantia da
  União"* and publishes a 6-C analysis. **The only escape is a GUARANTEE FUND that substitutes a public balance
  sheet for the missing private one — FGI PEAC — and it is a term loan against her company, not an advance against
  an order.** ⇒ **Hunt guarantee funds, not lenders, for any founder with no history.**
- **★★ A CREDIT GATE IS PUBLISHED, AND IT VARIES BY AN ORDER OF MAGNITUDE ACROSS SUPPLIERS IN THE SAME TRADE.**
  One distributor: *"no mínimo 1 ano de fundação"* plus two years of accounts. Another: *"avaliado… a partir da
  segunda compra"*. **Read the credit policy before the price list.**
- **★★ INSURANCE AGAINST A RISK CAN INCREASE THE COUNTERPARTY'S CAUTION.** A trade-credit policy obliges the
  insured seller to verify the buyer's payment history *and prove it at claim time* — so the insured distributor
  has a contractual reason to refuse a new buyer that an uninsured one does not. **Before proposing insurance as
  an unlock, read who the insured is and what the policy makes them do.**
