HOW TO USE THIS. Read all of it before proposing anything. The three most common
failure modes, in order:
  1. Proposing something already killed, for a reason recorded in Section 4.
  2. Proposing something that violates a hard constraint in Section 1.
  3. Asserting a fact taken from a vendor's marketing page.

If your honest conclusion after reading is "this constraint set admits no
business", say so and name the binding constraint. A well-evidenced no is worth
more than a manufactured yes. There have been sixty-seven manufactured yeses.

Not preferences. Not negotiable. Will not be relaxed later. A proposal violating

SECTION 2 — THE LAWS
================================================================================

Each was paid for with a dead candidate.

LAW 1 — THE FREE COMPETITOR LAW
  Content, templates, checkers and tools cost almost nothing to produce, so
  every funded incumbent gives them away as lead generation. A solo operator
  selling an artefact competes not with a product but with someone's MARKETING
  BUDGET, which is designed to give that artefact away.
  METHOD: hunt the free competitor BY FUNDING MODEL, first, before anything.
  Verified instances: Levelset · Estratégia/QConcursos · iNymbus/SPS · Insparisk
  · FamilySearch · Sebrae · ANVISA · APEX Accelerators ($55M/yr, 92 offices) ·
  Arelle · EDItEUR · W3C/DAISY · DDEX · the MLC · Grants.gov · Google Merchant
  Center · CMS (open-sources its own validator) · Drata · Vanta · FAO Whisp ·
  MAPA's AgroBrasil+Sustentável · Office Ally · Availity · Dollar For.

CORRECTED LAW A1 — WHEN AN INCUMBENT ACTUALLY KILLS
  Only when it is FREE, or above ~20% MEASURED adoption in the target segment.
  A paid PARTIAL substitute does NOT kill. (This correction exists because a
  good candidate was wrongly killed by citing Adobe Acrobat against a job
  Acrobat cannot do.)

THE ARBITRAGE LAW
  A Brazilian cost base is only an advantage against a buyer who does NOT have
  one. STRONGLY prefer USD/GBP/EUR.
  COROLLARY, learned 14 Aug: the arbitrage often runs the WRONG way. Indian and
  Filipino bureaus publish £4/page and $8–12/hour with unlimited revisions.
  Brazil is not the cheap option in most back-office work.

THE BRAZIL ACCESS LAW (new, 14 Aug)
  > Where Brazil is a WALL, the wall is manned by a credentialed professional.
  > Where Brazil is a DOOR, the door is already open to foreigners.
  > Being Brazilian buys nothing at either.
  ANVISA registration holder, INMETRO certificate holder, responsável técnico,
  despachante aduaneiro, tradutor público, cartório — all reserved. Meanwhile
  the CNPJ database and JUCESP certidão simplificada are free and open to
  foreigners with no CPF.
  AND: her timezone gives OVERLAP with US hours, not INVERSION. Overlap is only
  monetisable SYNCHRONOUSLY, which C1 forbids. The "work while America sleeps"
  play requires UTC+5:30 to +8 — India and the Philippines. Brazil cannot make
  it. Her timezone contribution to any async product is exactly zero.
  AND: native pt-BR is the most ABUNDANT qualification in global localisation
  supply, not a scarce one. Floor is $0,05–0,08/word.
  NET: Brazilianness is a cost advantage and only ever a cost advantage. Stop
  looking for a Brazil-specific product. Sell in hard currency, live in reais.

THE B2B BUYER LAW
  A business buyer brings a negotiation, a diligence, an incumbent, and a
  $5–50/month price anchor. A consumer brings none.
  RUNNING TALLY: THIRTY-PLUS B2B MODELS. ZERO SURVIVORS.
  Not a prohibition — a heavy prior. A B2B proposal must explain why it escapes
  what killed the others.

THE INTEGRATION TAX
  Anything living inside the client's stack is sold on a call and needs access.
  A 50-idea list was run through this and 38 died on it alone.

THE VERIFIABILITY LAW (with its 6 Aug correction)
  NOT "does she hold the credential?" but:
  *** AFTER THE BUYER OPENS THE FILE, CAN THEY TELL WHETHER IT IS RIGHT? ***
  YES → her standing is irrelevant. Nobody asks who wrote their tax software.
  NO  → borrowed authority is the product, and it cannot be borrowed.

THE STAMP LAW — read this twice
  The property that makes a document business worth $33.600 instead of $99 is
  that A PERSON IS STANDING BEHIND IT: legally exposed, insured, reachable.
  A faceless seller with no credential and no professional indemnity cannot hold
  the stamp, and therefore cannot capture the price the stamp commands.
  TEST: NAME THE PERSON WHO GETS SUED IF THIS IS WRONG. If the business only
  works when that person is the seller, stop.
  This retroactively explains ten prior compliance kills: each was the
  recurring/painful/document-heavy artefact priced as if it carried the stamp.
  It also restates the Brazilian reserved-profession kills — the reserved
  perimeter and the pricing premium are the same feature, because the state is
  deciding who may hold the stamp.
  UNIVERSALITY CHECK: a $23M/month operator's own list of nine high-value
  compliance categories was checked item by item. NINE OF NINE stamp-gated.

THE STAMP LAW'S COROLLARY — the escape hatch that isn't (new, 14 Aug)
  The obvious inference is: "if she can't hold the stamp, sell clients to
  whoever does, and take a commission." NINE AGENTS INCLUDING A RED TEAM TESTED
  THIS. It dies on four independent Bucket A facts (Section 4). The reason:
  > AN INTRODUCTION IS A TRUST INSTRUMENT TOO. Its price is set by how much the
  > referrer's NAME is worth to the referee. A FACELESS SELLER CANNOT HOLD THE
  > STAMP AND CANNOT VOUCH FOR THE PERSON WHO DOES. THOSE ARE THE SAME
  > DISABILITY, NOT TWO.
  A cold email from an anonymous stranger transfers no trust. It is a LEAD, not
  a referral — and leads price at cost-per-lead, not 10% of the deal.
  > **WHEN A CREDENTIAL CREATES A PRICE, IT ALSO REGULATES WHO MAY BE PAID TO
  > INTRODUCE IT.** Never propose selling introductions into a licensed
  > profession without first reading that profession's referral-fee rule. It
  > exists, in every one of them.
  ISO/IEC 17065:2012, clause 4.2.3 NOTE 1 lists what threatens a certification
  body's impartiality, and it names "...PAYMENT OF A SALES COMMISSION OR OTHER
  INDUCEMENT FOR THE REFERRAL OF NEW CLIENTS."
  *** CORRECTION, SAME DAY: THIS IS A RISK TO BE MANAGED UNDER 4.2.4, NOT A
  PROHIBITION, AND I INITIALLY OVERSTATED IT AS A KILL. ***
  European Accreditation's Certification Committee FAQ, Question 34.4, works a
  numeric example of exactly this arrangement — an external agent paid €100 per
  signed contract plus €500 for retention — and states there is no requirement
  preventing it. The ONLY hard restriction is that an agent paid contingent on a
  POSITIVE audit outcome cannot participate in the certification process for
  their own introduced clients, which is irrelevant to someone who never audits.
  ISO/IEC 17025 cl. 4.1 constrains LABORATORY PERSONNEL, not outside introducers.
  AND IT IS SETTLED COMMERCIALLY: QIMA, an accredited inspection, testing and
  certification business, runs a PUBLIC affiliate programme paying 5% of paid
  revenue FOR FIVE YEARS, explicitly open to individuals ("IF YOU ARE ACTING ON
  BEHALF OF YOURSELF AS AN INDIVIDUAL"), with no call at any stage.
  SO THE CERTIFICATION VERTICAL IS LEGALLY OPEN. The corollary above still holds
  for the LICENSED professions — law, accountancy, insurance, customs — where
  named referral-fee rules genuinely do bar it. Do not generalise from those to
  every credentialed field. CHECK EACH ONE.

THE GATE FRAME, AND WHY IT FAILED (proposed and falsified the same day)
  HYPOTHESIS: a machine can adjudicate in place of a person. Where a portal or
  validator ACCEPTS or REJECTS, the buyer needn't trust the preparer.
  SEVEN FAMILIES SWEPT IN ONE DAY: customs · retail/marketplace feeds ·
  publishing and music metadata · financial and corporate e-filing · product and
  environmental compliance portals · healthcare claims · government procurement.
  RESULT: ZERO SURVIVORS. Two failure modes close the space:
    (a) THE OPERATOR WANTS YOU TO PASS, so it funds the free validator itself.
        Standards bodies, marketplaces and governments all want more conforming
        supply. Every conforming submission is revenue to them.
    (b) THE GATE ADJUDICATES IDENTITY, NOT THE ARTEFACT. Amazon checks a LUCID
        number, not a risk assessment. Customs checks a licence and a bond.
  THEREFORE:
  G4 — THE GATE OPERATOR MUST HAVE AN INCENTIVE TO KEEP CONFORMANCE SCARCE.
  Hunt only gates whose operator is INDIFFERENT or ADVERSARIAL to you passing.
  After seven families we are not confident such a gate exists in any volume. A
  gate proposal must clear G4 explicitly, with evidence.
  ALSO: a validator that REPORTS FINDINGS is not a gate. A gate returns a
  DECISION the submission cannot proceed without. (Ace by DAISY looked like a
  gate and is not one — see Section 4.)

THE TRANSPOSITION LAW — this one GENERATES. Most productive law here.
  A gap exists where two mature industries sit adjacent and neither has crossed,
  because each is branded, priced and staffed for its own side.
  WORKED EXAMPLE: life-story services (StoryWorth, Remento, Memoirji) are
  branded for elders and legacy. Pregnancy keepsakes are a paper-goods industry
  selling blank journals. Two mature businesses, touching, neither crossing.
  The gap: a written pregnancy memoir, a hardcover arriving already written,
  bought as a baby-shower gift. That is the file's best candidate.

THE CHAT-WINDOW TEST
  If a competent person with ChatGPT produces the deliverable in one sitting,
  it is dead. The deliverable must be something a chat window CANNOT produce:
  A PHYSICAL OBJECT · A THIRD PARTY CONTACTED · CUSTODY HELD.
  She cannot hold custody (C4) and cannot contact third parties by voice (C1),
  so A PHYSICAL OBJECT IS THE MOST RELIABLE ESCAPE.

THE SUBSTITUTE-COUNT LAW
  A print-on-demand product with a thousand substitutes needs bought traffic.
  One with ZERO substitutes needs only to be found once. The manufacturing model
  doesn't set the economics; the substitute count does. A t-shirt design is a
  commodity. A book about one specific person's life has exactly one possible
  buyer and no substitute at any price.

NEVER SELL THE AGENT, SELL THE OUTPUT
  Any agent worth selling is free on GitHub within a year. Never sell a guide to
  configuring one.

THE ALERT FINDING
  When the recipient is an OPPORTUNIST who profits by acting on the information,
  the alert sells by itself at real prices (PropertyRadar $119–599/mo, Owler
  $39–350/mo). When the recipient is the SUBJECT of the problem, the alert is
  free and all the money is in the fix. NO EXAMPLE WAS FOUND, ANYWHERE, of a
  pure alert sold at a meaningful price to the party the alert is about.
  RULE: sell the trigger to whoever profits from it, never to whoever it happens
  to. (NOTE: this law is what makes the one surviving candidate in Section 6
  work — it sells to the party that profits.)

GATE 0 — CAN THE BUYER PAY?
  Prove it with a MEASURED size distribution before designing anything. A
  complete business case was once written and committed before anyone checked
  the target's bank balance: 70% were under US$50.000 annual revenue.

ADVERSE SELECTION
  Companies with a visible public defect are frequently defective BECAUSE they
  are under-resourced. Neglect and poverty are the same phenomenon from two
  angles. The mechanism that makes a faceless seller credible — quoting the
  buyer's own public defect — selects for buyers least able to pay.
  Escapes: (W1) sell to a different, solvent party bearing the exposure;
  (W2) target defects that scale with SIZE and COMPLEXITY, not neglect;
  (W3) find a counterparty with power who withholds money until proof arrives.

"NOBODY SELLS THIS" HAS TWO EXPLANATIONS, AND THE SECOND IS MORE COMMON
  Before building on an unserved defect, ask what happens TODAY when it goes
  uncaught. If "a free system catches it" or "nothing, it self-corrects", the
  space is empty for a reason.
  TEST: name the specific person who loses a specific amount of money, and the
  mechanism. If the loss is "minutes of rework", stop.

THE TWO-BUCKET RULE — how to kill honestly
  BUCKET A — STRUCTURAL. These kill. Only five kinds qualify:
    A1  Someone ships the IDENTICAL product to the IDENTICAL buyer
    A2  A NAMED law reserves it to a credential she lacks
    A3  THE LIST DOES NOT EXIST — the buyer cannot be enumerated
    A4  THE PHYSICS FAIL
    A5  A cheaper substitute does THE WHOLE JOB, or a regulator gives it free
  BUCKET B — UNPROVEN. THESE MAY NOT KILL ANYTHING. Whether buyers pay this
    price; whether a gap over a PARTIAL substitute is worth paying for; the
    conversion rate; whether the buyer knows they have the problem; whether they
    trust an unknown vendor. EVERY Bucket B objection must arrive WITH THE
    CHEAPEST TEST THAT SETTLES IT — under two weeks, under US$200, with a
    NUMERIC PASS THRESHOLD. A Bucket B objection without a test is not a
    finding; it is a guess wearing the costume of rigour.
  WHY: a false kill costs more than a missed risk. A missed risk shows up in a
  cheap test. A FALSE KILL NEVER SHOWS UP AT ALL.

THE UNEXPLOITED PRICING MECHANISM
  All 67 candidates were priced cost-plus or against a substitute. NOT ONE was
  priced against WHAT THE BUYER LOSES. A $33.600 certification quote was paid
  unnegotiated because it was priced against the downside.
  Absorbing a LIABILITY downside requires the stamp. But one downside requires
  no stamp: A MISSED WINDOW — a launch date, a season, a deadline, a shipping
  cut-off. Nobody signs an indemnity for speed; you either hit the date or you
  don't, and the buyer can see which. STILL UNEXPLOITED.

TWO ENUMERABLE POPULATIONS (new, 14 Aug)
  A lead or data business needs TWO enumerable populations — the leads AND the
  buyers — and THE BUYER POPULATION MUST BE LARGE ENOUGH TO SURVIVE THE FUNNEL'S
  CONVERSION RATE. One candidate died because the leads were enumerable and the
  buyers were not. Another died because the buyers were enumerable but there
  were only 173–293 of them: at a 2% reply rate the entire universe yields 3,5
  replies in ONE pass, and there is no second campaign.

CORRECTION 1 — CAN-SPAM. The file's framing was materially wrong.
  US$53.088 is a STATUTORY MAXIMUM, not an expected cost, and has never been
  applied at anything near per-email scale. Verkada sent "more than 30 million
  commercial emails over a three-year period" and paid US$2,95M — the largest
  CAN-SPAM penalty the FTC has ever obtained. That is **US$0,098 PER EMAIL**,
  and it was bundled with a data-security case in which a hacker viewed 150.000
  live cameras in psychiatric hospitals and women's health clinics.
  The four violations charged were purely mechanical: no unsubscribe option,
  unsubscribe requests not honoured, no clear opt-out, no physical address.
  THERE IS NO PRIVATE RIGHT OF ACTION — only the FTC, state AGs and ISPs may
  sue. State AG damages cap at $250/email and $2M total; ISPs $25–100/email and
  $1M. CAN-SPAM is OPT-OUT, not opt-in. Compliant B2B cold email is LAWFUL.
  => At ~50 emails/week with accurate headers, a real postal address and a
     working opt-out honoured within 10 business days, exposure is not small.
     IT IS ZERO, BECAUSE THERE IS NO VIOLATION.

CORRECTION 2 — Google/Yahoo bulk-sender rules do not bind her.
  They trigger at 5.000+ messages per DAY to Gmail accounts. At ~10/day she is
  500× below. DMARC and one-click unsubscribe are not required of her; SPF or
  DKIM, valid PTR, TLS and spam rate under 0,3% are, and all are free.
  Her real problem is the OPPOSITE of what the file assumed: at that volume she
  is TOO SMALL TO BUILD SENDER REPUTATION.

CORRECTION 3 — the "you look like the scam" finding was overstated.
  USPTO's warning specifically concerns entities using names similar to the
  agency, sending official-looking notices, and falsely claiming a service is
  mandatory. Outreach doing none of those is not the described pattern. A
  copywriting risk, not a kill.

AND ONE KILL THE FILE HAD MISSED ENTIRELY:
  Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 5061(a): "a person engaged in the practice of public
  accountancy shall not: (1) pay a fee or commission to obtain a client."
  (f) defines "fee" to include "a commission, rebate, preference, discount, or
  other consideration, whether in the form of money or otherwise" — closing the
  rename. Mirrored by AICPA ET §1.520.001 and WAC 4-30-045.
  => Every California CPA firm is barred from paying her to bring a client, and
     SOC 2 reports can ONLY be issued by CPA firms. That closes SOC 2, financial
     audit and CPA-delivered specialty tax. Engineering-delivered work (cost
     segregation, 179D) is not public accountancy and stays open.
