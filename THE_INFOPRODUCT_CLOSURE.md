## 1. ★★ THE AFFILIATE ESCAPE — the priority question, answered, and it dies on a number

The whole hunt rested on one hope: **on Hotmart, Kiwify and Eduzz the AFFILIATES hold the audiences, so a
faceless company-branded producer could sell without one.** Half of that is true. The half that matters is not.

**THE HALF THAT IS TRUE — facelessness at the entity level is genuinely fine.**

- Hotmart accounts register as *Pessoa Física, MEI or Pessoa Jurídica* — a company is a normal producer.
- **The Blueprint score is the gate to the affiliate marketplace, and not one of its eleven published criteria
  mentions a face, a video, an author's authority or a personal brand.** They are: *"nome adequado ao produto
  · categoria correta · formato configurado corretamente · descrição clara · qualidade das imagens · qualidade
  e organização do conteúdo · clareza das informações e cuidados com gramática · qualidade da página de vendas
  · coerência entre preço e valor entregue · comissão atrativa para afiliados · disponibilidade de mídias."*
  → `help.hotmart.com` — *Blueprint: como ter uma boa avaliação*
- What affiliates are told to evaluate: *"comissão, regras do programa, descrição do produto e materiais de
  apoio."* **No mention of the producer's authority.**
- **Measured, n=23**, Hotmart's own marketplace sorted `most_rated&ratings=4.5`, reading `hotmarter.name` off
  each product page: **12 of 23 producers are companies or institutions** — Venturini Cursos, Top Cursos, Meta
  Smart Group, Instituto Nefertari, Editora Luz do Evangelho, CoEditora ×2, ADIGITAL, Barber Day, Los Academy,
  Instituto Saber Consciente, Instituto Óliver — against 7 personal names.
  ⚠ **Do not over-read it: "most rated" is satisfaction, not volume, and a company-owned product can still put
  an instructor on camera.** What it establishes is narrow and real: **a company-branded producer is normal at
  the top of the marketplace and is not a handicap.**

**THE HALF THAT KILLS IT — the cold start is gated on sales she has not made.**

> ### ★★★ Kiwify, verbatim: **"O produto precisa ter pelo menos mais de R$5 mil em vendas na vida toda"** — to appear in the affiliate marketplace **at all**.
> Ranking is then driven by *"faturamento recente do produto"*.
> → `ajuda.kiwify.com.br` — *como funciona o ranking de produtos no marketplace de afiliados*

**Hotmart is the same shape without the number.** Listing needs Blueprint >60%, which is achievable cold — but
**every sort is a proof-of-sales sort**: *"Mais quentes: os produtos com maior temperatura"*, *"Mais queridos…
depende das avaliações dos clientes"*, *"Mais recentes."* And temperature: *"Todos os produtos cadastrados na
Hotmart começam com a temperatura baixa. Conforme as vendas acontecem ao longo do tempo, essa temperatura pode
aumentar."*

**And the platform's own fix costs the escape.** Hotmart's answer to recruiting affiliates is a **Gerente de
Afiliados**, *"ponto de contato entre o Produtor e os Afiliados"* — who **must be registered as a Coprodutor to
be paid**. That is a third revenue share *and* a permanent human relationship job.

> ### ⇒ THE AFFILIATE ESCAPE IS A CHICKEN-AND-EGG PROBLEM EXACTLY AS BAD AS THE ONE IT WAS MEANT TO SOLVE.
> Affiliates are recruited by *proof of sales*, and proof of sales is what an operator with no audience cannot
> manufacture. **You need distribution to get the distribution.** The face is not the gate — **the sales
> history is** — and she has neither.

## 2. Two distribution kills worth keeping on their own

- **★ PAID ADS ARE A C2 VIOLATION, NOT A BUDGET PROBLEM. Google's Ads Transparency Center publishes the
  advertiser's NAME AND LOCATION.** A verified advertiser is a public identity. *(New. The archive had only ever
  killed paid acquisition on the US$3.000 capital cap.)*
- **★ SEO IS HALVING, MEASURED: Pew put click-through at 8% with an AI summary present against 15% without.**
  This sharpens the standing position — *indexation is free, ranking is out of reach* — by showing the prize
  itself is shrinking.

## 3. Why the category closes, in three gates

**(i) Every distribution route costs a face or money she does not have.** Affiliate marketplaces gate on sales
history · paid ads publish her identity · SEO click-through is halving · audience-rent needs 25.000–45.000
subscribers at 0,62% median conversion.

**(ii) The price floor for pure information is zero, and FOUR separate parties fund it there.** A frontier model
(the settled AI-marketplace law) · a standards body shipping a free validator · **the Brazilian state** (the
open-state inversion: CNPJ records at R$0,01, CNES, DataJud, ECADNET, LAI) · and whoever earns margin on the
future flow. **Compulsion does not rescue it — the measured template floor for a Brazilian mandatory document
pack is R$97,90 for 186 editable Word files.**

**(iii) Nothing in the category has a clock.** A course, an ebook, a report and a template are each bought once.
Recurrence must be imported from outside the information — a filing deadline, a licence term, an annual return —
**and once imported, the sale is no longer the information.**

## 4. The law this hunt produced

> ### ★★★ INFORMATION DOES NOT PRICE. WHAT PRICES IS THE NON-INFORMATIONAL THING WELDED TO IT.
> Four things price, and information is only ever their delivery medium: **accepted liability for a rejection ·
> a physical original that exists exactly once · an access right the buyer is barred from holding · a
> signature.** **This archive has repeatedly fooled itself by reading the MEDIUM as the PRODUCT.**
> ⇒ **Of anything that looks like an information business, ask: strip the information away — what is left, and
> is THAT what the buyer is paying for? If nothing is left, the price is zero and four parties are already
> funding it there.**
>
> ★ **AND THE SAME-DAY REFINEMENT, which CARTA's death supplies for free: "accepted liability" only prices
> where NOBODY WHO COULD ACCEPT IT MORE CHEAPLY IS SELLING.** The judge named CARTA as its worked example of
> liability-as-product hours after a US consulate was found selling the same certification at **US$50**. So the
> law needs its second clause: **welding a non-informational thing to information is NECESSARY and not
> SUFFICIENT — you must still price against the buyer's NEED, including the state's own counter.**

## 5. The practical instruction

**Stop hunting Brazilian consumer infoprodutos. The route is closed and re-testing it costs weeks.**
The category is not closed because it is small — it is enormous — but because **every one of its distribution
routes is priced in a currency she does not hold**, and the product itself has no floor.

**Explicitly also killed: the PUBLISHER SHAPE** — she is the company-branded producer and contracts a named
expert as the face. *(CoEditora and Editora Luz do Evangelho in the measurement above are exactly this.)*
**It dies anyway: if the contracted expert has an audience they do not need her; if they do not, there is no
distribution; the content is generatable; and a course still has no second purchase.**
