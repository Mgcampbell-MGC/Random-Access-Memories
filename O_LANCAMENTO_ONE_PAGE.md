# O LANÇAMENTO: the one-page manual

**You send the product. We deliver the campaign.** Untested: no buyer has paid R$2.990 yet. The 20 calls decide.

## 1. What we sell
| Product | What's in it | Time | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **O LANÇAMENTO** | Hero + up to 2 supporting products · key visual · 6 campaign images · 15 s film + two 6 s cuts · 3 store images · report · 1 revision | 5–7 working days | **R$2.990 per launch** |

Also: A LINHA (3 products) R$4.900 · TEMPORADA (4 launches prepaid) R$9.900. No 3D turn.

## 2. Who buys
- Small Brazilian beauty brands that just filed new products with ANVISA: the CRM list.
- Hair, face and body first. Makeup and fragrance are on the list, marked as untested pack shapes, so offer the PRÉVIA first.
- Size decides the offer: EPP and bigger get O LANÇAMENTO; ME (micro) brands get VITRINE first.
- Out: sunscreen and children's lines.

## 3. The one rule: AI never draws the label
- Scenes use a flat blue stand-in where the pack goes.
- The real pack goes in by code, only from the file on the signed Ficha.
- A machine checks every image and film frame. Sol checks every label at 100% zoom. Failures never ship.
- No AI edits after the pack is in. Text goes on by code.

## 4. How a launch runs
1. Signed order, contract, both Fichas, 50% paid. Nothing starts before.
2. Day 1–2: files in; the brand picks one of three worlds, in writing.
3. Stand-in scenes → pack in → check → hand-finish the scene, never the pack.
4. Day 3–4: key visual approved. Then text, film, cuts, all checked.
5. Day 5–7: 12-point checklist, written approval, delivery, second invoice.

Sol: ~4–4,5 h per launch (estimate).

## 5. How we sell
- Portuguese calls to beauty founders and marketing leads. Never industrial buyers.
- Book in writing first, with *"responda SAIR"*. Never a bought list.
- Ask first: what did the last launch's images and video cost? Log it in the CRM.
- Show the PRÉVIA. Never discount. Never close on the phone; send the offer sheet the same day.

**The 20-call test** (the CRM dashboard scores it automatically):
- **KEEP R$2.990** if ≥6/20 state ≥R$2.500 of last-launch spend and ≥2 buy within 30 days (or ≥4 VITRINE).
- **SWITCH** to ESSENCIAL R$1.490 + FILME R$990 if ≥8/20 state ≥R$1.000.
- **STOP** if ≥12/20 state <R$1.000.

## 6. Contracts and promises
- Exclusive licence + written cessão + our promise never to reuse. Never "the images are yours".
- Guarantee: redo free within 5 working days; if that fails, refund that piece.
- Phrases only from the signed Ficha de Alegações, word for word.
- 50% Pix on order, 50% on delivery, nota fiscal each time.

## 7. Never
- Before/after · cure or treatment claims · lab coats or clinics · synthetic testimonials · faces like real people · ingredients not on the label · anything aimed at children.
- The brand's art in any generator · a failed piece · running the client's ads.

## 8. The numbers
- Target: US$2.000–2.500/month net (R$10.407–13.009) ⇒ R$12.341–15.771 gross.
- ≈4 launches a month (e.g. 4 LANÇAMENTO + 2 EXTENSÃO = R$13.340) ≈ 16 active brands.
- ≈45–55 of 87 hours a month. Demand is the limit, not time.
- ≥3 of the first 10 clients must buy again within 6 months, or it's a treadmill.

## 9. A week *(suggested)*
Mon: CRM, booking messages · Tue–Wed: calls (5–7 h) · Thu: one launch (4–4,5 h) · Fri: follow-ups, invoices (1 h). Monthly: refresh the CRM list.
