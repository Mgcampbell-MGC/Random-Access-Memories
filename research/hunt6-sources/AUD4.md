## The rule (primary)
- https://www.gov.br/mdic/pt-br/assuntos/camex/resolucoes/gecex/resolucoes-compiladas/resolucao-gecex-no-512-de-16-agosto-de-2023 — compiled Res. Gecex 512/2023. Art. 3 §1: the filer registers as "empresa ou associação de classe, com personalidade jurídica brasileira". **§2: "será permitido ao pleiteante constituir representante legal para ter acesso ao sistema em seu nome".** Art. 4 requirements (TEC-style plural description, translated catalogues, proforma, investment project); the text contains **no engineer, ART, lawyer or sworn-translation requirement**. Art. 5 / 5-A (Res. 760/2025): a renewal needs the full art. 4 documents, "descrição… idêntica àquela vigente", and §3 "tratamento unificado" for renewals of the same Ex. Art. 11–12: 10 working days to rebut a contestation, otherwise archived. Art. 14 sole para.: "função essencial" excludes productivity, interoperability and operating cost. Art. 15 II: investments under way toward national production can block a grant. Art. 21: a denied pleito may be refiled only after one year. Art. 24: MDIC must publish the full list of granted and denied pleitos. (200)
