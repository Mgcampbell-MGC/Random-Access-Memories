## ANVISA: primary statutes and pages
- https://anvisalegis.datalegis.net/action/ActionDatalegis.php?acao=abrirTextoAto&tipo=RDC&numeroAto=00000907&seqAto=000&valorAno=2024&orgao=RDC%2FDC%2FANVISA%2FMS (wave-1 copy in work/h6c/rdc907.html, read in full): RDC 907/2024. Art. 47 sets the labelling deadline at **3 Oct 2025** for products regularised by 3 Oct 2022, so that deadline has **PASSED**. Art. 49 cancels processes not adequated. Art. 40: registered products are valid 10 years, revalidation falls in the first semester of the last year, caducidade otherwise. Art. 41 §§1–3: notified products need a continuity declaration "nos últimos seis meses do decênio" or they are cancelled. Art. 13 lists the mandatory label fields (INCI composition, process number, AFE, CNPJ…). Art. 20 par. único requires the composition in Portuguese per RDC 898. Art. 21: imported products may be adequated by sticker, which cannot be digital. DOU 23/09/2024.
- https://anvisalegis.datalegis.net/action/ActionDatalegis.php?acao=abrirTextoAto&tipo=RDC&numeroAto=00001030&seqAto=000&valorAno=2026&orgao=RDC%2FDC%2FANVISA%2FMS&codTipo=&desItem=&desItemFim=&cod_menu=9434&cod_modulo=310&pesquisa=true : RDC 1.030/2026, **DOU 15/06/2026**. It adds to the prohibited list (RDC 529 annex): boric acid and borates (sodium borate, perborates, zinc borate…), POLYURETHANE-18/-19, **ketoconazole**, dichloromethane, **BUTYLPHENYL METHYLPROPIONAL (Lilial)**, **HYDROXYISOHEXYL 3-CYCLOHEXENE CARBOXALDEHYDE (Lyral)**, and alters entries including TPO and DMPT. It moves benzoyl peroxide, methyleugenol, hydroquinone and azelaic acid to the restricted list. **Art. 5:** "prazo de 12 (doze) meses para adequação dos produtos já regularizados… contado a partir da data de publicação… com exceção do BUTYLPHENYL METHYLPROPIONAL… e do HYDROXYISOHEXYL… prazo de dezoito (18) meses". **No sell-through clause.**
- https://anvisalegis.datalegis.net/action/ActionDatalegis.php?acao=abrirTextoAto&tipo=RDC&numeroAto=00001029&seqAto=000&valorAno=2026&orgao=RDC%2FDC%2FANVISA%2FMS&codTipo=&desItem=&desItemFim=&cod_menu=9434&cod_modulo=310&pesquisa=true : RDC 1.029/2026, DOU 15/06/2026. It covers part 1 of the restricted list (MERCOSUL/GMC 06/25). Art. 2 gives **24 months** to adequate regularised products. The annex carries many new label warnings (e.g. "Usar luvas adequadas").
- https://anvisalegis.datalegis.net/action/ActionDatalegis.php?acao=abrirTextoAto&tipo=RDC&numeroAto=00000529&seqAto=000&valorAno=2021&orgao=RDC%2FDC%2FANVISA%2FMS&codTipo=&desItem=&desItemFim=&cod_menu=9434&cod_modulo=310&pesquisa=true : RDC 529/2021 art. 8 gave a 36-month window **plus** a sell-through clause: "poderão ser comercializados até o final dos seus prazos de validade". RDC 1030 amends this annex but repeats no such clause.
- https://anvisalegis.datalegis.net/action/ActionDatalegis.php?acao=abrirTextoAto&tipo=RDC&numeroAto=00000898&seqAto=000&valorAno=2024&orgao=RDC%2FDC%2FANVISA%2FMS&codTipo=&desItem=&desItemFim=&cod_menu=9434&cod_modulo=310&pesquisa=true : RDC 898/2024 (DOU 02/09/2024). The composition must appear in Portuguese; the official ANVISA translation list is mandatory (art. 3); a digital/QR format is allowed (art. 2 §3). There is a **36-month** stock-exhaustion window each time a translation changes (art. 3 §§2–3). No petition is needed (art. 5).
- https://anvisalegis.datalegis.net/action/ActionDatalegis.php?acao=abrirTextoAto&tipo=RDC&numeroAto=00000951&seqAto=000&valorAno=2024&orgao=RDC%2FDC%2FANVISA%2FMS&codTipo=&desItem=&desItemFim=&cod_menu=9434&cod_modulo=310&pesquisa=true : RDC 951/2024 as amended by RDC 965/2025, the SGAS→Solicita migration. Art. 3: migrate during validity "sob pena de cancelamento". Art. 4: from 1/1/2026 the continuity declaration happens only by migration. Art. 2 §4: no TFVS fee. Art. 6: labels with the old SGAS number may be used for 6 years if migrated 07/04/2025–31/12/2026, 4 years for 2027–28, and 2 years from 2029.
