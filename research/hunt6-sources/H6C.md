## Advertising / AI rules
- https://conar.wpenginepowered.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/260525_GUIA_INFLUENCIADORES_CONAR_v6.pdf — CONAR Guia de Influenciadores v6 (25 May 2026), §1.3.1 "Inteligência Artificial". The CBAP applies in full to AI-generated, edited or segmented ads. "Influenciadores, Anunciantes, Agências e demais partes da cadeia de criação ... permanecem responsáveis". Footnote 6: creates NO new disclosure duty ("não versando em particular sobre avisos (disclosure)").
- https://conar.wpenginepowered.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Codigo_CONAR_2026.pdf — CBAP 2026, 83 pp. No AI article. Art. 27 §2 ("informação de texto ou apresentação visual" must not mislead); art. 27 §9(a)–(c) (testimonials must be genuine; unpersonalised models only as "licença publicitária"); art. 27 §9(e) ("uso de sósias depende de autorização"); art. 34(a) (images of living people need prior express authorisation); art. 45(a)/(b) (advertiser has total responsibility, agency is jointly responsible); art. 46 (anyone in "planejamento, criação, execução" responds); art. 47; art. 50 (penalties).
