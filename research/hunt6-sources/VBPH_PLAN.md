# VIRTUAL BEAUTY PRODUCTION HOUSE — the plan as supplied by another LLM (verbatim summary of the founder's paste, 25 Sep 2026)

One sentence: take a finished beauty product and its existing brand assets and turn them into a complete premium launch campaign in 7 business days — without the brand coordinating photographer, model, studio, videographer, retoucher or agency. Brazil-first; established independent HAIRCARE and SKINCARE brands. Not an agency, no social media management, no ads, no retainer — fixed-price campaign production.

Founder role: CREATIVE DIRECTOR (taste: does it look expensive, model fit, pose, believable skin/hair, product accuracy, hero selection). AI does production.

Customer: existing Brazilian professional haircare/skincare brand preparing a launch / line launch / relaunch / packaging refresh / trade show. ~10–100 SKUs, multiple launches/yr, professional packaging, good packshots, 1–few approvers who can approve R$5.000–10.000 without procurement. Avoid: tiny handmade, dropshippers, no final packaging, multinationals, clinical claims, makeup shade fidelity, bulk catalogue seekers.

SKU 1 HERO LAUNCH R$5.900 — one product: 1 key visual, 1 hero film 15–20 s, 8 campaign stills, 3 motion cutdowns 5–8 s, commerce/distributor kit, vertical/square/horizontal exports; 7 business days; 1 direction; 1 consolidated revision round.
SKU 2 FAMILY LAUNCH R$9.900 — up to 4 products of one family: unified concept, family key visual, family hero film, group + individual hero visuals, synthetic-model scenes, motion, commerce assets, exports.
Later add-on EVENT READY ≈R$2.900 (trade-show assets).

Process: intake (packshots, artwork, logo, approved claims) → PRODUCT TRUTH SHEET "Product Lock" (exact geometry/label/colour/claims, client approves once; "AI creates the world, we protect the product") → 3 creative routes, client picks one → 1 KEY VISUAL approval → full production (generate broadly, auto-filter, founder picks winners, precision repair in Photoshop — "core capability, not backup") → 4 QA gates (Product, Model, Brand, Claim) → one proof board (Frame.io), one consolidated revision → organised delivery. No invented efficacy, no AI before/after, no fake testimonials.

GTM: trigger-based outbound (new product, relaunch, packaging, Beauty Fair participation, "coming soon"); for the best prospects make ONE private watermarked sample concept from public product imagery; "VISUAL PROOF > SALES EXPLANATION". 30-second pitch: "One product R$5.900; four-product line R$9.900. No retainer."

Stack first 15 campaigns: ChatGPT/OpenAI, Higgsfield, Photoshop, CapCut, Canva, Frame.io, Google Drive, Sheets. After 15: n8n automation; later Creatomate, Supabase.

Economics (plan's own illustrative revenue, not forecasts): 5 HERO + 3 FAMILY = R$59.200/month; 8 HERO + 3 FAMILY = R$76.900/month. Target founder time HERO 4–6 h, FAMILY 7–10 h after maturity.

Moat claimed: beauty taste, product-fidelity workflow, model-routing knowledge, recipes, failure library, beauty QA, brand memory, speed, coherence.

Kill criteria (plan's own): buyers compare only to ultra-cheap AI generators · HERO >8 founder hours after learning period · fidelity unreliable · major creative changes routine · prospects love work but reject ~R$5.900 · cannot build a national list of several hundred suitable brands · customers want social/ad management instead · founder dislikes the creative-direction work.
Validation: 15 real campaigns (mostly paid), then review and freeze SOP.
