## TikTok Shop Brasil — platform rules and money (PRIMARY: TikTok's own pages)
- https://seller-br.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=24428156307201&lang=pt-BR — "Tarifa de Comissão da Plataforma", Aplica-se a: Brasil, dated 12/06/2026: from 15 Jul 2026 a seller pays 10% commission + R$4,00/item below R$50, and 6% + R$6,00/item at R$50 or more. New sellers who finish a mission get 0% for 60 days, capped at R$17.000.
- https://seller-br.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6580476438529809&default_language=pt-BR — "Colaborações do Affiliate" (BR): Open vs Target collaboration; the seller sets the commission per product; the seller Affiliate Center has "Descobrir criadores" with a creator card showing performance data. The DISCOVERY layer is free to sellers.
- https://seller-br.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=1517752301717265&lang=pt-BR — "Comissão afiliada para criadores" (BR, 01/07/2026): commission = base × a rate set by the seller; paid only after the seller's order settles (7, 3 or 15 days); refunds reverse it. Outcomes are platform-attributed and platform-paid.
- https://seller-br.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=5635030781101840&lang=pt-BR — LIVE and video quality guide (BR). Verbatim, under "O que evitar / Durante uma transmissão ao vivo": "Não use interações verbais que não sejam em tempo real, como vozes geradas por IA, gravações de áudio ou rádio." Videos: "Crie conteúdo gravado em um ambiente real … garantindo que elas mostrem seu rosto e o produto físico." ⇒ TikTok's own rule requires a live human voice in LIVE. This is the CONSENT price-holder.
- https://seller-br.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=2324329879832321&lang=pt-BR — "Restrições e requisitos para conteúdo gerado por IA" (BR). AI content is allowed when disclosed and not misleading. Using AI to imitate another person's image or voice is forbidden. The product must match exactly. So AI is not banned outright; the hard line is the real-time voice in LIVE.
- https://seller-br.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=1413208980588305&default_language=pt-BR — TikTok Shop content policy (BR): §3.1 AIGC is not allowed if it misleads; beauty rules forbid AI before/after wrinkle comparisons and deceptive beauty filters.
- https://seller-br.tiktok.com/partners — the BR seller-centre partner page, reading its embedded i18n strings. BR-specific: "sc_leaderboard_partners_list_page_sub_ranking_name12_br_explanation": "This leaderboard ranks TAPs (TikTok Affiliate Partners) based on their monthly GMV from partner collaborations". Also: "To collaborate with these affiliate partners … check out their campaigns and register your products within their preferred commission rate range." ⇒ A TAP leaderboard exists for Brazil, and a seller joins by registering its products. No credentials change hands.
- https://partner.tiktokshop.com/api/v1/document/detail?document_id=64f1989064ed2e0295f3c1b5&workspace_id=3 — "Partner service categories" (updated 30 Oct 2025). Affiliate Partner, registration region Brazil → target market Brazil = "Seller and Scalable Creator Match-up — Connect scalable creators with sellers/supply chains". Brazil TSP categories: Shop Operation, LIVE Stream Management, Short Video, Affiliate Management, Paid Media, plus Mass Recruiting and Mass Tutoring. MCN/CAP also exists for Brazil. "Host/Operations Recruitment and Training" is listed for cross-border and UK only, NOT for Brazil.
- https://partner.tiktokshop.com/api/v1/document/detail?document_id=64f19899cb677b0286e75d67&workspace_id=3 — "Partner registration application requirement" (updated 14 Sep 2026). A TAP needs a company registration and at least ONE of: a signed creator contract or screenshots of a cooperated creator's profile · a seller partnership agreement · another e-commerce affiliate platform account with 7 days of sales data · a self-developed affiliate tool.
- https://partner.tiktokshop.com/api/v1/document/detail?document_id=64f198679495ef028184fa10&workspace_id=3 — "Non-US TAP" (last updated 7 May 2025, the day BEFORE the BR launch, so stale on markets): lists ID/MY/PH/TH/VN/UK. TAPs "generate exclusive product links … earn commissions based on the sales of all products promoted by creators". Payout goes through Partner Console > Finance.
- https://partner.tiktokshop.com/api/v1/document/detail?document_id=661e23f026367902bfae36f8&workspace_id=3 — TAP sample requests: the agency requests samples on behalf of authorised creators and the seller ships them. The TAP never touches goods.
- https://partner.tiktokshop.com/api/v1/document/detail?document_id=669013c0de15e502edf3f4df&workspace_id=3 — "TAP Data Overview": TikTok gives TAPs revenue, commission and creator diagnostics ("Attention" / "Potential" creators).

## Legal
- https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l6533.htm — Lei 6.533/78:
  - art. 2 I: an artist is one who "interpreta ou executa obra de caráter cultural … através de meios de comunicação de massa".
  - art. 3 par. único: the law also applies "às pessoas físicas ou jurídicas que agenciem colocação de mão-de-obra" of those professionals.
