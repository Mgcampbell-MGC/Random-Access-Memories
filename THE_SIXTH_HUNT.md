## 2. THE VIRTUAL BEAUTY PRODUCTION HOUSE — the supplied plan, verified

**The plan:** sell AI-produced launch campaigns to established Brazilian haircare and skincare brands.

- **Tiers:** HERO LAUNCH R$5.900; FAMILY LAUNCH R$9.900 (up to 4 products).

what parts it donates.

### 2.1 What is TRUE in the plan, and better than it knew

- **The demand trigger is a statute, and it is free.** RDC 907/2024 requires a cosmetic to be notified to ANVISA
  before sale. ANVISA publishes the whole register daily:
  - Source: `dados.anvisa.gov.br/dados/CONSULTAS/PRODUTOS/TA_CONSULTA_COSMETICOS.CSV`, 227,5 MB, no key, no
    login.
  - Freshness: stamped 25 Sep 10:22, newest record 23 Sep 23:51, so about 1,5 days' lag.
  - Volume: about **3.400 hair/skin notifications a month** (41.037 in 12 months).
  - Lead time: the median launch reaches the shelf **about 79 days after notification**.
  - Notification date = `DT_VENCIMENTO` − 10 years for electronic *Notificado* rows (RDC 907 art. 41 §1).
  - Brand and category must be parsed from `NO_PRODUTO`.
  - **This is the first PRIVATE-buyer instance of `THE COMPELLED-ANNOUNCEMENT LAW`** (see §7). The plan's own GTM
    line, *"trigger-based outbound (new product…)"*, is its strongest element and can run at R$0.
- **It is legally buildable, with no blocker** (H6C):
  - No statute or CONAR rule requires an AI label on commercial ads (CONAR Guia de Influenciadores v6, 25 May
    2026, §1.3.1 fn 6).
  - PL 2338/2023 and PL 841/2024 are not law.
  - Higgsfield's ToS (26 Jul 2026) lets her pass Output rights to clients.
- **The Claim QA gate is the legal core, not an add-on.** An AI shine or skin render can be a misleading efficacy
  claim with no words at all:
  - Lei 6.360 art. 59, CDC art. 37, CBAP art. 27 §2.
  - CDC art. 67 criminalises whoever *"faz"* a misleading ad, and CBAP arts. 45(b)/46 name the whole creation
    chain, so the producer is exposed, not only the brand.
- **Tax: fator r, not the Anexo III/V split** (AUD3):
  - Set pro-labore at 28% of RBT12 (≈R$3.400–4.400/month, which is part of her take-home anyway).
  - The all-in rate is then **9,08–9,35% on every tier**, cheaper than the split for stills-heavy work.
  - It also removes the UNVERIFIED risk that Receita classes AI-only film as design (Anexo V).
  - CNAEs 5911-1/02 + 7410-2/99; avoid 7311-4/00. MEI is impossible.

### 2.4 THE REPAIR — O LANÇAMENTO

   - Every product pixel is the brand's real packshot, composited locally (Photoshop) into AI-made scenes.

   - Supers and claims are added in the editor.
   - **This is one move that fixes two problems:**
     - **Fidelity:** no label is ever generated, so none can drift.
     - **Pre-launch confidentiality:** H6C found Higgsfield trains on inputs absent an Enterprise Agreement, and
       every buyer here is by definition pre-launch. An unreleased label that never enters a generator cannot be
       trained on.
   - Sell it as a guarantee: **"every character on the pack matches your approved PDF, or the asset is redone

3. **The claim check is a gate the client signs per asset.**
   - Each image is checked against what the ANVISA notification and label support.
   - Add a voluntary *"imagem ilustrativa criada com IA"* line wherever a result is depicted.
   - It is her liability as much as a feature, so it is priced in, not given away.
4. **Prospecting is the ANVISA diff plus Portuguese calls, 60–80 days before shelf.**
   - The holder CNPJ is the contract manufacturer for ~55% of brands (AUD2's 12-month recount; H6A said 73%), so

5. **Hours** (AUD3):
   - Target volume does **not** fit at month 3 (kit-led, central 127 h).

   - At month 18 hours are not the binding constraint; demand and pool are.

## 8. O PLEITO — the out-of-domain alternate (H6G, AUD4)

**What it is:** drafting Ex-tarifário pleitos for importers of capital goods, sold by Portuguese calls through the

- **Arithmetic:** 16–20 grants a year at R$10k. That is at or above an established firm (Tradeworks ≈16/yr).

- **Strongest version: RENOVA 2027.** Renewal descriptions must be IDENTICAL by law (art. 5-A §1, Res. 760/2025),

## 8c. A BANCADA — audit

**Verdict: DEAD as a standalone business.** Two host questions survive inside the O LANÇAMENTO calls. Floor

  - Only 5–6 employers (~10–12%) use a recruiter.

  - Catho and Empregga let brands post free.

- **R$2.000 per hire is above market.** Empregga publishes an average ticket of R$1.516 per vacancy and sells

  recruiting live hosts exists.
- **The law is sharper than W2E reported.** Decreto 82.385 art. 5 lets only firms registered under Lei 6.019
  (≥R$100.000 capital) place "artistas". A beauty sales host is probably not an artista; a fashion try-on host is

  - 12,5–24 h per paid hire;
  - 5,7–13,6 hires a month needed;

## 9. LAWS FROM THIS HUNT

- **★★★ THE TOOL VENDOR IS THE FRONT DOOR.**
  - In late 2026 the company that sells the generator also sells the deliverable as a template: Higgsfield
    *Marketing Studio* "one-click product ads", *DTC Ads* with brand kits, a product-photoshoot agent, US$49/month.
  - A São Paulo studio publishes the done-for-you version at R$53/image.
  - ⇒ **A solo operator cannot sell PRODUCTION of anything a tool vendor ships as a template.** She can sell only
    what the template cannot supply: a consent, a clock, a channel, an outcome, or a bounded guarantee.
  - **2026 tools help her SELL — first contacts, samples, speed — far more than they help her CHARGE.**
  - *(`THE BUILDABILITY THRESHOLD` confirmed a fourth time: capability changes her cost of operating, never her
    right to charge.)*
- **★★ THE STOCK VENDOR IS THE FRONT DOOR, and A CALENDAR IS NOT A CLOCK UNLESS IT CONSUMES THE UNIT.**
  - The catalogue brands that refresh imagery every cycle buy stock: Adobe Stock BR is R$950/month for 750 images +
    AI edits + indemnity = R$1,27/image.
  - The ones that do not refresh reuse 25–86% of last cycle's faces.
  - ⇒ **A fixed publishing cadence proves the buyer PUBLISHES on schedule, not that it BUYS on schedule.**
    Measure reuse across two consecutive editions before calling a calendar a consumption clock. Face-matching two
    PDFs is an afternoon's work.
- **★★★ THE COMPELLED ANNOUNCEMENT HAS A PRIVATE EDITION — ANVISA'S DAILY NOTIFICATION FILE.**
  - RDC 907/2024 forces every cosmetic onto a public register before sale.
  - ANVISA publishes the whole register daily, keyless: ~3.400 hair/skin launches a month, **about 79 days before
    shelf**.
  - It is the first PRIVATE buyer list in this archive that announces itself by statute. It is the mirror of Lei
    14.133 art. 54 for public buyers.
  - ⚠ Three traps:
    - there is no date field (notification = `DT_VENCIMENTO` − 10 years);
    - there is no brand or category field (parse `NO_PRODUTO`);
    - **the holder CNPJ is the contract manufacturer for ~55% of brands.**
  - Reusable for **anything sold to a Brazilian cosmetic brand at launch**.
- **★★★ THE CONTRACT MANUFACTURER IS THE FREE FRONT DOOR FOR EVERYTHING A SMALL BRAND NEEDS AT LAUNCH.**
  - `THE NO-MARGIN-ON-THE-FLOW LAW` at the factory: the terceirizadora earns on the production run, so it bundles
    label, identity, mockup, and increasingly marketing and campaigns, *"sem custo adicional"*, to win the order.
  - Before selling any launch service to a private-label brand, **read its manufacturer's services page.**
- **★★★ THE THIN-FIELD PREMIUM BELONGS TO WHOEVER IS ALREADY THERE** (full statement, §7).
  - Price a lot at what OUT-OF-STATE winners clear, never at what the winner got.
  - Reject presencial, post-hoc-published and >2×-births lots before pricing.
  - `THE CLEARING PRICE IS SET BY WHO BOTHERED TO BID` stays true; its corollary *"bid thin fields"* is
    **WITHDRAWN for any bidder who is not local.**
  - A distant bidder either removes the premium by arriving, or, where the field is directed, is removed.
- **★★ FREIGHT SCALES WITH VOLUME, NOT VALUE.**
  - A bathtub kit costs 58% of its value to ship to São Luís by PAC; a dense textile box costs 7%.
  - **Classify every SKU by cubic weight before bidding outside the road radius.**
  - *(Extends `THE FREIGHT STUDY DOES NOT TRANSFER ACROSS DENSITY` from a warning to a bid rule.)*
- **★★ THE GENERATOR NEVER SEES THE LABEL.**
  - Composite the real packshot locally, and let AI make only the world around it.
  - One structural choice removes label drift (Photoroom: text/logo distortion in 20,1% of base generations) AND
    pre-launch confidentiality exposure (inputs may be trained on).
  - **When one design choice dissolves two risks, it is the product, not a technique.**
- **★★ CHECK A SUPPLIED PLAN'S PRICE AGAINST THE SAME SELLER'S TRADITIONAL LIST, and read the sentence a quote
  came from.**

  - I misread its "fidelidade" clause (garments, not labels) and nearly carried it as evidence.
