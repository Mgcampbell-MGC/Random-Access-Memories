> ### ⚠ A DATA TRAP THAT MUST BE HANDLED EXPLICITLY, and it is the archive's own unit error in a fourth costume

> handled.)* **State the handling, or the number is not a number.** The tables below use the most conservative

### What this says, plainly

- **The quantity ceiling is ~450–1.100 units, not ~2.400.** `QUANTITY IS THE PRICE VARIABLE` was right about
  the shape and the archive's ~2.400 ceiling was set against an unverified cost. At the best verified SP cost
  the caneta break-even falls **between 450–1.100 and 1.100–2.400**; at the ME-tier cost the 450–1.100 band is
  already down to 27,8%. **Tighten the parser's quantity ceiling to 1.100 and treat 450 as the comfortable
  line.**
- **Papel A4 is out.** It is the single biggest line in an expediente basket and it is **loss-making above 50
  reams** at her verified cost, against a state that pays **R$19,83 in São Paulo**. A resma she pays R$29,00
  for is sold to the state at R$20,80. **Do not bid paper at the retail tier. Ever.**
- **Borracha and pasta com elástico are out at every quantity** — the ME-tier price is already above what the
  state pays for sixty of them.
- **Caneta in small lots and clips in mid lots are the live lines** — and they are exactly the lines the
  archive said the margin sits in. **The thesis survives; the SKU list shrinks.**

## 5. The structural finding: the real wholesale tier publishes nothing

**SINAPEL** — *Sindicato do Comércio Atacadista de Papel, Papelão, Artigos de Escritório e de Papelaria do
Estado de São Paulo*, Rua do Paraíso 529, São Paulo — publishes its member distributor list at
`sinapel.com.br/distribuidores.html`. **Fifteen named companies**: ABC Distribuidora · AMV Papéis ·
Artepaper · CAC Papéis · Camila Papéis · Central Advance · Labate Papéis · OnPaper · Papel Ecológico ·
Passalacqua · Rio Branco · Rymo · Tecpel · Vitália Papéis · WG Papéis.

**Fetched all fifteen. Result: 14 of 15 display ZERO prices.** One (Rymo) has a working B2B cart — and Rymo
is in **Amazonas**. Nine returned a live site with no price anywhere; five did not resolve at all.

And the one published account-opening rule found on the way says why a first-year company cannot reach that
tier: **VPA Atacadista** (R. Tiers 550, Pari, São Paulo — a real SP wholesaler) —
*"O valor mínimo para faturamento por pedido é de R$600,00 e está disponível apenas para pessoa jurídica.
Salientamos que a empresa deverá ter **12 meses de atividade ou mais**."*

> ### ★★ THE PUBLISHED-PRICE TIER IS THE RETAIL TIER
> **A wholesale price that is published is, by that fact, not a wholesale price.** The visible SP layer is
> Simples micro-retailers and cash&carry; the genuine distributor layer is quote-only and gated on twelve
> months of trading — **the same credit gate `THE_WORKING_CAPITAL_ANSWER.md` found on terms, showing up a
> second time on price.** This is `THE MONITORING PINCER` on the supply side: above the retail line the
> published price disappears.

## 6. What to do about it — the test, and it costs nothing and needs no phone

The distributor tier is unmeasured, not proven absent. **The measurement is fifteen emails**, which is this
repo's own `test design that works` (*"email thirty franchisors one question and count the answers containing
a number"*), run against a register the sindicato publishes for free:

> *"Sou ME em São Paulo capital, fornecedora para órgãos públicos. Preciso de preço faturado para: papel A4
> 75 g (caixa 10 resmas), caneta esferográfica azul (caixa 50), pasta aba+elástico, clips 4/0. Vocês atendem
> venda à ordem com entrega direta ao órgão? Qual o pedido mínimo e as condições para primeira compra?"*

**The bar, committed in advance: papel A4 at or below R$19,50/resma faturado in SP, and caneta azul at or
below R$0,70/unidade.** Those are the numbers that make the two biggest lines bidable at the quantities where
the state's money actually is. **Count how many of fifteen answer with a number that clears the bar.** If
fewer than three do, the expediente basket is a small-quantity business permanently, and the plan's volume
assumption must come down to match.

⚠ **Three costs not yet in any model, all found today:** interstate purchase adds **~6 points (LC 123 art. 13
§1 XIII h)** plus **RICMS-SP art. 426-A antecipação** — so an out-of-state supplier must beat an SP one by
more than 6% to be worth using; the R$600 minimum per faturamento order at VPA; and freight, which is in none
of the prices above.
