# THE MAINTENANCE WINDOW — census of 16 instances, 19 Aug 2026

**The pattern hunted:** a business's own capability is **temporarily unavailable on a date they already know,
because they scheduled it themselves.** Three properties nothing else in this project has: the buyer schedules
it months ahead · it is UNCORRELATED with their busy season (maintenance is booked to avoid it) · **★ the buyer
TELLS YOU** — every other clock in this file had to be inferred from a register; this one is volunteered.

**And the referral structure that comes with it:** the party performing the maintenance loses revenue when a
customer postpones it, so it has a commercial incentive to refer whoever covers the gap.

**Verdict: the pattern is real and it does NOT transpose to her. The reason is structural, and it produces two
laws that are worth more than the sixteen instances.**

---

## 1. ★★ THE TWO LAWS

> ### **THE SEPARATION LAW — the maintenance window only produces a business for a THIRD party where something FORBIDS the maintainer from covering the gap it created.**
>
> In the seed example that something is **CAPITAL**: a calibration lab does not own a rental fleet, because a
> rental fleet is ~US$1M of instruments. **The referral is not generosity — it is a structural inability to
> compete.**
>
> **Strip the capital out and the two roles collapse into one.** The implementation partner sells the
> reconciliation. The payroll bureau sells the parallel run. The registration agent sells the renewal. The
> consultant sells the audit prep. **The pattern's beautiful property — "a partner who benefits from sending
> you work" — is a property of CAPITAL INTENSITY, not of maintenance.**
>
> **The only non-capital separator found in sixteen instances is a STATUTORY INDEPENDENCE RULE:**
> **ISO/IEC 17021-1 §5.2.5** — *"The certification body … shall not offer or provide management system
> consultancy"* · MDR Annex VII for notified bodies · Reg (EU) 537/2014 art. 5 for statutory auditors.
> **That is a finite, statute-defined list, and it is where to hunt next.**

> ### **THE COVER-IS-THE-SAME-THING LAW — what fills a gap is whatever made the gap. A credentialed capability needs a credential to cover it; a physical capability needs an asset. She has neither, so she can only cover gaps in UN-CREDENTIALED THROUGHPUT — and un-credentialed throughput does not go away on a schedule, BECAUSE NOTHING SCHEDULES IT.**
>
> Maintenance is scheduled precisely *because* the thing being maintained is regulated or physical. **The very
> property that makes the window predictable is the property that locks her out. That is not bad luck; it is
> the definition of the pattern.**

## 3. THE ONE THAT SURVIVED — and its honest bind

**THE CUTOVER RECONCILIATION PACK.** During an accounting/ERP cutover the buyer deliberately freezes the
ledger: legacy closed to posting, target not yet live, **2–10 days**, set on a plan agreed weeks ahead. Cutovers
park at fiscal year boundaries — **1 Jan (US/EU), 6 Apr (UK), 1 Jul (AU/NZ)** — three markets, three peaks,
none overlapping. *(The "opposite seasons fill each other's trough" note in `CLAUDE.md`, arriving on its own.)*

**Shape: S5 + S3, deliberately not S1.** Two files arrive by email; one document goes back — record counts by
object, control totals by GL account and period, trial-balance tie-out, orphan foreign keys, duplicate keys, a
row-level delta list, and a SHA-256 manifest of both inputs. **It reports differences; it never says whether a
difference is acceptable.** C1 clean, C4 clean, expert veto clean, nothing physical.

**Buyer is the conversion/implementation PARTNER, not the end client** — `THE WHOLESALE ESCAPE` exactly.
Enumerable from vendor partner directories (Xero, Intuit ProAdvisor, NetSuite, Sage, Acumatica) and dedicated
conversion firms (Dataswitcher, Movemybooks, Jet Convert, WOW BookSwitch). **Businesses, so email is on their
own websites.**

**The three reasons it fails, in descending severity:**
1. **★ NO SEPARATOR.** The partner already sells reconciliation — it is billable hours on their SOW.
   Subcontracting it converts revenue into cost. **A calibration lab *cannot* also be a rental fleet; a
   conversion partner *can* also be a reconciler.** This is `THE SEPARATION LAW` biting its own lead candidate.
2. **`THE FREE FRONT DOOR`.** Xero gives basic QuickBooks→Xero conversion away
   ([its own page](https://www.xero.com/us/accounting-software/convert-from-quickbooks/)); observed paid
   conversions run **£200–£600** for complex work. Anything adjacent to migration is pulled toward zero by
   platforms with an acquisition budget.
3. **Schema fragmentation** — build-once only if she picks ONE corridor and refuses the rest.

**Arithmetic, and it is the bind.** Build ~60–80 h once; ~1,5 h per pack after. **US$11.100/month needs ~45
packs at $250 (= 68 h, leaving nothing for acquisition) or ~15 at $750 — and $750 is above the observed price
of the entire conversion.** **The reconciliation pack's own price is UNVERIFIED; no observed instance found.**

**The test, and it costs ~4 hours and no phone:** email thirty accounting-conversion firms one question —
*"when you convert a client's ledger, who produces the source-to-target reconciliation, and what do you charge
for it?"* — **and count the answers containing a number.**

## 4. THE OTHER TWO WORTH A LINE

**Payroll parallel-run variance report** — same shape, same missing separator, and one extra kill: **a $12 net
difference is either rounding or a misconfigured pre-tax deduction, and telling them apart is judgement. The
moment she is asked "which of these matter?" she is opining.** Also: **no statute mandates a parallel run — it
is a practice, not a requirement**, and the sources describing it are vendor blogs.

**ISO certificate-expiry coverage index** — **the only instance in sixteen with a statutory separator**
(§5.2.5 forbids the certification body from consulting), and it dies on distribution: the accredited-certificate
directories publish **company name and address, no email**. `THE POSTAL LIST LAW` on register eleven. **The
separator is real and the front door is bricked up.**

**Also killed and worth remembering:** TLS/code-signing certificate expiry — the window count is about to
multiply by law ([CA/Browser Forum SC-081v3](https://cabforum.org/2025/04/11/ballot-sc081v3-introduce-schedule-of-reducing-validity-and-data-reuse-periods/):
398 → **200 days Mar 2026** → 100 Mar 2027 → 47 Mar 2029) **and it still dies on `THE MONITORING PINCER`**,
because cert monitoring is bundled free into every uptime tool and Let's Encrypt automates renewal outright.
**More windows is not more business when the window is free to watch.**
