## Where Climate 2.0's positioning is soft

- **"Tropical performance haircare"** is a category name no woman will say out loud.
- **"Cabelo pronto pra vida lá fora"** describes a lifestyle. It is not a promise; it does not say what happens
  at 18h.
- **It treats the TRESemmé gap as a footnote — and reaches for the wrong half of it.** "Instrumentally-tested
  humidity claim in stick format" is TRESemmé's game in a smaller tube. They have the lab and the 72-hour number.

The plan already found the real insight and buried it in the R&D section (§10): *"multiple touch-ups without
the hair looking progressively worse."* **That is not an R&D target. That is the position.**

## The reframe: reapply, don't prevent

All 25 sticks sell the same thing — *a fix for a problem*: anti-frizz, SOS, a wax that corrects. TRESemmé sells
the purest version of that frame: **apply once, no touch-up for 72 hours.** Prevention. The touch-up as failure.

Flip it. **The touch-up is not failure; it is a ritual, like lip balm.** Nobody is ashamed to reapply manteiga
de cacau four times a day. It lives in the bag; reapplying is the point. Hair has never had a product with that
status.

> **A gente não promete que você não vai precisar retocar.**
> **A gente promete que pode.**

Structurally: **TRESemmé cannot follow.** Its whole claim architecture is *"you won't need to reapply."* To
match this pole it would have to contradict its own 72-hour promise. The incumbent is locked out by its own
success — the definition of a defensible position.

## What positioning does and does not do here

It does not shrink the shelf — there are still 25 sticks. It takes her out of the anti-frizz race she would lose
as entrant #26 and puts her alone at a pole the biggest incumbent structurally cannot reach. It converts the
recurrence question from hope into design: **three touch-ups a day is a stick a month — the consumption clock is
in the promise** (`THE CONSUMPTION-CLOCK TEST`). And it makes the three-touch claim the thing every real of
proof, content and creator spend compounds.

**The condition is the one the plan set for itself:** the formula has to survive three applications without
build-up. If it does, this is a category of one. If it does not, no positioning saves it — and she would know
in the first field test, before the PO.
