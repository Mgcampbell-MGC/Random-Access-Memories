## 1. ★★ THE FIX — resolve "does she write every book?" by subtraction

Candidate #3's stated kill-condition was *"is the writing a machine that runs once, or her hands on every
book?"* — an empirical question. **The sweep's answer: stop asking it. Drop generated prose entirely.**

The artifact family that needs **zero prose** is 100% deterministic machine work per unit:
- **enhanced portrait series** from the parents' own uploaded scan images
- **heartbeat-soundwave print** (they upload the audio; the waveform is typeset)
- **conception/birth star chart** (pure computation)
- **name-and-dates typesetting**

> **What this buys: the per-unit-work question stops being empirical and becomes architectural.** Her role is
> QA and exception-handling, not authorship. The production ceiling that killed candidates 70–72 is not
> negotiated with — it is designed out.

**★ And the structural reason THIS subject works where pets, homes and weddings failed:**

> ### **THE INACCESSIBLE-SUBJECT SCREEN — the scan keepsake monetises ACCESS to a subject that cannot be photographed. A fetus has no free substitute; a puppy competes with the adopter's own phone from minute one.** Ask of any keepsake/artifact candidate: does the machine provide access, or decoration? Decoration loses to the camera in everyone's pocket.

## 4. ★ THE LAW THE SWEEP PRODUCED — and it closes consumer domains generally

> ### **THE PLATFORM-ARBITER LAW — the file's evidence structures all presuppose an arbiter who accepts records the parties don't already hold (a court, the FMC, a tariff dispute desk). In every consumer domain hunted, the recurring dispute path runs through a PLATFORM (card networks, Airbnb/Booking, iFood, Amazon KDP/ACX) that is SIMULTANEOUSLY THE JUDGE AND THE KEEPER OF THE RECORD, and accepts only evidence it already holds or the merchant's own conduct.**
>
> ### **No third-party evidence market can exist downstream of a platform. Thirty-second screen: "who is the arbiter of this dispute, and do they accept a record they don't already hold?" If the arbiter is a platform, stop.**
>
> This is why the demurrage seed found no analogue in weddings, pets, food or consumer travel: it is B2B,
> statute-governed, with an external arbiter.
