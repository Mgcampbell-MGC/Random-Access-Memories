## 1. What she sells, in one sentence

**Campaign images and a short film for a beauty brand's launch, where AI makes the hands, the skin, the scene and
the light, and the brand's real packaging is laid in afterwards, on her computer, so no letter of the label is ever
redrawn.** Every piece comes with a report proving the label is identical, and a written promise to redo it if not.

**What is NOT scarce, so it is not the headline** (OL1, all verified at the vendor's own page): a product standing
on a surface with a generated background. Google Product Studio does it free in Brazil; Pebblely US$9, Mokker US$13,
Pixelcut US$10, Photoroom US$12,99, Claid US$15 a month. **Ten self-serve routes keep the product's own pixels.**

**What IS scarce** (OL1):
1. **The real pack in a hand or in use**, with fingers over it. Cutout tools stand products on surfaces; tools that
   make hands redraw the label. Tested: Higgsfield's own image model misspelled 2 ingredient names and its one-click
   Marketing Studio 4 (`O_LANCAMENTO.md` Part F).
2. **The real pack moving inside a scene.** Every AI video tool regenerates the frames. The only label-exact
   alternative is 3D/CGI, which is slow and dear: **WekArts R$3.990, 20–40 working days**
   (`wekarts.com.br/product/embalagem-3d-para-produtos/`); Criativoo sells a CGI+AI hybrid, price on request.
3. **A whole launch in one visual world in 5–7 working days**, with that world kept on file for the next product.
4. **A written redo guarantee and a per-piece report, sold to a small brand.** The only other seller of a fidelity
   guarantee is Photoroom, enterprise-only, food and fashion, yearly contract (`photoroom.com/enterprise/guarantee`,
   launched 1 Sep 2026).
5. **Confidentiality:** the brand's unreleased art never enters an AI service (§3, §7).

**The headline, in Portuguese:**
> *"Fotos com o produto em uso e um filme em movimento — a IA cria as mãos, a pele, o cenário e a luz; a sua
> embalagem nunca passa por ela. Campanha completa, num só universo visual, em 5 a 7 dias úteis."*

**There is no technology moat, and the file says so plainly.** The composite method is public (Lamina publishes it,
Higgsfield's own recipes composite logos). The position is **price, speed and accountability** between self-serve
(R$0–300) and CGI studios (R$3.990, 20–40 working days).

## 4. The machine — three commands, tested today

All three live in `tools/` and run on a normal laptop (Python, OpenCV, `pip install imageio-ffmpeg`). Sol is not a
developer: each is one line in a terminal, and `tools/LEIA-ME.md` gives the lines in Portuguese.

```
python3 compor.py  CENA.png  EMBALAGEM.png  PECA.png          # lays the real pack into the placeholder
python3 filme.py   PECA.png  FILME.mp4 --formato 9:16         # 15 s film, H.264, label locked
python3 relatorio_fidelidade.py EMBALAGEM.png PECA.png FILME.mp4   # APROVADA / REPROVADA, per piece
```

**Test results, 25 Sep 2026** (one real bestselling tube, private test data in scratchpad, not committed; every film
checked on **all 360 frames**; "control" = the planted one-patch error, which must score below 0,80):

| Asset | Result | Worst tile / 5th pct | Planted control |
|---|---|---|---|
| In-hand still, fingers over the label | APROVADA | 0,943 / 0,973 | caught (0,24) |
| Same, scene tilted 12° | APROVADA | 0,942 / 0,982 | caught (0,25) |
| Product on a sill, tapered tube | APROVADA | 0,861 / 0,991 | caught (0,27) |
| In-hand still, pack not enlarged | APROVADA | 0,936 / 0,978 | caught (0,28) |
| Film, product on a sill, parallax on, 9:16 | APROVADA | 0,915 / 0,957 | caught (0,25) |
| Film, in hand, 4:5 | APROVADA | 0,889 / 0,947 | caught (0,22) |
| Film, in hand tilted, 1:1 | APROVADA | 0,875 / 0,951 | caught (0,24) |
| Film, in hand, pack not enlarged | APROVADA | 0,828 / 0,950 | caught (0,30) |
| **Negative:** still from a pack with ONE altered word | **REPROVADA** | **0,219** / 0,959 | — |
| **Negative:** film from the same altered pack | **REPROVADA** | **0,194** / 0,928 | — |
| **Negative:** Higgsfield image model, 2 misspellings | **REPROVADA** | 0,269 / 0,806 | — |
| **Negative:** Higgsfield Marketing Studio, 4 misspellings | **REPROVADA** | −0,001 / 0,494 | — |

**Read the altered-word row twice:** its 5th percentile (0,959) would have PASSED. **Only the worst-tile rule catches a
single wrong word**, which is why that rule never moves and why a planted error is checked against it.

### 4.1 What was fixed today, and why it matters
- **The checker now tests itself on every delivery.** It plants a one-letter-sized mirror in a copy of each still
  and of each film's middle frame, and confirms the worst-tile rule catches it; **a piece passes only if the planted
  error is caught.** *A pass that could not
  have failed proves nothing.*
- **Films are checked on every frame, not a sample.** Checking every 12th frame passed a film that failed at every 2nd.
- **Alignment retries before failing.** One frame in two films scored 0,54 between neighbours scoring 0,98. The pack
  moves as one block, so a single frame cannot really differ: it was the aligner locking onto a wrong fit. The checker
  now retries two stricter fits and keeps the best. **A misspelled letter cannot be aligned away by one global fit**,
  and both negative controls still fail at the same scores.
- **The composite tool reads tilt from the shape's axis**, not a tight box (a tapered tube fooled the box into 6°),
  and treats glare and deep shadow on the placeholder as placeholder, not as fingers.

### 4.2 Known limits, stated before a client finds them
- **The placeholder must be the WHOLE product in one flat chroma colour, cap included, matte.** A white cap or a
  bright glare on the placeholder is left for Sol to paint out by hand. *(Put it in every prompt.)*
- **One master image per camera angle.** The pack is laid in flat; a 3/4 view needs a 3/4 master from the brand.
- **Thresholds were calibrated on one flat tube.** Before the first client of a new packaging type (pump, jar,
  dropper, glass), run three exact composites and confirm the planted-error control is caught. *(The control now runs
  automatically on every still, which is most of this check.)*
- **Films have their own calibrated rule, stated openly.**
  - Exact films bottom at a 5th percentile of 0,945–0,957, because every frame resamples the pack.
  - Maximum encoder quality (CRF 6) and a gentler zoom both leave it at 0,953, so compression is not the cause.
  - The film rule is therefore **worst tile ≥0,80 in every frame, 5th percentile ≥0,93**. The still rule
    (0,80 / 0,95) is unchanged, and so is the worst-tile bar, the one that catches a wrong word.
  - **Recalibrate on each new packaging type.**
- **Integration quality is not measured by any tool:** halo, contact shadow, cap tint, the gap where a finger meets
  the pack. Step 7 is manual and it is where the work looks cheap or expensive.

## 5. The guarantee and the report

**On the sheet** (OL1 + OL3, final):
> *"Garantia de embalagem: em nenhuma imagem e em nenhum quadro do filme a IA inventa, troca ou deforma uma letra, o
> logo ou a forma da sua embalagem, e a cor fica dentro da tolerância aprovada na ficha. Todo caractere legível é
> conferido peça a peça. Se alguma peça falhar, refazemos sem custo em até 5 dias úteis. Partes da embalagem podem
> ficar escondidas pela mão, pelo ângulo ou pelo foco — escondidas, nunca alteradas."*

**The report, per piece:** found / not found · tiles compared · share hidden · worst tile · 5th-percentile tile ·
planted-error control caught · APROVADA/REPROVADA. Under it, always: *"Confere a embalagem contra os seus arquivos. Não
é parecer regulatório."*

**What the guarantee does NOT cover** (contract clause 7): errors in the art the brand sent; text already illegible in
the master image; crops, filters or re-compression by Instagram, TikTok or marketplaces. **If a redo fails, the brand
gets that piece's share of the fee back** — never "redo only", because an adhesion contract cannot waive the remedy
(Código Civil art. 424, OL3).

**Why this is sellable, not decoration:** the buyer can check it without trusting her (`THE MACHINE TEST`, limb 2),
and it is the one thing the US$49 tool visibly could not do on the first real sample.

## 6. The law — who answers for what

### 6.1 The allocation (OL3; contract in `oferta/CONTRATO_PADRAO.md`, 16 clauses + 3 annexes)
- **The brand owns the claims.** Every phrase, number or seal in a piece comes word for word from the signed Ficha de
  Alegações; the brand declares each is on the notified label or substantiated (Lei 6.360 arts. 59 and 67 I; CDC arts.
  36 and 38; RDC 752/2022 art. 5 I).
- **She can still be reached, and the contract cannot stop it.** CDC art. 67 criminalises whoever *makes* an ad they
  "know or should know" misleads; CDC arts. 7 and 25 §1 make the chain jointly liable to consumers; Lei 6.437 art. 3
  reaches whoever "contributed"; CONAR names the whole creation chain. **So the protection is the PROCESS, not the
  paper:** the signed Ficha, the refusal list, the written approval, and images that show mood, never results.
- **Recourse and a cap:** the brand defends and reimburses any claim arising from its content (clause 6); her total
  liability is the fee for that order, no lost profits, media or recall costs (Código Civil art. 421-A).

### 6.2 What she never makes (clause 4)
Before/after · a result sequence · treatment or cure claims · lab coats, clinics, stethoscopes · a synthetic person
giving a testimonial or review · anyone resembling a real person · ingredients not in the formula · appeals aimed at
children.

### 6.3 The 12-line checklist, before every delivery (OL3 §b, in full there)
Report OK · phrases only from the Ficha · alert words removed unless on the label or substantiated (*trata, cura,
previne, clinicamente comprovado, %, em X dias, aprovado pela Anvisa, natural, vegano, cruelty-free…*) · image ≠
result · no lab coat · synthetic face resembles nobody and its generation log is saved · ingredients shown are on the
label · Grau 2 or children's products carry only label indications · **no client file sent to any cloud AI** ·
music and fonts licensed for client work, provenance marks kept · written "aprovado" filed · delivery note included.

### 6.4 AI labelling, copyright, data and calls
- **No law requires an "AI" label in Brazil today.** PL 2338/2023 is *"Aguardando Parecer"* (last event 2 Sep 2026);
  PL 841/2024 and PL 4349/2019 are pending; CONAR's 2026 guide applies its code to AI content but creates no label duty
  (Câmara open-data API, fetched 25 Sep). **TikTok requires a label on realistic AI people and scenes; Meta labels
  "AI info" from provenance marks.** So the delivery note tells the brand to switch the platform label on, and a
  voluntary *"imagem ilustrativa criada com IA"* goes wherever a synthetic person appears. **Re-check quarterly.**
- **Copyright:** the author is a natural person (Lei 9.610 art. 11), and purely AI-made elements may not be protected.
  So she sells an **exclusive licence without term plus a written cessão** of whatever rights she holds (arts. 49–50),
  and **her own promise not to reuse** — never "the images are yours" or "copyright". Sol signs a one-time internal
  cessão to her company (contract Anexo C).
- **The brand's own art:** the brand warrants it holds the photographer's and designer's rights (clause 10). **The
  PRÉVIA uses a public packshot the brand did not send:** ask permission on the call, confirm in writing, watermark it
  *"PRÉVIA — uso interno, não publicar"*, never post it, delete after 30 days without an order, and composite it
  locally.
- **Prospecting data (LGPD):** legitimate interest with a one-page balancing test; a sheet recording source, date and
  purpose for every contact; the first message names the source and says *"responda SAIR"*; delete non-responders after
  6 months; **never a bought list.** An ME needs no DPO but must publish a contact channel (ANPD Res. 2/2022).
- **Calls:** dial only published commercial numbers or numbers given in writing; otherwise e-mail or DM first for a
  slot. **Never close on the phone; close on a signed proposal** (SP Lei 17.832/2023). Whether São Paulo's
  telemarketing block list reaches a B2B call to a founder's mobile is **UNVERIFIED** — the written-slot rule makes it
  irrelevant.

## 13. What stays open, plainly

1. **Price.** Seller-side, R$2.990 is 43,5% of the cheapest verifiable traditional São Paulo launch with a film
   (R$6.878) and equals Make Move's AI minimum (R$3.000). **Buyer-side, every observed beauty-visual budget was under
   US$500 a project, and UGC runs R$100–250 a video.** Brands that shoot on a phone anchor at R$53–250 a piece, and to
   them R$2.990 looks dear. **The calls are the only instrument left.**
2. **Photoroom could move its guarantee down-market; Pixelcut's "On Model" could already keep labels.** Either removes
   part of what is scarce. Step 1 tests the second today; the first is a watch item.
3. **Price pressure from below:** AI done-for-you studios in São Paulo (Magotte R$1.080 for 20 images, Fábrica R$53 an
   image) will get cheaper. The guarantee, the film and the claim lock are the wedge; nothing else is.
4. **Tool terms can change under her** (Higgsfield changed once already). The pipeline is built so the generator is
   swappable.
5. **The integration step is manual and is where quality lives.** Only dry runs show whether her hand is good enough.
