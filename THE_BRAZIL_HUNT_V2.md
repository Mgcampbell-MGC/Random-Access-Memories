## ★★★ THE LAW THE HUNT NEEDED AND DID NOT HAVE

> ### **THE TEMPLATE FLOOR TEST — COMPULSION IS A DEMAND PROOF, NOT A PRICE PROOF.**
> A statutory duty guarantees the buyer must buy. It says nothing about **from whom** or **for how much**.
> Because the duty is public, identical across buyers, and discharged by **any conforming artefact**, the
> price is set by the cheapest conforming supplier — and for a document, that is a **template**.
>
> **Before proposing any compelled-artefact candidate: ask what the cheapest conforming version sells for
> today, and what forces the buyer to pay above it. If the only answer is "a signature," the price is the
> signature's market rate — not your number. Run it BEFORE the arithmetic.**

This is `THE RENEWAL SCISSORS` — already in CLAUDE.md as *"no statute can force an annual DOCUMENT to be
expensive"* — and **the hunt ran 35 candidates into it without invoking it once, including mine.**
