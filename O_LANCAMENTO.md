## PART A — the founder's version, as supplied (condensed)

**Positioning.** A productised VIRTUAL PRODUCTION company:

- not an AI-image generator;
- not a social-media, paid-media or branding agency.

The brand supplies:

- the real product assets;
- its identity;
- its approved claims.

O LANÇAMENTO supplies creative direction, a visual world, a key visual, campaign stills, a short film, cutdowns and
commerce assets. **"We do not sell images. We sell the campaign."**

**Customer.**

- Established Brazilian independent haircare and skincare brands with finished packaged products: ~10–100+ SKUs,
  several launches or refreshes a year, one decision-maker who can approve a few thousand reais, good packshots,
  no entrenched agency.
- **Start with:** masks, shampoo, conditioner, leave-in, oils, serums, cleanser, moisturiser, bodycare, simple
  salon products.
- **Avoid:** exact-shade makeup, complex fragrance bottles, application demos, before/after, medical claims,
  unfinished packaging.

**The core SKU — O LANÇAMENTO, R$2.990 per product** ("the initial commercial price to test"). It contains:

- 1 Campaign Key Visual;
- 6 planned campaign images (hero · environment · macro · texture/ingredient world · model/lifestyle where
  appropriate · clean commerce hero);
- 1 hero film ~15 s;
- 2 cutdowns of 5–8 s;
- 3 commerce assets;
- standard exports (vertical / square / landscape).

Delivery is 5–7 business days after complete inputs and Product Lock approval. There is one consolidated revision
round; fidelity or technical errors she introduces are fixed without using it.

**Later: A LINHA, ≈R$6.900–7.900.** Up to 3 products of one family in one campaign world. Only after the
one-product workflow is reliable.

**Inputs:**

- packshot;
- packaging artwork (AI/PDF);
- logo, colours and identity;
- approved product statements;
- the exact product name;
- optional references.

No discovery call, no questionnaire, no workshop.

**PRODUCT LOCK.** A Product Truth Sheet (shape, colour, cap, logo, label placement, visible copy, proportions,
artwork, approved claims and terms), approved once; every asset is checked against it. The guarantee:

> *"We do not knowingly deliver a campaign asset with a materially altered package, logo, label, product shape or
> approved brand color. Any fidelity error introduced by us is corrected at no extra charge."*

**CLAIM LOCK.** Every factual product statement in a delivered asset must come from client-approved copy. It is
not a regulatory opinion; it is a QA function.

**PRÉVIA DE CAMPANHA (the "puppy dog").**

- What it is: one private, watermarked, low-resolution key visual (optionally a 3–5 s motion) made from the
  brand's public or supplied packshot.
- Rules: A-grade prospects only; one per brand; no usable file, no licence, no revisions, no invented claims.
- Target: 10–20 min of founder attention once automated.
- **The sale becomes visual: "Can I show you what your product could look like?"**

**Outreach.** One short message offering the private preview, then:

> *"O LANÇAMENTO custa R$2.990, uma vez, com entrega em 5–7 dias."*

Then stop.

**Prospecting signals, scored:**

- **A:** ANVISA notifications. Treated as a company/product signal, then checked against the market; **explicitly
  NOT a guaranteed "75 days before launch" feed.**
- **B:** Instagram and brand-site launch words (*lançamento, novidade, em breve, nova linha, nova embalagem*).
- **C:** the Beauty Fair exhibitor directory.
- **D:** weak launch assets on a strong product.

**Creative process:** intake → Product Lock → 3 routes (Clean Luxury / Beauty Editorial / Product World) → the
client picks one → key visual first → *(truncated)*.

## PART C — what makes it better

**1. FIDELITY BY CONSTRUCTION, NOT BY INSPECTION: THE GENERATOR NEVER SEES THE LABEL.**

- The supplied guarantee (*"we do not knowingly deliver…"*) is honest but it is an inspection promise. It rests on
  catching the 20,1% of base generations that distort text or logos (Photoroom benchmark, 6 Jul 2026), on curved
  labels, which are the stated hard case.
- **Build every product pixel from the brand's own packshot and artwork, composited locally (Photoshop) into
  AI-made scenes.**
  - Product motion is a camera move over locked stills, or a ≤15° keyframed turn.
  - Supers and claims go in the editor.
  - The AI makes the world, never the bottle.
- **Then the guarantee can be absolute and checkable by the buyer's own eyes:** *"Todo caractere da embalagem é
  idêntico à arte aprovada — ou refazemos o asset sem custo."* That is `THE ACCEPTANCE FINDING` at a ticket where
  money alone can back the promise.
- Model shots with the product in hand: generate hand and pose, then composite the real pack in post.

**2. PRE-LAUNCH CONFIDENTIALITY — the same move solves it.**

- Every buyer found through a launch signal is, by definition, working on something unreleased.
- H6C read Higgsfield's terms as allowing training on inputs absent an Enterprise Agreement. **(VER1 is confirming
  this at the terms page.)**
- **Unreleased packaging artwork should never be uploaded to a generator.** With compositing it never needs to be.
  Say so in the proposal: *"sua arte nunca sai do nosso computador para uma IA."*
- It is a selling point that costs nothing.

**3. CLAIM LOCK MUST COVER WHAT AN IMAGE SHOWS, NOT ONLY WHAT IT SAYS.**

- Under Lei 6.360 art. 59, CDC art. 37 and CBAP art. 27 §2, **an image can be a misleading efficacy claim with no
  words at all**: an impossible shine, a skin texture the product cannot produce, a hair "transformation" sequence.
- **CDC art. 67 reaches whoever *"faz"* the ad**, and CBAP arts. 45(b)/46 name the whole creation chain, so the
  producer is exposed, not only the brand (H6C).
- Add a VISUAL claim rule to the lock: **models convey mood, never a result.**
  - No depicted outcome the approved claims do not support.
  - No before/after or implied sequence.
  - No skin or hair rendering beyond what the product claims.
- The client signs off per asset.
- Add a voluntary *"imagem ilustrativa criada com IA"* line wherever a model or result is shown. It is cheap
  insurance; no rule requires it today (CONAR Guia v6, 25 May 2026).

**4. THE PREVIEW MUST NOT DEPEND ON A PACKSHOT THAT DOES NOT EXIST YET.**

- **The ANVISA signal and the preview mechanic pull against each other.** The signal fires *before* the product
  is public, which is when a public packshot usually does not exist. **(VER1 is measuring how often.)**
- The fix: **make the preview from the brand's current BEST-SELLER's public packshot.** Then pitch the coming
  launch:
  > *"Isso é o [produto atual] no mundo de campanha. Quando o [novo] sair, fazemos o mesmo com ele."*
- The signal times the call; the bestseller supplies the picture.

**5. MEASURE THE PREVIEW FUNNEL; IT DECIDES THE HOURS.**

- At 4–5 sales a month, a preview→sale rate of 1 in 10 needs 40–50 previews a month. At 15–30 min each that is
  **10–25 h**, on top of:
  - production (4–5 launches × 6–10 h mature, 9,5–18 h at the start);
  - calls;
  - admin.
- **That fills 87 h.**
- ⇒ Add the preview→sale rate to the test with a bar committed in advance: **≥1 sale per 8 previews.**
- Below 1 in 15, the preview is too expensive to be the front door. Keep it for inbound only.

**6. REPEAT BUSINESS IS THE REAL ECONOMICS — PRICE AND SERVICE FOR IT.**

- A fitting brand runs a **median of 3 launch windows a year** (H6A, n=34, IQR 2–5). About 25–30 active brands at
  ~2 purchases a year sustain 4–5 launches a month.
- A brand that is happy after launch 1 is the cheapest sale in the business:
  - keep its Product Truth Sheet and campaign world on file;
  - offer the next launch at the same price, turned faster.
- A LINHA's cost advantage (reused world, light and cast) is the same mechanism.
- **Track the second-purchase rate from the first ten clients; it is the number that tells you whether this is a
  business or a treadmill.**

**7. THE TEST — R$0, Portuguese calls, bars committed in advance.**

- **(a) Price:** call 20 own-brand EPP/Demais hair/skin brands with a launch in the last 60 days or visible *"em
  breve"*. Ask what the last launch's images and video cost and who made them.
  - PASS if ≥6/20 name ≥R$2.500 of external spend per launch.
  - FAIL if ≤2.
- **(b) Preview:** send the preview to the 10 best of them.
  - PASS if ≥2 buy at R$2.990 within 30 days.
- Fold in the 12 venda-direta catalogue brands from `THE_SIXTH_HUNT.md` §3 as a second segment.
- **If (a) fails, stop — do not discount. The R$1.030–1.150 AI-studio band is already occupied by studios with
  published prices.**

**8. SMALLER CORRECTIONS**

- **Lead scoring:** drop "female founder publicly identified" as a criterion. It predicts nothing about buying and
  is a poor targeting rule. Use "a named decision-maker reachable by phone or WhatsApp."
- **Tax and entity:**
  - ME on Simples with CNAE 5911-1/02 (primary) + 7410-2/99; **avoid 7311-4/00** (publicity agency).
  - Set pro-labore at 28% of RBT12 so the fator r applies: **≈9,1–9,4% all-in on every tier** (AUD3).
  - Not MEI.
- **Payment:** 50% on order, 50% on delivery, by Pix against a nota fiscal. No card checkout (`INVOICE, DON'T
  CHECKOUT`).
- **Rights language:**
  - The client's packshot and artwork stay the client's.
  - AI-generated elements are licensed for the client's use.
  - **Do not warrant exclusivity or copyright in AI-generated elements.** No generator warrants it to her (H6C;
    Higgsfield ToS 26 Jul 2026).
- **Synthetic models:** use one consistent persona per client, never a real person's likeness, and keep a record
  of how each face was generated.
- **The treadmill:** self-serve label-lock is estimated at 12–18 months (H6D).
  - What survives it: taste, speed, the claim lock, the brand's campaign world on file, and repeat clients.
  - The supplied positioning ("the campaign, not images") is already the right defence. Keep the price at the
    campaign level; never itemise per image.

## PART D — O LANÇAMENTO, AUTOMATED: MORE CUSTOMERS, LESS OF HER TIME (founder's ask, 25 Sep 2026)

> *"must be a way for O LANÇAMENTO to make more .. automate it some how make it easy as anything so more customers
> can use it"*

**Automation alone cannot deliver this.** It cuts her cost and time; it does not create buyers or raise what they
pay. Four changes together do.

### D1 — Automate HER side: from ~9–15 h per launch to a target of 1,5–3 h

The customer's side stays: *send two files, pick one of three looks, approve.* Everything behind it becomes a
pipeline. **Her hours go only to taste, selection and the call.**

| Step | Today (manual) | Automated | Tool (verified 25 Sep) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intake | Emails, a Truth Sheet by hand | One link or WhatsApp form. Label PDF + packshot + approved claims arrive as FILES (C4-clean). The Product Truth Sheet is auto-drafted from the PDF (text, colours, proportions); the client approves one page | local script |
| Cutout | Photoshop | Automatic | Photoroom Remove Background API **US$0,02/image** (training opt-out in Data Control), or local open-source for unreleased art |
| Scenes | Prompting per client | **A library of pre-built "campaign worlds" per route (Clean Luxury / Beauty Editorial / Product World) that she curates ONCE and re-uses.** New scenes are generated WITHOUT the product in them | **Gemini API paid tier: *"Google doesn't use your prompts… or responses to improve our products"*** (free tier does, so never use it for client work); Photoroom AI Backgrounds/Shadows **US$0,10/image** |
| Composite | Hand work | **The label region is locked. Grade the world to the bottle, never the bottle to the world** | local |
| **Fidelity check** | Her eyes | **A machine compares every asset's label region with the approved packshot (pixel similarity + OCR of visible text against the PDF text) and writes a one-page *Relatório de Fidelidade* per asset: pass/fail.** Failures are regenerated before she ever sees them | local |
| Film | 3–6 h | 15 s built from the approved stills with depth-parallax camera moves rendered LOCALLY (no generator ever sees the label), supers from approved claims, licensed music, cutdowns automatic | local |
| Exports | 30–40 files by hand | Automatic: vertical / square / landscape + marketplace specs | local |

- **Cost per launch in API calls: roughly US$2–10.** (ESTIMATE: ~60 scene generations + cutouts.)
- **The fidelity report is the automation that sells.**
  - It turns the guarantee into something the buyer checks without trusting her (`THE MACHINE TEST`, limb 2).
  - VER1 found fidelity is table stakes at the cheap end, where every studio promises it and nobody proves it.
  - **Proving it on every asset is the differentiator.**

### D1b — THE STACK IS HIGGSFIELD (founder, 25 Sep 2026: *"HIGGSFIELD is what she would use"*)

Run the pipeline on Higgsfield. Photoroom and the Gemini API are fallbacks only. Higgsfield covers:

- image and video generation;
- background removal, outpainting and upscaling;
- consistent synthetic faces (Soul ID);
- product-photoshoot recipes;
- an agent-drivable interface.

**Plans and credits (primary, the Higgsfield plan page read this session):**

- **ULTRA US$129/month** (US$99 annual): 3.000 credits.

  - a full O LANÇAMENTO ≈700 credits ≈US$34;
  - a VITRINE ≈50–100 credits (ESTIMATE);
  - so PLUS carries ~1 full launch + several VITRINEs a month, and ULTRA is needed from ~2 full launches a

1. **No unreleased artwork goes into a self-serve plan.** `higgsfield.ai/terms-of-use-agreement` (26 Jul 2026)

2. **Fidelity is checked OUTSIDE Higgsfield.** Its own product-photoshoot workflow says *"Do not claim tiny text

   local check (label region vs approved art, plus OCR vs the PDF text).

**Say it to the client, because it sells:**

> *"Sua arte nunca entra numa IA antes do lançamento — e cada imagem sai com um relatório de fidelidade da
> embalagem."*

### D4 — The arithmetic after automation: capacity stops binding, demand is the only question

The bar is ≈R$12.200–15.800 gross a month.

| Mix (per month) | Gross | Her production hours |
|---|---|---|
| 2 LANÇAMENTO + 6 VITRINE + 4 EXTENSÃO | R$11.680 | ≈16 h |

- **Self-serve with no call** (a web checkout) is where Orne and Villo already live at R$5–20 an image. **Keep one

## PART F — the first real sample, and the offer sheet (25 Sep 2026)

### F1. Higgsfield on one real bestselling tube: the label test

The founder asked to *"run the Higgsfield sample on one real bestseller bottle… so you can see the actual quality."*
The subject was one real bestselling Brazilian skincare tube, a public packshot. Its label has rotated text and small
ingredient lines, which is the hard case. **The images stay out of this repository, because they show a real brand's
product.**

| | Method | Label result |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Higgsfield image model, packshot as reference (ran as `nano_banana_2`) | **FAILED: 2 errors.** *"CEKAMIDAS"*, *"alfo-bisaboiol"* |
| **B** | Higgsfield Marketing Studio, one click | **FAILED: 4 errors.** Product name, *"CERANIDAS"*, *"PANIENOL"*, *"biaobolol"* |
| **C** | **Her method.** Higgsfield generates the scene with a blank placeholder tube. The real packshot is composited locally over it with shading matched from the placeholder, and the label is checked locally. | **PASSED: text identical.** High-pass correlation on the label block **0,984** |

**What it proves:**
- **Both of the tool vendor's own best options misspell the label, including its one-click product-ad product.**
  The words they break are ingredient names, which is exactly where a brand's regulatory copy lives.
- **So the guarantee on the offer sheet is not decoration. It is the one thing the US$49 tool visibly cannot do.**
  It holds because the label never passes through the generator, which also means the generator never sees
  unreleased art (Higgsfield's terms, §4.4).

**What it does NOT prove:**
- **One product, three images.** A tube with a flat front label is the easy geometry. A curved bottle, a pump, glass
  and a metallic cap are UNTESTED. The composite relies on the scene's placeholder matching the product's
  silhouette, so it needs a placeholder per packaging shape.
- **The fidelity number measures structure, not meaning.** 0,984 says the label pixels were not redrawn. It does not
  check that the approved claims were used. The claim lock is still a human reading, until it is automated against
  the brand's approved-claims list.
- **Time was not measured as production time.** This was a first build, not a run of the pipeline. Part D6's
  ≤3 h per kit bar is still open.
- **Cost:** 6 of the trial's 7,88 credits. Some models are plan-gated: `gpt_image_2_5` requires a paid plan, and
  `flux_2` returned a 429 rate limit once before succeeding.
