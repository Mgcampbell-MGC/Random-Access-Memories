# The Plan

**Written 11 August 2026, after 48 dead candidates. For execution on resume.**

---

## 0. What "failure is not an option" can honestly mean

It cannot mean *guaranteed success*. The base rates are known and they are brutal: of 937
Stripe-verified indie founders, **54% earn nothing** and roughly **5% exceed US$8.333/month**. Anyone
who promises around that is selling something.

It can mean something harder and more useful:

> **Never fail for a reason we could have found for free. Never spend the budget on something a
> R$0 check would have killed. And never let a candidate die from a desk when it should have died on
> the phone — or survive at a desk when the phone would have ended it.**

That is achievable, and the last two days proved it: **two front-runners killed in 48 hours, for
nothing, before a single call.** That is not failure. That is the machine working. The failure mode we
must actually prevent is different and specific — **spending eight weeks building the wrong thing**,
which nearly happened twice.

- The last two — **FECHA MÊS** and **ESCALA DA FESTA** — were killed by the same four-part sequence,

**The honest diagnosis: we have been screening candidates when we should have been screening
segments.** Neither finalist died of a product flaw. Fecha Mês died because trade-marketing agencies
number 108–500, not 179.702. Escala died because buffets with a real roster problem number 2.000–8.000,
not 128.000. **Both were segment failures wearing candidate clothes.**

## 4. The plan

### PHASE 0 — Build the Segment Register · 5 days · R$0 · *This has never been done and every kill needed it*

Take the **top 60 Brazilian CNAEs by non-MEI company count** and put each through a fixed audit:

1. **IBGE CEMPRE count** (excludes MEI) — the real firm count, not the CNPJ count
2. **Size distribution** — firms with 10+ and 20+ people occupied (CEMPRE agg. 9529, PAS)
3. **The occupation-name test** — grep Anexo XI for that CNAE. Does it read *"independente"*?
4. **The counter-terminal test** — does this buyer's customer pay across a card machine? If yes, an
   acquirer funds vertical software to zero. **Segment excluded at source.**
5. **Phone contactability** from the Receita base, by porte
6. **The ERP price floor** — is there a Brazilian vertical ERP at R$99–299 covering this trade?
7. **Association reality check** — but read correctly: for *professional* trades a directory is the
   universe; for *local retail-like* trades it is not

**Output: a permanent, ranked register of segments that genuinely hold 20.000+ real operating firms,
reachable by phone, with no counter terminal and no whole-ERP floor.** Reusable forever. Every future
candidate starts here instead of dying here.

**Gate 0:** if fewer than eight segments pass, the constraint set itself is the problem and we say so
before spending another week.

### PHASE 1 — Generate against verified segments only · 4 days

Multiple lenses, reversed screen (**adoption, not existence**), but pointed **exclusively** at
Phase 0 survivors. No candidate may be proposed against an unaudited segment. Every schema carries a
`whatIEliminatedAndWhy` field — a zero-candidate run must still return its value.

**Target: 20–30 candidates.**

### PHASE 2 — Run the eight-test gauntlet · 3 days · R$0

In the order in §3. Cheapest killer first. **A candidate that fails any test stops immediately** — no
appeal, no repositioning. *Do not reinterpret a failure as "we need better positioning."*

**Gate 1:** at least two candidates clear all eight. If none does, return to Phase 1 once. If a second
pass also returns nothing, the constraint set is binding and that becomes the conversation.

### PHASE 3 — Contact · 12 days · under R$500 · **this is the phase that has never happened**

**This is the forcing function and it is non-negotiable: she is on the phone by day 25, with whatever
survived, even if it is imperfect.**

- Build the list from the Receita base, filtered to non-MEI with a phone, in São Paulo first
- **200–250 dials**, not 120 — a 5-in-120 threshold expects 2,4 closes at a true 2% and would kill a
  working business on noise
- Open with the **prefilled-claim** shape: an artefact about *them*, then *"posso te mandar?"*
- **Log the disqualifying question on every single call.** Fifty owners answer for free the thing no
  amount of research could settle
- **Pass bar, adopted verbatim from the best external memo we received:**
  > *"Interest, deposits, positive feedback and 'we would use this again' do not count."*
  Money committed, on the call, or it did not happen.

**Gate 2:** ≥2 unrelated prepayments. Below that, the candidate stops and Phase 1 restarts — but with
something no previous round has ever had: **real buyer language, real objections, and a measured
conversion rate.**

### PHASE 4 — Build only after money · 2–3 weeks

Smallest sellable version. **AI reads and drafts; code decides and calculates money.** No credential
custody, no per-transaction founder labour, no Meta dependency in the core promise. No-code stack with
Brazilian rails: **Clicksign** for contracts, **Asaas** for Pix and payouts, Supabase with row-level
security.

## 6. What changes about how I work

Four commitments, each earned by a specific failure of mine in this record:

1. **No number reaches her without its source and its unit checked.** I produced *"R$11.750 per client
   per month"* by dividing a global 2024 revenue figure by a *"+900 brands worldwide"* marketing claim
   from a different page. I gave a segment count for one subclass that exceeded IBGE's count for the
   whole class. **Numerator and denominator must share a source, a date and a unit.**
2. **Segment before candidate. Always.** Both finalists died on facts a one-hour audit would have
   surfaced.
3. **Every hunt schema carries `whatIEliminatedAndWhy`.** Twelve lenses once returned zero and their
   elimination work survived only in transcripts.
4. **I flag my own uncertainty before the user has to find it.** Three of my four stated concerns about
   Escala were wrong. That is worse than saying nothing, because it aims the search at phantoms.

## 7. The decisions that are hers, not mine

I will not spend another week guessing at these:

1. **Is R$30.000/month in 12 months still the target?** Half of what has been killed would survive at
   R$10.000/month, or at R$30.000 in three years. This single number has done more filtering than any
   law in the file.
2. **Does the audience/network exclusion still hold?** It has been named as one of only three
   load-bearing constraints since candidate 11 and has never moved. An **anonymous topic page** is
   already permitted and has never been used.
3. **How much per-transaction work is genuinely unacceptable?** "No founder labour" killed several
   candidates that would work at two hours a week.
4. **Which buyer would she genuinely enjoy for two years?** She rejected a fully validated candidate on
   this, correctly. It is a real constraint and it is anti-correlated with Law 0 — the buyers she likes
   mostly take cards at a counter, and acquirers fund their software to zero.
