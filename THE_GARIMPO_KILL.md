## What was true and is worth keeping
1. **The statutory bar on foreign bidders is real** — *"Estrangeiro somente poderá participar mediante a
   procuração em nome de um representante legal estabelecido no Brasil"*, plus CPF and Receita regularity.
   **It is real and it is small**, because the foreign trade does not run through Brazilian auction rooms; it
   runs through Brazilian dealers who already export.
2. **My asking-versus-clearing correction stands and was the right instinct** — Wright sold a Jangada chair
   *and* ottoman at **US$7.560** against a **US$18.500** asking. It just was not the binding problem.
3. **A live sourcing-agent market for Brazilian furniture does exist** — Vista Furniture Co., 100+ certified
   manufacturers across Bento Gonçalves, São Paulo, Minas, Paraná and Santa Catarina, coordinating factory
   visits and sample shipments for international buyers. **But that is NEW furniture from factories, not
   vintage** — it is the maker-desk shape, which ApexBrasil already undercuts at zero.
